## Grant blitz to spells you cast, priced at their mana cost

Size: **medium**. 1 card waits on it.

**Henzie "Toolbox" Torre** {B}{R}{G} — Legendary Creature — Devil Rogue (NCC)
> Each creature spell you cast with mana value 4 or greater has blitz. The blitz cost is equal to its mana cost. (You may choose to cast that spell for its blitz cost. If you do, it gains haste and "When this creature dies, draw a card." Sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step.)
> Blitz costs you pay cost {1} less for each time you've cast your commander from the command zone this game.

**The clause.** Each creature spell you cast with mana value 4 or greater has blitz. The blitz cost is equal to its mana cost. / Blitz costs you pay cost {1} less for each time you've cast your commander from the command zone this game.

**The gap.** A permanent cannot grant blitz: checkGrantedAlternativeCosts (server/internal/cards/effects/granted_alternative_cost.go, the o.Dash || o.Blitz || o.Sneak refusal) panics on a granted offer carrying Blitz, and game/granted_alternative_cost.go has no entry hook that applies blitz's haste, draw grant and end-step sacrifice off a granted key. Extend game.GrantedAlternativeCost the way granted evoke and granted warp were (GrantedAltCostEvoke, effects/granted_evoke.go): a 'granted-blitz' key with PricedAtManaCost, a MinManaValue 4 creature filter, the riders applied through game.ApplyBlitzRidersForEffect, and a reduction that applies to blitz costs only (printed and granted) counted from commander casts.

**Checked.** grep -i 'has blitz', 'GrantBlitz', 'blitz cost is equal' over effects+game comments: 0. grep Blitz in granted_alternative_cost.go: only the refusal (effects :383) and onlyMana (game :576). Precedents that DO exist: PricedAtManaCost (Herigast emerge), EvokeForSpellsYouCastFromHand, commander-cast counts ('cast your commander from the command zone this game' in 18 files, e.g. commanders_insight.go).
