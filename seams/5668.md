## Build casualty X with a modified copy

Size: **medium**. 1 card waits on it.

**Ob Nixilis, the Adversary** {1}{B}{R} — Legendary Planeswalker — Nixilis (SNC)
> Casualty X. The copy isn't legendary and has starting loyalty X. (As you cast this spell, you may sacrifice a creature with power X. When you do, copy this spell. The copy becomes a token.)
> +1: Each opponent loses 2 life unless they discard a card. If you control a Demon or Devil, you gain 2 life.
> −2: Create a 1/1 red Devil creature token with "When this token dies, it deals 1 damage to any target."
> −7: Target player draws seven cards and loses 7 life.

**The clause.** Casualty X. The copy isn't legendary and has starting loyalty X.

**The gap.** Casualty(n) (server/internal/cards/effects/casualty.go) is a fixed 'creature with power n or greater' sacrifice, and the copy is the engine's own trigger (game/casualty.go resolveCasualty), which copies with no exceptions. Needed: a casualty cost whose N is announced as X (any creature may be sacrificed, X is its power, read from the sacrificed permanent's last-known power on PaidCost), and a copy exception on that trigger (not legendary, starting loyalty X) for a permanent-spell copy that becomes a token.

**Checked.** grep -i 'Casualty X', 'CasualtyX', 'casualty.*XValue' over effects+game: no implementation. Copy exceptions on spell copies exist elsewhere ('copy ... isn't legendary' in 31 files, double_major.go), and 'copy of a permanent spell becomes a token' in 17 files, so only the casualty trigger and the variable cost are missing. The Devil token of the -2 exists (pugnacious_pugilist.go, tibalt_rakish_instigator.go).
