## Add 'whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed' (CR 702.122e)

Size: **medium**. 2 cards wait on it.

**Mobilizer Mech** {1}{U} — Artifact — Vehicle (NEO)
> Flying
> Whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed, up to one other target Vehicle you control becomes an artifact creature until end of turn.
> Crew 3 (Tap any number of creatures you control with total power 3 or more: This Vehicle becomes an artifact creature until end of turn.)

**Mindlink Mech** {2}{U} — Artifact — Vehicle (NEO)
> Flying
> Whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed for the first time each turn, until end of turn, this Vehicle becomes a copy of target nonlegendary creature that crewed it this turn, except it's 4/3, it's a Vehicle artifact in addition to its other types, and it has flying.
> Crew 1

**The clause.** "Whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed, ..." (Mobilizer Mech); "Whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed for the first time each turn, until end of turn, this Vehicle becomes a copy of target nonlegendary creature that crewed it this turn, except it's 4/3, it's a Vehicle artifact in addition to its other types, and it has flying." (Mindlink Mech)

**The gap.** CR 702.122e: the trigger is 'whenever a crew ability of this Vehicle resolves'. The engine only emits EventCrewedVehicle, once per creature tapped while the cost is paid (game/events.go:1118, game/activated.go:2378), which is the wrong moment and the wrong count. Saddle already has the resolution-time sibling EventBecameSaddled; crew has none. Expect a new EventKind emitted from the crew effect's resolution (effects CrewEffect / game/activated.go), a trigger helper in triggers_common.go, and 'for the first time each turn' on top of it. Mindlink Mech then composes become_copy.go's until-end-of-turn copy with except edits and CrewedThis(...) for the target.

**Checked.** git grep -i 'BecameCrewed|became_crewed|EventCrew' internal/game: only EventCrewedVehicle. 'becomes crewed' in effects: two comments (ethersworn_shieldmage.go, spring_loaded_sawblades.go: 'whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed would not see it; nothing on the card asks'), no trigger. 'becomes crewed for the first time': 0. 'crewed it this turn': getaway_car.go and 4 others use CrewedThis, so the target clause exists. become_copy.go:196 has 'becomes a copy of target X until end of turn'. cr.txt line 4903 = 702.122e.
