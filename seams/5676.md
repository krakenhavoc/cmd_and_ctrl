## Make exploiting an event other permanents can watch

Size: **small**. 8 cards wait on it.

**Skull Skaab** {U}{B} — Creature — Zombie (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> Whenever a creature you control exploits a nontoken creature, create a 2/2 black Zombie creature token.

**Diver Skaab** {3}{U}{U} — Creature — Zombie (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, target creature's owner puts it on their choice of the top or bottom of their library.

**Fell Stinger** {2}{B} — Creature — Zombie Scorpion (VOW)
> Deathtouch
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, target player draws two cards and loses 2 life.

**Graf Reaver** {1}{B} — Creature — Zombie Warrior (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, destroy target planeswalker.
> At the beginning of your upkeep, this creature deals 1 damage to you.

**Mindleech Ghoul** {1}{B} — Creature — Zombie (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, each opponent exiles a card from their hand.

**Repository Skaab** {3}{U} — Creature — Zombie (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, return target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand.

**Rot-Tide Gargantua** {3}{B}{B} — Creature — Zombie Kraken (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, each opponent sacrifices a creature of their choice.

**Stitched Assistant** {2}{U} — Creature — Zombie (VOW)
> Exploit (When this creature enters, you may sacrifice a creature.)
> When this creature exploits a creature, scry 1, then draw a card. (To scry 1, look at the top card of your library, then you may put that card on the bottom.)

**The clause.** Whenever a creature you control exploits a nontoken creature, create a 2/2 black Zombie creature token.

**The gap.** Exploit is modelled in cards/effects/exploit.go as a reflexive trigger private to the exploiting card (exploitSacrificed -> ReflexiveTrigger); nothing is published, so Skull Skaab cannot see another creature's exploit, or tell that the sacrificed creature was a nontoken. Needs an event (exploiter, sacrificed object with last-known information) raised when the sacrifice settles, in game's event kinds and Exploit's keyed run. The other seven exploit cards use the existing Exploit(cardName, payoff) as is and ride with this item because they share the helper.

**Checked.** grep -rl 'Exploit' server/internal/game: 0 files. grep -rnE 'EventExploit|Exploited' game: 0 hits. grep 'exploits a' cards/effects: only exploit.go and its test. Exploit users on develop: overcharged_amalgam.go.
