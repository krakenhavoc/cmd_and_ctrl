## Add a flashback cost that removes X counters and sets the spell's X

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Light Up the Night** {X}{R} — Sorcery (MID)
> Light Up the Night deals X damage to any target. It deals X plus 1 damage instead if that target is a creature or planeswalker.
> Flashback—{3}{R}, Remove X loyalty counters from among planeswalkers you control. If you cast this spell this way, X can't be 0. (You may cast this card from your graveyard for its flashback cost. Then exile it.)

**The clause.** Flashback—{3}{R}, Remove X loyalty counters from among planeswalkers you control. If you cast this spell this way, X can't be 0.

**The gap.** AlternativeCost.RemoveCounters (game/alternative_cost.go:342-363, #5390) is the right component but is fixed-count only: its comment says effects.Register holds it 'to the among form with a from clause and a fixed count'. Here the number removed is announced by the caster, must be at least 1, and becomes the spell's X (the printed cost {X}{R} is not paid). Extend CounterRemovalCost.Variable (already used by activated abilities) to the cast path, feed the announced total into the cast's X, and add a FlashbackRemoveCounters constructor in effects/alternative_cost.go beside FlashbackSacrifice.

**Checked.** grep -rniE 'loyalty counters from among|Remove X loyalty' effects game: 0. grep -n 'RemoveCounters: ' effects/alternative_cost.go: one use (line 347, fixed N). game/counter_cost.go:60-110: Variable exists for abilities ('any number', Mage-Ring Network).
