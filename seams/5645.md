## Grant jump-start to graveyard cards that are exactly two colours

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Niv-Mizzet, Supreme** {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} — Legendary Creature — Dragon Avatar (MAT)
> Flying, hexproof from monocolored
> Each instant and sorcery card in your graveyard that's exactly two colors has jump-start. (You may cast that card from your graveyard by discarding a card in addition to paying its other costs. Then exile it.)

**The clause.** Each instant and sorcery card in your graveyard that's exactly two colors has jump-start.

**The gap.** A granted graveyard keyword is a game.CastPermission with an AltCostKey (Lier: "flashback"). server/internal/game/cast_permission.go resolves granted keys case by case (miracle, sneak, mayhem, madness, harmonize, flashback) and has none for jump-start, whose price is the card's mana cost PLUS discarding a card (CR 702.133a) and exile on leaving the stack; and PermissionFilter can say ColorsAny but not 'exactly two colours' of the card in the zone. Both are additive fields/branches in cast_permission.go; the offer itself can reuse cmdr2S39JumpStart's shape. Hexproof from monocolored already exists.

**Checked.** grep -rliE 'ha(s|ve) jump-start|ha(s|ve) flashback|ha(s|ve) escape' -> lier_disciple_of_the_drowned.go, underworld_breach.go, iroh_grand_lotus.go (flashback/escape only). grep -i 'jump-start' in server/internal/cards/effects/ -> radical_idea.go and cmdr2_s39_helpers.go (the card's OWN alternative cost, not a grant). grep 'jump-start|JumpStart' in server/internal/game -> nothing. Read PermissionFilter's fields: no colour-count filter.
