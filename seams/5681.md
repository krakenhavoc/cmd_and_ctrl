## Add a static 'roll one more die and ignore the lowest' replacement and carry roll modifiers on the roll

Size: **medium**. 9 cards wait on it.

**Pixie Guide** {1}{U} — Creature — Faerie (AFR)
> Flying
> Grant an Advantage — If you would roll one or more dice, instead roll that many dice plus one and ignore the lowest roll.

**Barbarian Class** {R} — Enchantment — Class (AFR)
> (Gain the next level as a sorcery to add its ability.)
> If you would roll one or more dice, instead roll that many dice plus one and ignore the lowest roll.
> {1}{R}: Level 2
> Whenever you roll one or more dice, target creature you control gets +2/+0 and gains menace until end of turn.
> {2}{R}: Level 3
> Creatures you control have haste.

**Farideh, Devil's Chosen** {2}{U}{R} — Legendary Creature — Tiefling Warlock (AFR)
> Dark One's Own Luck — Whenever you roll one or more dice, Farideh gains flying and menace until end of turn. If any of those results was 10 or higher, draw a card.

**Netherese Puzzle-Ward** {3}{U} — Enchantment (AFC)
> Focus Beam — At the beginning of your upkeep, roll a d4. Scry X, where X is the result.
> Perfect Illumination — Whenever you roll a die's highest natural result, draw a card.

**Critical Hit** {1}{R} — Instant (AFR)
> Target creature gains double strike until end of turn.
> When you roll a natural 20, return this card from your graveyard to your hand. (A natural 20 is a roll that displays 20 on the die.)

**The Deck of Many Things** {5} — Legendary Artifact (AFR)
> {2}, {T}: Roll a d20 and subtract the number of cards in your hand. If the result is 0 or less, discard your hand.
> 1—9 | Return a card at random from your graveyard to your hand.
> 10—19 | Draw two cards.
> 20 | Put a creature card from any graveyard onto the battlefield under your control. When that creature dies, its owner loses the game.

**Danse Macabre** {3}{B}{B} — Sorcery (AFC)
> Each player sacrifices a nontoken creature of their choice. Roll a d20 and add the toughness of the creature you sacrificed this way.
> 1—14 | Return a creature card put into a graveyard this way to the battlefield under your control.
> 15+ | Return up to two creature cards put into graveyards this way to the battlefield under your control.

**Revivify** {2}{W} — Instant (AFC)
> Roll a d20 and add the number of creature cards in your graveyard that were put there from the battlefield this turn.
> 1—14 | Return all creature cards in your graveyard that were put there from the battlefield this turn to your hand.
> 15+ | Return those cards from your graveyard to the battlefield.

**Song of Inspiration** {3}{G}{G} — Instant (AFC)
> Choose up to two target permanent cards in your graveyard. Roll a d20 and add the total mana value of those cards.
> 1—14 | Return those cards to your hand.
> 15+ | Return those cards to your hand. You gain life equal to their total mana value.

**The clause.** "If you would roll one or more dice, instead roll that many dice plus one and ignore the lowest roll." (Pixie Guide, Barbarian Class). "Roll a d20 and subtract the number of cards in your hand." / "Roll a d20 and add the toughness of the creature you sacrificed this way." / "...add the number of creature cards in your graveyard that were put there from the battlefield this turn." / "...add the total mana value of those cards." read together with "If any of those results was 10 or higher, draw a card." (Farideh), "Whenever you roll a die's highest natural result" (Netherese Puzzle-Ward) and "When you roll a natural 20" (Critical Hit).

**The gap.** (a) The only dice replacement is the one-shot ScopedEffect ModRollExtraDieIgnoreOne in game/dice_roll.go, whose extra die is ignored by a chooser. A permanent's static replacement on RepEventRollDice that adds a die and raises DiceRoll.IgnoreLowest is missing (two of them stack: two more dice, two lowest ignored; CR 706.6 lets the roller pick among tied lowest). Expect: a card-side ReplacementEffect on RepEventRollDice in dice_roll.go/replacements.go plus a helper in effects/random_effects.go, with a Label. (b) EventRollDie.Amount is the face shown and nothing carries the modifier, so a trigger cannot tell the natural result from the result (CR 706.2). Expect: a Modifier on game.DiceRoll and on the EventRollDie event (additive snapshot field), RollDice{Modifier}, and WheneverYouRollDice handing the tail both. The four modifier cards then use it instead of adding inside their tails as diviners_portent.go does; the natural-result triggers must keep reading the unmodified face.

**Checked.** grep RepEventRollDice server/internal: only game/ (dice_roll.go, replacements.go, pending_choice.go, zone_route.go, scoped_replacements.go), no card. Normalized grep of effects/*.go for 'ignore the lowest roll', 'plus one and ignore', 'highest natural result', 'natural 20', 'subtract the number' = 0 files each. grep 'Natural|Modifier|Modified' game/events*.go game/effect_api_random.go = nothing. Read dice_roll.go header, random_effects.go, diviners_portent.go, CR 706.2 and 706.6 in cmdr/cr.txt.
