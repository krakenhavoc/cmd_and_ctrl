## Add an 'exile a spell you control' activation cost

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Synthesis Pod** {3}{U/P} — Artifact (ONC)
> ({U/P} can be paid with either {U} or 2 life.)
> {1}{U/P}, {T}, Exile a spell you control: Target opponent reveals cards from the top of their library until they reveal a card with mana value equal to 1 plus the exiled spell's mana value. Exile that card, then that player shuffles. You may cast that exiled card without paying its mana cost.

**The clause.** {1}{U/P}, {T}, Exile a spell you control: Target opponent reveals cards from the top of their library until they reveal a card with mana value equal to 1 plus the exiled spell's mana value. ...

**The gap.** No AbilityCost moves an object from the stack to exile as a cost and remembers its mana value for the effect. Expect a new cost in cards/effects/activated.go beside ExileAPermanentYouControl / ExileFromHand, with the stack removal in game/activated.go and the exiled spell's last known mana value on the paid cost. The {U/P} in an activation cost already exists (game/activated.go).

**Checked.** Listed every 'func X(...) game.AbilityCost' in activated.go: exile costs cover permanents, hand, graveyard, library top and the source, none the stack. grep -i 'Exile a spell you control|ExileSpellCost|exile.{0,20}spell you control': 0 hits.
