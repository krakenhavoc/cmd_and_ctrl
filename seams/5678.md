## Replace a life total being reduced to 0 or less

Size: **medium**. 1 card waits on it.

**Enduring Angel // Angelic Enforcer** — Creature — Angel // Creature — Angel (MID)
> Enduring Angel: Flying, double strike
> You have hexproof.
> If your life total would be reduced to 0 or less, instead transform this creature and your life total becomes 3. Then if this creature didn't transform this way, you lose the game.
> //
> Angelic Enforcer: Flying
> You have hexproof.
> Angelic Enforcer's power and toughness are each equal to your life total.
> Whenever this creature attacks, double your life total.

**The clause.** If your life total would be reduced to 0 or less, instead transform this creature and your life total becomes 3. Then if this creature didn't transform this way, you lose the game.

**The gap.** The only life floor is game/life_floor.go (PlayerStatic.LifeFloorOne, Angel's Grace): it clamps DAMAGE at 1 and has no 'instead' body. Enduring Angel replaces any event that would take the life total to 0 or less (damage, life loss, a set-life effect), runs a body (transform, set life to 3), and must know whether the transform happened (CR 701.28 can't-transform cases, a second Angel already transformed). Expect a new player-level replacement beside life_floor.go, applied where damage and life loss settle, plus a 'did it transform' result from the transform call.

**Checked.** grep -rniE 'reduced to 0 or less' effects: 0. grep -rniE 'life total would|LifeFloor|less than 1' effects game: angels_grace.go and game/life_floor.go only (damage only). grep -rniE 'EventWouldLose|WouldLoseGame|LoseGameReplacement' game: 0. grep 'didn.t transform|TransformForEffect.*bool' effects game: only CanTransformForEffect (game/cant_transform.go:55). captive_audience.go has 'your life total becomes 4' for the set-life half.
