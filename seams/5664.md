## Add 'it can't phase in for as long as you control this'

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**The Phasing of Zhalfir** {2}{U}{U} — Enchantment — Saga (DMU)
> Read ahead (Choose a chapter and start with that many lore counters. Add one after your draw step. Skipped chapters don't trigger. Sacrifice after III.)
> I, II — Another target nonland permanent phases out. It can't phase in for as long as you control this Saga.
> III — Destroy all creatures. For each creature destroyed this way, its controller creates a 2/2 black Phyrexian creature token.

**The clause.** Another target nonland permanent phases out. It can't phase in for as long as you control this Saga.

**The gap.** The only phase-in lock is the world rule PermanentsCantPhaseIn (game/restrictions.go:140, read in game/phasing.go:288), which stops every permanent. Needs a lock on one phased-out object tied to a source and its controller, ending when the Saga leaves or changes controller (so the permanent phases in at its controller's next untap step after the Saga is sacrificed), and saved in a snapshot (additive). Chapter III is ordinary but declares Sweep: destroy all creatures, each controller creates a 2/2 black Phyrexian token per creature destroyed (new token row). Read ahead exists.
