**The clause.** Enlist (As this creature attacks, you may tap a nonattacking creature you control without summoning sickness. When you do, add its power to this creature's until end of turn.) / Whenever this creature enlists a creature, scry 2.

**The gap.** CR 702.154: an optional cost to attack (CR 508.1g) paid by tapping another untapped creature that was not declared as an attacker and has haste or has been under your control since the turn began, plus a linked trigger giving +X/+0. Nothing in internal/game offers a per-attacker optional tap of ANOTHER creature at declaration. Expect to extend game/attackers.go beside the exert choice (attackers.go:105-121, game/exert.go, ADR 0130), the declare-attackers protocol/client prompt (which creature is enlisted), the legal probe, the bot's declaration, and an 'enlisted' event for Guardian of New Benalia's trigger. An enlisted creature cannot also be an attacker, and one creature cannot be tapped for two enlists.

**Checked.** grep -rli enlist server/internal/game -> none; cards/effects -> cenns_enlistment.go (card name only), retrace_followups_test.go. grep 'enlists a creature' -> 0. grep 'OptionalAttackCost|AttackCostChoice|508.1g' in game -> only exert files (attackers.go, exert.go, declaration_control.go, card.go, mutations.go), which tap/exert the attacker itself.
