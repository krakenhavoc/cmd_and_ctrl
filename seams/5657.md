## Add offering: sacrifice a permanent as you cast, reduce by its mana cost, cast at instant speed

Size: **medium**. 1 card waits on it.

**Blast-Furnace Hellkite** {7}{R}{R} — Creature — Dragon (BRC)
> Artifact offering (You may cast this spell as though it had flash by sacrificing an artifact and paying the difference in mana costs between this and the sacrificed artifact. Mana cost includes color.)
> Flying, double strike
> Creatures attacking your opponents have double strike.

**The clause.** Artifact offering (You may cast this spell as though it had flash by sacrificing an artifact and paying the difference in mana costs between this and the sacrificed artifact. Mana cost includes color.)

**The gap.** CR 702.48a-c: an OPTIONAL additional cost chosen at CR 601.2b that (a) reduces the total cost by the sacrificed permanent's MANA COST symbol by symbol (coloured reduces the same colour, excess reduces generic), and (b) grants instant timing only when it is paid. Emerge (effects/alternative_cost.go:515) is the nearest: it is an alternative cost, reduces generic by mana value, and has no timing leg. Expect an Offering(match) helper beside Emerge, a cost-reduction-by-mana-cost step in game's cast cost computation, and the legal-timing probe in server/internal/legal offering the flash cast only with a sacrifice chosen.

**Checked.** grep -rnE '702\.48|[Oo]ffering \(|(artifact|fox|rat|snake|goblin|moonfolk) offering|difference in mana costs' effects game -> no hit. grep 'offering' game/keywords.go -> one unrelated comment. No Patron of the Orochi/Kitsune/etc. in the catalog. The rest of the card ("Creatures attacking your opponents have double strike") is an ordinary static.
