## Add protection from colorless as a quality and as a choice

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Angelic Intervention** {1}{W} — Instant (MOM)
> Target creature or planeswalker you control gains protection from colorless or from the color of your choice until end of turn. If it's a creature, put a +1/+1 counter on it. (It can't be blocked, targeted, dealt damage, enchanted, or equipped by anything with that quality.)

**The clause.** "Target creature or planeswalker you control gains protection from colorless or from the color of your choice until end of turn."

**The gap.** game/protection.go's ProtectionQualityKind has Color, AnyColor, Monocolored, multicolored, card type, subtype, mana value … but no colorless quality (CR 702.16a allows any characteristic value); parseQuality mints no token for a quality it cannot parse. The choose-a-colour prompt (color_choice.go protectionFromChosenColor, center_soul.go) needs a sixth option.

**Checked.** grep -i colorless game/protection.go: 0; grep 'protection from colorless', 'from colorless' effects: 0; 9 files with 'protection from the color of your choice' (center_soul.go, blessed_breath.go, emerge_unscathed.go).
