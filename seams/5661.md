## Add 'mana abilities of this land cost an additional 1 life'

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Thran Portal** — Land — Gate (DMU)
> This land enters tapped unless you control two or fewer other lands.
> As this land enters, choose a basic land type.
> This land is the chosen type in addition to its other types.
> Mana abilities of this land cost an additional 1 life to activate.

**The clause.** Mana abilities of this land cost an additional 1 life to activate.

**The gap.** Thran Portal is the chosen basic land type in addition, so its mana ability is the intrinsic one of that type (CR 305.6, game/mana_ability_text.go), not a printed ability a card file can price with PayLife. Needs a cost increase in life on every mana ability of one permanent, including intrinsic and granted ones. CostModifier (game/cost_modifier.go) has Activations but its Unit is a mana cost; the only life surcharge is TargetTax.Life (target_tax.go), for spells. Auto-tap and the bot must know the land costs life. The other three lines have precedents: fastlands.go / conditional_enters_tapped.go ('two or fewer other lands') and land_types.go ChooseBasicLandTypeAsEnters, attachments.go SetsChosenBasicLandType (multiversal_passage.go).

**Checked.** grep 'Mana abilities of this land cost' -> 0; 'cost an additional|costs an additional .* life' -> spec.go, target_tax.go, terror_of_the_peaks.go (spells that target, not activations); grep -i 'LifeIncrease|AdditionalLife|ExtraLife' in game -> none.
