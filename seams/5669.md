## Add 'untap N tapped creatures you control' as an activation cost

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Halo Fountain** {2}{W} — Artifact (SNC)
> {W}, {T}, Untap a tapped creature you control: Create a 1/1 green and white Citizen creature token.
> {W}{W}, {T}, Untap two tapped creatures you control: Draw a card.
> {W}{W}{W}{W}{W}, {T}, Untap fifteen tapped creatures you control: You win the game.

**The clause.** {W}, {T}, Untap a tapped creature you control: ... / {W}{W}, {T}, Untap two tapped creatures you control: ... / {W}{W}{W}{W}{W}, {T}, Untap fifteen tapped creatures you control: You win the game.

**The gap.** game.AbilityCost (game/activated.go) has TapOthers (tap untapped permanents) but no component that untaps tapped ones. Add its mirror (an UntapOthers *TapOthersCost or a flag on TapOthersCost), with the picker wording, the enumerator in server/internal/legal and the protocol list it rides on.

**Checked.** Listed every field of AbilityCost: Tap, TapOthers, Waterbend, Sacrifice*, Mana*, Life*, Energy*, Loyalty*, Crew, Saddle, RemoveCounters, AddCounter, Blight, Discard*, Reveal*, ReturnToHand, Exile*, ReturnSelf, BottomSelf, *Grantor, UnattachEquipment, Exert, PutFromHandOnLibraryTop, ExileFromLibraryTop, Mill, Either, Forage: none untaps. grep 'Untap (a|two|X|four) tapped' and 'UntapOthers|UntapTapped' over effects+game: 0.
