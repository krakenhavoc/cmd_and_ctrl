## Give a token only the triggered abilities of the cards exiled to pay for it (Vecna)

Size: **small**. 3 cards wait on it.

**The Book of Vile Darkness** {B}{B}{B} — Legendary Artifact — Book (AFR)
> At the beginning of your end step, if you lost 2 or more life this turn, create a 2/2 black Zombie creature token.
> {T}, Exile The Book of Vile Darkness and artifacts you control named Eye of Vecna and Hand of Vecna: Create Vecna, a legendary 8/8 black Zombie God creature token with indestructible and all triggered abilities of the exiled cards.

**Eye of Vecna** {2} — Legendary Artifact (AFR)
> When Eye of Vecna enters, you draw a card and you lose 2 life.
> At the beginning of your upkeep, you may pay {2}. If you do, you draw a card and you lose 2 life.

**Hand of Vecna** {3} — Legendary Artifact — Equipment (AFR)
> At the beginning of combat on your turn, equipped creature or a creature you control named Vecna gets +X/+X until end of turn, where X is the number of cards in your hand.
> Equip—Pay 1 life for each card in your hand.
> Equip {2}

**The clause.** "{T}, Exile The Book of Vile Darkness and artifacts you control named Eye of Vecna and Hand of Vecna: Create Vecna, a legendary 8/8 black Zombie God creature token with indestructible and all triggered abilities of the exiled cards."

**The gap.** ADR 0155's borrow (game/borrowed_triggers.go, effects/borrowed_triggers.go HasActivatedAndTriggeredAbilitiesOf) lends activated abilities and, with AbilityBorrow.Triggered, triggered ones as well; there is no triggered-only borrow, and giving Vecna the Book's and the Hand's activated abilities would be stronger than printed. The lenders here are the cards exiled as this activation's cost (one, two or three cards, as they last existed), fixed for a token rather than a permanent's own static; LastChosenExiledCardLender covers one chosen card only. Also needed: a cost that exiles the source plus every artifact you control with either name. Expect: a TriggeredOnly flag on game.AbilityBorrow, a lender reading the cost's exiled cards, and the token row. Eye of Vecna and Hand of Vecna are built here so the three are tested together (Hand's 'equipped creature or a creature you control named Vecna'; 'Equip—Pay 1 life for each card in your hand' should reuse game/life_cost_count.go).

**Checked.** Normalized grep of effects for 'all triggered abilities of the exiled' and 'has all triggered abilities' = 0; 'AllTriggeredAbilitiesOf|triggered abilities of' = 9 files, all the activated-and-triggered borrow (borrowed_triggers.go, Koh) or trigger doublers. Read effects/borrowed_triggers.go.
