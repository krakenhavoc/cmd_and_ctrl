## Roll dice as an activation cost and as a permanent enters

Size: **medium**. 2 cards wait on it.

**Clay Golem** {4} — Artifact Creature — Golem (AFC)
> {6}, Roll a d8: Monstrosity X, where X is the result. (If this creature isn't monstrous, put X +1/+1 counters on it and it becomes monstrous.)
> Berserk — When this creature becomes monstrous, destroy target permanent.

**Neverwinter Hydra** {X}{X}{G}{G} — Creature — Hydra (AFC)
> As this creature enters, roll X d6. It enters with a number of +1/+1 counters on it equal to the total of those results.
> Trample
> Ward {4} (Whenever this creature becomes the target of a spell or ability an opponent controls, counter it unless that player pays {4}.)

**The clause.** "{6}, Roll a d8: Monstrosity X, where X is the result." (Clay Golem). "As this creature enters, roll X d6. It enters with a number of +1/+1 counters on it equal to the total of those results." (Neverwinter Hydra).

**The gap.** Every roll goes through RollDiceThenForEffect from a resolving effect, with the rest as a registered tail. A roll that is part of paying a cost (its result fixed before the ability is on the stack, visible to 'whenever you roll', replaceable by s1's static and able to pause for Squid Fire Knight's question mid-payment) has no cost kind; and no enters-with replacement rolls dice while the permanent is entering (the entry would have to wait on the dice window). Expect: a RollDieCost in effects/activated.go with the result stored on PaidCost (game/paid_cost.go) for the effect to read, and an as-enters hook that rolls before counters are placed (game/dice_roll.go + the enters-with path). Build after s1: both edit dice_roll.go.

**Checked.** Listed every exported *Cost constructor in effects (grep '^func [A-Z][A-Za-z]*Cost('): no roll cost. Normalized grep of the game package for 'roll a d[0-9]+ as a cost|RollAsCost|CostRoll' = 0; effects for 'Roll X d6' = 0; no effects file contains both RollDice/RollDie and EntersWith/AsEnters. Monstrosity itself exists (effects/monstrosity.go, hydra_broodmaster.go 'Monstrosity X').
