## Let a cast permission ask for a creature sacrifice in addition

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Maestros Ascendancy** {U}{B}{R} — Enchantment (SNC)
> Once during each of your turns, you may cast an instant or sorcery spell from your graveyard by sacrificing a creature in addition to paying its other costs. If a spell cast this way would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead.

**The clause.** Once during each of your turns, you may cast an instant or sorcery spell from your graveyard by sacrificing a creature in addition to paying its other costs. If a spell cast this way would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead.

**The gap.** game.CastPermission (game/cast_permission.go ~:782-835) carries SacrificeLandInAddition, SacrificeNonlandPermanentInAddition, LifeInAddition and RemoveCountersInAddition, each a fixed clause; there is no 'sacrifice a creature' one. Add the sibling (or generalise to a TargetSpec) beside them. The rest of the card has precedents: 'Once during each of your turns, you may cast' (16 files), 'If a spell cast this way would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead' (finale_of_promise.go).

**Checked.** grep 'InAddition' in game/cast_permission.go: only the four fields above. grep 'sacrific(e|ing) a creature in addition' over effects+game: 0. Falco Spara's 'by removing a counter from a creature you control' is NOT part of this seam: RemoveCountersInAddition (dawnhand_dissident.go) with N=1 on PlayFromTopOfYourLibrary (into_the_pit.go) covers it; it is a watch item in batch 20.
