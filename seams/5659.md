## Let a permanent offer '2 life' on coloured permanent spells, with the linked coloured discount

Size: **small**. 5 cards wait on it.

**Defiler of Dreams** {3}{U}{U} — Creature — Phyrexian Sphinx (DMU)
> Flying
> As an additional cost to cast blue permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {U} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of blue mana you pay.
> Whenever you cast a blue permanent spell, draw a card.

**Defiler of Faith** {3}{W}{W} — Creature — Phyrexian Human (DMU)
> Vigilance
> As an additional cost to cast white permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {W} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of white mana you pay.
> Whenever you cast a white permanent spell, create a 1/1 white Soldier creature token.

**Defiler of Flesh** {2}{B}{B} — Creature — Phyrexian Horror (DMU)
> Menace
> As an additional cost to cast black permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {B} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of black mana you pay.
> Whenever you cast a black permanent spell, target creature you control gets +1/+1 and gains menace until end of turn.

**Defiler of Instinct** {2}{R}{R} — Creature — Phyrexian Kavu (DMU)
> First strike
> As an additional cost to cast red permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {R} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of red mana you pay.
> Whenever you cast a red permanent spell, this creature deals 1 damage to any target.

**Defiler of Vigor** {3}{G}{G} — Creature — Phyrexian Wurm (DMU)
> Trample
> As an additional cost to cast green permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {G} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of green mana you pay.
> Whenever you cast a green permanent spell, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.

**The clause.** As an additional cost to cast blue permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {U} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of blue mana you pay.

**The gap.** The grant half has a seam: game/granted_optional_cost.go (GrantedOptionalCost, Silverquill the Disputant). Missing: a cost reduction that applies only when that granted cost was announced. CostQuery.OptionalCosts (game/cost_modifier.go) carries the printed indices; granted costs are recorded by key on PaidCost.GrantedOptional and no cost modifier reads them. Also to confirm: a granted cost priced in life, a reduction that takes only a coloured symbol (CostModifier.Unit), one offer per Defiler on the battlefield (EachSource), and that Karn's Sylex (s3) switches the offer off. Start from granted_optional_cost.go and cost_modifier.go; if the reduction can already see the announcement, this is five ordinary cards.

**Checked.** grep 'As an additional cost to cast .* spells, you may pay' -> 0; 'reduces only the amount of' -> 0; 'you may pay 2 life' -> 9 files, none a grant to other spells; life_for_mana.go (K'rrik) replaces a mana symbol with life, which is a different rule. grep GrantedOptionalCost in cards/effects -> casualty.go, conspire.go, silverquill_the_disputant.go, raiding_schemes.go, hugh_first_of_many.go.
