## Add a mana restriction tag for paying a disturb cost

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Unblinking Observer** {1}{U} — Creature — Homunculus (MID)
> {T}: Add {U}. Spend this mana only to pay a disturb cost or cast an instant or sorcery spell.

**The clause.** {T}: Add {U}. Spend this mana only to pay a disturb cost or cast an instant or sorcery spell.

**The gap.** Mana restrictions are a closed tag vocabulary in game/mana_restriction.go. 'Cast an instant or sorcery spell' is ManaRestrictAnyType and the OR is ManaRestrictAnyOf, but no tag names the alternative cost a cast is being paid with: ManaSpendContext carries zone, purpose and characteristics, not the AlternativeCost.Key. cast:from-graveyard is too wide (it would admit flashback and escape). Add an 'alt:<key>' tag and carry the claimed alternative's key in ManaSpendForCastParams.

**Checked.** sed -n 81,200p game/mana_restriction.go: the tag list (purpose:*, cast:from-exile, cast:from-graveyard, type:, subtype:, color:, ...). grep -nE 'alt:|AltKey|Alternative|disturb' game/mana_restriction.go: only the SpendOnlySources comment at :506. grep -rni 'disturb cost' effects: 26 files, all Disturb(...) declarations, none a spend restriction.
