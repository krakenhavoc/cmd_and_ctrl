## Add 'players can't pay life to cast spells or activate non-mana abilities'

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Karn's Sylex** {3} — Legendary Artifact (DMU)
> Karn's Sylex enters tapped.
> Players can't pay life to cast spells or to activate abilities that aren't mana abilities.
> {X}, {T}, Exile Karn's Sylex: Destroy each nonland permanent with mana value X or less. Activate only as a sorcery.

**The clause.** Players can't pay life to cast spells or to activate abilities that aren't mana abilities.

**The gap.** No restriction of this kind exists. Paying life is gated only by 'can't lose life' (game/effect_api.go PayLifeForEffect, payLifeAsCostLocked in life_tail.go). Needs a world rule in game/restrictions.go read by spell and ability cost payment (printed life costs, Phyrexian mana, K'rrik's life-for-mana, granted costs such as s2), by the legal offers and by the client's payment choices; mana abilities stay exempt. The rest of the card (enters tapped; {X},{T}, exile: destroy each nonland permanent with mana value X or less, sorcery speed, declares Sweep) is ordinary.

**Checked.** grep -i "can't pay life|CantPayLife|cannot pay life" in game and cards/effects -> only comments about a player who cannot lose life (effect_api.go:602, corpses_of_the_lost.go, sylvan_library.go, teferis_reproach.go). game/restrictions.go has no life-payment rule.
