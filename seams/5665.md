## Add a this-turn form of 'activate loyalty abilities twice'

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Urza Assembles the Titans** {3}{W}{W} — Enchantment — Saga (DMU)
> Read ahead (Choose a chapter and start with that many lore counters. Add one after your draw step. Skipped chapters don't trigger. Sacrifice after III.)
> I — Scry 4, then you may reveal the top card of your library. If a planeswalker card is revealed this way, put it into your hand.
> II — You may put a planeswalker card with mana value 6 or less from your hand onto the battlefield.
> III — You may activate the loyalty abilities of planeswalkers you control twice this turn rather than only once.

**The clause.** You may activate the loyalty abilities of planeswalkers you control twice this turn rather than only once.

**The gap.** The rule exists only as a printed static of a permanent: CardDef.LoyaltyTwiceEachTurn / YourPlaneswalkersLoyaltyTwiceEachTurn (game/carddef.go:393-403; oath_of_teferi.go, urza_planeswalker.go). A chapter ability needs the same permission as an effect that lasts until end of turn for one player. Expect a turn-scoped player rule read where the loyalty limit is checked. Chapters I and II are ordinary (scry 4 then reveal the top card; put a planeswalker card with mana value 6 or less from hand onto the battlefield).

**Checked.** grep 'loyalty abilities .* twice|twice each turn rather than only once' -> oath_of_teferi.go, urza_planeswalker.go, spec.go; grep LoyaltyTwice in game -> carddef.go and ability_rows.go only, no duration form.
