## Record and expose that a creature attacked a battle

Size: **small**. 4 cards wait on it.

**War Historian** {2}{G} — Creature — Human Monk (MOM)
> Reach
> This creature has indestructible as long as it attacked a battle this turn.

**Thrashing Frontliner** {1}{R} — Creature — Phyrexian Lizard (MOM)
> Trample
> Whenever this creature attacks a battle, it gets +1/+1 until end of turn.

**War-Trained Slasher** {3}{R} — Creature — Wolverine Dinosaur (MOM)
> Menace (This creature can't be blocked except by two or more creatures.)
> Whenever this creature attacks a battle, double its power until end of turn.

**Rampaging Geoderm** {2}{R}{G} — Creature — Dinosaur Beast (MOM)
> Trample, haste
> Whenever you attack, target attacking creature gets +1/+1 until end of turn. If it's attacking a battle, put a +1/+1 counter on it instead.

**The clause.** "This creature has indestructible as long as it attacked a battle this turn." / "Whenever this creature attacks a battle, …" / "If it's attacking a battle, put a +1/+1 counter on it instead."

**The gap.** The turn tally knows that a creature attacked and which players (game/turn_tally.go AttackedThisTurn, AttackedPlayersThisTurn) but not that the attack target was a battle; War Historian's static needs that fact after combat. 'Attacks a battle' and 'is attacking a battle' can be read today from Card.AttackingTarget + ClassifyAttackTargetForEffect (game/attack_target.go), but no card does it, so the same worker should add the one shared predicate/trigger shape and the tally field together.

**Checked.** grep 'attacks a battle', 'attacked a battle', 'as long as it attacked', "if it's attacking a" effects: 0 each; 'attacking a battle' appears only in comments excluding battles (mage_slayer.go, soc_b14_helpers.go); grep 'func (g \*Game) Attacked' game: no battle variant.
