## Record damage one source dealt to one player this turn

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Hidetsugu Consumes All // Vessel of the All-Consuming** — Enchantment — Saga // Enchantment Creature — Ogre Shaman (NEO)
> Hidetsugu Consumes All: (As this Saga enters and after your draw step, add a lore counter.)
> I — Destroy each nonland permanent with mana value 1 or less.
> II — Exile all graveyards.
> III — Exile this Saga, then return it to the battlefield transformed under your control.
> //
> Vessel of the All-Consuming: Trample
> Whenever this creature deals damage, put a +1/+1 counter on it.
> Whenever this creature deals damage to a player, if it has dealt 10 or more damage to that player this turn, they lose the game.

**The clause.** "Whenever this creature deals damage to a player, if it has dealt 10 or more damage to that player this turn, they lose the game." (Vessel of the All-Consuming, back of Hidetsugu Consumes All)

**The gap.** TurnTally keeps source-to-PERMANENT records (DamagedObjects, game/dealt_damage_by.go) and source-to-player records only for NONCOMBAT damage (NoncombatDamageRecord.Victim, game/turn_tally.go:1267); combat damage to players is a per-controller total (CombatDamageToPlayers). Nothing answers 'how much has this object dealt to that player this turn, combat and noncombat'. Expect an additive tally record keyed by source ObjectRef and victim player, written from EventDealDamage, plus a ForEffect reader. Chapter I of the front face is a board wipe and declares its Sweep.

Read turn_tally.go:470-487 and :1267, dealt_damage_by.go:56 (ObjectDamageRecord is permanents only).
