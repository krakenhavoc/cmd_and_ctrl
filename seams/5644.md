## Carry the amount of mana a mana ability produced on its event

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Tyvar the Bellicose** {2}{B}{G} — Legendary Creature — Elf Warrior (MAT)
> Whenever one or more Elves you control attack, they gain deathtouch until end of turn.
> Each creature you control has "Whenever a mana ability of this creature resolves, put a number of +1/+1 counters on it equal to the amount of mana this creature produced. This ability triggers only once each turn."

**The clause.** Each creature you control has "Whenever a mana ability of this creature resolves, put a number of +1/+1 counters on it equal to the amount of mana this creature produced. This ability triggers only once each turn."

**The gap.** game.EventManaAbilityActivated (server/internal/game/mutations.go, emitted by both the clicked activation and the auto-tapper) carries Actor, Source, CardID, Label and Exhaust but no amount, and it is emitted before the slots are minted. A trigger needs the count of mana that ability actually produced (after CR 106.12b-style replacements such as tripling, not counting other permanents' triggered mana). Expect an additive Amount (or a new 'mana ability resolved' event) in mutations.go / produce_mana.go, then a granted trigger with the existing once-each-turn guard (docs/adding-cards.md '"This ability triggers only once"', #5245). The 'Elves attack, they gain deathtouch' half is ordinary.

**Checked.** grep -rliE 'Whenever a mana ability of|amount of mana .* produced|tap(s|ped)? .* for mana, put' in server/internal/cards/effects/ -> no file. grep 'ManaAbilityResolved|ManaProduced|EventManaAdded|EventManaAbilityActivated' in server/internal/game -> the event exists (manaActivationPing in mana_rider_helpers.go watches it for Zhur-Taa Druid) but neither it nor ManaProduced (mana_trigger.go: Source, Controller, Colors) is delivered to an ordinary trigger with a total. EventManaAdded is one event per unit with no ability grouping.
