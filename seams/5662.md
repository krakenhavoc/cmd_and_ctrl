## Report the life paid on an ability's activation event

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Verrak, Warped Sengir** {1}{W}{B} — Legendary Creature — Vampire (DMC)
> Flying, deathtouch, lifelink
> Whenever you activate an ability that isn't a mana ability, if life was paid to activate it, you may pay that much life again. If you do, copy that ability. You may choose new targets for the copy.

**The clause.** Whenever you activate an ability that isn't a mana ability, if life was paid to activate it, you may pay that much life again. If you do, copy that ability.

**The gap.** PaidCost.LifePaid is computed at activation (game/activated.go:2343-2397, including Phyrexian symbols) and survives on an ability copy (ability_copy.go:297), but the Event struct has no life-paid field, so a trigger on EventActivateAbility cannot ask 'was life paid, and how much'. Small: put the amount on the event (or expose the stack item's PaidCost to the trigger), then build the trigger from the ability-copy precedents (rowan_kenrith.go, the_enigma_jewel.go, lithoform_engine.go).

**Checked.** grep 'if life was paid|pay that much life again' -> only vraska_betrayals_sting.go (compleated). grep -i 'LifePaid|PaidLife' in game -> paid_cost/activated.go/ability_copy.go only; the Event struct (game/events.go) has no such field. No WheneverYouActivate helper reads a paid cost (triggers_common.go:818 is exhaust only).
