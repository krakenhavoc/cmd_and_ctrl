## Emit a proliferate event and add a 'whenever you proliferate' trigger

Size: **medium**. 4 cards wait on it.

**Ezuri, Stalker of Spheres** {2}{G}{U} — Legendary Creature — Phyrexian Elf Warrior (ONE)
> When Ezuri enters, you may pay {3}. If you do, proliferate twice.
> Whenever you proliferate, draw a card.

**Scheming Aspirant** {1}{B} — Creature — Phyrexian Advisor (ONE)
> Whenever you proliferate, each opponent loses 2 life and you gain 2 life.

**Venser, Corpse Puppet** {U}{B} — Legendary Creature — Phyrexian Zombie Wizard (ONE)
> Lifelink, toxic 1
> Whenever you proliferate, choose one —
> • If you don't control a creature named The Hollow Sentinel, create The Hollow Sentinel, a legendary 3/3 colorless Phyrexian Golem artifact creature token.
> • Target artifact creature you control gains flying and lifelink until end of turn.

**Voidwing Hybrid** {U}{B} — Creature — Phyrexian Bat (ONE)
> Flying
> Toxic 1 (Players dealt combat damage by this creature also get a poison counter.)
> When you proliferate, return this card from your graveyard to your hand.

**The clause.** Whenever you proliferate, ... / When you proliferate, return this card from your graveyard to your hand.

**The gap.** CR 701.34 proliferate is a keyword action with a CR 614 window (game/keyword_action.go, game/proliferate.go) but nothing is emitted after it: game/events.go says 'there is no EventProliferate at all' (#976), while scry and surveil have EventScry / EventSurveil. Expect a post-action EventProliferate (Actor = the proliferating player, fired even when nothing was chosen, once per proliferate of 'proliferate twice') from ProliferateForEffect, a WheneverYouProliferate helper in triggers_common.go, and the graveyard-zone form for Voidwing Hybrid.

**Checked.** grep -ri 'you proliferate' game cards/effects: 0 hits. grep 'Event[A-Za-z]*Proliferat': only the comment in events.go:888 saying it does not exist. 'Whenever you scry' has precedents (celeborn_the_wise.go, diviner_of_victory.go) on EventScry, so the shape to copy exists.
