## Remember that an opponent's creature or planeswalker was dealt excess damage this turn

Size: **small**. 1 card waits on it.

**Rith, Liberated Primeval** {2}{R}{G}{W} — Legendary Creature — Dragon (DMU)
> Flying, ward {2}
> Other Dragons you control have ward {2}.
> At the beginning of your end step, if a creature or planeswalker an opponent controlled was dealt excess damage this turn, create a 4/4 red Dragon creature token with flying.

**The clause.** At the beginning of your end step, if a creature or planeswalker an opponent controlled was dealt excess damage this turn, create a 4/4 red Dragon creature token with flying.

**The gap.** Excess damage is rebuilt per event inside a trigger (cards/effects/excess_damage.go, noncombat and creatures only); nothing keeps it for the turn. Needs a turn tally (game/turn_tally.go) set when damage, combat or not, exceeds lethal on a creature or the loyalty of a planeswalker an opponent of the reader controlled, per controller, and a condition helper. The rest (ward {2}, other Dragons have ward {2}, Dragon token) is ordinary. The 4/4 red Dragon with flying is also made by Rohgahh (batch 18); reuse the tokens_table.go row.

**Checked.** grep 'excess damage this turn|dealt excess damage' -> only fall_of_cair_andros.go (a per-event trigger). grep -i ExcessDamage in game -> none; grep -i excess game/turn_*.go -> none.
