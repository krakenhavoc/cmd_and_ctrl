**The clause.** Prototype {2}{R} — 3/2 (You may cast this spell with different mana cost, color, and size. It keeps its abilities and types.)

**The gap.** CR 702.160 / CR 718: a cast-time choice that gives the SPELL and the PERMANENT it becomes a second mana cost, power, toughness and (from the cost's coloured symbols) colour; the choice is part of the copiable values (CR 718.2a, 718.3c-d) and ends when the object leaves the stack or battlefield (CR 718.4). Nothing in the engine carries a second set of printed characteristics on one face. Expect: a prototype declaration on the Spec (cost, P/T) beside AlternativeCosts in effects/alternative_cost.go; a cast offer and CastProvenance flag in game (cast path, PaidCost); the layered view / copiable values in game reading the alternative mana cost, mana value, colour and base P/T while prototyped; the snapshot field (additive); protocol + client: the cast choice prompt and showing the prototyped cost and P/T on the stack item and the permanent (Scryfall layout 'prototype' is one face, the inset is only in oracle_text). Affinity/cost reducers, 'mana value' readers, colour readers and 'its power' readers must all see the prototyped values. The seam worker builds the eleven plain prototype cards; batch 21 holds the seven whose other text is rules-heavy.
