**Ninjutsu, an entry that puts a CARD onto the battlefield attacking, and an exported "unblocked attacker"** (#1227, CR 702.49 / CR 506.3c / CR 509.1h, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)'s, [ADR 0045](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md)'s and [ADR 0073](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)'s 2026-09-23 amendments) — the last two halves of the keyword #1221 declared out of scope, and the zone half needed nothing: `ActivatedAbilityShape.Zones = {ZoneHand}` has reached ninjutsu since #660, the `ReturnToHand` component landed with #1213, and `AbilityNeedsPermanentSource` already admitted it because the permanent a return cost returns is not the source. Not one line of `ActivateCatalogAbility` changed. **`ZoneEntryOptions.Attacking uuid.UUID`** is seeded onto **`ReplacementEvent.EntersAttacking`** exactly as `Tapped` is onto `EntersTapped`, so the entry's one settled record carries the fact and a CR 616 resume holding only the event still knows what the entry was for; the TOKEN door now carries the same field (`enterCreatedTokensLocked` copies the minted token's own `AttackingTarget` onto its entry event), which leaves **`stampEntryAttackerLocked`** as the single place CR 506.3c's "never DECLARED as an attacker" marking happens for both doors. That marking is three facts, and the third was a hole: the `AttackingTarget` stamp, `noteAttackAnnouncedLocked` (so the declaration's lock-in emits no `EventAttack` and no "whenever ~ attacks" payoff sees a ninja), and `invalidateLayersForAttackChangeLocked` — #1218's FOURTH exit, which neither door had, so an Ohran Frostfang reading "attacking creature" kept a stale answer after a Parhelion II Angel arrived. `DeclareAttackerWith` is deliberately not the route: it refuses outside `StepDeclareAttackers`, it taps, it checks summoning sickness and it emits the event, wrong on all four counts. **`Game.UnblockedAttackerForEffect`** is the first exported reader of the blocked record and answers three facts rather than one — attacking, the cursor at declare blockers or later (CR 509.1h decides "unblocked" as PART of the declaration, which is why the Gatherer ruling says ninjutsu waits for blockers), and nothing blocking it, meaning both `blockedAttackers` AND a block that is staged and not yet locked in (#830). It inherits `blockers.go`'s declared simplification whole: nothing marks the declare-blockers turn-based action COMPLETE, so an attacker at the top of the step reads as unblocked — a beat early, never after a block. **`PaidCost.ReturnedAttacking`** is `Sacrificed`'s neighbour and exists for a stronger version of its reason: the returned permanent's exit clears `Card.AttackingTarget` and LKI carries no combat state, so the payer reads it BEFORE the bounce. The timing restriction is the COST rather than an `ActivatedAbilityShape.Condition` — CR 118.3 already refuses an unpayable activation, and the three consumers all narrow off the one candidate walk (#544): the validator refuses, `returnCostOptions` ships an empty picker and `returnPayments` offers the bot no move. **No wire change at all** — `return_label` / `return_options` / `return_ids` are #1213's and Board's announce chain never inspects a card's zone. One client fix: `returnShortfall` is factored out of `contextMenu.logic.ts` and asked by `ManaAbilityMenu.svelte` too, the popover a hand card and the zone browser open, which had no return arm — a ninjutsu row is unpayable for nearly the whole game, so a row that never greyed would be wrong more often than right. **4 cards, all `full`:** Ninja of the Deep Hours, Ingenious Infiltrator, Moonblade Shinobi and Prosperous Thief. **Still open:** "put onto the battlefield BLOCKING", which CR 509.1 has no entry for and no card asks for. Commander ninjutsu closed with #1278, below.
