**Every counter placement names its placer** (#3942, [ADR 0056 amendment 2026-10-16](decisions/0056-infect-wither-toxic.md)) — `ReplacementEvent.CounterPlacer` and `EventCounterPlaced.Actor` were set only where a caller named the placer (damage results, proliferate, counter costs, blight), and the "you put" readers guessed the rest from the target's controller or the last `EventResolve` in the log, which outlives its resolution. `game/counter_placer.go` now fills in the placer before the CR 614 window opens: the innermost named scope (a mana ability's activator, the player taking a special action), else the controller of the open resolution (CR 608.2c; open until its CR 704.3 boundary, and read off the parked tail after a restore), else the permanent's controller or the player getting the counters. Counters a permanent enters with are its controller's (CR 122.6a), named at the drain and the loyalty, defense and Saga-entry stamps; the sandbox verb names the seat editing. A Saga's turn-based lore counter (CR 714.3c) now goes through the window, marked `CounterFromTurnBasedAction` so Doubling Season (CR 614.16) still declines it. The readers (`b11CounterPlacerOf`, `b12CountersPlacedBy`, All Will Be One, Lae'zel, the loyalty-counter trigger) read the placer alone. **Cards:** Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider and Innkeeper's Talent ship `full`.
