**Cipher, cascade, discover and paradigm cast during their resolution** (#4243, CR 608.2g, CR 707.12, CR 702.85a, CR 701.57a, CR 702.99a, CR 702.192a; [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)'s 2026-10-23 amendment) — the four doors that kept the pass-limited cast permission after #2947 now ask the `resolution_cast` prompt inside the resolution. Cipher's and paradigm's copy offers set `CardCopyOffer.DuringResolution`. Cascade's hit and discover's card keep the pass-closed permission ADR 0099 gave them as the data under the prompt, and `CascadeForEffect` and `DiscoverForEffect` queue a `resolution_cast` over the one card in exile (a real card, not a copy); the cast is made during the trigger or spell resolving, with targets, modes and X chosen then, or declined. A decline lapses the permission as a pass did (a discovered card to the hand, a cascade hit to the bottom), and the cards passed over ride the prompt as `PendingChoice.CastPile` (additive snapshot field `castPile`) and are bottomed in one random order with an uncast hit. `Discover.Then` is replaced by a step record (`effects.DiscoverStepKey`) so a later instruction waits for the answer as data; Hit the Mother Lode is `ResolveSteps(Discover, Instruction)`. A table paused on any of the four is a restore point. **Cards that changed behaviour:** every cipher card's copy (15), paradigm's copy, every cascade card (Shardless Agent, Bloodbraid Elf, Maelstrom Wanderer, Call Forth the Tempest, ...) and every discover card (Hidden Caves, Daring Discovery, Primordial Gnawer, Hit the Mother Lode, ...). Hand-cast offers (#2110) and the impulse-style grants keep the stand-in (#4250).
