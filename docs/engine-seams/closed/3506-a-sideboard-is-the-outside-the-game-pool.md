**A sideboard: cards outside the game** (#3506, CR 400.11, 400.11c, 903.5e, [ADR 0191](decisions/0191-a-sideboard-is-the-outside-the-game-pool.md)) — the owner's decision of 2026-10-14 answers what ADR 0172 left open: a deck's sideboard is the player's pool of cards owned from outside the game, in Commander too. A deck's `Sideboard` section (text, Moxfield, Archidekt) reaches the game as `Player.Sideboard`, a second `ZoneOutside` container beside the companion's that no `ZoneRef` or card lookup reaches, up to 15 cards (`deck.MaxSideboard`, `sideboard_too_large` warns and seats no sideboard). The `sideboard_not_supported_in_commander` note now says what the section is for. A lone companion card in the sideboard is still read as the companion; any other sideboard stays a sideboard. Its owner reads it (`PlayerView.sideboard`, a read-only zone-browser tab); everyone else gets `sideboard_count`. An effect brings a card in through `game.QueueOutsidePickForEffect` (a `choose_cards` over the container, answerable by the enumerator and priced by the heuristic), `RevealOutsideForEffect` and `BringFromOutsideForEffect`, and the controller of the owner's turn is offered nothing (CR 723.4). Snapshot: additive in v8. **Cards:** Extrapolate the Impossible, Burning Wish, Living Wish, Mastermind's Acquisition (all Full). Still waiting: Karn, the Great Creator, Spawnsire of Ulamog's {20}, Research // Development, learn, and the other wishes.
