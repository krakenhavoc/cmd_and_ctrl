**Players can't play lands** (#1895, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §4, PR 5) — a land play is a special action, not a cast (CR 305.1, CR 116.2a), so `CastGateLocked` never reached one. A land play now has its own gate, `Game.LandPlayGateLocked(player, card, fromZone)`, asked before the drop count because CR 101.2 makes "can't" beat "can" (an extra land drop does not lift it). It has two sources: **statics on permanents** (`Spec.LandPlayRestrictions`, a `game.LandPlayRestriction{Label, Forbids(LandPlayQuery), ActiveWhen}` read from the battlefield through `CatalogAbilityKey`, with constructors for "players", "you", "your opponents from a zone", "a land with a name" and "each opponent who controls more lands") and the **"this turn" ban** from a resolved spell or ability (`ModCantPlayLands`, a `ScopedEffect` naming one player, swept at cleanup, CR 514.2; a ScopedEffect and not a cast-ban kind so an older binary refuses the file instead of banning nothing). The gate has four callers, found by grep: the land branch of the play path, the land play a resolution instructs (hideaway's free play, CR 305.2a), the legal-move enumerator, and the view, so a bot is never offered a land the server refuses and the client greys it with the refusing clause on the land's own `cant_cast`. `PlayerView.cant_play_lands` names a seat's blanket ban. `game.ArabianNightsNames` is CR 206.3a's list. **Cards:** City in a Bottle, Territorial Dispute, Aggressive Mining, Pardic Miner, Turf Wound, Solfatara, Moonhold, Rock Jockey, Ward of Bones and Experimental Frenzy (Full). **Moved to other rows:** Tomik (waits on the graveyard-targeting row, ADR 0109 PR 6), Worms of the Earth (its own "lands can't enter" row), Damping Engine (ignoring an effect by sacrificing) and Shaman's Trance (playing from other players' graveyards).
