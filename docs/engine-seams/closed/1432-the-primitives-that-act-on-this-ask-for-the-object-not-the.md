**The primitives that act on "this" ask for the object, not the card** (#1432, CR 400.7 / 603.7c / 611.2b / 701.12b, [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md) Decisions 17-19; the follow-on to #1418, entry below). About a hundred catalog files resolve with `Target: item.SourceCardID` (`AddCounter` 64 literals, `SacrificePermanent` 13, `UntapTarget` 12, the until-EOT pumps 9, bounce, exile, transform, crew's zero target) or call a game mutator on the source directly, and a source that left and came back under the same instance ID while its ability waited was acted on as the new object: stronger than printed. **One check, at the primitive:** `Game.AbilitySourceIsNewObjectForEffect(item)` / `effects.Context.isNewSourceObject(target)` (`effects/source_object_guard.go`) — the target is the item's own source, the item is a stamped ability, and the card is on the battlefield as an object other than `StackItem.SourceObject` that this resolution did not put there. Every permanent-acting primitive asks it (the list is in Decision 17), as do `b09SourceStillOnBattlefield`, the "for as long as ~" duration builders, fight, the ~20 direct-mutator bodies, and the engine's evoke sacrifice trigger. **The card-following rule** (Decision 18): off the battlefield the primitive follows the card ("when this dies, return it", "return this card from your graveyard"), and a move this resolution made itself is followed (CR 400.7: unearth's haste; `resolvingItem.sourceEpoch` tells the two apart). Delayed and reflexive triggers follow a source their creator put onto the battlefield (`followedSourceObjectLocked`, Decision 19), so unearth's end-step exile still finds its creature. Two regression scans fail a new primitive, or a new direct-mutator body, that does not ask. Proof cards: **Bartolomé del Presidio**, **Underworld Breach**, **Devoted Druid**, evoked **Mulldrifter**, and **Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin**, which ships `full` (last-known-power Goblins, no counter on a Krenko that is not the attacker). The "each creature you control" loop that reached its own returned source and skipped it (weaker than printed) is closed by #1463 (Decision 20): a loop over a set read off the board applies with `ctx.asGroupMember()`, sites that name the source by construction ask `isNewSourceObjectAsThis`, and a third scan fails an "each" loop that forgets; proof cards Steel Overseer, Mazirek, Kalonian Hydra. Still open, stated in the amendment: hand-built durations; damage to "this". Trackers [#881](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/881).
