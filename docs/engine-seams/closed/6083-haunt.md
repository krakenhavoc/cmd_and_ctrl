**Haunt (CR 702.55)** (#6083) — a card exiled "haunting" a creature, and the abilities that trigger from exile when that creature dies. The link is plain data on the exiled card (`Card.HauntedID`, `HauntedEpoch`, `HauntingEpoch`, additive snapshot fields): the haunted OBJECT by instance ID and CR 400.7 epoch, and the exiled card's own epoch, so the link ends by itself when the haunted creature is blinked or the card leaves exile. `Game.HauntForEffect` exiles the card through the ordinary route and writes the link only if it arrived; `Game.CreatureItHauntsDiedForEffect` is the "the creature it haunts dies" condition. On the card side `effects.Haunt(name)` is the keyword trigger and `effects.WhenThisEntersOrTheCreatureItHauntsDies` is the one printed ability with two conditions, declared from the battlefield and exile (`TriggeredAbility.Zones`, CR 702.55c) and controlled from exile by the card's owner (CR 108.4). Haunt on an instant or sorcery ("when this spell is put into a graveyard during its resolution") is not built: no catalogued card needs it yet. **Cards:** Orzhov Euthanist, Blind Hunter, Orzhov Pontiff (Full).
