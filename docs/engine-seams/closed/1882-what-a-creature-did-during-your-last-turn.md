**What a creature did during your last turn** (#1882, CR 502.3 / CR 508.1c / CR 508.4 / CR 400.7 / CR 500.7, [ADR 0108 §6](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md)) — Goblin Rock Sled's and Tangle Kelp's "doesn't untap … if it attacked during your last turn", Giant Turtle's "can't attack if it attacked during your last turn", Avenge's "costs {2} less if a player attacked you during their last turn" and O-Kagachi's "if that player attacked you during their last turn". `TurnTally.Attacks` is replaced as each turn begins, so the fact has to live on the player: **`Player.LastTurnAttacks`** is the attack records (attacker, object epoch, defender) of the last turn that player took. `recordLastTurnAttacksLocked` copies the ending turn's tally onto its active player at the top of `beginNextTurnLocked`, before the turn and the tally are replaced, so "your last turn" is the most recent turn **you** took: an extra turn replaces the record (CR 500.7), a seat that never begins a turn (CR 800.4k) leaves its record alone, and an empty record is written too. Only declarations are recorded (CR 508.4) and each is keyed by object (CR 400.7). **`Game.AttackedDuringControllersLastTurn(card)`** reads the card's current controller's record; **`AttackedYouDuringTheirLastTurn(player, you)`** and **`AnyPlayerAttackedYouDuringTheirLastTurn(you)`** read a player's, where an attack on a planeswalker or battle you control is not an attack on you. The card side is `doesntUntapIfItAttackedDuringYourLastTurn` (an `UntapStepRestriction`, self or attached), `CantAttackIfItAttackedDuringYourLastTurn` (a layer-6 `CantAttack` static whose condition changes only as a turn begins, where the layer cache is already invalidated), `APlayerAttackedYouDuringTheirLastTurn` (a cost predicate) and `TargetNonlandPermanentOfThePlayerHit` (a `TargetsFrom` clause read off the trigger's event; O-Kagachi re-checks its intervening "if" as the ability resolves, CR 603.4). One additive snapshot field, `lastTurnAttacks`, recorded in the shape file and classified `carried`. Goblin Rock Sled, Giant Turtle, Tangle Kelp, Avenge and O-Kagachi ship `full`. **Still open and a different seam**: Weathered Sentinels' "can attack players who attacked you during their last turn as though it didn't have defender", a per-defending-player waiver of defender, now on its own row.
