**Reveal-from-hand entry choice** (#1198, CR 614.1c, [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md) §5z, [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-09-22](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — *"As this land enters, you may reveal an Island or Swamp card from your hand. If you don't, this land enters tapped."* The CR 614 apply-loop could stop and ask three questions before this, and all three are yes/no-shaped: a "may" (`Optional`), a shockland's "you may pay 2 life" (`EntryLifeCost`) and a copy selector's "enter as a copy of what?" (`CopySelector`, which names a permanent on the battlefield). None of them can name a card in a HAND, which is the gap the census filed in batch 02 under its own name — *a card choice inside a replacement effect*. **`ReplacementEffect.EntryHandReveal`** is the fourth branch on that loop and joins `asksItsOwnQuestion`, so the two windows that cannot put a question to anybody (`ev.mustSettleNow`, and a CR 616 ordering whose affected player has left) skip it un-applied — the weaker branch, the posture every un-prompted path in ADR 0013 already takes. Its three apply-it-inline guards are `EntryLifeCost`'s one for one: **no resume** (`ev.entryResumable` — pausing an entry with nothing to finish it strands the card in its old zone), **nothing in hand the clause admits** (the ABSENCE of the question, not a refusal of it), and **a chooser who has left** (CR 800.4a). **`PendingChoiceEntryRevealFromHand`** is one new prompt kind and the THIRD member of `isCardSetPickKind` after `choose_cards` and #826's `untap_choice`: it carries the choose-cards payload and a `chooseCardsFrame{zone: ZoneHand}`, so `checkChooseCardsPicksLocked` (#1017) stays the single copy of "would this answer be accepted" for the submit path and for `ChooseCardsPickLegalLocked` in the enumerator. It is a separate KIND because its continuation is a PAUSED EVENT rather than a card's next sentence (it settles through `finishSettledReplacementLocked`, the replacement pipeline's resume contract), because the sentence the client renders is different, and because the SIGN is inverted for a bot — the `choose_cards` branch scores an answer by what it does NOT name (#798), and a card named here is revealed and KEPT, so through that branch every reveal-land would enter tapped forever. **Declining is a real answer**: the floor is zero, which makes "reveal nothing" the enumerator's `AlwaysLegal` move and this prompt un-wedgeable even with every candidate gone from the hand — the property that also keeps it deliberately out of `pruneCardSetChoicesLocked`, whose drop would strand the entry it is pausing. **The reveal itself is `RevealForEffect`**: nothing moves (CR 701.20b — the card is still in hand when the land finishes entering), the whole table becomes entitled to read it (CR 701.20), and the other seats learn what was shown through the log, because the picker and its bounds reach the chooser alone (`choose_cards`' redaction, since how many cards in a hand match the clause is itself hidden information). **Card side is one clause and no per-card prompt code**: `EntersTappedUnlessYouRevealFromHand(name, clause, matches)`, beside `EntersTappedUnlessYouPayLife` and `SelfEntersTappedUnless`, with `Matches` a `func(game.Card) bool` over a card in hand (printed characteristics — a land-type grant on the battlefield says nothing about a card being revealed) and a `Then` continuation for a card that wants to do more. **Cards: the whole ten-card cycle**, one file and one row each — Choked Estuary, Foreboding Ruins, Port Town, Fortified Village, Frostboil Snarl, Furycalm Snarl, Game Trail, Shineshadow Snarl, Necroblossom Snarl and Vineglimmer Snarl, each shipped with a caveat for the same reason the shocklands did — a reveal-land a spell PUTS onto the battlefield out of a hand or library entered tapped, because that batch was built without `entryResumable` — and each complete since #1322 made the batch resumable. **Still open and each a different shape**: Temple of the Dragon Queen (#386) prints *"unless you revealed a Dragon card this way **or you control a Dragon**"* AND a second CR 614.12 clause (choose a colour) — two clauses on one entry; **exiling** from hand as a permanent enters (Chrome Mox's imprint is an ETB TRIGGER, not CR 614.12, and ships complete as one — #578's distinction, observable because a Mox flickered in response to its own trigger imprints nothing); and **discarding** inside an entry replacement (Mox Diamond), which is a cost and has its own row above.
