**Choose a color** (#742, PR #772) - `PendingChoiceColor` (`choose_color`, CR 105.4) in two forms. Stored: `ChooseColorAsEnters(purpose, label)` / `ChooseColorOtherThanAsEnters(purpose, label, color)` on `Spec.AsEnters` write `Card.ChosenColor` (carried by the snapshot, cleared on leaving the battlefield, the S26 `NamedTribe` pattern), read back with `ProducedChosenColor()`, `ProducedColorOrChosen(color)` and `ChosenColorAnthem(p, t)`. At resolution: `ChooseColorThen(purpose, g, chooser, source, question, then)`, a continuation counted in the census, which chains for "each player chooses". Every prompt declares a `game.ColorPurpose` (#780: `ColorForMana` / `ColorForBenefit` / `ColorForHarm` / `ColorForFilter` / `ColorForProtection`), enforced by `color_purpose_guard_test.go` and carried to the wire as `color_purpose`, because CR 105.4 makes all five colours legal and nothing else says which one the card wants. "N mana of any one color" is one `mana_pick` minting N tokens: the produced-mana grammar takes a per-colour count (`{W3|U3|B3|R3|G3}`, built with `OneColorOfAmount(n)` / `ProducedOneColor(fn)`), and the auto-tapper PLANS such a source (#779): one solver candidate per colour, mutually exclusive, with the booked colour carried through to the executor ([ADR 0040](decisions/0040-mana-pipeline.md) #779 addendum). `resolve_choice` routes `color` by kind. Shipped on Thriving Isle, Thriving Heath, Thriving Grove, Thriving Bluff, Thriving Moor, Sea Gate, Citadel Gate, Manor Gate, Cliffgate, Black Dragon Gate, Uncharted Haven, Crossroads Village, Mirage Mesa, Valgavoth's Lair, Coldsteel Heart, Heraldic Banner, Gilded Lotus, Lotus Field (#332), Nyx Lotus, Mona Lisa, Science Geek, White Lotus Tile, Ilysian Caryatid, Sanctum of Fruitful Harvest, Wash Out, Selective Obliteration and Oona, Queen of the Fae. Still waiting on something else: Temple of the Dragon Queen (#386 — the "Reveal-from-hand entry choice" row closed with #1198, but the Temple prints TWO as-enters clauses on one entry, the reveal being conditional besides ("unless you revealed a Dragon card this way or you control a Dragon"), which that row explicitly did not answer); Sunken Citadel (#466, a "spend only to activate abilities of land sources" restriction); Glittering Stockpile (#383, the sacrificed source's last-known stash counters); Throne of Eldraine (#300, spend restrictions keyed to the chosen colour); Caged Sun (#301) and Gauntlet of Power (#386 — their "whenever a land is tapped for mana, add one mana of the chosen color" is now writable as a `ManaTrigger`, #763, and they wait only on being catalogued); Brave the Elements (#399), Flickering Ward (#450), Benevolent Blessing (#459) and the Mother of Runes family (protection, #662 — Mother of Runes herself shipped with it, declaring `ColorForProtection`). Thran Portal (#451) chooses a basic land type, not a colour. The "any combination of colors" cards (Vivi Ornitier, Cascading Cataracts, Axebane Guardian) are a different shape and still unexpressed.
