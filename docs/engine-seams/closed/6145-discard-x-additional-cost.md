**Discard X cards as an additional cost** (#6145, CR 601.2f, CR 107.3a) — `AdditionalCost.DiscardX` (built with `effects.DiscardXCost()`, `DiscardXLandCost()` or `DiscardXCreatureCost()`) is "As an additional cost to cast this spell, discard X cards", on a spell with no {X} in its mana cost: the caster announces X on `CastSpellParams.XValue`, the slot pay-X-life and exile-X-cards ride, and names exactly X cards on `discard_ids`, each one the clause accepts (`DiscardAccepts`, which gained `DiscardCreature` beside `DiscardLand`). The discards are paid with the spell on the stack and recorded on `PaidCost.Discarded`, so "the discarded cards" reads them at resolution (`ctx.Discarded()`), and a target count bound to X ("Destroy X target lands") is the existing `CountFromX` clause. X = 0 is legal and discards nothing. The ceiling is the cards the hand can pay, read from one place, `Game.DiscardXCeilingForEffect`, by the view (`additional_cost.discard_x` / `discard_x_max` with `demands_x`, and `discard_options` for a narrowed clause) and the enumerator, which offers X of 1 or 2 with exactly X cards named and no open X range. The client asks X first, then opens the discard picker for exactly X. `Register` refuses it on an optional cost, an either/or branch, beside a fixed discard, or beside another cost paid with X. **Cards:** Channeled Force (Full).
