**Casting a spell out of another player's hand** (#5457, CR 608.2g, 601.2, 701.20) — "Target opponent chooses X cards from their hand. Look at those cards. You may cast a spell from among them without paying its mana cost" is the `resolution_cast` offer (`game.OfferResolutionCastForEffect`) over cards that sit in another seat's hand. The cast's source-zone lookup follows a stored hand permission to the hand that holds the card (`foreignHandForCastLocked`, the graveyard's and the library's #1022 / #1035 rule one zone over). A hand is the one zone its owner casts from with no permission (CR 601.2), so such a cast is held to the offer twice: the prompt names the permission's claim, and a cast out of a hand that is not the caster's with no claimed permission is refused (`ErrCardNotFound`), so the card is never castable at its printed cost or after the offer closes. The same lookup and guard, text for text, are on the branch that adds Silent-Blade Oni (`feat/5457-leftovers-b`), which looks at the whole hand (`game.LookAtHandForEffect`). `game.LookAtHandCardsForEffect` is the look: the looker becomes a knower of those cards and nobody else does, which is also what lets the prompt show them to its chooser and to no other seat. The offer is worded with `free_cast` and the decline label "Leave it in their hand" (`mayCast.ts` reads "it stays in their hand"). The spell records `hand` as the zone it was cast from, so an effect that asks "from your hand" by zone alone reads such a cast as one; no catalog card is known to tell them apart today. No snapshot change, no new wire field. **Cards now Full:** Extract Brain.
