**Layer invalidation on hand / life / attack / graveyard / spells-cast state** (#74, #1117, #1218, #1325) — closed on the 2026-09-24 re-triage (#1386): the row was fully stale. Its text, as it stood:

  > **What was missing:** **Hand, life and graveyard delivered** — hand size with #74, life total and graveyard contents with #1117. `StaticAbility.DependsOnLifeTotal` is `DependsOnHandSize`'s sibling: an opt-in invalidation hint, gated on the same battlefield walk, so a table holding nothing that reads a life total pays nothing for the many life changes a game of Commander makes. Its bump rides the WRITE to a life total (`Game.invalidateLayersForLifeChangeLocked`, three call sites) rather than an event, because damage to a player changes a life total without emitting `EventChangeLife` at all and emits `EventDealDamage` on OPPOSITE sides of the write on the combat and non-combat routes — a listener arm alone would recache the stale answer on the commonest route there is. The graveyard half is deliberately NOT gated on a flag: it widens the existing zone-keyed condition so any event whose `OldZone` / `NewZone` crosses a graveyard boundary bumps, arrivals and departures alike. The statics that read a graveyard are a pre-existing family of about ten (Tarmogoyf, Lord of Extinction, Nighthowler, Nighthawk Scavenger, Consuming Aberration, Jarad, Multani, Wight of the Reliquary, Elvish Reclaimer, The Warring Triad) that could not have declared a flag they predate, several of them built by shared helpers, and no test can catch a Lhurgoyf that forgets one; measured, the widening is one atomic add at emit and one 46us recompute at the next read. It was invisible until now because a creature DYING is a battlefield move and already bumped — only the mill, the discard and the resolving spell did not. **Attack-declaration invalidation is delivered too (#1218)** — the quarter #1117 left open. `StaticAbility.DependsOnAttackingStatus` is the third `DependsOnHandSize` sibling: the same opt-in walk, so a table with nothing reading "is this creature attacking" pays nothing for a declare-attackers step. The bump has THREE call sites because attacking status has three exits and only one of them is an event: `layerVersionBump.OnEvent`'s `EventAttack` arm covers the DECLARE half (the lock-in stamps `Card.AttackingTarget` before the event fires), and `Game.invalidateLayersForAttackChangeLocked` is called directly from `removeFromCombatLocked` (a control change pulling a permanent out mid-declaration, CR 506.4) and from `clearCombatLocked` (combat ending, CR 511.3) — neither mutates through an event that names every affected card, the same reason turn advance bumps from `onTurnBeganLocked` rather than a listener arm. **A fifth quarter, spells-cast invalidation, is delivered too (#1325)** — a dimension none of the first four rows named, found when Stoic Sphinx's "hexproof as long as you haven't cast a spell this turn" needed it. `StaticAbility.DependsOnSpellsCast` is the fourth `DependsOnHandSize` sibling, gated on the same opt-in walk; its bump is a single unconditional-shaped arm, `layerVersionBump.OnEvent`'s `EventCast` case, because the tally it reads (`Game.SpellsCastThisTurn`) has exactly one write site and that site already bumps the tally before the event fires (mutations.go), so there is no "removed mid-turn" exit the way attacking status has. All five quarters of this row are now closed.
