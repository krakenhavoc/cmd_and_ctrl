**Switching a creature's power and toughness** (#3996, CR 613.4c / 613.4d) — "switch target creature's power and toughness until end of turn" is a `ScopedEffect` with one `game.ModSwitchPT` mod (`"switchPT"`, `game.SwitchPTMod()`) in layer 7d, after every characteristic-defining ability, set, pump and P/T counter, whatever their timestamps (the engine's `SubLayer7E_Switch` is named for the older rules that put counters in 7d). P/T counters are added after the layer pass, so the switch records `Characteristic.PTSwitched` and `ptWithCounters` crosses the counters' deltas. A 1/4 with a +1/+0 counter is 2/4 switched to 4/2, and two switches cancel. The record is snapshotted like every scoped effect, and a binary that does not know `switchPT` refuses the restore point. The card side is `effects.SwitchPTUntilEOT{Target|Match}`, `SwitchTheModesTarget` for a modal bullet, `switchThisPT` for an ability's own creature and `switchEachTargetPT` for every still-legal target. **Cards** (24, all Full): Twisted Reflection, About Face, Aeromoeba, Aquamoeba, Calcite Snapper, Crag Puca, Crookclaw Transmuter, Dwarven Thaumaturgist, Flatman, Fluxcharger, Inside Out, Inversion Behemoth, Invert // Invent, Mannichi, the Fevered Dream, Merfolk Thaumaturgist, Myr Quadropod, Phantasmal Fiend, Reverse the Polarity, Transmutation, Turtleshell Changeling, Twisted Image, Valakut Fireboar, Wandering Fumarole and Windreaver.
