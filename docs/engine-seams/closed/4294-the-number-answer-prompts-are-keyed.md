**The number-answer prompts are keyed** (#4294, follow-up to #4289, amendments of 2026-10-24 to [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md), [ADR 0041](decisions/0041-game-persistence.md) and [ADR 0150](decisions/0150-other-players-pay-any-player-offers-and-assist.md)) — a table waiting on one is a restore point. `PayEnergyAmount`, `ChooseNumber`, `PayLifeAmount`, assist's helper prompt and the split of "tapped and attacking" tokens were the last `pay_amount` prompts on a Go closure. **The registry.** `game.RegisterNumberThen(key, fn)` registers a branch handed the number, the chooser and source, the card's `Carry` / `Words` / `Nums` and the asking item rebuilt from `AskedItem`; `effects.NumberThen` registers a `*Context` body; the key rides `PendingChoice.PickThen` (so an older binary refuses the file), the data rides `OptionCarry`, the new `PickWords` / `PickNums` and `Asked`, and the cause rides `PayCauseController` / `PayCauseItem`. `game.PayEnergyAmount` and `game.ChooseNumber` gain `Branch` in place of `Then`; the effects builders take a branch only. **Callers keyed:** the ten `PayEnergyAmount` cards (Harnessed Lightning and Galvanic Discharge share `number/deal-that-much-damage-to-the-target`; Aether Spike, Die Young, Aether Refinery, Suppression Ray, Localized Destruction, Territorial Aetherkite, Rampaging Aetherhood and Wrath of the Skies have one each), the `ChooseNumber` cards (Volcano Hellion, Expel the Interlopers, Arcane Denial, Forgotten Ancient, and Glissa Sunslayer and Resourceful Defense through `registerCounterChoice`, whose chain of questions keeps its place in the data), `PayLifeAmount` (Necrodominance, Plunge into Darkness), Phyrexian Processor's as-enters payment, assist (`assist/help-pay-for-a-spell`, the parked cast as `AssistAnnounce` and the spend context as plain data) and the attack split (`token/attack-split`, `game.AttackSplit`, a keyed tail registered with `AttackingTokensThen`: Tilonalli's Summoner, Dalkovan Encampment, Dorothea's Retribution, Mobilize, Sandstorm Crasher and Sauron, the Necromancer; Brimaz, General Kreat, Hero of Bladehold and Leonin Warleader have no tail). **Snapshot:** `pendingChoices[].pickWords`, `.pickNums`, `.attackSplit` and `.assist`, additive in v9 (`-update-shape`, no fixture edited). **Still a closure:** Calamity, Galloping Inferno's split, the myriad split of a token creation (`chooseTokenAttackSharesLocked`), `MayChoice`, ward's life, sacrifice and discard confirms, and the multi-seat discard runs. **Tests** (`effects/keyed_number_restore_test.go`, `game/number_then_test.go`, each a strict restore with the prompt open): Harnessed Lightning (both answers, the cause), Galvanic Discharge, Die Young, Suppression Ray, Volcano Hellion, Necrodominance, Glissa Sunslayer restored at both questions, Tilonalli's Summoner restored at both split questions, an assist helper's prompt (with an X, and a helper who leaves), the announcement through JSON, the split at each question, and an unknown number key and an unknown tail refused. **Back-outs:** see ADR 0129's amendment.
