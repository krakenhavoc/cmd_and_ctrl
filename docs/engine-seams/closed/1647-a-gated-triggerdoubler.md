**A gated TriggerDoubler** (#1647, CR 603.2d, ADR 0071 amendment). `game.TriggerDoubler` gets its own `ActiveWhen game.Designation` field: a `TriggerDoubler` is not an ability-list entry (`TriggersForCard` / `StaticAbilitiesForCard` never see one), so the ADR 0071 gate that switches Frostcliff Siege's lines on and off cannot reach it through those accessors. `triggerDoublersLocked` checks the gate directly against the doubler's own candidate card before consulting `Applies`, so an inactive doubler (the unchosen word, or an unanswered as-enters prompt — `DesignationChosenOption` never matches an empty `Card.ChosenOption`) contributes nothing, in the engine and at the wire alike. Card side: `DoublerWhenChosen(word, doubler)`, `choose_option.go`'s `WhenChosen` / `StaticWhenChosen` one door over. `specDesignations` and its two callers (Register's door guard, `TestEveryAnchorWordGateIsOffered`) walk the new slot too, so a card that gates a doubler on a word its own prompt never offers still fails at boot. **Windcrag Siege** ships `full` with both modes — Mardu is Isshin, Two Heavens as One's `DoublesAttacking(Creature())` gated to the word, Jeskai is Legion Warboss's token shape with lifelink and haste instead of an attack requirement — closing the gap #1572's entry left. **Glacierwood Siege**, **Hollowmurk Siege** and **Monastery Siege** ship `full` alongside it: a target-reading `Targeting()` trigger plus a `GatedCastPermissions` standing graveyard land-play permission, an any-kind `EventCounterPlaced` watcher (once each turn) plus an `OncePerBatch` "whenever you attack" trigger, and a draw-step `lootOne` plus a target-reading `CostModifier` (`TargetsYouOrYourPermanent`), respectively — no new machinery for any of the three.
