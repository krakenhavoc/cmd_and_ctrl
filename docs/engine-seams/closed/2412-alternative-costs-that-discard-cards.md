**Alternative costs that discard cards** (#2412, [ADR 0135](decisions/0135-alternative-costs-that-tap-discard-awaken-and-emerge.md) §2, PR 2) — "You may discard a Forest card rather than pay this spell's mana cost" needed no new component: `AlternativeCost.DiscardFromHand` arrived with retrace (#2528) and is paid through the one discard helper's cost path with the spell on the stack, so madness and discard payoffs see the card and a countered spell does not give it back. `effects.DiscardInstead(label, preds…)` is that offer with no mana; the subtype is read from the card in hand, so a nonbasic Forest or Dryad Arbor pays Snag. Foil's "an Island card and another card" widens #2526's set rule to a hand discard (owner decision 2): `TargetSpec.EachOf` on `DiscardFromHand` (`effects.DiscardEachInstead`), a new "any card" kind (`game.SacrificeKind.Any`, `effects.AnyCard`), and a matching that reads each candidate from the zone its component names (`costSetFitsLocked`). The validator, the offer's payability check, the enumerator's one set payment (`AltCostSetPaymentLocked`) and `pay_options.each_of` all read it, and `effects.Register` refuses a set rule on any other alternative-cost component. The offer view says `discards`, and the heuristic credits an alternative cost's discards with the bot's discard payoffs. The Infamous Cruelclaw's "cast that card by discarding a card rather than paying its mana cost" is `CastPermission.DiscardCard`, an additive snapshot field, on the exile-until-nonland grant it now shares with Amped Raptor. **Cards:** Snag, Abolish, Flameshot, Outbreak, Foil and The Infamous Cruelclaw (Full).
