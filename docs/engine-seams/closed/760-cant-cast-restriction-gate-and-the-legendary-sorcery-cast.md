**"Can't cast" restriction gate and the legendary-sorcery cast condition** (#760, [ADR 0073](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)) — closes both the "\"Can't cast\" restriction gate" row (count 4, audit 23 / 31) and the "Legendary-sorcery cast-condition hook" row (count 3), which is why they were one ADR. ONE function, `Game.CastGateLocked(caster, card, zone, params)`, with two callers and one view stamp: `CastSpell` at the point [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md) named (after every CR 601.2b choice, before any cost is paid, so a refused cast costs nothing and every free cast passes through it), `legal.castMovesForCard`, and `protocol.stampLegalTargets`, which turns the refusal into `cant_cast` on the card and clears `castable_here`. Two sources: a `CastRestriction` contributed by a battlefield permanent, collected through `CatalogAbilityKey` in the `CostModifier` / `CostQuery` shape — so ability removal turns it off, a designation gate switches it off, and nothing is stored, which IS the duration — and the spell's own `Spec.CastCondition`, read off the CardDef under `CatalogKey`. A refusal is a `*CantCastError` wrapping `ErrCantCast` and carrying the printed clause. Split second deliberately stays OUT: it restricts taking an action, blocks activations too, and correctly does not block a land play (CR 702.61b). **Cards:** Rule of Law, Grafdigger's Cage (with a caveat — only its second sentence is a cast restriction), Urza's Ruinous Blast and Rakdos, Lord of Riots, whose "unless an opponent lost life this turn" the audit had misattributed and which is the legendary-sorcery shape exactly. Still open and named in the ADR: bans with a DURATION (Silence's "this turn", Reflector Mage's "until your next turn") want #755's registries, and a ban on a chosen card NAME (Meddling Mage, Nevermore) wants a choose-a-card-name prompt the engine does not have. A nonartifact cast count (Ethersworn Canonist) is one line when its card arrives.
