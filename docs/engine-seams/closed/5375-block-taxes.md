**Block taxes** (#5375, CR 509.1c, 509.1d, 509.1f, [ADR 0221](decisions/0221-block-taxes.md)) — "creatures can't block unless their controller pays {1} for each of those creatures", Archangel of Tithes' attacking half; the mirror of the attack tax ([ADR 0080](decisions/0080-attack-taxes.md)). **`game.BlockTax`** is a catalog static (`Spec.BlockTaxes`, `effects.BlockTax` / `BlockTaxWhileAttacking`) read through one accessor, priced per blocking creature by concatenation, with no structural "you" because the printed clause has none. Blocks stay staged and revisable, so nothing is charged when one is staged: the price is determined and paid as the defender's declaration completes — `finish_blocks` pays with a new optional `{auto_tap, locked_sources, phyrexian_life}` posture and is refused unpaid (`block_tax_unpaid`, declaration still open), a pending defender's pass pays from the pool or is refused, and the step ending takes back blocks the pool can't pay for. A taxed creature that isn't blocking is left out of the CR 509.1c requirement search. The enumerator offers a block only while the staged blocks plus it are affordable and stamps `MoveCost.mana`; `finish_blocks` carries the whole price and `auto_tap`; the heuristic weighs it like an attack tax. The wire adds `TurnView.block_taxes`, which the client shows on block staging and pays with auto-tap. No snapshot change. **Cards now Full:** Archangel of Tithes. **Still open:** no block-tax lock-a-land picker in the client (it auto-taps, as the attack dock's one-click path does).
