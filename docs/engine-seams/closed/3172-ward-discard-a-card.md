**Ward with a discard cost** (#3172 slice cav-e, CR 702.21a, CR 118.3) — `effects.WardDiscard(n)` is a fifth `WardCost` component beside mana, waterbend, life and sacrifice: the caster confirms, then picks the cards from their own hand; a caster with fewer cards than the cost is countered with no prompt. Cards: Graveyard Trespasser // Graveyard Glutton, Gideon the Oathless.
