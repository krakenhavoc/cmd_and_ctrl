**Discarding X cards as a cost** (#2527, [ADR 0113](decisions/0113-small-seams-for-the-s58-deck-requests.md)'s 2026-10-07 amendment, CR 107.3a / 602.2b) — a discard cost had a fixed count, so Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor's "{4}{B}{B}{B}, Discard X cards" could be written only as a different card or a free one. `game.DiscardCost.CountFromX` (`effects.DiscardX`) is the discard twin of `SacrificeX` and `TapXUntapped`: the count is the X announced with the activation, the activator names exactly that many distinct cards in hand (`discard_ids`, their number is `x_value`), and the effect reads the same number with `ctx.X()`. The mana cost stays the printed one whatever X is. `Register` refuses it beside `{X}` in the mana cost, a sacrifice-X or tap-X clause, another hand-spending component, or on a mana ability. The wire carries `discard_cost_count_from_x` (public; the options stay controller-only), the client always asks how many with its discard picker and skips the X stepper, and the enumerator offers the cheapest 1, 2 and 3 cards, never the X = 0 no-op for a card that declares `XMatters`, and nothing for an empty hand. `ExileTopWithPermission.Free` ("without paying its mana cost") joins `WhileExiled` for the free play that lasts as long as the card stays exiled. **Cards:** Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor (now `full`). **Still open:** the form as a cast's additional cost or a mana ability's cost, which no printed card uses.
