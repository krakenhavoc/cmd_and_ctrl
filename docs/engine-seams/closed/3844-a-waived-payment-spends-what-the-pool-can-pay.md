**A waived payment spends what the pool can pay** (#3844, [ADR 0068 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0068-the-mana-spent-on-a-spell.md)) — a permissive cast or activation whose pool cannot cover the cost, and a strict-mode Cast anyway, used to leave the pool untouched and record `PaidCost{OnPaper: true}` with no tokens, so no spend rider could fire and every mana-spent reader read zero. Now the pool pays the share it can (`ManaPool.SpendWhatPaysForWith`, the same `allocate` solver a funded payment uses, restrictions and colour strategy included) and only the shortfall is waived: the record holds the tokens that paid beside `OnPaper`, riders fire on them, and the counting readers (`ManaSpent.Total`, `Count`, `Colors`, `CountFrom`, `From`) read them as a lower bound while `None` stays false. A Cast anyway the pool covers in full is an ordinary paid cast (no cost warning, `EventCast.Unpaid` false). **Cards made Full (18):** Path of Ancestry, Cavern of Souls, Boseiju, Who Shelters All, Delighted Halfling, Hall of the Bandit Lord, Arena of Glory, Generator Servant, Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind, Domri, Chaos Bringer, Biophagus, Opal Palace, Scaled Nurturer, Pyromancer's Goggles, Primal Wellspring, Tecutlan, the Searing Rift, Hired Hexblade, Rain of Riches and Kalain, Reclusive Painter. **Reworded, still caveated:** the colour and amount readers (sunburst, converge, adamant, "the amount of mana spent", Coin of Mastery's artifact-source count) now say only pool mana counts; the "if no mana was spent" readers say the engine can't tell a spell paid outside the pool from a free one.
