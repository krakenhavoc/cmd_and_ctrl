**Attack taxes** (#1063, CR 508.1a, [ADR 0080](decisions/0080-attack-taxes.md)) — "creatures can't attack you unless their controller pays {2} for each creature they control that's attacking you". [ADR 0045](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) named this as deliberately outside the restriction vocabulary and predicted the shape it wanted; this is that shape. **`game.AttackTax`** is a per-permanent static on the DEFENDER's side, catalog-contributed like `CostModifier` and gated in one accessor (`AttackTaxesForCard`, through `CatalogAbilityKey`, so a permanent under CR 613.1f stops taxing and the engine and the enumerator cannot price a declaration differently). Its "you" is **structural** — a tax protects its own controller and nobody else, so no card file can typo its way into taxing the table — and the one line cards differ on, "you" versus "you or planeswalkers you control", is `AttackTaxScope` with the NARROWER reading as the zero value, so a card that forgets under-taxes. `ManaCost` returns a cost STRING per attacking creature, because a declaration sums several by concatenation (`{2}` three times is `{2}{2}{2}`, six generic, no arithmetic to get wrong) and because a count-scaled tax renders its count once (Sphere of Safety) and a hybrid or Phyrexian tax is already what the parser eats (Norn's Annex, when it is written). **`PriceAttackDeclaration`** is `PriceCast`'s sibling, not `PriceCast`: what the two share is the mana machinery one level down, which `payAbilityManaCostLocked` already shared between casts, activations and special actions — so the declaration became that payer's fourth caller and `ManaTrigger` fires for its taps with no second copy of anything. The cost is charged **at CR 508.1a, on the declaration, before anything is staged**: `DeclareAttackerWith` / `DeclareAttackersWith` price the ELIGIBLE set, pay it ALL OR NOTHING, and leave the board untouched on a refusal (`ErrAttackTaxUnpaid`). No `pay_unless` prompt — CR 508.1a is part of the turn-based action, so the action that declares the attack pays for it, exactly as ADR 0073 has the action that casts a spell pay its additional costs. `legal/combat.go` prices every candidate through the same function, drops what the seat cannot afford (#544), stamps `MoveCost.Mana` so a policy that may not import `internal/game` can weigh it, and sets `auto_tap` when the move is offered on the strength of the tapper. The heuristic subtracts it in `attackValue`. Propaganda, Ghostly Prison and Sphere of Safety ship `full`. **Still open and a different seam**: the count-limit shape (Silent Arbiter, Crawlspace), which is a property of the whole declaration rather than a price on it and belongs beside `blockerBoundsLocked`.
