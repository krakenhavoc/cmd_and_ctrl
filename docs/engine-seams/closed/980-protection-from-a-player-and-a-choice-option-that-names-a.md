**Protection from a player, and a choice option that names a seat** (#980 / #994, [ADR 0072 §7 amendment 2026-09-19](decisions/0072-protection.md), [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-09-19](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — closes the last piece of the protection row (CR 702.16k) and the last piece of the choose-a-player row, which were the same piece. **`Card.ChosenPlayer`** is the CR 614.12 "as this enters, choose a player" answer, modelled on `Card.ChosenColor` to the letter: per instance, `carried` by clone and the snapshot, cleared at BOTH CR 400.7 sites (`zone.go`'s battlefield exit and `resetAsNewObjectLocked`'s new-object reset, because a blinked permanent reaches the second without passing the first), and not a copiable value (CR 707.2) — which needs no rule, since `CopiableValuesOf` projects printed characteristics and never looks at it. **`Game.QueueChoosePlayerAsEntersForEffect(chooser, source, question, among)`** writes it, queued from the `AsEnters` hook rather than by pausing the CR 614 pipeline (S26's declared simplification, third use), with `effects.ChoosePlayerAsEnters(label, pool)` and `ChosenPlayerOf` as the card-facing pair. **No new prompt kind, again**: it is #929's `option_pick` with #929's option list, so the gate, the enumerator, the wire and the client modal are untouched. **The quality** is `ProtectionQualityPlayer`, token `game.ProtectionFromChosenPlayer`, and it is the one entry in the closed grammar tested against the source's CONTROLLER rather than any characteristic of it — which is what `TargetSource.Controller` and `Characteristic.Controller` on the damage LKI were already carrying. The seat is NOT in the token: the parser records only the kind and the READER binds `ProtectionQuality.Player` off the protected card (`bindProtectionQuality`, called by `ProtectionQualities` and `MatchedProtection`), so all four DEBT checks keep their existing `ProtectedFrom(card, chars)` call unchanged and `Printed` stays "the chosen player" — no raw UUID reaches a display string. An unanswered prompt leaves the permanent protected from NOBODY, never from everybody. **#994, the seam under it:** `ChoiceOption.Player` lets an option name its subject, `uuid.Nil` on every option that is not about a seat. A departed player is now pruned off every open option list — `reassignChoiceLocked` when the chooser is the one leaving, `pruneDepartedSeatOptionsLocked` (from `leaveGameObjectsLocked`) when anybody else is — where before, eligibility was filtered once at queue time and a prompt went on offering somebody who was not a player (CR 800.4a). A list pruned to nothing is dropped rather than left unanswerable. Because the list can shrink, a seat prompt is answered with its SEAT and not its index: `OptionPickPrompt.ThenSeat` receives `PickOptions[i].Player` off the list the chooser was shown, and `PickOptionView.player` puts the same id on the wire (public by CR 400.2, so no redaction). **2 cards:** True-Name Nemesis (new — the quality end to end) and Sawhorn Nemesis (new — the same stored field read by a damage replacement, touching no protection code, which is the evidence the field is general as-enters machinery and not a protection back door). A PLAYER having protection was the last unrelated piece and closed with #1197 (its own entry above).
