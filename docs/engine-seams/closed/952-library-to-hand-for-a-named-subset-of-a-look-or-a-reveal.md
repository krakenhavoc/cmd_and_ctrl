**Library to hand for a named subset of a look or a reveal** (#952) — "look at / reveal the top N, take a subset, do something with the rest" is one of the most common templates in the format and the engine had no door for its middle step. `Game.TakeFromLibraryToHandThenForEffect(player, ids, then)` and its fire-and-forget twin (`server/internal/game/library_to_hand.go`) are that door, on the ordinary route machinery: `takeToHandRoute` is a sibling of `bounceRoute` carrying the taking player as `Actor`, the move goes through `routeAllThenLocked` so it is one simultaneous exit with the CR 614 window open and CR 903.9 offered, and `then` is told what LANDED (CR 400.7) rather than what was asked to move. Cards that are no longer in a library are skipped, which is the whole difference between this and the bounce the catalog had been borrowing: `zoneRoute.CardID` finds a card's zone by scan, so "return it to its owner's hand" happens to move a card that was never on the battlefield, and Goblin Ringleader shipped on that accident. The card side is `effects.TakeFromLibraryToHand` (`take_from_library.go`), `put_from_library.go`'s twin clause for clause — `Match` / `Max` / `Optional` / `All` over a `choose_cards` prompt with `Zone: ZoneLibrary`, a `Reveal` flag for "you may REVEAL a creature card from among them" (the take becomes public, the rest of a LOOK stays private), and a `Then` that is handed `Taken` and `Rest` — with `TakeRestOnBottomInRandomOrder`, `TakeRestIntoGraveyard`, and the whole-sentence helpers `LookAtTopThenMayTakeToHand` and `RevealTopThenTakeToHand`. Shipped on **Horn of the Mark** (full: the card prints "in a random order", so no caveat), and `b41RevealTopThenTakeMatching` — Goblin Ringleader — moved onto it off the bounce. **#952's triage correction:** of the four cards filed against the closed #744, only Horn of the Mark was waiting on this, and none of the four was waiting on a random-order bottom, which is what #744 was. **Ao, the Dawn Sky** puts cards onto the BATTLEFIELD, not into a hand; it waits on a set-level "total mana value 4 or less" rule over the picked set — the row above. **Chaos Wand** is not blocked at all and can be written today: `effects.MillToZone{To: ZoneExile, Until: …}` is the exile-until and takes any player's library, `CastPermission.Player` is keyed to a PLAYER rather than the card's owner (`{0}` + `CastOnly`, cascade's grant), and `PutOnBottomInRandomOrderForEffect(actor, ZoneExile, ids)` routes the leftovers to that library's bottom. It carries cascade's declared simplification as a caveat: the engine has no inline cast during a resolution, so "you may cast it" is a permission taken as a separate action. **Sandstone Oracle** reads a hand size and draws — Slithermuse's sentence, already written — and waited only on the choose-an-opponent prompt, #929, which landed in the row below while this was in flight; it is unblocked and ready to write. The wider roadmap row this leg belongs to — "Library-top placement and ordered look", 45 / 31 / 37 sole unlocks across the three passes — finally has a tracker, **#996**, and the ordered bottom ("in any order") that Goblin Ringleader's caveat names is the largest thing still open on it. Ao's one-field gap is **#998**.
