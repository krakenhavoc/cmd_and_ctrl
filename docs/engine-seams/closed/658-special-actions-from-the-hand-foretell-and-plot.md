**Special actions from the hand, foretell and plot** (#658, #1342, [ADR 0062](decisions/0062-abilities-and-special-actions-from-the-hand.md) Decision 4) — the CR 116.2 verb, built: one `special_action {card_id, kind}` wire verb, one engine entry (`game.PerformSpecialAction`), one per-kind timing table (`SpecialActionTimingOKLocked`) that the engine, the bot enumerator and the wire's `available` flag all read. No stack, no announce, nothing to respond to. Foretell is its first kind: the special action pays {2} and exiles the card through the shared exit primitive with `FaceDownKind = foretold`, so ADR 0069's viewers table makes the owner the only knower; the cast out of exile is a per-instance `game.CastPermission` (ADR 0066) scoped to that one object, keyed `"foretell"`, priced at the card's foretell cost and floored at `NotBeforeTurn = turn + 1` (CR 702.143a). "If this spell was foretold" (CR 702.143c) is `StackItem.Foretold`, stamped at announce from the card's face-down state because CR 406.3a turns it face up as it is cast — so it is true of a foretold card cast any way, not only of one cast for the foretell cost. CR 702.143f's game-end reveal joins the owner-leaves half ADR 0069 shipped. `effects.Foretell(cost)` is the card-file line; `Spec.SpecialActions` is the slot; `Register` refuses at boot a kind the engine cannot perform. "foretell" joined `canonicalKeywords`. Shipped on **Saw It Coming**, **Behold the Multiverse** and **Cosmic Intervention** (whose caveat claiming `CastableZones` + `AlternativeCost.FromZone` already covered it was wrong twice and is gone). **A special action's cost is priced through the CR 601.2f pass too, since #1319** — the special action verb used to parse its printed string and pay it directly, so an effect that discounts a special action had nowhere to reach. `CostQuery.SpecialAction` and `CostModifier.SpecialActions` are `.Ability` / `.Activations`'s third door (a cast, an activation and a special action price through the same pass and never share a modifier), `Game.SpecialActionManaCostForEffect` is `AbilityManaCostForEffect`'s special-action twin, and `Game.ForetoldThisTurn` / `ForetoldCountThisTurn` is the per-turn tally "the first card you foretell each turn" reads — bumped in `foretellLocked` only once a foretold card actually lands in exile. `effects.SpecialActionCostsLess` is the card-file constructor. Shipped on **Ranar the Ever-Watchful** (its "create a Spirit" trigger — #1320's own row above — still carries the one-event-batch caveat, #1341). **Plot is the fourth kind, since #1342** ([ADR 0062 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0062-abilities-and-special-actions-from-the-hand.md)): `SpecialActionPlot`, a hand kind whose window is `sorcerySpeedOpenLocked` (the keyword's window, not the card's — an instant is plotted at sorcery speed too), and `plotLocked` exiles the card face up with `MoveCauseSpecialAction` and calls #1318's `PlotExiledCardForEffect`, so a card plotted from hand and one Aven Interrupter plotted share one permission. `effects.Plot(cost)` is the card-file line and "plot" joined `canonicalKeywords`; the enumerator, wire row and client menu row needed no code. Shipped on **Djinn of Fool's Fall**, **Spinewoods Paladin**, **Beastbond Outcaster** and **Plan the Heist**. **"When this card becomes plotted" is an event, since #1382** ([ADR 0062 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0062-abilities-and-special-actions-from-the-hand.md)): `game.EventBecomesPlotted`, emitted only by `PlotExiledCardForEffect` once the card is in exile and plotted, so the special action and "it becomes plotted" effects both fire it and a plain exile never does; `Actor` is the plotter, `Source` what did it. `effects.WhenThisBecomesPlotted` watches it from exile (#925's `TriggeredAbility.Zones`), and the trigger is the owner's. Shipped on **Longhorn Sharpshooter** and **Aloe Alchemist**. **A special action granted to another card, since #1391** ([ADR 0062 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0062-abilities-and-special-actions-from-the-hand.md)): `game.SpecialActionGrant` on `CardDef.SpecialActionGrants` lets a permanent give a kind to a card in another zone — only plot from the top of the controller's own library is built — optionally at the card's own mana cost. `Game.SpecialActionsOfferedLocked(actor, card, zone)` is the one accessor the engine, the enumerator and the view ask; `special_action` gained an optional `cost` to pick between two offers of one kind; the view stamps the rows on the library top (owner's frame only) and the client shows them as pills beside the library pile. Shipped on **Fblthp, Lost on the Range**. Still open on this family: Starnheim Unleashed's {X}{X}{W} foretell cost, and the "was foretold" payoffs (Poison the Cup, Haunting Voyage) — the bit they read exists, the cards are not written.
