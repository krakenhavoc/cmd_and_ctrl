**Meld** (#2699, [ADR 0145](decisions/0145-meld.md)): CR 701.42 and 712.4. A melded permanent is one `game.Card` whose printed fields, oracle ID and printing are the combined back face's own Scryfall record, carrying its two cards in `Card.MeldedFrom`; the deck importer stamps every meld card with that back face (`Card.Meld`, from the record's `meld_result` part, which `cards.Index` now keeps and attaches). `effects.MeldWith` checks the printed "if you both own and control" condition at resolution and calls `Game.MeldForEffect`, which exiles both as one simultaneous exit and returns them melded through the ordinary exile entry; a pair that isn't one stays in exile (CR 701.42c). `MoveCard` splits the permanent into its two cards anywhere but the battlefield and the stack (CR 712.21): the carrier keeps the permanent's ID and its one leaves-the-battlefield event, the other card gets its own zone move, the owner is asked their order in a graveyard or library (CR 712.21a), a melded commander sends only its commander card to the command zone (CR 903.9c), and an exile return finds both cards (CR 712.21c). Its mana value is the sum of its cards' (CR 712.8g); it can't be turned face down (CR 712.16) or transformed (CR 712.4c). Deck validation drops the meld banner and refuses a combined back face listed as a card. The client's hover panel shows the two cards and a meld card's back face. Urza, Planeswalker brought two small engine pieces: `Spec.LoyaltyTwiceEachTurn` (CR 606.3's limit as two) and `NextSpellPromise.EveryMatchingSpell` (a reduction for every matching spell this turn). **Cards:** Urza, Lord Protector, Urza, Planeswalker, Hanweir Battlements and Hanweir, the Writhing Township (Full); The Mightstone and Weakstone and Hanweir Garrison lose their meld caveats.
