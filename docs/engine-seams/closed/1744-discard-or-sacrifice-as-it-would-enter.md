**Discarding or sacrificing as a permanent would enter** (#1744, [ADR 0098](decisions/0098-discard-as-a-permanent-would-enter.md)): "if this would enter, you may discard a land card instead … if you don't, put it into its owner's graveyard" and "sacrifice a Forest instead" are one CR 614.1a entry replacement with a choice inside it. #1198's `EntryHandReveal` is now `ReplacementEffect.EntryCardChoice` with an `Action` (reveal, discard, sacrifice), asked through `entry_discard_from_hand` and `entry_sacrifice` beside `entry_reveal_from_hand`. The discard is an effect's (`DiscardCauseEffect`, so Library of Leng and every discard payoff see it) and the sacrifice goes through `SacrificeAllThenForEffect`; either may pause, and the paused entry rides a frozen copy of the event. "If you do" means the card really left. A redirected entry is now moved to where its window sent it (`moveRedirectedEntryLocked`, inside `executeEntryToBattlefieldLocked`), fixing two latent bugs: three entry sites treated a redirect as a cancel (stranding a resolving permanent on the stack) and the stack-resolution and land-play sites put the card onto the battlefield anyway. A window that cannot ask now runs the decline of a question whose decline IS the replacement (`declineIsReplace`: a shockland or reveal-land enters tapped, a Mox goes to the graveyard) instead of skipping it, and the apply-loop and `ResolveReplacementOrder` share one question dispatcher (`offerOwnQuestionLocked`), which gives an ordered reveal-land its prompt back. **Cards:** Mox Diamond, Heart of Yavimaya, Kjeldoran Outpost, Lake of the Dead, Balduvian Trading Post, Soldevi Excavations, Lotus Vale and Scorched Ruins, all complete. Still open on the row: Indominus Rex, Alpha.
