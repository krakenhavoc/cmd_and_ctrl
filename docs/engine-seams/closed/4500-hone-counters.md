**Hone counters** (#4500, CR 122.1j, CR 613.4c) — a hone counter on an Equipment gives +1/+0 to the creature it is attached to. The counter sits on one permanent and changes another, so it cannot ride the counters-after-the-pass step that +1/+1 counters use. `game.CounterHone` (`"hone"`) is a card counter kind, and `honeCounterEffectsLocked` (`game/hone_counters.go`) is one more source list in the layer pass's gather, beside the keyword counters: one source-less layer 7c effect per attached Equipment that carries hone counters, targeting the permanent it is attached to, at the Equipment's CR 613.7e timestamp, worth one power per counter.
- The gather re-reads the battlefield on every pass, so the bonus follows the Equipment when it is re-equipped, ends when it is unattached or leaves, tracks every counter added or removed (both already invalidate the pass), and adds up across several Equipment on one creature. A hone counter on a permanent that is not an Equipment, or on an Equipment attached to nothing, gives nothing. A "loses all abilities" on the Equipment does not switch the counters off: a counter is not an ability.
- **Snapshot:** unchanged. `hone` is one more key in the free-form `Card.Counters` map, which an older restore point never holds, so nothing is added to the shape and no fixture is edited. **Closure ratchet:** unchanged (no func or interface route from `Game`). **Client:** a pinned `counterTypes.ts` style, so the pip reads "Hone" and not "hon".
- **Cards (2, both Full):** Sting, Bilbo's Sword (flash; counters for each creature the target opponent controls, then attach to up to one target creature you control; Equip {3}) and Dwalin, Weaponmaster (first strike; a hone counter on each Equipment you control when it enters or attacks).
- **Tests:** `game/hone_counters_test.go` (unattached gives nothing, attach gives +N/+0, counter changes, moving and unattaching, two Equipment stack, layer 7c after a 7b set, a non-Equipment, an Equipment that leaves, snapshot round trip) and `cards/effects/hone_counters_cards_test.go` (Sting's count and optional attach, an opponent with no creatures, no creature chosen, flash, equip; Dwalin's enter and attack triggers). **Back-outs:** leaving the gather out of `activeStaticAbilitiesLocked` fails eight engine tests; dropping the Equipment check fails the non-Equipment test; moving the effect to layer 7b fails the 7c ordering test; counting every creature instead of the target opponent's fails four Sting tests; making Dwalin's trigger enter-only fails the attack test.
