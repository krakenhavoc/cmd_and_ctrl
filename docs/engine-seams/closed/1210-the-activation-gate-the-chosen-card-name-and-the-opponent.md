**The activation gate, the chosen card name, and the opponent-activation watch** (#1210, CR 602.5 / CR 101.2 / CR 614.12, [ADR 0073 amendment 2026-09-22](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md), [ADR 0018 note 2026-09-22](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — closes the **"Can't be activated" activation gate** row (count 3) and the **"Whenever an opponent activates an ability" watch** row (count 2), and re-triages the **Ability-suppression static** row above, whose stated primitive had already shipped. ONE function, `Game.ActivationGateLocked(activator, card, zone, ability)`, a field-for-field twin of #760's `CastGateLocked` and written as a twin rather than a widening — a cast has a spell, a source zone, a face and an announcement, an activation has a source OBJECT that stays where it is, an ability INDEX inside it and a CR 605.1a mana/non-mana split no cast has, and one merged query would have made every field optional. `ActivationRestriction` is contributed by a battlefield permanent through `CatalogAbilityKey` in the `CastRestriction` / `CostModifier` shape — so CR 613.1f silences a restrictor, [ADR 0071](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)'s designation gate switches one off, and nothing is stored, which IS the duration. A refusal is a `*CantActivateError` wrapping the EXISTING `ErrCantActivate`, so every `errors.Is` in the tree still matched and no caller changed. **`ActivationAbility.Mana` rides the QUERY and not the call site**, which is the decision the whole design turns on: Pithing Needle prints "unless they're mana abilities" and Cursed Totem does not, so the exemption belongs to the restriction — a call site that decided it would have made Cursed Totem unwritable without a second gate. **FOUR callers**: `ActivateCatalogAbility` (after the CR 113.6 zone check, before timing, X, targets and every cost — a refused activation pays nothing), `ActivateManaAbility`, `legal.abilityMovesForSource` / `legal.manaMoves` behind a board-wide fast negative (#544), and — the one the cast gate has no equivalent of — **`gatherTapSources`**, because the auto-tapper never goes through `ActivateManaAbility` at all (`materializePlanLocked` taps the permanent and mints its mana directly), so without it the plan SUCCEEDS and produces mana the rule forbids. `ProducibleManaLocked` deliberately does NOT ask: CR 106.7 is about what an ability would do IF IT RESOLVED, and #1183's exhaust narrowing stays the one declared exception. The view stamp is `cant_activate` on the ABILITY ROW (`ActivatedAbilityView` / `ManaAbilityView`), carrying the printed clause the way `cant_cast` does and sitting beside `condition_unmet` and `exhausted` rather than merging with either — the three recover differently and the client says so. `CardView.Restrictions`' existing `cant_activate` TOKEN is the per-permanent Arrest bit and is untouched. **`Card.ChosenName`** is the as-enters family's fourth member (after S26's creature type, #742's colour and #980/#1007's player) and the piece ADR 0073 §7 named as missing in its own file. The one way it differs from its three siblings: **there is no vocabulary**. CR 201.2 lets a player name ANY card name, so `ResolveCardNameChoice` checks the SHAPE (trimmed, non-empty, `MaxChosenNameLen`) and nothing else, and the wire's `name_options` is a SUGGESTION list rebuilt from PUBLIC zones only — a convenience for the picker, never a legal set, and never a hidden-information leak. `CardNameMatches` is the one comparison and asks every FACE (CR 201.2b), because `Card.Name` is the ACTIVE face's name and a direct comparison would stop matching the moment a named permanent transformed. Carried by clone and the snapshot (`chosenName`, `carried` in `snapshot_drift_test.go`), cleared at both CR 400.7 sites, and not copiable (CR 706.2), which falls out of `CopiableValuesOf` never looking there. **Meddling Mage and Nevermore, ADR 0073 §7's own out-of-scope item, are now buildable** and are not written here. **The watch needed no engine change at all**, which is what #1184 claimed when it built `EventActivateAbility` wider than its two cards: `effects.WheneverAnOpponentActivates(label, of, includeMana, build)` is one constructor over that event, with "if it isn't a mana ability" expressed as the ABSENCE of `EventManaAbilityActivated` from `Watches` rather than as a predicate a card file could forget, the source predicate run against the ability's source looked up live, and the activator handed to a `Build` closure the way Ob Nixilis, the Hate-Twisted captures a drawer — so "that player" does not target. **Cards (6, all `full`):** Cursed Totem, Collector Ouphe, Linvala, Keeper of Silence, Pithing Needle, Harsh Mentor and Runic Armasaur. **Still open and named in the ADR:** an activation ban with a DURATION ("activated abilities can't be activated this turn"), which wants #755's registries and slots into `ActivationGateLocked` without changing its signature; split second, which stays outside the gate exactly as it stays outside the cast gate; and a restriction on LOYALTY abilities as a class — **the bool arrived with #1208** (`ActivationAbility.Loyalty`, derived from the loyalty cost component so a catalog entry cannot forget CR 606.3), read by the timing twin below and by nothing in the gate, so a restriction that wants it has the field waiting.
