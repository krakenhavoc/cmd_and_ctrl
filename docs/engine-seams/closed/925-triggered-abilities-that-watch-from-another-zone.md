**Triggered abilities that watch from another zone** (#925, CR 113.6) — `TriggeredAbility.Zones []ZoneKind` is the trigger-side sibling of `ActivatedAbilityShape.Zones` and of `CastableZones`: nil means the battlefield, `{ZoneGraveyard}` is "when you cycle this card" (CR 702.29c), Bloodghast's landfall and Narcomoeba's arrival, `{ZoneExile}` is suspend's countdown (CR 702.62b). ONE narrow harvest (`harvestFromDeclaredZones`, `server/internal/game/trigger_zones.go`) walks only the zones some registered card declared for THIS event kind, off an index `effects.Register` builds once at boot — so an event kind nothing declares costs one map lookup and the battlefield walk's per-event cost is unchanged. A declared zone list REPLACES the default rather than adding to it, checked in the same three places the battlefield is walked (`harvestFromZone`, `harvestLTB`, the simultaneous-exit pass), so a graveyard ability can never fire off a permanent. "You" is the card's OWNER while it sits outside the battlefield (CR 108.4), which is what decides both the predicate and who gets the stack item. Everything downstream is unchanged: `dispatchTriggerLocked`, the CR 603.3d target drop, the CR 603.5 prompt, `OncePerBatch` / `BatchKey`, the APNAP drain and undo across the prompt. Constructors: `effects.InGraveyard`, `effects.InExile`, `effects.WhenThisIsPutIntoYourGraveyardFromYourLibrary`. `Register` panics at boot on any zone but the graveyard and exile (`game.TriggerZoneUnsupported`), including `ZoneStack`, which is `FromStack`. Shipped on Narcomoeba and Bloodghast (the latter with its conditional haste declared as a caveat — a static keyed on a life total goes stale, the "Layer invalidation on hand / life / attack / graveyard state" row). Suspend's exile countdown (#659) has since landed on it, and Magmakin Artillerist's own "when you cycle this card" trigger (#1392) is the first `EventCycle` reader — `InGraveyard(On(game.EventCycle, Self, …))` — dropping its cycle-trigger caveat (it keeps one unrelated caveat, the printed CR 603.1 discard-batching behaviour). Decree of Pain is still open on this row.
