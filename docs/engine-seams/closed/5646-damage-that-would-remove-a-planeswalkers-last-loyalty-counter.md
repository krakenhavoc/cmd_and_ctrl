**Damage that would remove a planeswalker's last loyalty counter** (#5646, [ADR 0032](decisions/0032-planeswalkers.md), CR 120.3c, 120.4c, 306.8, 614.1a) — "As long as you control a creature, if damage dealt to a planeswalker you control of the chosen type would result in all loyalty counters on it being removed, instead all but one of those counters are removed" is a battlefield static, `Spec.DamageLastLoyalty` (`game.DamageLastLoyaltyStatic{OfChosenType, WhileYouControlACreature}`, `game/damage_last_loyalty.go`), read live through `game.CatalogDamageLastLoyalty` keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey`, so a permanent that has left or lost its abilities caps nothing (the `DamageAsThough` pattern, #1889). It replaces a result of the damage, not the damage (CR 120.4c), so it is read in the one place damage becomes loyalty removal, `applyDamageToPermanentLocked`, after the CR 614 damage window has settled the amount: the damage is dealt in full, lifelink gains all of it, a planeswalker that is also a creature has all of it marked, and "damage can't be prevented" changes nothing. Only the counters removed are capped, at one fewer than the planeswalker has, so one at a single counter keeps it. A loyalty cost or a counter-removing effect is not damage and is unchanged. "Choose a planeswalker type" as the permanent enters is `effects.ChoosePlaneswalkerTypeAsEnters`, the as-enters option pick over CR 205.3j's types; the answer is `Card.ChosenOption`, which the snapshot already carried, so there is no new snapshot field, effect key or wire field. **Cards:** Deification (Full).
