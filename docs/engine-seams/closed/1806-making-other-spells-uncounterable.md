**Making other spells uncounterable** (#1806, [ADR 0106](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md) §4, PRs 2 and 3) — every form of "can't be countered" is a rule-modifying effect (CR 613.11), read at one gate, `spellCantBeCounteredLocked`, which every counter verb and the stack chip ask. It has five sources, asked in order: the spell's printed rider; the mana that paid for it (#1547); **marks on the stack item** (`StackItem.CantBeCountered`, a `game.CounterShieldMark` per source — "Target spell can't be countered", Vexing Shusher, lasting while that object is on the stack, CR 400.7, kept by a stolen spell, never inherited by a copy, CR 707.2); **the battlefield statics** (`Spec.SpellsCantBeCountered`, `game.CounterShieldStatic` in its "you control", "you cast" and "any player" forms, read live and keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey`); and **a player's "this turn" grants** (`PlayerStatic.CantBeCountered`, a `game.CounterShieldGrant` with a `PermissionFilter`, "you control" or "you cast", and an `Except` object for "other spells", ended at cleanup, CR 514.2). The same payload with `NextOnly` is **the one-use promise** ("the next creature spell you cast this turn can't be countered"): never read at the gate, it is spent at CR 601.2i by the first matching spell its player casts, and turned into a mark on that spell; two matching promises are both spent; a copy (CR 707.10) and a land spend nothing. Both new fields are plain data on disk (additive in v7; an older binary refuses a file with a mark). The player view lists live grants and unspent promises as `counter_shields`, shown on the seat as a NO COUNTER badge. **Cards:** Chimil, the Inner Sun (Full) and thirteen other printed statics in PR 2; Insist, Overmaster, Mistrise Village, Vexing Shusher, Domri, Anarch of Bolas and Bound // Determined (Full) and Veil of Summer (Caveats: no "hexproof from" keyword, ADR 0038 §6) in PR 3. **Moved to other rows:** Savage Summoning (its flash and +1/+1 counter promises), Spider-Punk (riot), Frenzied Baloth (damage that can't be prevented), Taigam, Ojutai Master (rebound), Malevolent Hermit // Benevolent Geist (disturb) and Goldbug (More Than Meets the Eye).
