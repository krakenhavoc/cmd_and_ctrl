**A waived payment asks what was paid on paper** (#3855, [ADR 0068 amendment 2026-10-15, "what was paid on paper"](decisions/0068-the-mana-spent-on-a-spell.md)) — closes what #3844 left caveated. When a payment is waived with a shortfall (strict mana off with a short pool, or Cast anyway) and something reads the mana spent — the spell (`Spec.ReadsManaSpent: game.ReadsOwnPayment`), a permanent watching casts (`ReadsYourCasts`, `ReadsOpponentsCasts`, `ReadsEveryCast`) or the activated ability (`ReadsOwnActivations`) — the cast is refused before anything is paid with `*game.PaperPaymentRequiredError` (wire `paper_payment_required`, the shortfall slot by slot), and the client re-sends it with `paper_payment`: the mana paid outside the pool, which must be a legal payment of exactly the shortfall, or `free`, cast without paying its mana cost. The record becomes exact either way, and the declaration is public in the log. A cast nothing reads stays one click and records the shortfall's amount (`PaidCost.PaperMana`). `TestManaSpentReadersDeclareIt` scans the card files so a new reader cannot forget to declare. **Cards made Full (40):** the sixteen sunburst cards, Lux Artillery, Solar Array, Painful Truths, Memory Deluge, Slaying Fire, Boros Fury-Shield, Ribbons of Night, Gruul Scrapper, Deceit, Kurbis, Mockingbird, Abby, Mana Sculpt, Protective Sphere, Coin of Mastery, Vexing Bauble, Boromir, Roiling Vortex, Satoru, Freestrider Commando, Primeval Spawn, Moonhold, River's Grasp and Aetherflux Conduit.
