**The CR 608.2b target re-check for an ability whose source has left** (#1429, CR 608.2b / 608.2h / 400.7 / 702.16b, [ADR 0072 amendment 2026-09-24 (#1429)](decisions/0072-protection.md), [ADR 0018 note 2026-09-24](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — the "still open" clause of the #1417 entry below. `stackItemSourceLocked` (`game/targets.go`) is the `TargetSource` the resolution re-check and a retarget are judged under. For an ability whose source had left the battlefield it looked the source up in its current zone and got the graveyard card, a new object with printed colours. Its comment said the value was source-less, which was wrong. So protection was tested against the wrong object: a painted-red source that died kept a pro-red target, and a bleached red source that died lost one and fizzled. It now returns a snapshot of the source's `lastKnownPermanents` record, found by `departedAbilitySourceLocked` from the item's `SourceObject` (#1418): the live object while it is on the battlefield, its record once it has left, and the card where it is for an object that was never a permanent. So a graveyard trigger from a card that died earlier this turn is judged as the graveyard card, and an activation that sacrificed its own source is judged as that source even after the card comes back. Unstamped items from pre-#1418 snapshots keep ADR 0056 Decision 10's instance-ID rule. Spells never consult the record, and an ability with no record keeps the lookup. No caveat named the gap. The proof is #1417's Murderous Redcap turned blue by Cerulean Wisps and then killed, with Mother of Runes giving its target protection in response: protection from red no longer fizzles the trigger, and protection from blue now does. ~~Still open: an ability copy's re-target prompt (`game/ability_copy.go`, `abilitySourceCardLocked`) still reads the current-zone card (#1449)~~ — **closed by #1449**, the entry above.
