**A spell's own cost modifier** (#746) - `Spec.SelfCostModifiers`, read through `CardDef` by `game.SelfCostModifiersFor(card)` for the card being priced and never from the battlefield ([ADR 0048](decisions/0048-cost-modification.md) addendum). Self modifiers join the board's increase, reduction and floor passes (CR 601.2f), bound to the card as cast with the caster as its controller, so a reduction still stops at generic mana; they apply from any zone the spell is cast from (CR 113.6d), to an alternative cost (CR 118.9d), and for the face being cast. `CostQuery.Targets` carries the announced targets, shown only to a modifier that sets `ReadsTargets`; the legal-move enumerator prices each target set on its own when `CastPriceReadsTargetsForEffect` says something reads targets, and prices once up front otherwise. `CostModifier.Unit` lets an increase add coloured mana (strive). Constructors: `AffinityFor` (CR 702.41a, one instance per entry, CR 702.41b), `CostsMorePerTargetBeyondFirst`, `CostsLessIfItTargets`, plus counting amounts and conditions in `effects/self_cost_modifier.go`. The X picker prices a target-priced spell at one target and quotes the clause from `CardView.target_cost_notes`. Shipped on Thought Monitor, Myr Enforcer, Thoughtcast, Voyage Home, Ghalta, Primal Hunger, Vanquish the Horde, Fireball, Call the Coppercoats, Winged Words, Wizard's Retort, Into the Story, Hour of Revelation, Mortality Spear, Curtains' Call, Price of Fame, Shadow of Mortality, Witherbloom, the Balancer, Mycosynth Golem, Hamza, Guardian of Arashin, Ancient Stone Idol and Hagra Mauling; Blasphemous Act dropped its caveat. Still open: the auto-tap preview has no targets, face or non-hand zone on its query (#696); coloured reductions (CR 118.7c); cost modifiers on activated abilities (the row above); Earthquake Dragon and Metalwork Colossus also wait on #655. Unblocked and still to be written: Not of This World (#385), Sapling Nursery (#450), Thrumming Hivepool (#455), Diamond Weapon (#457), and the audit's clause-only cards (The Great Henge, Embercleave, Excalibur, Sword of Eden, Rumbleweed, The Magic Mirror, among others), each of which still needs its own check for other gaps.
