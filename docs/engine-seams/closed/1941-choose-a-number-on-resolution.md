**Choosing a number as an ability resolves** (#1941, [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md)'s amendment of 2026-10-09) — ADR 0129 §3's `pay_amount` prompt, built for "pay any amount of {E}", now asks for any number a resolving effect or an entering permanent needs (CR 608.2d, 614.12a). `PayAmountPrompt.Resource` says what each point costs: energy (the empty string, so every existing prompt is unchanged), life (CR 119.4: the ceiling is the payer's life total, and nobody is asked when it can't change, CR 119.8) or nothing. `NoMax` lifts the ceiling for a number with none printed (CR 107.1b only rules out negatives; the engine's overflow guard is a million), `Marks` names further numbers the enumerator offers, and `SelfDamage` tells a policy the number is also dealt to the chooser. `Game.QueueChooseNumberForEffect` is the entry point (`effects.ChooseNumber`, `PayLifeAmount`, and `PayAnyAmountOfLifeAsEnters` for `Spec.AsEnters`), the answer rides the same `amount` field and `chooseValueResume` frame, and the gate, departure rule, wire field, stepper and dispatcher are energy's. A number chosen as a permanent enters is stored on it as `Card.ChosenNumber`, an additive snapshot field cleared on leaving (CR 400.7), remembered in `PermanentInfo.ChosenNumber` for an ability that resolves after the permanent has gone (CR 608.2h), and shown on the card as a chip. The enumerator offers the floor, the card's goal, its marks and the ceiling, never the overflow guard; the heuristic answers the goal when what it costs in life leaves it at 10 or more, and the floor otherwise. A payment in mana ("you may pay {X}{R}", Tilonalli's Summoner) stays on #2727, which can choose X here and then queue the existing pay-unless prompt. **Cards:** Volcano Hellion, Phyrexian Processor and Necrodominance (Full).
