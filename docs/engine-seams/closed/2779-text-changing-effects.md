**Text-changing effects** (#2779, CR 612.2 / 612.3 / 612.5, [ADR 0160](decisions/0160-text-changing-effects-exchanging-text-boxes.md) and its 2026-10-15 amendment) — a spell can rewrite another object's text. Exchanging two whole text boxes (Exchange of Words, Deadpool, Trading Card) shipped first as a layer-3 substitution of the catalog key. The word-changing half is a second layer-3 mod, `game.ModChangeText` (`"changeText"`, one `{Kind: color | basicLandType | creatureType, From, To}`), pinned to a permanent or to a spell on the stack (re-pinned onto the permanent it becomes, CR 400.7a) and composed in timestamp order onto the derived `Card.TextWords`. The words are arguments rather than text, so each reader consults the object whose text they are in: the layer pass translates the baseline's type line and printed keywords (a Forest changed to an Island taps for {U}; swampwalk, protection and hexproof from a colour follow; a granted ability never does, CR 612.3), the targeting walk lends a candidate its source's words so `OfColor` / `NonBlack` / `Subtype` / `OfCreatureType` read the new word at announce and on resolution (a Doom Blade made "nonblue" fizzles on the blue creature it targeted), sweeps read their source's, and statics read `TextColor` / `TextSubtype` / `TextKeyword` (lords' tribes, granted landwalk). The choice is two `option_pick` prompts, or the creature-type prompt twice with the new `ExcludedTypes` ("can't be Wall"); `CardView.text_changes` shows the changed words. **Cards:** Glamerdye, Sleight of Mind, Alter Reality, Magical Hack, Mind Bend, Crystal Spray, Whim of Volrath and Artificial Evolution, all Full. Words a card file hard-codes in a closure are listed in the amendment; Spectral Shift, Trait Doctoring, New Blood and Balduvian Shaman wait on entwine (#3866), cipher (#3867), a tap additional cost (#3868) and granting cumulative upkeep (#3869).
