**"When this is put into a graveyard from anywhere, shuffle it into its owner's library"** (#2038, [ADR 0113](decisions/0113-small-seams-for-the-s58-deck-requests.md) amendment 2026-10-16) — the Eldrazi titans' graveyard trigger (`WhenThisIsPutIntoAGraveyardFromAnywhere`, ADR 0113 §2) already fired from every zone, once per arrival, but nothing shuffled the card itself in, and nothing told the card the trigger was about from one that left the graveyard and came back. `effects.WhenThisIsPutIntoAGraveyardFromAnywhereShuffleItIn` stamps the graveyard object (`Params.Object`, the card's ID and graveyard `ObjectEpoch`) in a fill-in `Build`, and its body tucks the card into its owner's library with the shuffle as the tuck's continuation (a CR 903.9b pause finishes first) while it is still that object (CR 400.7), or shuffles the owner's library anyway when it is not (CR 701.24c, the Guile example). It does not look back in time (CR 603.6c, 603.10a), so a creature that lost its abilities on the battlefield still shuffles itself in, and a card Rest in Peace exiles never triggers it. Hostility's "a spell you control" is `effects.FromASpellYouControl` over the new `Game.DamageSourceSpellControllerForEffect` (a spell on the stack or resolving), and Vigor's "another creature you control" is the recipient `ToAnotherCreatureYouControl`. One new on-disk body key, `hostility/elemental-shamans`. **Cards** (9, all Full): Hostility, Purity, Vigor, Dread, Serra Avatar and Worldspine Wurm; and the replacement form, Blightsteel Colossus's `ShuffleIntoOwnersLibraryInstead`: Darksteel Colossus, Progenitus and Legacy Weapon, whose competing Rest in Peace is the card's controller's choice (CR 616.1). **Moved to its own row:** Guile, whose counter-replacement has no window (#4056).
