**Damage doubled or tripled this turn by a spell** (#1890, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §3, PR 2) — "it deals double (triple) that damage instead" from a resolving spell or ability (CR 614.1a) is a `ScopedEffect` replacement kind, `multiplyDamage` (`game.ModMultiplyDamage`, `game/multiply_damage.go`), written by `Game.MultiplyDamageForEffect` until end of turn (CR 514.2) or until a player's next turn (CR 611.2b). `Mod.Amount` is the multiplier. Which damage is one source (`Objects` + `SourceZone`, read as the next-damage shield reads its chosen source), or a new `Mod.Sources` value, `yours` or `creatures`, read off the source's last-known information as the damage would be dealt (CR 611.2c, 608.2h), or any source. To what is a new `Mod.Recipients` value, `opponents`, `opponentsAndTheirPermanents`, `playerAndTheirPermanents` (with `Player`) or `creatures`, read live. `CombatOnly`, and a new `Mod.Next` for "the next time": one instance of damage (CR 615.8), spent through `SpentInstance` as the next-damage shield is, so a trampler's damage to its blocker and the player is doubled together and nothing after. A source that would deal 0 deals none (CR 120.8): nothing is multiplied and nothing is spent. It is not a prevention effect, so "damage can't be prevented" leaves it alone, and two multipliers commute, so no CR 616 order is asked between them (two Insults are ×4). `choose_source` gained a controller filter (`ChooseSourcePrompt.Controller`; a card nobody controls answers by its owner, CR 108.4a), and the next-damage shield a `CombatOnly`. Restore refuses a `sources` or `recipients` value this binary does not know, and the three fields on any other kind. The table sees a banner line per record (`GameView.damage_multipliers`). A v7 corpus fixture holds every shape. **Cards** (all Full): Insult // Injury, Isengard Unleashed, Desperate Gambit, Goblin Goliath, Blind Fury, Impulsive Maneuvers, Quest for Pure Flame, Lightning, Army of One. **Moved to other rows:** Jeska, Thrice Reborn (loyalty-cost-x, #1944: her −X), Overblaze (splice, #1989).
