**Paying {X} as an ability resolves** (#2727, [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md)'s amendment of 2026-10-09) — "you may pay {X}{R}" asked as a trigger resolves (CR 608.2d, 118.12) is two prompts the engine already had, as #1941's amendment proposed. `Game.QueueMayPayXForEffect` (`effects.MayPayX`) first asks for X on the `pay_amount` prompt with `PayResourceNone`, from 0 to the most the chooser can pay right now: `largestPayableXLocked` probes `canPayCostLocked`, the payment the second prompt makes, on a throwaway clone, galloping up and halving, so the pool and the auto-tapper both count and no X is offered that the payment refuses. It then queues the ordinary `pay_unless` "you may pay" for the cost with X settled ("{3}{R}"), optionally held in the current step, so Always / Never, the strict-mana gate and the bot's pay move are unchanged. A chooser who can't pay even X = 0 is not asked (CR 118.3), and an empty cost at X = 0 is paid by doing nothing (CR 118.5). Tokens put onto the battlefield attacking now attack what their controller chooses (CR 508.4): `TokenGroup.Attacking` gives each share of one creation its own player, planeswalker or battle, and `effects.CreateTokensAttackingYourChoice` asks how many go at each, the attacked one first, with the last taking the rest. `effects.ExileUnlessCitysBlessing` is the end-step exile that reads the city's blessing as it resolves. **Cards:** Tilonalli's Summoner and Flameblast Dragon (Full).
