**The hand-cast offers and the one-off grants cast during their resolution** (#4250, CR 608.2g; [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)'s 2026-10-24 #4250 part 2 amendment) — every "you may cast it" a resolving spell or ability offers in card code now asks the `resolution_cast` prompt inside that resolution, through one door (`Game.OfferResolutionCastForEffect`): the "cast a spell from your hand without paying its mana cost" offers (Buster Sword, Kari Zev's and Rishkar's Expertise, Surtland Elementalist, Great Intelligence's Plan, Cecily, the Mad Wizard's Lair, Aetherflux Conduit, The Great Synthesis), the library looks (Aetherworks Marvel, Sunbird's Invocation) and the one-off grants (Torrential Gearhulk, Arcane Heist, Memory Plunder, Emet-Selch, Strago and Relm, Crabomination, Amped Raptor, The Infamous Cruelclaw, Fevered Suspicion, Etali, Kefka, Hazoret's Undying Fury, Primeval Spawn, Emergent Ultimatum, Ensnared by the Mara, The Key to the Vault, Cosmium Catalyst, Conduit of Worlds, Goliath Daydreamer, Power Pack, Summon: Esper Valigarmanda, the Sieges). A hand card is a candidate held to its permission's claim, "cast a spell" is one permission spent by the one cast, "any number" stays open until the decline, and what the resolution does after the casts (The Great Synthesis's flicker, Kefka's life loss, "put the rest on the bottom") is the prompt's keyed continuation. A table paused on any of them is a restore point. Nicol Bolas, God-Pharaoh's +2 keeps its turn-long window.
