**Discarding a card with mana value X as a cost** (#2190, [ADR 0113](decisions/0113-small-seams-for-the-s58-deck-requests.md)'s second 2026-10-07 amendment, CR 202.3e / 602.2b) — a discard cost could not say anything about the card it takes, so Kozilek, the Great Distortion's "Discard a card with mana value X: Counter target spell with mana value X" could not be written. `game.DiscardCost.ManaValueX` (`effects.DiscardCardWithManaValueX`) makes X the number the activator announces and the mana value the discarded card must have, with no mana paid for it; the spell clause is `TargetSpell(...).WithManaValueEqualsX()`, so the card and the target must agree at announce and the target is re-checked as the ability resolves. A spell on the stack counts {X} as the value chosen for it (the X bound now reads `Game.ManaValueForEffect`), a card in a hand counts it as zero. `Register` refuses it beside `{X}` in the mana cost, another claim on the announced X, a predicate, another hand-spending component, or on a mana ability. The wire carries `discard_cost_mana_value_x` (public); the client does not ask for X, it sends the picked card's mana value as `x_value` and narrows the target picker by it, and the enumerator offers one payment per distinct mana value in the hand. **Cards:** Kozilek, the Great Distortion (now `full`). **Still open:** the form as a cast's additional cost or a mana ability's cost, which no printed card uses.
