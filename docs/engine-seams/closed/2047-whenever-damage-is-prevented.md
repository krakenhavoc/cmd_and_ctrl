**"Whenever damage is prevented"** (#2047, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) amendment of 2026-10-16) — CR 615.13's triggers now have an event to watch. `runReplaceLocked` measures every prevention effect it applies (a scoped shield, a catalog static, protection's built-in, a shield counter) and, when the effect prevented some damage, owes an announcement on the follow-up queue (`PreventionFollowUp.Announce`), keyed on the effect, the damage instance and the recipient, so one application to the simultaneous events of one instance is one `EventDamagePrevented` with the total prevented (Target the recipient, Amount the damage prevented). Two prevention effects are two events; a second instruction is a second event. Damage that can't be prevented (CR 615.12) is settled without the effect's Replace and announces nothing, so Leyline of Punishment and Skullcrack switch the trigger off with the prevention. The announcement is emitted where a follow-up runs (as the instance ends, or before the state-based actions at the priority boundary), and it rides a restore point (`preventionFollowUps[].announce`, additive within v9). The card-side condition is `effects.DamageToYouPrevented` (`WheneverDamageToYouIsPrevented`). **Cards** (1): Selfless Squire (Full).
