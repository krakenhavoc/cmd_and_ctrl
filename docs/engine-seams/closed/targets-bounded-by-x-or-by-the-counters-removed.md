**Targets bounded by X or by the counters removed** (#1842, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §9, PR 7) — the announced-X target bound is now one bound with a statistic (`ManaValueAtMostX`, `ManaValueEqualsX`, `PowerAtMostX`, `ToughnessAtMostX`) and an input: the announced X (a mana {X}, a variable sacrifice or tap), or, with `BoundByCountersRemoved`, the counters the activation's cost removed. `game.AnnouncedBound` binds the clause at announce, after the payment is validated and before the targets (CR 602.2b, 601.2c), and again from the stack item for the CR 608.2b re-check; nothing new is stored. `effects.Register` refuses a bound with nothing to bind it to. The view ships each candidate's power, toughness or mana value with the bound's flags, the client narrows by the X or the counter payment, and the enumerator filters every (payment, targets) pair through `TargetsWithinBoundForEffect`. It also stamps `mana_value_equals_x` now, so the client no longer offers Lazav a superset. **Cards:** Simic Manipulator, Quillmane Baku, Killing Glare, Minamo Sightbender, Aryel, Knight of Windgrace, Finale of Eternity and Entrancing Lyre (Full); Ruthless Technomancer gains its reanimation ability. **Moved to other rows:** Gang Up (assist, #1857).
