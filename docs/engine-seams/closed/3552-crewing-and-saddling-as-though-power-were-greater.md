**Crewing and saddling as though a creature's power were greater** (#2705, [ADR 0186](decisions/0186-crewing-and-saddling-who-paid-and-what-they-count-for.md)) — the counting rule is `Characteristic.CostPower`, written by the layer 6 statics `CrewsAsThoughPowerGreater` and `CrewsUsingToughness` and read through `Card.CountedPower` by the crew and saddle validator, the enumerator and the client picker. The Pilot token templates (`PilotToken`, `CrewPilotToken`), the Pilot creatures and, as the last slice (#3552), every card that makes a saddle-and-crew Pilot ship on it. **Cards:** Cloudspire Coordinator, Defend the Rider, Valor's Flagship, Back on Track, Roadside Assistance, Country Roads, Foul Roads, Reef Roads, Rocky Roads and Wild Roads (all Full).
