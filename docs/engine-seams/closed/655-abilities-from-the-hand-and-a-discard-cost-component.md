**Abilities from the hand, and a discard cost component** (#655 / #660, [ADR 0062](decisions/0062-abilities-and-special-actions-from-the-hand.md)) — closes "Discard-a-card cost on an activated ability" (count 10) and the HAND half of "Ability activatable from a non-battlefield zone" (count 10, 6 of them the cycling cards on both rows). `ActivatedAbilityShape.Zones []ZoneKind` is the CR 113.6 dimension on the ONE activation path: nil means the battlefield, `{ZoneHand}` is cycling, and `ActivateCatalogAbility` finds its source in whatever zone holds it, takes the OWNER as "you" off the battlefield (CR 108.4), and refuses a mismatch with `ErrActivationZoneNotAllowed` before anything is validated or paid. `effects.Register` panics at boot on a non-battlefield ability that declares a tap, sacrifice-this, crew or loyalty component — none of them has a permanent to pay with. `AbilityCost.DiscardSelf` is cycling's "Discard this card"; `AbilityCost.DiscardCards` (`DiscardACard()`, `DiscardCardsMatching(n, label, match)`, `DiscardN`) is the general "Discard N cards" clause, its picks named at announce as `discard_ids`. Both pay through the one discard helper with `DiscardCauseCost`, so `EventDiscardCard` fires per card for every discard payoff, the CR 614 window runs over the exit, and a commander pitched to a cost goes to the graveyard (CR 601.2h / 602.2b — a cost cannot pause on CR 903.9's "may"). `effects.Cycling(cost)`, `Typecycling(keyword, cost, what, pred)` and `BasicLandcycling(cost)` build the ability; activating one emits `EventCycle` (CR 702.29b) and `effects.WheneverYouCycle` is the trigger constructor over it. "cycling" joined `canonicalKeywords`. The wire carries `hand_abilities` on a hand card (owner only) plus `discard_self` / `discard_cost_n` / `discard_cost_label` / `discard_cost_options` on `ActivatedAbilityView`; the enumerator loops the seat's own hand through the same cost solve the battlefield loop uses. Shipped on Raffine's Tower, Ketria Triome, Marauding Mako, Scrounging Skyray, Magmakin Artillerist and Sylvan Reclamation (basic landcycling), all six of which dropped their cycling caveats, and on Fauna Shaman, added as the general component's first card. "When you cycle THIS card" fires from the graveyard (CR 702.29c) via the `TriggeredAbility.Zones` dimension #925 built (the row above) — Magmakin Artillerist dropped its cycle-trigger caveat with #1392; Decree of Pain is not added yet. Unblocked by the cost and still to be written: Tortured Existence (#307), Cryptbreaker (#311), Survival of the Fittest (#384) and Glint-Horn Buccaneer (whose other gap, the activation condition, closed with #743). Foretell (#658) and suspend (#659) are the same ADR's Decision 4, and are now built (see below).
