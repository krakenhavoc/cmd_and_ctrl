**Fading** (#5802, CR 702.32a) — "This permanent enters with N fade counters on it" and "At the beginning of your upkeep, remove a fade counter from this permanent. If you can't, sacrifice the permanent." Vanishing's sibling (CR 702.63a), built the same way: `effects.Fading(n)` is the entry replacement (`b10EntersWithCounters` with the new `game.CounterFade`, so the counters go through the counter window and Doubling Season doubles them), and `effects.FadingUpkeep()` is one upkeep trigger stamped `effects.KeywordFading`. Unlike vanishing it has no intervening "if" and no last-counter trigger: it always fires, reads the counters as it resolves, removes one if there is one and otherwise sacrifices the permanent, so a Fading 3 permanent goes at the fourth upkeep and nobody chooses anything. `FadingEntersTapped(n)` folds "enters tapped" into the same replacement (no CR 616 order for Skyshroud Behemoth), and `RemoveAFadeCounterFromThis()` is the ordinary counter-removal cost. `TestEveryFadingCardHasBothHalves` holds every card to one entry and one trigger. No snapshot change: counters are a string-keyed map. **Cards** (13, all Full): Ancient Hydra, Blastoderm, Cloudskate, Defender en-Vec, Jolting Merfolk, Phyrexian Prowler, Rejuvenation Chamber, Rusting Golem, Saproling Burst (its Saprolings read the fade counters on the Burst object that made them through `Card.CreatedWith`), Skyshroud Behemoth, Skyshroud Ridgeback, Tangle Wire and Woodripper. The Parallax cards wait on their other text.
