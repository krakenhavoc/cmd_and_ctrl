**Post-departure LKI / "exiled with this" record — the last piece: a departed damage source's lifelink and deathtouch** (#1396, CR 608.2h / 400.7 / 702.15b / 702.2b, [ADR 0056 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0056-infect-wither-toxic.md) Decisions 9-11, closing its Decision 2 step 4). The row had three parts. #1218 closed two: the "exiled with this" record already existed (`b27ExiledWith`), and counters at departure became `Game.lastKnownCounters` (The Ozolith; Valakut Exploration and Currency Converter shipped with no new primitive). #1379 then added durable resolution-time LKI (`Game.lastKnownPermanents`, the entry below). The one piece left was the damage tail: `effectDamageTailLocked` read lifelink and deathtouch off the battlefield only, so a source that had left dealt plain damage. It now reads the departed object's record (`departedDamageSourceLocked` in `game/damage_tail.go`): deathtouch from its recorded abilities, and lifelink credited to its recorded controller. A damage call names its source two ways. **By object**, the new `Game.DealDamageFromObjectForEffect(ObjectRef, …)` / `effects.DealDamage{SourceObject: &ref}`, reads that object's record and never a new object the card has become since. **By instance ID**, every existing caller, reads the last object the card was, and only while its epoch is exactly one past the record's (it has not moved since it left), or when the card is a token that has ceased to exist. So every "when this dies, it deals damage" card gets the departed creature's keywords with no change to its file. `PermanentInfo` also gains `Tapped` (ADR 0056 Decision 11) for Mana Vault's resolution-time "if this artifact is tapped". The combat tail is unchanged (a combat source is on the battlefield or has a CR 510.1c frame). Proof cards: Warstorm Surge ships `full`; Murderous Redcap keeps only its persist caveat; Mana Vault's caveat narrows to a Vault that leaves and comes back before its draw-step trigger resolves (a triggered item does not carry its source's object epoch, #1418 — closed, entry above). Still open, stated in the amendment: ~~the damage event's `sourceLKI` (CR 702.16e protection colour) still reads the card in its current zone (#1417)~~ closed, entry above; infect, wither and toxic join the same reader when ADR 0056 PR 2 wires them into the tail.
