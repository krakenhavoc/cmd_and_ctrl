**Saddle** (#2695, CR 702.171, [ADR 0071](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md) amendment 2026-10-08) — "Tap any number of other untapped creatures you control with total power N or more: This permanent becomes saddled until end of turn. Saddle only as a sorcery." **`AbilityCost.Saddle`** is crew's cost over OTHER creatures: it runs through `validateCrewCostLocked`, which now takes the source and refuses it as a payment, and the creatures ride `crew_ids` and are written onto the item's payment record (`PaidCost.TappedOthers`) so the effect knows who saddled the Mount. `effects.Saddle(n)` is the whole ability at sorcery speed. **Saddled** is the seventh ADR 0071 designation and the only one that lasts a turn: `Card.Saddled` / `Card.SaddledBy`, set by `Game.SaddleForEffect` (a no-op for anything that is not a Mount, so "becomes saddled if it's a Mount" is the primitive itself), swept in `sweepTurnEndLocked` with the layer version bumped, cleared at both CR 400.7 sites. `EventBecameSaddled` fires only for a Mount that was not already saddled, which makes "for the first time each turn" the event's own shape. `Game.SaddlersOf` answers "creatures that saddled it this turn" with the survivors, as the same objects. The vocabulary is `AttacksWhileSaddled`, `WhenBecomesSaddled`, `Saddled()` (the gate), `SaddledKeywords` and `BecomeSaddled`. Wire: `CardView.saddled` and `activated_abilities[i].saddle`, which rides crew's `crew_cost` / `crew_options` (the Mount left out of the options); the enumerator leaves the Mount out of its own payment. The client gains a SADDLED badge and a "Saddle" picker. Guidelight Matrix loses its caveat; Gilded Ghoda, Drover Grizzly, Seraphic Steed, Gloryheath Lynx, Bounding Felidar, District Mascot, Bulwark Ox, Ornery Tumblewagg, Caustic Bronco, Stubborn Burrowfiend, Rambling Possum and Guardian Sunmare ship. **Still open and different seams**: cards that act on a particular saddler (a target restricted to the saddlers, a sacrifice, a copy, a flicker) are #2704, and the Pilot cards' "as though its power were 2 greater" is #2705.
