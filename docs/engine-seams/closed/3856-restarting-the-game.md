**Restarting the game** (#3856, CR 727, [ADR 0201](decisions/0201-restarting-the-game.md)) — Karn Liberated's −14 was the one ability the catalog left out on purpose. `Game.RestartTheGameForEffect` records the request on `Game.RestartPending` and parks priority; the resolution's CR 704.3 boundary ends the game and begins the new one, the way CR 724.1's end-the-turn waits for it. The new game is a fresh `NewGame` adopted in place through `RestoreFrom`, so every game-scoped field starts over, and only a named list is carried: the table's identity and settings, the event log and its counters, and each in-game seat's identity and preferences (`restartedPlayer`, held complete by `TestRestartClassifiesEveryPlayerField`). Every card any in-game player owns, from every zone, goes back to their deck as a new object with a new instance ID (tokens and copies do not), commanders to the command zone; the pregame is `Start`'s own (`beginPregameLocked`, `dealAndBeginLocked`) with the restarter as the starting player (CR 727.1a), and a deck short of seven loses at the first check (727.3). The exempted cards wait in exile on `Game.RestartTail` and enter together under the restarter's control when the mulligans end, before the first untap step (727.4). The public log begins again with a `game_restarted` line, which the mulligan sheet reads for its first-turn headline. Both new fields are additive in snapshot v9. **Karn Liberated** links its +4 and −3 exiles to the activating object (CR 607.2a) and ships `full`.
