**A cast permission filtered by power or toughness, from the graveyard** (#4964, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)'s 2026-11-04 amendment, CR 604.3, 113.6a, 208.2a, 208.3, 122.1h, 601.3) — "During your turn, you may cast creature spells with power or toughness 1 or less from your graveyard" had no clause: `game.PermissionFilter` spoke about types, subtypes, colours, counters and arrivals, and nothing about the two numbers. It is now `PermissionFilter.PowerOrToughnessCapped` with `PowerOrToughnessAtMost` (the flag, because 0 is a legal N), an OR over power and toughness on a creature card, so a 2/1 and a 1/2 both qualify under "1 or less" and a 2/2 does not; a card that is not a creature has no power or toughness for the clause (CR 208.3). The hard half is the `*/*` card: a characteristic-defining ability functions in every zone (CR 604.3, CR 113.6a, CR 208.2a), so Tarmogoyf in a graveyard has the power its own ability counts there, which a pure-data `Matches(Card)` cannot work out. The lookups that hold the game lend the card its reading instead, one per face, as `ThroughTextOf` lends a text to a target predicate: `Game.withPTAnywhereLocked` (over #2115's `cdaAnywhereLocked`) through `lendPTLocked` and `permissionForSpellLocked`, on the stored, standing, per-face, per-type and offer paths, so the permission, the view's `castable_here`, the bot enumerator and `CastSpell` read one answer. A card with such an ability that reaches the filter unlent, and a `*` the engine cannot compute at all, do not qualify: unknown is never "1 or less", so a missed lookup makes a permission narrower than printed, never wider. The reading is derived and never persisted (`Card.ptAnywhere`, cleared by `MoveCard`); the two filter keys are additive in v9. **Cards:** Leonardo, Sewer Samurai (Full), whose finality-counter rider is #4679's `CastPermission.EntersWithCounterKind` and whose sneak is #4586's.
