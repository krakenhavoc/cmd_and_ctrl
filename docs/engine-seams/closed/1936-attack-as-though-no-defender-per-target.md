**Attacking certain players as though it didn't have defender** (#1936, CR 702.3b, CR 609.4, CR 508.1d, [ADR 0106 amendment 2026-10-16](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md)) — Weathered Sentinels "can attack players who attacked you during their last turn as though it didn't have defender". Defender refused every declaration from `AttackerEligible`, which knows no target, so a waiver that names whom the creature may attack had nowhere to live. `Characteristic.DefenderWaivers []game.DefenderWaiver{Source, SourceName, OnlyPlayersWhoAttackedYou}` is that waiver, and `defenderRefusalLocked` asks defender of each target: `AttackerEligible` lets a creature with defender through only when it has a waiver, and `canAttackTargetWithLocked` (both declaration verbs), `AttackTargetsForAttackerForEffect` (the enumerator, so the ADR 0105 digest, the client's per-attacker gate and the bot) and the CR 508.1d search (a goaded Sentinels owes an attack only where it may attack) refuse a target no waiver covers. `OnlyPlayersWhoAttackedYou` reads ADR 0108 §6's `AttackedYouDuringTheirLastTurn(target, controller)` live at declaration, per defending player and players only. The keyword stays (CR 609.4). The refusal is a `*DefenderWaiverError` wrapping `ErrDefender`: "Weathered Sentinels has defender: it can attack only players who attacked you during their last turn." The unconditional form for a turn is a new `ScopedEffect` mod kind, `waiveDefender` (`game.WaiveDefenderMod`, layer 6, an effect rather than a granted ability, so "loses all abilities" leaves it). Card side: `effects.CanAttackPlayersWhoAttackedYouAsThoughNoDefender()` and `CanAttackThisTurnAsThoughNoDefender(label)`. Snapshot: additive (`DefenderWaivers` on `lastKnownBattlefield`, one new mod kind). **Cards:** Weathered Sentinels and Hightide Hermit (Full).
