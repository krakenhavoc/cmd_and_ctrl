**Winning the game by an effect, and "can't lose" / "can't win"** (#749, CR 104.2b / 104.3 / 104.4a / 614.17, [ADR 0057](decisions/0057-win-and-lose-by-effect.md) and its 2026-09-24 amendment) — the engine names a result. `game/game_end.go` gives every departure a `LossCause` (`life`, `empty_draw`, `poison`, `commander_damage`, `effect`, `concede`), splits elimination into `loseGameLocked` (reads the gate) / `leaveGameLocked` (CR 800.4a, unchanged) / `checkGameOverLocked` (the last player standing wins, CR 104.2a, no gate read; everyone gone is a draw, CR 104.4a), and records `Game.Outcome` (`{win|draw, winner, last_standing|effect|all_lost, source}`) through one writer, `endGameLocked`, which also clears every prompt. **`WinTheGameForEffect`** is immediate (the Jace ruling) and eliminates nobody — a four-seat game won by Felidar Sovereign ends with three seats standing; a prevented win emits `EventWinPrevented` once. **`LoseTheGameForEffect`** is now immediate too (CR 104.3e has no SBA clause); an active player who loses mid-resolution leaves at once and the rotation waits for the next SBA pass (`Game.ActiveSeatLeftPending`, ADR 0059 Decision 6). `effects.WinTheGame` / `effects.LoseTheGame` return `game.ErrStopResolution` when the resolution must stop, which `EmitEvent` drops rather than logging as an effect error. The **SBA loss pass** is collect-then-apply (CR 704.3, CR 104.3f) and consumes the CR 704.5b empty-draw flag on every pass, gated or not. **Gates** (`game/game_end_gates.go`): derived from the battlefield through `Spec.GameEndGates` → `CatalogGameEndGates` keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey` (Platinum Angel, Herald of Eternal Dawn, Abyssal Persecutor), and granted for a CR 611.2 duration as a fifth `PlayerStatic` payload (Angel's Grace) — the amendment's change from the ADR's separate registry. Concession is never gated. **Wire:** `GameView.outcome`, `PlayerView.cant_lose` / `cant_win` / `end_gates`, log `cause` plus `game_over` and `win_prevented` lines, and one line per concession. **Client:** the banner reads the winner and cause from `outcome` (survivors only as a fallback), and a seat shows `CAN'T LOSE` / `CAN'T WIN` badges. **Room:** once a game has ended only the admin can undo. **Bots:** a seat that can't lose to its life total is never a lethal target, never hopeless, and its danger penalty is clamped at 0. **7 cards:** Herald of Eternal Dawn (#1306), Platinum Angel, Abyssal Persecutor, Felidar Sovereign, Laboratory Maniac and Thassa's Oracle complete; Angel's Grace with one declared caveat (its life floor is a separate damage-result replacement; its second, split second, was cleared by #1519). **Still open, each its own seam:** a "would lose the game" replacement (The Golden Throne), granting a non-keyword static (Cloudsteel Kirin, The Book of Exalted Deeds), emblem gates (Gideon of the Trials), a `Causes`-narrowed gate's first card (Phyrexian Unlife, Pact Weapon), the `outcome` column on ADR 0051's `games` row (#1520 — the user database shipped before this, so it is now a migration; `winner_seat` is written from the outcome already), and the rest of the ADR's first wave, which no longer wait on this seam and are checked for other blockers in their own card PRs (Test of Endurance, Revel in Riches, Simic Ascendancy, Helix Pinnacle, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries, Strixhaven Stadium and the others ADR 0057's question 3 lists).
