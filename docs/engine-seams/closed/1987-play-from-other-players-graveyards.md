**Playing cards from other players' graveyards** (#1987, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md) amendment of 2026-10-16) — the permission needed no new machinery: a stored `ScopeStanding` `CastPermission` with `ZoneOwner` set already reached another seat's pile (Xanathar's library shape, #1035), and the cast path, the legal-move enumerator and the view's per-holder stamps (#1022) already walked other seats' graveyards. Shaman's Trance grants one per other seat at the printed cost, until end of turn. Two engine changes: `CastPermissionOnCardForEffect` now asks the pile rule of a stored permission too, so a grant over one seat's graveyard is not stamped as the public `exile_play` on every graveyard's cards; and `validateCastPathLocked`'s rule 3 gives way to a permission at the printed cost, so a flashback card under that grant (or Yawgmoth's Will, Muldrotha) may be cast for its mana cost as well as flashed back. "Can't play lands or cast spells from their graveyards" is a new rules-gate ScopedEffect kind, `cantPlayFromOwnGraveyard` (`Game.CantPlayFromOwnGraveyardThisTurnForEffect`, `game/graveyard_play_ban.go`), one mod per banned player, read by both `CastGateLocked` and `LandPlayGateLocked` for a card in that player's own graveyard; an older binary refuses a restore point naming it (fixture `v9/shamans_trance.json`). **Cards:** Shaman's Trance, with a caveat: the caster's own "your graveyard" permissions (Underworld Breach's escape) do not reach the other graveyards.
