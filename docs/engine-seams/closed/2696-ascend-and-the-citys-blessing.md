**Ascend and the city's blessing** (#2696, CR 702.131, [ADR 0096 amendment 2026-10-08](decisions/0096-the-monarch-from-a-card-effect.md)) — the catalog approximated "if you have the city's blessing" with a live "you control ten or more permanents", so a board that shrank shut every card that asked. `Player.CitysBlessing` (`game/citys_blessing.go`) is the designation (CR 702.131c): written only by `grantCitysBlessingLocked`, which emits `EventCitysBlessing` (the layer pass bumps on it and the log narrates it), never cleared, carried by `Clone`, undo and the snapshot (`seats[].citysBlessing`, additive in schema 7) and on the wire as `PlayerView.citys_blessing`, which the client shows as a marker beside the monarch and initiative markers. `ascend` is a canonical keyword the deck importer stamps, with two consumers: `citysBlessingSweepLocked`, CR 702.131b's static ability on a permanent, run from `stateBasedActionsLocked` after the layer recompute (not a state-based action; it gives the blessing to a player who controls an ascend permanent and ten permanents), and `ascendSpellLocked`, CR 702.131a's spell ability on an instant or sorcery, run as the spell resolves before its other instructions. `AttackTargetRestriction` gains `ControllerMustHaveCitysBlessing` for Wayward Swordtooth's "can't attack unless you have the city's blessing". Card side (`effects/citys_blessing.go`): `YouHaveTheCitysBlessing`, `YouHaveTheCitysBlessingCondition`, `YouHaveTheCitysBlessingNow`, `WhileCitysBlessing`, `SelfPumpWhileCitysBlessing`, `SelfKeywordWhileCitysBlessing`. **Cards (16):** Orazca Relic, Arch of Orazca, Tendershoot Dryad and Illustrious Wanderglyph lose their "blessing isn't kept" caveats and ship `full`; Wayward Swordtooth, Ocelot Pride, Twilight Prophet, Andúril Narsil Reforged, Vona's Hunger, Radiant Destiny, Detective of the Month, Expel from Orazca, Kumena's Awakening, Secrets of the Golden City, Slippery Scoundrel and Golden Demise are new and `full`. **Still open:** the other eleven ascend cards (#2706), which are card slices rather than engine work; Timestream Navigator also waits on a "put this on the bottom of its owner's library" cost, and Tilonalli's Summoner on a "may pay {X}{R}" as its trigger resolves.
