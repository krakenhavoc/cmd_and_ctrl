**Waterbend as an activated ability's cost, and as a ward's payment** (#1310 / #1311, CR 701.67a, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) and [ADR 0073 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)) — S22's `game.TapPermanentsCost` gained two owners rather than a sibling: `AbilityCost.Waterbend` ("Waterbend {N}:") and `CounterUnlessPaidPrompt.Waterbend` ("Ward—Waterbend {N}"). On both, the waterbend mana is the cost itself, so it stays in the ordinary mana component — X detection, the CR 601.2f cost-modifier pass, the view's cost chip and every affordability check read it unchanged — and the clause's `Extra` names the part the taps may cover (CR 701.67b). One budget (`game.WaterbendBudget`: the clause's generic, capped by what the priced cost still charges), one reduction (`game.WaterbendReduced`, applied after the modifier pass) and one walk (`Game.WaterbendOptionsForEffect`) are shared by the engine, the view and the enumerator (#544). Activation: `waterbend_ids` on `activate_ability`, validated with every other component, excluded from the auto-tapper (CR 118.3), tapped with the ability on the stack; not the {T} symbol, so a summoning-sick creature — and a source whose cost prints no {T} — may pay (CR 302.6). Prompt: `PendingChoiceView.tap_cost` and `resolve_choice { apply: true, tap_ids }` through `Game.ResolvePayUnlessWithTaps`; the mana remainder is settled first and nothing taps if it cannot be, and a malformed tap list is refused with the prompt left open. `effects.WaterbendCost` / `effects.WardWaterbend` build them; `Plus` sums a waterbend's mana with another mana component and no longer drops `TapOthers`. The enumerator offers one payment (free artifacts, then creatures, then mana sources; the largest reachable X), and the client reuses TapCostModal for the ability and lists waterbenders in the pay-unless modal. **Cards:** Aang, Swift Savior (caveat dropped, `full`); Katara, Water Tribe's Hope (`full`); The Unagi of Kyoshi Island (`full`); Avatar Kuruk's exhaust ability now waits on extra turns (#753) alone. Still open: CR 701.67c "whenever a player waterbends" (no card asks), waterbend on a mana ability (none printed), and convoke on an activated ability.
