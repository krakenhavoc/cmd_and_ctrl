**Tapping any number of creatures as an additional cost to cast** (#3961, CR 601.2b / 601.2f / 601.2h / 302.6) — #3868's `AdditionalCost.TapOthers` tapped a fixed number of permanents, so Burn at the Stake ("tap any number of untapped creatures you control … three times the number of creatures tapped this way") and Explosive Singularity ("you may tap any number … {1} less for each creature tapped this way") had no shape: `effects.Register` refused the open count on a cast. `game.TapOthersCost.AnyNumber`, built with `effects.TapAnyNumberUntappedCost(label, preds…)`, is the same cost with the count the caster's, from none up to every option, on the mandatory slot only.
- **The wire.** The creatures ride `cast_spell`'s `teamwork_ids`, as #3868's do, and are validated by the same walk (each once, the caster's own, untapped, matching the filter without targeting, not also convoking) and tapped with the spell on the stack. None is a legal payment, so the cost never makes a spell uncastable. The view ships `tap_others_options` with `min` 0 and `max` the number of options; the client opens the tap picker with no floor and skips it when there is nothing to tap.
- **The count.** At resolution it is the length of `PaidCost.TappedOthers` (`ctx.TappedToPay()`), which a creature that died in response still counts and a CR 707.10 copy keeps. At CR 601.2f it is `CostQuery.TappingToPay`, filled from the length of `teamwork_ids` by the one pricer, so `effects.CostsLessPerTappedToPay` is the price CastSpell charges, the auto-tap preview shows and the enumerator offers.
- **The bot.** The enumerator offers one cast per count: none, one, two and three off the policy's cheapest-to-tap order, and every option, each priced with its own discount, so an Explosive Singularity the lands alone cannot pay is offered at the counts that make it affordable. A damage entry declares `damage_per_tapped` (`effects.DamagePerTappedToTarget`), which the heuristic multiplies by the count the move names; with each tap charged, the smallest count that kills wins, and tapping none for no damage is declined.
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` refuses the form on an either/or branch, beside a fixed count, and on an activated ability, a mana ability or an alternative cost.
- **Cards** (2, both Full): Burn at the Stake and Explosive Singularity.
