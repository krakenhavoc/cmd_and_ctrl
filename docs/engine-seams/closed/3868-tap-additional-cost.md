**Tapping a creature as an additional cost to cast** (#3868, CR 601.2h / 118.3 / 302.6) — activated abilities (Earthcraft, station) and alternative costs (Orim's Cure, Zahid) could tap permanents you control as a cost, but a spell's additional cost could not, so New Blood ("As an additional cost to cast this spell, tap an untapped Vampire you control") had no way to be cast. `game.AdditionalCost.TapOthers` is that same `TapOthersCost` on a cast's mandatory slot or on one of its either/or branches, built with `effects.TapUntappedCost(n, label, preds…)`, so the candidate walk (`TapOthersOptionsForEffect`), the payability predicate and the validator are theirs.
- **The wire.** The caster names the permanents on `cast_spell`'s `teamwork_ids`, the list escalate's taps already ride (both are "tap permanents you control as a cost"; `Register` keeps a card from carrying two of them). They are validated at announce with the rest of the cost: exactly the clause's count, each once, the caster's own, untapped, matching the filter without the targeting gate (a hexproof Vampire pays), and not also named to convoke or waterbend. Tapping is not the {T} symbol, so a creature that came under the caster's control this turn may pay (CR 302.6).
- **The payment.** Tapped with the spell already on the stack, one `EventTapCard` each, and recorded on `PaidCost.TappedOthers` (the record station reads), so `ctx.TappedToPay()` names "the creature tapped to pay this spell's additional cost" as the object it was (CR 400.7). The auto-tap preview and `CastSpell`'s auto-tap never tap those permanents for mana.
- **Views.** `additional_cost.tap_others_label` / `tap_others_options` (the activation's picker view, off the same walk, on the cost and on each branch), the client's cast chain opens the sacrifice picker with the verb "Tap", and the cast gate greys a spell with too few to tap ("Nothing to tap"). The legal-move enumerator offers one payment per cast (the policy's cheapest to tap first), names it in the move label ("tapping Vampire") and on `teamwork_ids`, and offers nothing when the seat cannot pay; the bot prices each tap as it prices an alternative cost's.
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` refuses the component on an optional cost, the "tap X" and "another" forms, and beside teamwork or escalate's taps.
- **Cards** (8, all Full): New Blood (its creature-type change fixes the new word, `ChangeCreatureTypeWord.To`), Shared Discovery, Gaze of Justice (its flashback pays the tap too), Swallow Whole, Guardian of the Great Door, Warlord's Elite, Fear of Exposure and Disruption Protocol (a branch beside "pay {1}").
