**Mana abilities that pay energy, and the auto-tapper's energy tier** (#1995, [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md) §5 and owner decision 2, PR 2) — `ManaAbilityShape.EnergyCost` (`effects.ManaAbilityCost.Energy`) is "Pay N {E}" on a mana ability, which stays a mana ability (CR 605.1a). `ActivateManaAbility` checks it with the life before anything is paid (CR 118.3) and pays it after the life through `payEnergyLocked`, the one path that pays energy. **The energy tier** (`game/autotap_energy.go`): the auto-tapper plans such a source only when no plan that spends no energy pays the cost, before the pain tier (energy before life), the least energy first, in both comparators and the costed planner. The whole plan is held to the controller's energy (`spendBudget`, beside the pain tier's life budget), so two Aether Hubs with one energy between them pay one coloured pip, not two. The executor pays the energy as it taps; the `/autotap` preview names it (`sources[].energy`), and the client lists "Pay {E}" against the source and totals it in the summary. The view stamps `mana_abilities[i].energy_cost` and greys a row the controller is short for; the enumerator gates the move and names the energy on `MoveCost.energy`. **Cards** (4, all Full): Aether Hub, Servant of the Conduit, Solar Transformer and Conversion Apparatus. Closes `pay-energy-cost`.
