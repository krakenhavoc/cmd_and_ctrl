**Each player exiles their hand face down and gets it back at the next end step** (#5841, CR 406.3, CR 603.7, CR 400.7) — "each player exiles all cards from their hand face down and draws seven cards. At the beginning of the next end step, each player discards their hand and returns to their hand each card they exiled this way". `Game.ExileHandFaceDownKeyedForEffect` exiles one player's whole hand as one simultaneous event, the player as the one who exiles it, and the cards land `FaceDownExiled`, Necropotence's kind: no seat may look at them, their owner included, because no instruction allows it (CR 406.3). Its tail is told the cards that reached exile, so a CR 616 prompt on a leg is a restore point. Memory Jar runs it for each player in APNAP order through the keyed each-player chain (`eachPlayerInTurn`), whose last tail schedules the delayed trigger with every card exiled this way and the CR 400.7 epoch each landed with (`EffectParams.Objects`). The trigger's body is two continuation steps: every player discards their whole hand (a real discard, with the Jar as its source), then each listed card that is still in exile as the same object returns to its owner's hand (CR 603.7c), after any prompt a discard asked. Nothing new is persisted: the queue, the params and the parked steps already snapshot. **Cards:** Memory Jar (Full).
