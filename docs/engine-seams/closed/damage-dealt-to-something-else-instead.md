**Damage dealt to something else instead** (#1905, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §9, owner decision 1, Delivery PR 9) — one primitive, `redirectDamageEventLocked` (`RedirectDamageEventForEffect` for a catalog static), rewrites a damage event's recipient and its damage tail's kind together, so redirected damage lands through the path its new recipient takes, and does nothing when CR 614.9 says so. The damage keeps its source, combat status, deathtouch, lifelink, infect, wither and commander tally (CR 120.4b), meets the new recipient's shields and protection (CR 616.2), and is never redirected by the same effect again (CR 614.5). A redirection is a replacement, not a prevention effect: "damage can't be prevented" leaves it alone, and "can't be dealt instead" (Lava Burst, Whippoorwill) stops it unspent. The scoped kind, `ModRedirectDamage`, reads the one source vocabulary, what it protects, "the next time" (spent per damage instance), a charge (CR 615.7, divided by the protected player when it meets several events at once — the Harm's Way ruling — and split off an event it only partly covers), or all turn, and a destination: `Mod.To` (one object, epoch-pinned) or `Mod.ToSourceController`. With no destination and a follow-up it is Eye for an Eye. Two static redirections are two effects the affected player orders (the Pariah ruling). A banner line names each live redirection. **Cards** (51): Aegis of Honor, Beacon of Destiny, Captain's Maneuver, Carom, Daughter of Autumn, Empyrial Archangel, Eye for an Eye, General's Regalia, Gideon's Sacrifice, Glarecaster, Goblin Psychopath, Harm's Way, Harsh Judgment, Hazduhr the Abbot, Jade Monolith, Karona's Zealot, Kor Chant, Kor Dirge, Lancers en-Kor, Martyrs of Korlis, Mirror Strike, Mirrorwood Treefolk, Nomads en-Kor, Opal-Eye, Konda's Yojimbo (with bushido), Oracle's Attendants, Outrider en-Kor and Zhalfirin Crusader (with flanking), Palisade Giant, Pariah, Pariah's Shield, Protector of the Crown, Razia, Boros Archangel, Reflect Damage, Refraction Trap (its follow-up's target carried on the shield, and its trap cost read from the turn's casts), Reverberation, Saving Grace, Shaman en-Kor, Shield Dancer, Shimian Night Stalker, Soltari Guerrillas, Spirit en-Kor, Treacherous Link, Turn the Tables, Vassal's Duty, Veteran Bodyguard, Ward of Piety, Warrior en-Kor, Weathered Bodyguards, With Great Power . . . and Zealous Inquisitor (Full); Shining Shoal (Caveats: its alternative cost isn't offered). **Moved to other rows:** Sivvi's Valor (#2030, its alternative cost taps a creature), Nova Pentacle (#2055, a target of an opponent's choice), Blood of the Martyr, Kjeldoran Royal Guard and Ascent of the Worthy (#2056), Heroic Sacrifice (#2057), Ancient Adamantoise (#2058), Personal Incarnation and Martyrdom (#1947).
