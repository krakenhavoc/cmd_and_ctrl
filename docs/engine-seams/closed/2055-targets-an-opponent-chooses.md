**Targets an opponent chooses** (#2055, [ADR 0019 amendment 2026-10-16](decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md), CR 601.2c, CR 602.2b, CR 603.3d) — "target … of an opponent's choice" is `effects.OfAnOpponentsChoice`, and "… they control" `effects.TheyControl`: data on the clause (`TargetSpec.ChosenByOpponent`, `ControlledByChooser`), judged for the controller in the one shared walk, so an opponent cannot name their own hexproof creature. The controller's announcement names targets for their own clauses only; a cast or activation is then parked before anything is paid, the controller names an opponent (asked only when more than one could choose), and that opponent answers an ordinary `pick_target` (`chosen_for` on the wire); each answer re-runs the announcement from scratch. A trigger's walk asks the same two questions per step. `StackItem.TargetChoosers` records who chose, for the resolution re-check and a retarget ("they control" stays the chosen opponent's). The client's walk skips the clause (`chosen_by_opponent`), the enumerator names no target for it, and a bot choosing for somebody else's effect gives up the least. **Cards:** Nova Pentacle, Evangelize, Arena, Magus of the Arena, Cuombajj Witches, Volcanic Offering and Mausoleum Turnkey (Full). Held for other text: Preacher (#4061), Diaochan, Artful Beauty (#4062) and Karplusan Minotaur (#4063).
