**Shields against sources described by what they aren't, their power, combat status or controller** (#2026, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §7, amendment of 2026-10-05) — a `preventFromSource` shield carries one plain-data `DamageSourceFilter` beside its property queries: properties the source must not have ("non-Spider", "nongreen", "without trample", "other than Werewolves and Wolves", Inspire Awe's "except … enchanted creatures and enchantment creatures"), objects pinned as the shield resolves (Haze Frog's "other creatures", Terrifying Presence's "other than target creature", CR 400.7), a power bound with counters included, colorless (CR 105.2c), no counters of a kind, attacking or unblocked (CR 509.1h), and the source's controller relative to the shield's ("you don't control", "your opponents control", "that player controls"). Every part is read as the damage would be dealt (CR 609.7b, 615.9; a prevention effect's set of sources is never locked in, CR 611.2c), and a source that has left the battlefield is read as it last existed there (CR 608.2h), which now records whether it was attacking or enchanted (Heavy Fog's ruling). "Target blocked creature" reads CR 509.1h's blocked record. A source shield's follow-up can run once per source within one instance of damage, and knows whether that source was a creature, for "that creature" (CR 603.2c). Everything is plain data: an older binary refuses a restore point carrying it, and an unknown key inside a filter is refused too. **Cards** (25): Al-abara's Carpet, Arachnogenesis, Benalish Missionary, Comeuppance, Deep Wood, Fog of War, Frontline Strategist, Galadhrim Ambush, Harmless Assault, Haze Frog, Heavy Fog, Hindervines, Hunter's Ambush, Inspire Awe, Judgment of Alexander, Lithomancer's Focus, Moonmist, Obscuring Haze, Repel the Abominable, Tanglesap, Terrifying Presence, Thwart the Enemy, Undergrowth (its {2}{R} is a plain optional additional cost, not a kicker), Vine Snare and Winds of Qal Sisma (Full). **Moved to other rows:** Channel Harm (#2040, its "you may" is asked as the damage is prevented), Encircling Fissure (#2411, awaken) and Snag (#2412, an alternative cost that discards a card).
