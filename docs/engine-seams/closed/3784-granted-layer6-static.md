**A granted static in layer 6: the equipped creature gives your commanders lifelink** (#3784, [ADR 0093 amendment of 2026-10-15](decisions/0093-abilities-granted-to-other-permanents.md)) — a bundle a layer-6 grant names may now carry a layer-6 static, and a layer-7b or 7e one beside 7c. The static has CR 613.7a's timestamp (the host's, or the grant's if that is later), recorded per pass on `layerPassState.granted` and never persisted. The layer-6 bucket runs through `game.applyGrantingBucketLocked`. After each effect, it inserts the layer-6 statics of the bundles that effect just granted into the rest of the bucket, at their timestamp. That is the CR 613.8a dependency on the grant. Each also goes after any waiting removal that applies to the host, the second dependency. When its turn comes, it applies only while the host still has the grant. So a host that loses all abilities after the grant stops passing the keyword on, one that lost them before keeps it (CR 613.6), and a removal on a recipient sorts against the granted keyword by timestamp. `game.GrantedStaticProblem` still refuses layers 1-5, 7a (CR 604.3a) and a granted removal, grant or borrow. **Cards:** Dancer's Chakrams, Rune of Flight, Rune of Might, Rune of Mortality, Rune of Speed, Rune of Sustenance and Giant's Amulet (all Full).
