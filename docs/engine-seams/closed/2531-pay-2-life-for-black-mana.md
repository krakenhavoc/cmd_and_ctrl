**Paying 2 life for {B}: K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth** (#2531, [ADR 0131](decisions/0131-krrik-pay-life-for-black-mana.md), CR 107.4f / 601.2b / 602.2b) — a player static, `Spec.LifeForMana` (`effects.YouMayPayLifeForMana("B")`), marks each {B}, and the {B} half of a hybrid symbol, as payable with 2 life at the payment, before the spend-only fold and the any-colour widening, so a granted {B} plays exactly like a printed {B/P}: the same `phyrexian_life` claim, strike (`PhyrexianLifePlan`), ceiling and CR 119.4 / 119.8 gate. Generic mana and {C} are never reached, the price shown and the mana value stay printed, and the auto-tapper never pays life unasked. PR 1 covered casts (alternative and additional costs included), activated abilities and attack taxes. PR 2 adds the last two payments: a mana ability's own mana component (`ManaAbilityParams.PhyrexianLife`, wire `phyrexian_life` on `activate_mana_ability`, so a filter land's "{B}, {T}: Add …" can be paid with life) and a mana `pay_unless` (`ResolvePayUnlessWithLife`, `phyrexian_life` on the `resolve_choice` answer, for ward {B} and "unless that player pays {B}"). Both strike through the same helper pair the cast path uses, validate the claim before anything is paid (a malformed one leaves a pay-unless prompt in place), and pay the life only once the mana half is known payable. The view counts the viewer's symbols on mana-ability rows and on `pay_unless` prompts (`phyrexian_symbols`, `phyrexian_granted`); the enumerator offers the fewest life symbols that make a mana ability or a pay-unless payable, and the heuristic gives a life-paid ward the cast's floor of 10 life. **Cards:** K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth (Full).
