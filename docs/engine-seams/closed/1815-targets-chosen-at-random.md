**Targets chosen at random** (#1815, CR 115.7, CR 115.9c, CR 603.3d, [ADR 0019 amendment 2026-10-16](decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md)) — a target was always chosen by a player: CR 115.7's retarget is the `retarget` prompt, and a trigger's CR 603.3d target is the `pick_target` prompt. Two engine draws now stand beside them, both from the keyed random stream (ADR 0054, the "pick" stream keyed to the player and the source), so an undo replays the draw, and both logged as a choose-option line ending "(at random)".
- **Reselecting a spell's targets at random.** `Game.ReselectTargetsAtRandomForEffect(RandomRetarget{ItemID, Drawer, Source, Exclude})` (`game/random_retarget.go`) is CR 115.7 run by the engine. Each target slot, in announce order, is drawn uniformly from the clause's legal targets (judged for the ITEM's controller, as every retarget is), the current target included ("reselect" is not "change to another"; Grip of Chaos's reminder text and ruling), less `Exclude` and the permanents `Exclude` controls ("the new targets can't be you or a permanent you control"), and kept only when the list with it passes `retargetCheckLocked`, so the same-clause duplicate rule, Distinct, the set rules and the relations hold. A slot with nothing that could be selected keeps its target, even an illegal one. The item is rewritten through `RetargetStackItemForEffect`, so the new targets become targets (CR 115.7) and a division travels with its slot (CR 115.7f). `CopySpellKeepingTargetsForEffect` is the copy with the original's targets that returns the copy's id.
- **"Targets only a single permanent or player."** `Game.StackItemSoleTargetForEffect` is CR 115.9c: one DIFFERENT object or player among the item's targets, so a spell that chose one creature for two instances of "target" qualifies; `effects.TargetsOnlyASinglePermanentOrPlayer` then checks that target's current state (CR 115.9b: still on the battlefield, or still in the game).
- **"Target … chosen at random" on a trigger.** `TargetSpec.AtRandom`, built with `effects.ChosenAtRandom(spec)`. Honoured in `queuePickTargetStepLocked` (`game/pending_choice.go`): the step's legal set is computed as for a prompt, and `drawTriggerTargetLocked` draws one target from it; with none, a required clause removes the ability as it always did. No `pick_target` prompt is queued. A CR 603.12 reflexive trigger goes through the same walk. `effects.Register` refuses the flag on a spell's or an activated ability's clause, and on a clause that is not exactly one undivided target.
- **Determinism and snapshots.** Nothing new is persisted: the flag lives on the catalog's clause, re-derived on restore, and the drawn targets ride `StackItem.Targets`. Power Pack's delayed trigger adds the body key `power-pack/cast-exiled-free` to the effect-key ledger.
- **Cards:** Chef's Kiss, Witch Hunt, Scab-Clan Giant, Knight Rampager (its "choose an opponent at random" is a draw at resolution feeding the encore attack requirement) and Power Pack (Full). **Still open:** Grip of Chaos (#4050, nothing triggers on a triggered ability going on the stack), Goblin Test Pilot (#4051, an activated ability's random target) and Explosion of Riches (#4052, card work).
