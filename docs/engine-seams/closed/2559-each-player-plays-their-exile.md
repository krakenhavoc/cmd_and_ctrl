**Each player plays the cards they exiled, and can't play from their hand** (#2559, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md) amendment of 2026-10-10) — `effects.EachPlayerExilesTopAndMayPlay` exiles the top N cards of every seated player's library and stores one permission per player over the cards THEY exiled, held by them, for a window the card stamps against its controller ("until your next turn", "until your next end step"); it is play, not cast, so a land among them spends that player's land drop. "Can't play cards from your hand" is a new rules-gate ScopedEffect kind, `cantPlayFromHand` (`Game.CantPlayFromHandForEffect`, `game/hand_play_ban.go`), read by both `CastGateLocked` (a cast whose source zone is the hand) and `LandPlayGateLocked` (a land played from the hand), so the cast path, a land play during a resolution, the bot enumerator and the view's `cant_cast` stamps all see it; its duration is asked on every read. An older binary refuses a restore point naming the kind (fixture `v7/memory_vessel.json`). The wire grows `PlayerView.cant_play_from_hand` (the seat's NO HAND badge) and `exile_play.until` / `until_player` (when a grant ends and whose turn it counts), so the zone browser labels a card another seat may play ("Ana may play it until Bo's next turn"), a seat's exile chip counts what it may play, and the viewer's own button names its real window. The model bots' board text says which exiled cards each seat may play and that a seat can't play from its hand. **Cards:** Memory Vessel and Rocco, Street Chef (Full).
