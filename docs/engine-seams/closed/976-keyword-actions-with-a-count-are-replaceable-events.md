**Keyword actions with a count are replaceable events** (#976, [ADR 0013 §5s](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)): "If you would proliferate, proliferate twice instead" (Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus) and "if you would scry, scry that many plus one instead" (the Crystal Ball shape) replace the ACTION, and there was no event for one — `ProliferateForEffect` applied its choice directly and `lookAtTopForEffect` queued its prompt directly, so neither entered the CR 614 pipeline. One `game.RepEventKeywordAction{KeywordAction, KeywordActionCount, Actor, Source}` now opens once per INSTRUCTION at the one entry point of each action (proliferate CR 701.34, scry CR 701.22, surveil CR 701.25), on ADR 0061's token-creation shape. What the count means is per action and is the rules' own distinction: for proliferate it is the number of TIMES the whole action is taken (base 1, choice and all), for scry and surveil the number of CARDS. Card-side it is `KeywordActionBecomes(action, count, label)` with `ProliferateTwice` and `ScryPlusOne` as the named wrappers — the count function applies to the count the EVENT carries, so two of them compose the way CR 616.1 composes them. Identity and ordering are inherited, not declared: two Tekuthals are one declared effect (#792's skip) and proliferate four times with no prompt, while a doubler beside a "plus one" prompts because ×2 then +1 and +1 then ×2 differ. Both entry points can PAUSE on that prompt and carry what they owe on `keywordActionTail`, so a paused scry has queued nothing and `ScryThenForEffect` returns 0; a cancelled action still runs the continuation after "then". "Look at the top N cards of your library" is not a keyword action and opens no window. **Cards:** Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus goes to `full` — the last of the two things #943 left it blocked on. No catalog card prints the scry or surveil side yet.
