**Cards exiled with a permanent by a replacement, and playing them** (#2530, CR 607.2a, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-10-07](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)) — Valgavoth, Terror Eater's "you may play cards exiled with Valgavoth" was held back because a replacement effect runs before the card moves and cannot register a permission over the card that lands. The permission is now a derived standing one (`Spec.CastPermissions`, read off the battlefield on every query, never stored) and the link is card-carried: `Card.ExiledWith` names the permanent object `{instance, epoch}`, left on `ReplacementEvent.ExiledWith` by the replacement's `Replace` and stamped by `executeZoneRouteLocked` and `executeBattlefieldLeaveLocked` once the card has landed in exile (`stampExiledWithLocked`); `MoveCard` clears it on every move. `PermissionFilter.ExiledWithSource` narrows a standing permission to cards linked to the granting object, so it ends when the permanent leaves and a bounced, replayed permanent has no claim on the old object's cards (CR 400.7). `effects.GraveyardBecomesExile` gets `LinkExiled`. The price is Bolas's Citadel's life-for-mana-value alternative cost; "during your turn" is `TimingYourTurnOnly`. Snapshot: additive `exiledWith`. **1 card:** Valgavoth, Terror Eater (`full`; its "you can't play them" caveat is gone). Tracker [#2532](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/2532).
