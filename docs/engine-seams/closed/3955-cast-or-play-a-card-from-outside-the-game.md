**Cast or play a card from outside the game** (#3955, CR 400.11, 305.1, 608.2g, 601.2a, [ADR 0198](decisions/0198-outside-the-game-is-the-card-database-without-a-sideboard.md) amendment of 2026-10-16) — both pools (a deck's sideboard, the card database) end with the chosen card in `Player.Sideboard`; nothing could cast from it. A cast from outside the game is now a per-instance `CastPermission` over that container, claimed with an ordinary `cast_spell { from_zone: "outside" }`: `castZoneFromWire` and `castSourceZoneLocked` resolve it to the caster's own container (closed to a controlled player, ADR 0167 §5), only a permission opens it (`cardOpensZoneLocked` answers false and `effects.Register` refuses a `CastableZones` that names it), and the grant doors are `FreeCastFromOutsideForEffect` (free, any timing, cast-only, pass-closed, `LapseStaysOutside`) and, for Wish, `GrantOutsidePlayRightForEffect`: a standing, one-use, until-end-of-turn permission over the whole container, so the card is chosen when it is PLAYED, at its own timing and cost, a land included (a sideboard casts a card directly; the database pool names one with the new `outside_play` action, `Game.NameOutsideCardToPlay`, whose cancelled pick spends nothing; `PlayerView.outside_play` says so). A land played from outside keeps its entry replacements (`Game.outsideEntering` opens the card index to that one card for the span of its entry). `effects.PlayFromOutside` asks through `QueueOutsidePickForEffect` (new `Destination` on `OutsidePickPrompt`: `cast_free_outside`, `play_outside`), so both pools apply. The view stamps the owner's sideboard cards with the ordinary cast surface (`stampOutsideCasts`), the enumerator offers the cast and the land play ("... from outside the game"), the heuristic prices the pick and the cast, the auto-tap preview reads the caller's own container card, and the client's sideboard tab shows the button and opens itself after the pick. No snapshot field. **Cards:** Spawnsire of Ulamog's {20} (caveat cleared, Full), Wish (new, Full).
