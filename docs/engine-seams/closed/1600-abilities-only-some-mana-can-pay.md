**Abilities only some mana can pay, and mana for one-colour or gold spells** (#1600, CR 105.2a / CR 105.2b / CR 609.4b, [ADR 0040](decisions/0040-mana-pipeline.md)'s 2026-10-03 amendment) — the ability half and the token half of the "Costs that only some mana can pay" row. COST side: `game.AbilityCost.SpendOnly` (`game.ManaSpendOnly{Colors, ChosenColor, XOnly}`), built with `effects.SpendOnlyManaOfTheChosenColor()` ("Spend only mana of the chosen color to activate this ability") or `effects.SpendOnlyOnX(colors...)` ("Spend only black mana on X"), composed with `Plus` and checked at boot (`checkSpendOnlyClause`). The one ability pricer (`AbilityManaCostForTargetsForEffect`) resolves it against the source — the chosen colour read off `Card.ChosenColor` (#742) — and stamps it on the `ParsedCost`, whose `String()` ignores it, so the shown price stays printed. `costAsPaidByLocked` (now taking the announced X) FOLDS it into coloured requirements before the any-colour widening (`game/spend_only.go`): the activation and its auto-tap, `internal/legal`'s one affordability probe and the auto-tap preview all read it there, so the view's `legal_actions`, the bot's moves and the payment agree, and no solver changed. Under Chromatic Orrery any mana pays it — CR 609.4b, the restriction is about how the cost is paid, and the Celestial Dawn rulings say an as-though colour pays "an ability that can only be activated by spending another color of mana"; a cost with no chosen colour stays unpayable. TOKEN side: the restriction tags `monocolored` and `multicolored` (`game.ManaRestrictMonocolored` / `ManaRestrictMulticolored`, CR 105.2a–b; a hybrid spell is multicolored, CR 202.2d), which a mana restriction keeps under the Orrery. `effects.MonocoloredSpellsOfTheChosenColor()` and `effects.ProducedChosenColorAmount(n)` are Throne's mana. **Cards:** Throne of Eldraine, Crypt Rats, Crimson Hellkite, Pillar of the Paruns and Obsidian Obelisk (Full). **Not covered** (the row stays open as partial): the same clause on a SPELL (Drain Life, Consume Spirit, Soul Burn), "spend only mana produced by …" source restrictions (Imperiosaur, Myr Superion, Security Rhox), and Emblazoned Golem's one-of-each-colour cap. Atalya, Samite Master is writable now and not catalogued.
