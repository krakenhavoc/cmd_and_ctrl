**Cards exiled face down with a source, and a temporary play grant that cuts the cost** (#3787, CR 607.2a / 406.3 / 601.2f / 305.1, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md) amendment 2026-10-15, [ADR 0091](decisions/0091-hideaway.md) amendment 2026-10-15) — "look at the top card of your library, you may exile it face down" and, later, "until end of turn, you may play cards exiled with this. Spells you cast this way cost {2} less". `Game.ExileHiddenLinkedForEffect` is hideaway's face-down exile (`FaceDownHidden`, the exiling permanent's controller the only viewer) that also stamps `Card.ExiledWith`; `effects.ExileTopFaceDownLinked` is the look and the optional exile. `effects.PlayCardsExiledWith` stores a `ScopeStanding` permission over exile whose `PermissionFilter.ExiledWithSource` names the source object (so a card exiled after the activation is playable the same turn, and a source that left and came back has no claim, CR 400.7), with `PermissionFilter.OwnedByHolder` for "cards you own" and `CastPermission.CostLess` for "{N} less": generic mana read into `CostQuery.GrantReduction`, taken off after every increase and clamped at {0}, by the one pricer and the enumerator alike. The trigger on playing a land or casting a spell from exile needed no engine change (`Event.Played` and `EventCast.OldZone`). Snapshot additive within v9 (`costLess`, `filter.ownedByHolder`). **Cards:** Urianger Augurelt and Kayla's Music Box (Full).
