**Ward with a poison cost, and a keyword in a dies trigger's last-known information** (#4613, CR 702.21a, CR 118.12a, CR 122.1f, CR 704.5c, CR 603.10a, [ADR 0038 C6](decisions/0038-protection-style-keywords.md)) — `effects.WardPoison(n)` is a sixth `WardCost` component: a keyed confirm (`confirm/ward-get-poison`) whose accept branch gives the caster n poison counters through the CR 614 counter window, with the spell countered on a decline. It is never withheld, because a player can always get counters; the tenth is lost to the ordinary state-based action. The prompt declares `ConfirmPrompt.PoisonCost` (`PendingChoice.PoisonCost`, snapshot `poisonCost`, wire `poison_cost`), which the enumerator stamps on the accept move as `legal.MoveCost.Poison` and the heuristic prices on the life curve at two life a counter, refusing a payment that reaches ten. The client renders the prompt's own question and button labels, so it needed no change. The same PR stamps the post-layer keywords on every `EventLTB` (`Event.LastKnownKeywords`, read through `Event.WasKeyword` and the card-side `leftWithKeyword`), the sibling of the colours (#1689), so "another creature you control with deathtouch dies" is judged on what the creature was as it left. Both fields are additive within the snapshot version. **Cards:** The Serpent Society (`full`). Not this seam: "Ward—Get N poison counters" mixed with another component.
