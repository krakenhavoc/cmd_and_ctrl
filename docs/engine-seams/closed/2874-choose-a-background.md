**Choose a Background and the other partner pairings** (#2874, CR 702.124h, i, k, m, [ADR 0144](decisions/0144-choose-a-background-and-the-partner-pairings.md)) — `deck/partner.go` is the deck side, `effects/choose_a_background.go` the Backgrounds' shared shapes.
- **One pairing rule.** `deck.Validate` accepts two commanders when one partner ability pairs them, read off Scryfall's oracle text (`pairedBy`): both have partner, both have the same partner—[text] (Friends forever and the rest), each partner with names the other (#2142), one has choose a Background and the other is a legendary Background enchantment card, or one is a legendary creature with Doctor's companion and the other a legendary Time Lord Doctor with no other creature types. Abilities never combine (CR 702.124f). A pair no ability allows is `invalid_partner_pair`, naming the card at fault; two cards with no partner ability and no Background stay `too_many_commanders`. A lone Background is `not_a_legal_commander` unless it has choose a Background itself (Faceless One). Colour identity is the union.
- **Import.** `deck.Resolve` no longer refuses a partner commander; only companion is still `unsupported_mechanic`. A Background is listed after the commander that chose it (`backgroundsLast`), so the command zone, the fallback deck name and the avatar show the creature first.
- **Background grants.** "Commander creatures you own have …" is an ADR 0093 grant on `commanderCreatureYouOwn` (`grantToCommanderCreaturesYouOwn`): each commander creature the Background's controller owns, under anyone's control, only while the Background is on the battlefield. `wheneverThisAttacksAPlayerNoOpponentRicher` is the Baldur's Gate "attacks a player, if no opponent has more life than that player" trigger. Guild Artisan moved from a trigger on the Background to a real grant, so a stolen commander keeps it.
- **Game, client and bot.** Nothing assumed one commander: two cards share the command zone, each with its own tax and commander damage, and a Background is cast from there like any commander. The bot prices that cast (a test pins it). The client needed no change.
- **Cards:** Karlach, Fury of Avernus, Jaheira, Friend of the Forest, Ganax, Astral Hunter, Wilson, Refined Grizzly, Anara, Wolvid Familiar, Breeches, Brazen Plunderer, Ghost of Ramirez DePietro, Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator, Sakashima of a Thousand Faces and Guild Artisan lost their caveats; Flaming Fist, Sword Coast Sailor, Agent of the Shadow Thieves, Candlekeep Sage, Clan Crafter and Raised by Giants are new, all Full.
- **Not built:** companion. The other Backgrounds are card work (Veteran Soldier, Popular Entertainer, Criminal Past, Cultist of the Absolute, Master Chef and the rest), and Wizard from Beyond's Alchemy "Create a Character" is not read.
