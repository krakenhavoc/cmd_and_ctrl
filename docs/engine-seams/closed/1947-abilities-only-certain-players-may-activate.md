**Abilities only certain players may activate** (#1947, [ADR 0106](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md) §1 amendments 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-09) — three more activators beside `AnyPlayer`, all read by the one predicate `game.MayActivate`, so the engine, the enumerator and the view agree. `OpponentsOnly` is every player but the controller (Clergy and Knight of the Holy Nimbus), `OwnerOnly` is the CR 108.4a owner, who is not the controller of a stolen creature (Personal Incarnation), and `GrantorOnly` is a granted row's "Only you may activate this ability": the one player who may is the "you" of the effect that granted it, recorded by the layer pass on every grant it writes (`GrantedAbility.Activator`, carried to the row on `AbilityOrigin.Activator`). A creature that changes hands after Martyrdom resolved therefore gives its new controller nothing, and the caster, who no longer controls it, can still use the ability. The view marks the row `opponents_only`, `owner_only` or `grantor_only` (with the one `activator`), files the per-seat copy for a non-controller as it does an `any_player` row, and the client greys the row for a viewer who may not use it (`rowOpenToViewer`). `Register` refuses two named activators on one row, `GrantorOnly` on a card's own row, the `AnyPlayer` component and zone limits, and a `Purpose` on an opponents-only or grantor-only row. The redirections are ADR 0108 §9's `effects.RedirectDamage`: `RedirectToSourceOwner` for Personal Incarnation, and `RedirectToThis` aimed at a protected target for Martyrdom. A `GrantorOnly` row in a bundle used by a copy's "except it has" clause is open to nobody, because a copy grant has no layered grant to read a player from. **Cards:** Clergy of the Holy Nimbus, Knight of the Holy Nimbus, Personal Incarnation and Martyrdom (all Full).
