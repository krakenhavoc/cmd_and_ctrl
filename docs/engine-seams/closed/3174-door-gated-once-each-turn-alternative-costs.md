**Alternative costs granted behind a Room door, once each turn, or for life** (#3174, CR 118.9 / 118.9a / 119.4 / 202.3 / 400.7 / 709.5) — two Rooms kept a caveat because their second door is a standing alternative cost the granted-offer seam (ADR 0118 §3) could not say: Warped Space's "once each turn, you may pay {0} rather than pay the mana cost for a spell you cast from exile" and Access Maze's "once during each of your turns, you may cast a spell from your hand by paying life equal to its mana value rather than paying its mana cost".
- **A door gate.** `game.GrantedAlternativeCost.ActiveWhen` gates the static on a designation of its source. `effects.Room` stamps it from the new `Door.GrantedAlternativeCosts` slot and `specDesignations` lists it, so Register's door guard covers it. `grantedAlternativeCostsLocked` skips a source whose gate is shut, and the ability-row walk now words the slot per entry (`staticRowSlot.elemLabel`), so a locked door's row is hidden too.
- **Once each turn, per object.** `OnceEachTurn` stamps the derived offer with the key `ObjectTallyKey(source, epoch, offer key)`; `noteGrantedOfferClaimedLocked` writes it into `TurnTally.GrantedAltCostsClaimed` as the spell becomes cast, beside the cast tally. A failed cast spends nothing, two such Rooms give two claims, a Room that left and came back is a new object (CR 400.7), and the tally's turn reset brings the claim back. The gate is asked before the per-key dedupe, so a second source still offers when the first's claim is spent. `YourTurnOnly` is Access Maze's "during each of your turns".
- **Life equal to mana value.** `LifeEqualToManaValue` is Bolas's Citadel's price on a granted offer: `AlternativeCost.Life` equal to the mana value of the face being cast and no mana, so the life-payability check refuses a caster short of it (CR 119.4) and the enumerator's policy never pays a seat down to 0. A spell with {X} in its cost, whose mana value would count the X announced, or with no mana cost gets no offer: weaker than printed, never stronger. `Register` refuses the flag beside any other price.
- **Cards:** Charred Foyer // Warped Space, Cramped Vents // Access Maze (Full).
