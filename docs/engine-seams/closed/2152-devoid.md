**Devoid** (#2152, CR 702.114a, [ADR 0034 amendment 2026-10-05](decisions/0034-multi-face-cards.md)) — a devoid card is colourless in every zone instead of the colour of its pips. Scryfall's `colors: []` for a devoid card had always read as "not stamped", so the colour fell back to the mana cost. `devoid` joins `canonicalKeywords` (`game.KeywordDevoid`, `game/devoid.go`), so the deck importer stamps it on the ~130 devoid cards with no catalog entry, and the printed-colour rule reads it as the colour-defining ability it is: a stamped colour list first, then nothing for devoid, then the cost (`printedColorsOf`, and `Card.EffectiveColors` off the battlefield). Being layer 0, it reaches every colour read — target predicates, "colorless spell" payoffs, protection, mana restrictions, the wire's `colors` — and a layer-5 colour effect still applies on top. A copy keeps it (Keywords and the oracle ID are copiable values); a copy "except it's black" is black, because a stamped colour wins (CR 707.9d). Colour identity (CR 903.4) is unchanged. The importer leaves a devoid face's colours unstamped rather than deriving them from its pips (Drowner of Truth). No wire or snapshot field changed; a restore point written before this keeps an uncatalogued devoid card coloured for the rest of its game. **Cards:** Ugin's Binding's caveat is gone (now Full); Fanged Flames, Unnatural Aggression, Touch of the Void, Brutal Expulsion, Depth Defiler, Hideous Taskmaster, Propagator Drone, Serpentine Spike and Flaying Tendrils declare the keyword, and `TestEveryCatalogDevoidCardIsColourless` holds every catalogued devoid card to it.
