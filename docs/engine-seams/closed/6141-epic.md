**Epic** (#6141, CR 702.50) — `effects.Epic(...)` wraps a spell's `OnResolve`. Its controller gets an outright cast ban with `IndefiniteDuration` (the existing per-player cast-ban seam), and a step-conditioned delayed trigger at each of their upkeeps that copies the spell as it resolved, keeping X, modes, kicker and targets, and offers new targets (CR 707.10c). The trigger carries the spell as snapshot records (`DelayedTrigger.CopiesSpell`), so it needs no closure and not the original card. The copy loses the epic keyword (CR 707.9b), so it sets up nothing more. **Cards:** Enduring Ideal (Full). The other epic cards are left to their batch.
