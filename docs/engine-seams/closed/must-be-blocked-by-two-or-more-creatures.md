**Must be blocked by two or more creatures if able** (#6243, CR 509.1c) — `game.BlockRequirementTwoOrMore` ("twoOrMore") is a block requirement on the attacker, obeyed only by a declaration that gives it at least two blockers. It joins the requirement search as a special attacker with the "exactly one" modes (closed, one, two and more), and the refusal reads "<attacker> must be blocked by two or more creatures if able." `effects.MustBeBlockedByTwoOrMore()` is Gorm the Great's sentence as its two printed requirements, "must be blocked if able" beside it, so with one creature able to block that creature must block, and with two or more at least two must. **Cards:** Gorm the Great, built by its OLD batch (b204).
