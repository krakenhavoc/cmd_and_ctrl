**Beam me up, printed and granted, and "can't be beamed up"** (#4973, Star Trek; the pinned rules have no entry yet, so the reminder text governs; follows [granted sneak](engine-seams/closed/4680-granted-sneak.md)) — "Beam me up {2}{U} (You may cast this card from your graveyard for {2}{U} if you also return a creature you control to its owner's hand. Then exile this spell.)" is `effects.BeamMeUp(cost)`: `Flashback`'s graveyard cast and exile under the key `beam-me-up`, with `game.BeamMeUpReturnSpec` as the `ReturnToHand` half of the price. Scotty, Miracle Worker's "During your main phase, each card in your graveyard has beam me up … equal to that spell's mana cost. Scotty can't be beamed up to cast a spell this way" is `effects.CardsInYourGraveyardHaveBeamMeUpDuringYourMainPhase`, a gated standing `CastPermission` under `granted-beam-me-up` whose offer `AlternativeCostFor` builds (the card's mana cost, the return with the permission's source left out, the exile), for instants and sorceries only. Marooned's "can't be beamed up" is `Characteristic.CantBeBeamedUp`, written by a layer-6 static of the Aura and read by the return spec, so the host can't pay any beam me up cost. The characteristic field is additive in snapshot v9. **Cards:** Open Communications, Scotty, Miracle Worker, Marooned — Full.
