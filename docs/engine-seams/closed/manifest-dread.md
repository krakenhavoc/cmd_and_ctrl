**Manifest dread** (#2570, [ADR 0082 amendment 2026-10-07](decisions/0082-casting-face-down-and-turning-face-up.md), CR 701.62a) — `Game.ManifestDreadThenForEffect` looks at the top two cards of your library (only you become a knower), asks a `choose_cards` prompt (hideaway's, so the wire privacy, the enumerator and both bot policies already cover it), manifests the CHOSEN card through the CR 614 face-down entry and puts the other into its owner's graveyard (a plain zone move: not a mill, not a discard). The continuation is told `ManifestDreadResult{Manifested, Graveyarded}`; `EventManifestDread` carries both, for "whenever you manifest dread" and "a card you put into your graveyard this way", and the public log gets one card-less `manifest_dread` line. `effects.ManifestDread`, `ManifestDreadTimes`, `PutCountersOnManifested`, `AttachSourceToManifested` and `ManifestDreadWhenItDiesThisTurn` (keyed delayed body `dies/manifest-dread`) are the card vocabulary. **Cards:** Manifest Dread, Unsettling Twins, Innocuous Rat, Bashful Beastie, Unnerving Grasp, Under the Skin, Paranormal Analyst, They Came from the Pipes, Growing Dread, Threats Around Every Corner, Killer's Mask, Cursed Windbreaker, Conductive Machete, Dissection Tools, Break Down the Door, Twist Reality, Valgavoth's Onslaught, Turn Inside Out, Glitch Interpreter, Curator Beastie, Disturbing Mirth and Stay Hidden, Stay Silent (Full); Moldering Gym // Weight Room, Ticket Booth // Tunnel of Hate and Underwater Tunnel // Slimy Aquarium lose their caveat, and Experimental Lab // Staff Room keeps only the Staff Room one. **Moved to other rows:** Hauntwoods Shrieker and Zimone, Mystery Unraveler (turning a permanent face up as an effect), Defiant Survivor (an event for the second main phase) and Abhorrent Oculus (an exile-from-graveyard additional cost) stay on the `manifest-dread` row.
