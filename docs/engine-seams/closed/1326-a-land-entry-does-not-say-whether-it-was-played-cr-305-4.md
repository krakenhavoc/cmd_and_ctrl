**A land entry does not say whether it was PLAYED (CR 305.4)** (#1326, CR 305.1 / 305.4, [ADR 0061 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0061-token-creation-and-discard-are-replaceable-events.md)) — the *Aang is so flashy* deck triage (#1306) filed it against Deep Gnome Terramancer's "whenever one or more lands enter under an opponent's control **without being played**", which cannot be written against a guess. `Event.Played` is stamped by `announceEntryLocked` (the shared entry finisher above) from the settled entry's `landPlay` flag — the one the land-drop tally already reads — true for the land branch of `CastSpell` (a play from hand, an impulse-exile grant or a graveyard permission, whichever zone the branch draws from) and unset (the zero value) for every "put" path and for a token. The land branch's own push had never actually been migrated to call the shared finisher — unlike the stack-resolution site #653 fixed — so it duplicated the move, tally bump and events inline and would have stamped `Played` only on a PAUSED land; it now calls `executeEntryToBattlefieldLocked` and the duplicate is gone. Three catalog readers that used to GUESS the distinction from the entry's origin zone and the land-drop tally's arithmetic (`b20LandPlayed`, `b10AnotherLandPlayedByYou`) now read the field directly, closing the caveats on Horn of Greed, Prosper, Tome-Bound and City of Traitors — plus a fourth pair #1360 declared while this was in flight (a land `PutFromHandOntoBattlefieldForEffect` puts from hand still drew a card on Horn of Greed and The Endstone, stronger than printed), whose pinned test flips to assert no draw. **Cards:** Deep Gnome Terramancer (new, complete); Horn of Greed, Prosper, Tome-Bound, City of Traitors and The Endstone lose their land-play-guess caveat and are complete.
