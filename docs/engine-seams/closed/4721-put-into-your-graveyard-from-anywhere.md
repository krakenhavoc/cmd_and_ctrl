**Put into your graveyard from anywhere** (#4721, [ADR 0049 amendment 2026-10-30 (#4721)](decisions/0049-card-engine-seam-review.md), CR 603.2c, 603.6c, 108.2b, 111.7) — "whenever one or more creature cards are put into your graveyard from anywhere, … that many" and "whenever an artifact is put into your graveyard from the battlefield or an artifact card from anywhere other than the battlefield". The library-only reader generalises to `graveyardArrivals` (`cards/effects/graveyard_arrival.go`): a clause is a source-zone list, an excluded-zone list and a card filter, read two ways. `When()` is the per-event `AppliesTo` over the three kinds an arrival is announced by (`EventDiscardCard`, `EventMill`, `EventZoneMove`, exactly one per arrival), and `InBatch(g, batch, owner)` is the arrivals of one event batch, read as the ability resolves for "that many". A token is never a card, so the reader skips it; `WhenAnotherArtifactIsPutIntoYourGraveyard` reads the leaves-the-battlefield event for the battlefield half, which counts a token because it is put into the graveyard before it ceases to exist. One batch is one resolution or one turn-based action (`event_batch.go`). `cardsPutIntoGraveyardFromLibraryInBatch` is now the same reader narrowed to the library and to cards still there. No snapshot field, no new effect key. **Cards:** Voracious Brood, Ultron's Auxiliary and Ultron the Annihilator (Full).
