**Exert** (#2048, CR 701.43 / 508.1g / 607.2h, [ADR 0130](decisions/0130-exert.md)) — "You may exert this creature as it attacks" is an optional cost to attack (CR 701.43d), and nothing could choose it or record it. `exertLocked` (`game/exert.go`) is now the one exert primitive: ADR 0058's next-untap marker keyed to the EXERTING player (CR 701.43a, so a borrowed creature untaps during its owner's untap step), one `TurnTally.Exerts` record per exert, per object (CR 400.7), and `EventExert`. The marker's duplicate rule is load-bearing: a second exert before that untap step adds no second marker (CR 701.43b), and a test pins it for #2029. `Spec.ExertOnAttack` (`ExertAsItAttacks`, `ExertAsItAttacksUnless`) declares the static ability, read off the creature's effective abilities. `declare_attacker` and `declare_attackers` take `exert` per attacker; the verb validates it (`ErrCantExert`, the whole batch) and stages it on `Card.ExertOnAttack`, and `commitAttackDeclarationLocked` pays it as the declaration locks in, before the creatures become attacking creatures and in the same event batch (CR 508.1j, 508.1m), so its triggers go on the stack with the attack triggers. `effects.WhenExerted` is the linked "when you do", `effects.WheneverYouExert` the payoff, `Game.ExertedThisTurn` Combat Celebrant's condition. The enumerator offers each attack twice, plain and exerting (the heuristic ignores the exert move until ADR 0130 PR 3), the digest carries `exert_on_attack`, and the log says "<player> exerted <card>". The snapshot additions are additive within v7. **Oketra's Avenger**, **Combat Celebrant**, **Glorybringer** and **Resolute Survivors** are new and `full`. **Still open:** the client's choice (ADR 0130 PR 2), exert as an activation cost and its 8 cards (PR 4), and the other attack-exert cards (PR 5).
