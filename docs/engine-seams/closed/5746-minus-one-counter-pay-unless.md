**Pay-unless paid with a -1/-1 counter, and who put a permanent onto the battlefield** (#5746, CR 118.12, 118.12a, 118.3, 614.16) — "deals 3 damage to that player unless the player puts a -1/-1 counter on a creature they control" had no payment to ask with: the pay-unless helpers took mana, life, a discard, a sacrifice, a tap or a graveyard exile. `game.PayActionMinusOneCounter` (`"minus_one_counter"`) is a card-pick payment beside them: the options are the permanents of the clause's kind the payer controls that can have a counter put on them (CR 118.3, #4692), and paying puts one -1/-1 counter on each one named as a cost's counter (marked from a cost, so Doubling Season does not double it, CR 614.16; it is not blight, CR 701.68a). The view, the enumerator, the bot's price (a blight of one's, so the counter goes on a body that survives it) and the client's picker come with the kind. Card side (`cards/effects/minus_counter_payment.go`): `MinusOneCounterPayment(noun, of)` and `PayActionUnless{Chooser, Payment, Question, Else, Carry}`, a keyed pay-unless over any non-mana payment and any chooser, so the open question is a restore point. "Whenever a player puts a Swamp onto the battlefield" needed the putter: `EventETB` now carries `PutBy`, stamped from the same reading as Arboria's record (`entryPutterLocked`), read by `APlayerPutAPermanentWithSubtype`. Snapshot: `Event.put_by`, additive in v9. New effect key: `payunless pay-unless/chant-3-damage-to-that-player`. **Cards:** Thelon's Chant, Tourach's Chant (Full).
