**Echo, and pay-unless payments that are not mana** (#1888, CR 702.30a / CR 603.4 / CR 118.12a / CR 702.24a, [ADR 0108 §5](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md)) — "At the beginning of your upkeep, if this permanent came under your control since the beginning of your last upkeep, sacrifice it unless you pay [cost]." The fact is two counters: `Player.UpkeepsBegun` goes up as that player's upkeep begins, before any upkeep trigger is checked, and `Card.ControlledSinceUpkeep` is stamped with the controller's count as the permanent enters and at each control change the layer pass materialises. The condition is `ControlledSinceUpkeep >= UpkeepsBegun - 1` (`game.CameUnderControlSinceLastUpkeepForEffect`), read as the upkeep begins and again on resolution, so a permanent that left, came back as a new object or changed hands in response is not charged; counting upkeeps rather than turns means a skipped upkeep is no "last upkeep" and a permanent that entered in the untap step is charged once. `effects.Echo`, `EchoPayment` and `EchoX` build the trigger (stamped `game.KeywordEcho`) with the cost read on resolution (Volcano Hellion's life total), and paying emits `EventEchoPaid` (`effects.WhenEchoIsPaid`, Shah of Naar Isle). The upkeep pay-unless prompt learned two payments besides mana (owner decision 3): `game.PayAction` "discard N cards" and "sacrifice N permanents" of a `PermanentQuery`, plain data on the prompt. The payer names the cards on the "yes" (`card_ids`, `ResolvePayUnlessWithCards`), validated before the prompt is dequeued; an unpayable "yes" is a decline (CR 118.3). `effects.CumulativeUpkeepPaying` multiplies the count by the age counters and pays them all or none (CR 702.24a). The view ships `pay_cards` (action, count, options; a discard's options go to the chooser only) and a derived `echo_due` marker on the permanent; the enumerator offers each way to pay plus the decline, and the bot pays when it can, giving up its least valuable cards. Two additive snapshot fields under v7; no closure routes. **Cards:** 50 echo cards (Acridian through Yavimaya Granger, including Deepcavern Imp, Rakdos Headliner and Skizzik Surger) and the cumulative upkeeps Polar Kraken, Vexing Sphinx and Phyrexian Soulgorger, all Full. Volcano Hellion's echo works, but its "amount of damage of your choice" waits on a resolution-time number prompt.
