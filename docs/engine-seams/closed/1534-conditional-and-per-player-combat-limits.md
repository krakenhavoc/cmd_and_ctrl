**Conditional and per-player combat limits** (#1534, CR 508.1c / 509.1b, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-24 (#1534)](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) Decision 47). These are the three variants #1507 left for their cards, each one field read by the existing check. **`game.AttackLimit.While`** is a condition gate: `activeAttackLimitsLocked` skips a limit whose condition is false, read live, so a condition that turns true after the declaration unmakes nothing. **`AttackLimitAttackingThis`** is a third scope that counts only attacks naming the limit's own source permanent; its controller and their other planeswalkers stay open. **`BlockRule.LimitPerDefender`** makes `blockLimitRefusalLocked` group the counted blockers by controller and judge each defending player on their own. Mirri's trigger registers the per-defender rule through `effects.EachOpponentCantBlockWithMoreThanN` into `TurnScopedBlockRules`, with no snapshot, so a creature an opponent flashes in later is bound too. "This combat" is the turn's combat until extra combats exist (#753). The enumerator, the block option generator and the #328 signal run the same checks, so they needed no change. The constructors are `effects.AsLongAs` + `ThisIsTapped` and `NoMoreThanNCanAttackThisEachCombat`. **Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist** ships `full`. **The Eternal Wanderer** ships with one caveat, on its +1: the exiled card returns at the next end step rather than its owner's, because a delayed trigger cannot wait for a third player's turn. That gap is a `DelayedTrigger` field, not a combat seam.
