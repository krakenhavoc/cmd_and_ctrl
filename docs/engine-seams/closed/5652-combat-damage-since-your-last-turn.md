**Dealt combat damage since your last turn** (#5652, CR 500.7, 510.2, 603.4) — "At the beginning of your upkeep, if you haven't been dealt combat damage since your last turn, you draw a card and you lose 1 life" asks about a span that crosses every opponent's turn, and the per-turn tally (`PlayerTurnTally.CombatDamageTaken`) is replaced as each turn begins. The answer is kept on the player: `Player.CombatDamagedSinceLastTurn` (`game/since_last_turn.go`) is set by the same damage event that counts `CombatDamageTaken`, so prevented damage and damage to a planeswalker or battle the player controls do not set it, and it is cleared as each of that player's turns ends, beside `Player.LastTurnAttacks` and with the same meaning of "your last turn": an extra turn clears it (CR 500.7), a turn not taken clears nothing, and a player who has taken no turn yet is asked of the whole game so far. Combat damage dealt to a player during their own turn is cleared with that turn: it was dealt during their last turn, not since it. The reader is `Game.DealtCombatDamageSinceLastTurnForEffect(player)`. Card side (`cards/effects/since_last_turn.go`): `AtYourUpkeepIfNoCombatDamageSinceYourLastTurn(label, effect)`, built on `CondNoCombatDamageSinceYourLastTurn{}`, which is asked as the upkeep begins and again as the ability resolves (CR 603.4) and declares `game.FootprintCondNoCombatDamageSinceLastTurn`: the condition can only become false, by combat damage, which is dealt as a turn-based action (CR 510.2) and never by an item on the stack. Snapshot: one additive field, `seats[].combatDamagedSinceLastTurn`, classified `carried`; a file written before it restores with false. **Cards:** Invasion of Fiora // Marchesa, Resolute Monarch (Full).
