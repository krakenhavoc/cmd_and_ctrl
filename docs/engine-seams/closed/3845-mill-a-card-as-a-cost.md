**Mill a card as a cost** (#3845, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) amendment 2026-10-15, Decisions 65-66, CR 701.17a / 701.17b) — "Mill N cards" as an activation cost. The Warring Triad's "{T}, Mill a card: Target player adds one mana of any color" and Millikin's "{T}, Mill a card: Add {C}" had no cost to be built from. `AbilityCost.Mill` and `ManaAbilityShape.MillCost` (`effects.MillCardsCost(n)`, `ManaAbilityCost.Mill`) are one count with nothing to choose, validated with the other components and paid last (CR 601.2h) through the ordinary mill, so Rest in Peace and Bruvac the Grandiloquent apply and "whenever you mill" payoffs see each card. A library of fewer than N cards can't pay it (CR 701.17b, CR 118.3): the activation is refused with nothing paid, the enumerator emits no move, the view greys the row through `cant_activate`, and the auto-tapper never plans a mana ability that mills. The wire carries `mill_cost` on activated and mana rows. **Cards:** The Warring Triad (a targeted ability, so it uses the stack, CR 605.1a; the target player picks the colour and the mana lands in their pool) and Millikin (its condition-and-rider workaround is gone), both Full. The Triad's layer-cache note was stale and is corrected: a card reaching a graveyard from outside the battlefield already bumps the layer version.
