**Ignoring an effect by sacrificing a permanent** (#1986, CR 116.2d, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) amendment of 2026-10-23) — Damping Engine's "That player may sacrifice a permanent of their choice for that player to ignore this effect until end of turn" had both of its gates (`CastRestriction`, and `LandPlayRestriction` since #1895) and no escape. The escape is a CR 116.2d special action, and it is written in the two gates and nowhere else. **The declaration:** `Spec.IgnorableEffect` (`game.IgnorableEffect{Label, Applies}`) names the ability that can be ignored and whom it bears on, and the restrictions that are that ability carry the new `Ignorable: true`; `effects.Register` refuses the one without the other. **The record:** a `ModIgnoreEffect` `ScopedEffect` naming the player and the permanent as an object (id and epoch, CR 400.7), until end of turn, swept at cleanup; `effectIgnoredLocked` is its one reader, asked by `CastGateLocked` and `LandPlayGateLocked` for each `Ignorable` restriction before `Forbids`, so the cast, the land play, the enumerator and the view cannot disagree. **The action:** `special_action {kind: "ignore_effect", card_id, sacrifice_id}`, the seventh special-action kind, taken by the player the effect bears on (not the permanent's controller) at any priority, under split second, with no mana and no stack. The cost is a real sacrifice (`payCostSacrificesLocked`) of a permanent of the player's own choice, so it is named on the wire and never picked for a human. `Game.IgnoreEffectOffersLocked(player)` is the one accessor the engine, the enumerator and the view ask; `PlayerView.ignorable_effects` carries the rows to the seat's own frame, and `IgnoreEffects.svelte` on the client's own panel opens the sacrifice picker over them. The bot's enumerator offers one move per source and the heuristic prices it only for a card in hand it could play this turn. **Cards:** Damping Engine (Full).
