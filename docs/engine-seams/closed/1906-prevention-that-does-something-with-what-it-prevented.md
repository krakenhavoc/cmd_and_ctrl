**Prevention that does something with what it prevented** (#1906, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §8, owner decision 2, Delivery PR 8) — a prevention static declares its CR 615.5 additional effect as `ReplacementEffect.Then` (a registered body) and the unit it runs once per as `ThenPer`: per recipient for "if damage would be dealt to X" (the Phantoms: blocked by three, one counter comes off), per source for "if a source would deal damage to X" (Nine Lives: three attackers, three counters). Every apply-loop path fires `Replace` through one helper that measures the damage event before and after and owes the difference to the shared follow-up queue, keyed on the static's object (CR 400.7), its slot and the unit, once per damage instance; under damage that can't be prevented the additional effect is still owed, with nothing prevented (CR 615.12). A follow-up is handed both "the damage prevented this way" and "that damage", because the rulings read them differently under CR 615.12 (Polukranos still removes that many counters; Phyrexian Hydra puts on none). Register refuses `Then` without `Prevention` or `ThenPer`, and a census test fails a prevention static whose `Replace` does more than change the event. The scoped `preventDamage` and `preventCombatDamage` shields read `Then` too, `Mod.To` names the object a follow-up deals its damage to, and every prevention of one shield joins one end-step trigger. **Cards** (32): Phantom Centaur, Phantom Flock, Phantom Nantuko, Phantom Nishoba, Phantom Nomad, Phantom Tiger, Phantom Wurm, Oathsworn Knight, Unbreathing Horde, Undergrowth Champion, Polukranos, Unchained, Ugin's Conjurant, Magma Pummeler, Anti-Venom, Horrifying Healer, Panther Habit, Stormwild Capridor, Phyrexian Hydra, Ironscale Hydra, Nine Lives, Gloom Surgeon, Angel of Suffering, Swans of Bryn Argoll, Phyrexian Vindicator, The Mindskinner, Test of Faith, Temper, Sacred Boon, Scars of the Veteran, Acolyte's Reward, Vengeful Archon and Inkshield (Full), and Immortal Coil (Caveats: which graveyard cards to exile is asked after the damage's spell or ability finishes, #2040). **Moved to other rows:** Hostility, Purity and Vigor (#2038), Jared Carthalion (#2039), Khârn the Betrayer (#2040), Weeping Angel (#2041), Bloatfly Swarm (#2042), Protean Hydra (#1841) and Candles' Glow (splice, #1989).
