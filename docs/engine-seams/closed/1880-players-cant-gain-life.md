**Players can't gain life** (#1880, [ADR 0107](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md) §5 decision 6, owner decision 5, PR 6) — CR 119.7 is a rule-modifying effect (CR 613.11) read at one gate, `playerCantGainLifeLocked`, which every life gain asks BEFORE the CR 614 life window opens: a forbidden gain does not happen, no "if you would gain life" replacement sees it (CR 119.10), and no `EventChangeLife` fires for a "whenever you gain life" trigger. It has three sources: **battlefield statics** (`Spec.CantGainLife`, a `game.CantGainLifeStatic` for "players", "your opponents" or "enchanted player", read live and keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey`); **turn grants** (`ModCantGainLife`, every player or the record controller's opponents, ended at cleanup, CR 514.2); and **the rest of the game** (the same kind naming one player, with an indefinite duration, CR 611.2a). Lifelink damage is still dealt; only its gain is stopped (CR 702.15b). An exchange of life totals that would raise a forbidden total does not happen at all, nor one involving a locked total (CR 119.7, 119.8). "Gains no life instead" (Sulfuric Vortex, Flames of the Blood Hand) is a replacement, not this gate: a catalog replacement, or the new `ModGainNoLife` record. The player view carries `cant_gain_life`, shown on the seat as a NO GAIN badge. **Cards:** Skullcrack, Call In a Professional, Sunspine Lynx, Archfiend of Despair, Atarka's Command, Erebos, God of the Dead, Giant Cindermaw, Grievous Wound, Gríma Wormtongue, Havoc Festival, Knight of Dusk's Shadow, Quakebringer, Rampaging Ferocidon, Stigma Lasher, The Lord of Pain, Tibalt, Rakish Instigator and Sulfuric Vortex (Full); Leyline of Punishment (Caveats: no opening-hand start), Forsaken Wastes (Caveats: no world rule), Roiling Vortex (Caveats: needs strict mana for its no-mana trigger) and Screaming Nemesis (its can't-gain-life caveat dropped; its simultaneous-damage caveat stays). **Moved to other rows:** Witch Hunt (random targets, #1815), Everlasting Torment (#1889).
