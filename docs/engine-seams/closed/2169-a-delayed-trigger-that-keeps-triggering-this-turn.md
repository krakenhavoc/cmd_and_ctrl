**A delayed trigger that keeps triggering this turn** (#2169, CR 603.7a/b, CR 605.1b): an event-conditioned `DelayedTrigger` fired on its first match and was removed, so "whenever a creature you control deals combat damage to that player this turn" (Great Train Heist) had no shape. **`DelayedTrigger.Repeats`** keeps it queued after it fires; the end-of-turn duration sweep removes it (`clearExpiredDelayedTriggersLocked`, CR 514.2), the same one that ends a Full Throttle. It is not tied to its source, a match a suppressor stops neither fires nor ends it, and `dispatchEventDelayedTriggerLocked` now asks the trigger doublers like `harvestMatchLocked` does, for every event-conditioned delayed trigger. The additive snapshot keys are `repeats`, `manaTapSubtype` and `manaAdds`. The CR 605.1b twin is `ManaTapSubtype` / `ManaAdds` on the same record: `fireManaTriggersLocked` reads it beside the permanents' mana triggers and adds the mana at once, with no stack item (Bubbling Muck, High Tide). `effects.DelayedOnEvent{Repeats: true}` and `effects.TurnManaTrigger` are the catalog sides. Shipped on Great Train Heist, Bubbling Muck, High Tide, Glimpse of Nature and Benefactor's Draught. Still open: Dalkovan Encampment keeps its own trigger on the land, and Vizkopa Guildmage's "whenever you gain life this turn" is not built.
