**If a spell or ability you control would counter a spell, instead …** (#4056, [ADR 0113](decisions/0113-small-seams-for-the-s58-deck-requests.md) amendment 2026-10-19, CR 614.1a, CR 701.6a, CR 616.1) — a spell about to be countered by a spell or ability is a replaceable event of its own, `RepEventCounterSpell`, opened by the three effect counter verbs after the "can't be countered" gate and before the spell leaves the stack, so a hard counter, a declined counter-unless-pays, a ward and the destination counters (Hinder) all pass through it. It names who is countering (`CounterBy`, the resolving item's controller), watches `EventCounterSpell`, and can pause on a CR 616.1 ordering prompt put to the countered spell's controller; the cancel, the resume and an abandoned prompt all finish in `finishCounterSpellLocked`. A replacement answers with `Game.ExileCounteredSpellInsteadForEffect`: the spell is exiled without being countered (no `EventCounterSpell`, so nothing keyed to "countered" sees it), and the countering player may cast it for free (`Game.FreeCastFromExilePermission`: `{0}`, cast-only, X is 0; made during the countering spell's resolution through the `resolution_cast` prompt since #4286, a decline leaving the card in exile). A spell that can't be countered never opens the window, a countered ability is not covered, and the sandbox's counter action opens none. `effects.ExileSpellsYouWouldCounterInstead` is the card-side replacement. No snapshot change. **Cards:** Guile (Full).
