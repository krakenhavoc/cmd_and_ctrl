**Player protection and player hexproof** (#1197, CR 702.16i / CR 702.11d, [ADR 0072 amendment 2026-09-22](decisions/0072-protection.md)) — the row said it in one sentence: `game.Player` carried no ability slice, so "you have protection from everything" had nowhere to live and no consumer. **The grammar did not change.** A player's protection is the same token a permanent's is, parsed by the same closed-grammar reader in `protection.go` and matched against the same `Characteristic` snapshot; what is new is a second kind of thing that can HAVE a quality. The slot is **two halves, and only one is stored**: a permanent's printed static ("You have hexproof" — Leyline of Sanctity, Aegis of the Gods) is `effects.Spec.PlayerKeywords`, read off the battlefield through `game.CatalogPlayerKeywords` on every query and written nowhere, so two Leylines compose and one of them leaving cannot revoke the other's grant — the argument `CatalogNoMaxHandSize` (#338) and `land_drops.go` both make at length; a resolved spell's grant ("you gain protection from everything until your next turn" — Teferi's Protection, The One Ring) is `Player.Statics`, modelled on `Player.CastPermissions` to the letter (plain data with no closure, a CR 611.2 `Duration`, swept through the same `durationExpiredLocked`, cloned by value, mirrored into the snapshot, classified `carried`), because its source is in a graveyard a moment after it resolves. **Not a `ScopedStatic`**, and the reason is the layer engine: that registry is adapted into a `ContinuousEffect` whose `Apply` takes a `*Characteristic` and a `*Card`, and a player is neither — the same argument `Spec.NoMaxHandSize` and `Spec.CostModifiers` already make for themselves. **Three consumers, one predicate each, and every one of them was a branch that already existed and returned early**: targeting (`canPlayerBeTargetedByLocked` at both `TargetPlayer` sites in `targets.go`, on the existing `targeting` switch, so a cost payment that names a player is still not targeting); damage (the CR 702.16e built-in's non-permanent branch in `builtin_replacements.go`, which used to say "player protection has no home yet" — one branch covers combat AND noncombat, because every damage path routes through `damageThroughReplacementsLocked`); attachment (`attachmentLegalLocked`'s `TargetPlayer` host, for an "enchant player" Aura — Curse of Opulence is the card that proves that branch exists). The bot's enumerator and the client's `legal_targets` follow **by construction**: both read `legalTargetsLocked`, so neither learns the rule and neither can disagree with it. Hexproof keeps CR 702.11b's asymmetry verbatim — an OPPONENT's spell is refused, your own still reaches you, which Leyline of Sanctity needs to be playable at all; shroud on a player is absent because no card prints it. `PlayerView.keywords` puts a seat's tokens on the wire, public like emblems and effective like `max_hand_size`. **4 cards:** Leyline of Sanctity (`caveats` — the opening-hand clause is the Gemstone Caverns gap), Aegis of the Gods (`full`), Teferi's Protection (`caveats` — see below) and The One Ring, which had carried a caveat naming this exact gap since S40 and is now `full`, its "if you cast it" read off the event log the way Zacama and Tiamat read theirs. **Still open, and each a seam of its own rather than this one**: **phasing** (CR 702.26 — was "not modelled anywhere in this engine", Teferi's Protection's larger half and its first caveat; **closed by #1199 / [ADR 0084](decisions/0084-phasing.md)**, row above); **"your life total can't change"** (**closed by #1200 / [ADR 0085](decisions/0085-life-total-cant-change.md)**, row at the top of this section — it became a third payload on this row's own `Player.Statics`, not the duration on `TurnScopedReplacements` the issue proposed, and Teferi's Protection is `full`); **protection on a player FROM a player** (reachable, since the quality kind and the reader are shared, but no catalogued card prints it); and the **client badge** for a protected or hexproof seat, which is a client-only change now that the wire carries it (#1201).
