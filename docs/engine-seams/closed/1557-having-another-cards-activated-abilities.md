**Having another card's activated abilities** (#1557, [ADR 0155](decisions/0155-having-another-objects-activated-abilities.md)) — "has all activated abilities of …" is a layer-6 borrow: a static declares `StaticAbility.Borrows` (`effects.HasActivatedAbilitiesOf` / `GrantActivatedAbilitiesOf`), and the borrow step, last in the layer-6 bucket and ordered among borrows (CR 613.8), writes one `Characteristic.BorrowedAbilities` entry per lent row, named by the lender's catalog row and never composed into the ability key, so only activated and mana abilities are lent. The ability readers return them as a fourth half with `borrow:` refs; the stack item names the lender's catalog row, so it resolves and restores without the lender (CR 113.7a); the wire carries `borrowed_from` and CR 201.5b's rewritten label, and the client groups lent rows under "From <lender>". Lenders: `BattlefieldLenders`, `GraveyardLenders`, `ExiledWithLenders` (on the `Card.ExiledWith` stamp a resolving ability's linked exile now writes), `ExileLenders`, `CraftedWithLenders`, `TopOfLibraryLender` (with `DependsOnLibraryTop`'s watch on every library's top card) and `ChosenPermanentLender` (`Card.ChosenPermanent`, CR 614.12). A resolving ability's borrow is the `borrowAbilities` ScopedEffect mod, its rows captured as data at resolution (CR 608.2h, 611.2c). The riders: any-colour spend over one borrow's rows (`AbilityOrigin.LentByBorrow`), one colour of mana as any colour (`AnyColorSpendStatic.From`, and `BorrowedAbility.SpendFrom` for a resolved borrow's own), "each only once each turn", and "can't be activated" gates on the lender. A lender in a hand or a library is named only to the seats that may see it there. **Cards (26):** Marvin, Murderous Mimic; Experiment Kraj; Robaran Mercenaries; Drana and Linvala; Manascape Refractor; Sharkey, Tyrant of the Shire; Necrotic Ooze; Mirran Safehouse; Thranduil, the Elvenking; Trazyn the Infinite; Dark Impostor; Patchwork Crawler; Myr Welder; Hazel's Brewmaster; Agatha's Soul Cauldron; Steward of the Harvest; Territory Forge; Rex, Cyber-Hound; Mairsil, the Pretender; The Enigma Jewel // Locus of Enlightenment; Quicksilver Elemental; Grell Philosopher; Havengul Lich; Conspicuous Snoop; Skill Borrower; Scheming Fence. **Not covered:** an instance-carried row (a legacy token's closure) is lent only through its catalog row; a resolved borrow from an uncatalogued card lends nothing and shows no "not automated" line; Sharkey's "mana of any type" still leaves a colourless {C} alone (its caveat).
