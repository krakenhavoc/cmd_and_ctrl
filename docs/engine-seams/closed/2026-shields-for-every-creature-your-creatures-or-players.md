**Shields for every creature, your creatures, or players** (#2045, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §7, amendment of 2026-10-06) — a `preventFromSource` shield can protect a set: every player, or permanents that match every one of a list of queries ("creatures", "artifact creatures", "Dogs"), optionally only those you control. The set is read as each damage event would be dealt, never locked in as the shield resolves (CR 615.1; a prevention effect doesn't modify characteristics, so CR 611.2c doesn't fix its set): a creature that enters later is protected, and one you lose control of is not. It composes with the source fields, so "black and/or red sources … to creatures you control" and "damage that creatures would deal to players" are one shield each. A charged shield refuses a set, since CR 615.11 makes "the next N damage … to each creature" one shield per creature. Everything is plain data: an older binary refuses a restore point carrying it, and an unknown key inside the set is refused too. **Cards** (12): Blinding Fog, Chameleon Blur, Commencement of Festivities, Crystal Fragments // Summon: Alexander, Defend the Hearth, Divine Light, Ethersworn Shieldmage, Forfend, Loyal Unicorn, Pack Leader, Sivvi's Ruse (with the Nemesis free-spell condition) and Surge of Salvation (Full).
