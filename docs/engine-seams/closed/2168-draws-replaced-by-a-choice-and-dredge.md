title: "Draws replaced by a choice, and dredge"
date: 2026-10-05
issues: [2168, 2127]
---
**Draws replaced by a choice, and dredge** (#2168, #2127): a CR 614 replacement on a draw can now be an effect that stops to ask. `ReplacementEffect.DrawInstead` (`game/draw_instead.go`) names a body registered with `game.RegisterDrawInstead`; applying the effect cancels the draw, and once the window has settled the body runs, may queue a prompt, and calls `done` when it finishes. `drawRunLocked` performs a multi-card draw one individual draw at a time, each through its own window (CR 121.2), and a draw that pauses holds the rest of its instruction behind the prompt (CR 121.6b, `ReplacementEvent.drawTail`, a plain int copied by value into the resume frame, so undo and a snapshot taken mid-pause replay the same remaining count). A draw paused on a CR 616 ordering prompt or a "may" prompt now pays its tail too, which it did not before. A mandatory substitute (Underrealm Lich, Forbidden Crypt) replaces every draw of a doubled event; an optional one (dredge) replaces one and the rest are ordinary draws. **Dredge** (CR 702.52) is `effects.Dredge(n)`: an optional replacement declared on the card in the graveyard (`ReplacementEffect.FromGraveyard`, gathered from the drawing player's graveyard), applicable only with at least N cards in the library, asked through the ordinary yes/no prompt, doing the mill and the return together. Several dredge cards in one graveyard are asked in turn so the player takes one or none. Nine cards ship: Life from the Loam, Golgari Grave-Troll, Stinkweed Imp, Golgari Thug, Shambling Shell, Nightmare Void, Underrealm Lich, Forbidden Crypt and The Necrobloom (its granted dredge 2, which lost its caveat). Declared simplification: dredge is offered on each draw as it begins, not on the extra draws a doubler adds to it. Still open: the bot heuristic answers a dredge offer with its default "yes" for a "may" prompt, so a bot with a dredge card in its graveyard will dredge every draw.
