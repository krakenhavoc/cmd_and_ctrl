**A divided amount read off the board, and "up to" division** (#1657, CR 601.2d, [ADR 0065 amendment 2026-09-28 (b)](decisions/0065-modal-and-multi-target-clauses.md)). `game.DivideSpec` gains `AmountKey`, a registered amount rule (`game.RegisterDivideAmount`, declared with `effects.DivideBy(rule)`), and `UpTo`, "distribute UP TO that many" (`effects.UpTo(…)`). The rule is a key, not a func, so the ADR 0041 closure ratchet gains no route; it reads the announcing player, the source, a trigger's last-known source (CR 603.10) and the claimed alternative cost, and answers in the existing data shapes (`Total`, or `FromX`), so the announced X still applies and the client never sees the rule. `bindDivideAmountsLocked` reads it ONCE, at announce — the cast (with the claimed cost), the activation, and the moment a trigger's `pick_target` walk opens — into the steps' clause copies, so the prompt, its wire projection, the enumerator and `settleDistribution` read one fixed number and a land or a life gain after that changes nothing (Ureni's and Lathiel's rulings). `UpTo` relaxes the gate's sum to "at most the amount"; each chosen target still gets at least 1. The view resolves the rule for the viewer and for each `alternative_costs[]` entry, `divide` gains `up_to`, and `activated_abilities[].legal_targets` now carries `divide` for a single-clause divided ability (Mogg Mob had shipped without it). The client's divide modal and banner say "up to", and the X prompt opens for an alternative cost with its own `{X}` (Avacyn's Judgment's madness `{X}{R}`). **Cards:** Lathiel, the Bounteous Dawn is now `full`; Ureni, the Song Unending, Orca, Siege Demon and Avacyn's Judgment are new. **Still open:** Polukranos, World Eater, which needs monstrosity (CR 701.37) and a "becomes monstrous" event before its divided X can be read; divided prevention (Remedy).
