**"Can't attack you this turn", and a cast ban on players who attacked you** (#2719, [ADR 0063 amendment 2026-10-10](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md), [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-10-10](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md), CR 508.1c / 611.2a / 514.2 / 101.2 / 604.2) — `Game.GrantCantAttackPlayerThisTurnForEffect(attacker, protected, scope, label, source)` writes the same `PlayerStatic` `CantAttack` payload as the next-turn grant, with the window opening at the restricted player's current turn and an "until end of turn" duration. `CantAttackGrant.Scope` (`game.CantAttackScope{PlayerOnly, PlayerExempt, PlaneswalkersOnly, Subtype}`) narrows what of the protected player's is covered: "you or planeswalkers you control" (a battle you control stays open), "you" alone, or "Jaces you control"; the zero scope is the old "you or permanents you control", and the refusal sentence follows the scope. `AttackRecord.PlaneswalkerController` freezes who controlled an attacked planeswalker at the declaration, and `Game.AttackedYouOrYourPlaneswalkerThisTurn` reads it, so `effects.OpponentsWhoAttackedYouCantCast` is a battlefield `CastRestriction` that ends when its permanent leaves. Additive snapshot data (`seats[].statics[].cantAttack.scope`, `turnTally.attacks[].planeswalkerController`). **Cards:** Unstable Glyphbridge // Sandswirl Wanderglyph and Web of Inertia (Full); Jace, Multiverse Architect loses its combat-tax caveat.
