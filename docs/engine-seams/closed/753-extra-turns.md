**Extra turns** (#753, [ADR 0059](decisions/0059-turn-machinery.md) Decision 5 and its 2026-09-30 amendment): "take an extra turn after this one" (CR 500.7) is `effects.TakeExtraTurn`, over the one engine door `Game.TakeExtraTurnsForEffect`. The queue is a stack on `Game.ExtraTurns` popped by the single rotation seam, so the most recently created turn is taken first, normal rotation resumes from `Turn.OrderSeat`, `Turn.Round` never moves on an extra turn, and every per-turn reset runs (a planeswalker activates again, the land drop comes back). A queued turn of a player who has left is dropped as it would begin and still counts toward `TurnsBegun` (CR 800.4k / 800.4m). `DelayedTrigger.OnExtraTurn` binds "at the beginning of that turn's end step" to the turn, and a bound trigger whose turn is dropped or ends early is swept. The queue and the binding clone and snapshot as plain data; the wire has `TurnView.extra` / `extra_turns` and an `extra_turn` log line, and the board marks the turn. Time Warp, Time Stretch, Temporal Manipulation, Capture of Jingzhou, Temporal Mastery, Magistrate's Scepter, Final Fortune, Last Chance and Warrior's Oath joined the catalog, and Teferi, Master of Time's −10 and Avatar Kuruk's exhaust ability are wired. Still open: skipping a turn (CR 614.10 — Trouble in Pairs, Ugin's Nexus, Savor the Moment), and the extra combat and main phases of ADR 0059 Decision 3-4.
