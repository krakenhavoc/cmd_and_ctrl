**The Ring tempts you** (#2076, CR 701.54, [ADR 0114](decisions/0114-the-ring-tempts-you.md)) — one keyword action, `game.RingTemptsForEffect`: the Ring emblem at the first temptation (`emblem:the-ring`), its count up by one, a Ring-bearer chosen (automatically with one creature, through the `ring_bearer` prompt with two or more), then `EventRingTempted`, even with no creature to choose. `Card.RingBearer` has one write site and is cleared by CR 400.7 and by a change of control. The emblem's four abilities are each switched on by `game.RingTempted(n)`: legendary and unblockable by greater power, the loot on attack, the end-of-combat sacrifice of a blocker, and "each opponent loses 3 life" on combat damage to a player. The table shows a Ring chip with its level and a marker on the Ring-bearer. Card side: `effects.TheRingTemptsYou{Then}`, `WheneverTheRingTemptsYou`, `WheneverYouChooseARingBearer`, `IfYouChoseAnotherRingBearer`, `ringTemptsYouNext` (the tempt after a destruction, bounce, damage or scry that can pause) and `AtYourEndStepIfACreatureDied`. **Cards:** 46 of the 56 that mention the Ring: five with Sauron's deck (PR 3) and 41 in PR 5's three pools. Three carry caveats: Sauron, Lord of the Rings (#2085), Boromir, Warden of the Tower (strict mana) and Sauron, the Necromancer (its Wraith always joins Sauron's attack). **Still open, each on another seam's row:** Breaking of the Fellowship (#1943), Frodo, Adventurous Hobbit (partner with, #2142), Galadriel, Elven-Queen (voting, #2143), Frodo, Sauron's Bane (setting creature types, #2144), Lord of the Nazgûl (protection from Ring-bearers, #2145), Rangers of Ithilien (a target relative to its source, #2146), Sauron's Ransom (face-down piles, #2147), Shortcut to Mushrooms (a permanent you controlled left the battlefield, #2148), Witch-king of Angmar (creatures that dealt combat damage to you, #2149) and Aragorn, Company Leader (counters put on together by kind, #2150).
