**The cumulative-upkeep payments the prompt could not take** (#6189, CR 702.24a, 702.24b, 106.6, 118.3) — seven `game.PayAction` kinds, a mana cost paid with life, and mana that may pay nothing else. The kinds: `counter_opponent_creature` (a counter of `PayAction.CounterKind` on a creature an OPPONENT controls), `counters_on_source` (`Count` counters on the asking permanent, `PayAction.OnSource`, naming nothing), `opponent_gains_life` (`Amount` life to an opponent the payer names), `opponent_creates_token` (one token of `PayAction.TokenKey`, created by the named opponent — the catalog files the row with `game.RegisterPayActionToken`, as it files a conjurable), `gain_control_land` (a permanent the payer does NOT control, taken with an indefinite control effect), `draw` and `exile_library_top`. Two of them pick PLAYERS (`PayAction.PicksPlayers`, wire `pay_cards.picks: "players"`), and the choosing ones are repeatable (`PayAction.Repeats`, wire `pay_cards.repeats`): CR 702.24a makes each choice separately for each age counter, so one creature may take two counters and one opponent may gain life twice. "Cumulative upkeep {W} or {U}" needed nothing new — it is one hybrid symbol per age counter (`{W/U}` repeated), which is the same rule — and "Pay {B} and 1 life" is the mana cost with `UpkeepPayment.Life`, both halves multiplied by the age count. Snowfall's and Adarkar Unicorn's "Spend this mana only to pay cumulative upkeep costs" is `ManaRestrictCumulativeUpkeep` plus `SpendPurposeCumulativeUpkeep`, which the cumulative-upkeep prompt alone names (`PendingChoice.PaySpendPurpose`): every other pay-unless still refuses restricted mana (#352). Snapshot: `payActionClause.Amount / CounterKind / TokenKey / PicksPlayers / Repeats / OnSource` and `pendingChoices[].paySpendPurpose`, additive in v9. **Cards:** Jötun Owl Keeper, Krovikan Whispers, Arctic Nishoba, Earthen Goo, Sheltering Ancient, Wall of Shards, Herald of Leshrac, Aboroth, Psychic Vortex, Varchild's War-Riders, Thought Lash, Infernal Darkness, Snowfall and Adarkar Unicorn wait for their OLD batches.
