**Conditional blocking restrictions** (#750, CR 509.1b / 702.111b, [ADR 0045 addendum](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) Decisions 11–13 and its amendment of 2026-09-24, Decisions 40–42) — the card half. The engine half (slot 4 of `Game.BlockPairRefusalLocked`, `blockerBoundsLocked`, `DeclareBlockers` refusing an illegal count, `CatalogBlockRules` and `Game.TurnScopedBlockRules`) had no way for a card to declare a rule. `effects.Spec.BlockRules` and a token template's `BlockRules` slot now feed `CardDef.BlockRules`, which carddef.go publishes through `CatalogBlockRules`, keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey` so a permanent that loses its abilities imposes nothing. `effects/block_rules.go` pairs a SCOPE (`OnSelf`, `OnAttached`, `ControlledBySourceController`, `OnMatching`, `PowerLessThanSource`) with a rule (`CantBeBlockedExceptBy`, `CantBeBlockedBy`, `CantBeBlockedWhile`, `CantBlockAttackers`, `CantBlockOrBeBlockedBy`, `MaxBlockers`, `MinBlockers`), and `BlockRuleUntilEOT` registers any of them for a snapshotted set until end of turn. **Prowler's Helm**, **Legolas Greenleaf**, **Champion of Lambholt**, **Gingerbrute**, **Thieves' Tools**, **Vorrac Battlehorns** and **Rampaging Ceratops** ship `full`; **Departed Deckhand** and **Canopy Cover** lose their block-rule caveats and ship `full`; **Avatar Kuruk**'s Spirit token carries "can't block or be blocked by non-Spirit creatures" and the card keeps only its extra-turn caveat (#753). Still open, split out as #1507: `BlockRule.Limit` and the attack-side twin (Silent Arbiter, Crawlspace).
