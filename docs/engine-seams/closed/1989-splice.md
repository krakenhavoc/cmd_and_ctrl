**Splice onto Arcane: another card's text added to the spell** (#1989, CR 702.47, [ADR 0202](../../decisions/0202-splice.md)) — a card declares `Spec.Splice` (`effects.SpliceOntoArcane(cost)`, `SpliceOntoInstantOrSorcery(cost)`), and its own `Targets` and `OnResolve` are the text a spell gains.
- **The cast.** `cast_spell.splices` names the revealed hand cards in the order their effects happen (CR 702.47b), checked with the rest of CR 601.2b: in the caster's hand, not the spell, named once, and the spell has the quality. Each spliced text's targets ride the flat `targets` list with `splice` naming the text, judged against the card's own clause from the spell (CR 702.47c-d); a text whose required target can't be chosen can't be spliced. The splice costs' mana joins the one pricer at CR 601.2f. The cards are revealed and stay in the hand.
- **Resolution.** `StackItem.Spliced` carries the texts. The main text runs, then each spliced text against a view of the item holding its own targets and its source still the spell, waiting behind a prompt an earlier instruction opened (`modeWalk.splices`). The spell is countered by the rules only when every target of every text is illegal; a text whose own targets are all gone does nothing. A CR 707.10 copy copies the texts; undo and snapshots carry them (additive in v9).
- **View and client.** The hand card's `splice_offers` (per viewer) lists what may be spliced onto it, with each text's clause; the cast modal offers them as toggles beside the optional costs and adds their clauses to the target walk. The stack item shows `spliced`. Bots never splice.
- **Cards** (21, all Full): Overblaze, Glacial Ray, Desperate Ritual, Goryo's Vengeance, Candles' Glow, Kodama's Might, Wear Away, Consuming Vortex, Spiritual Visit, Vital Surge, Dampen Thought, Soulless Revival, Lifted by Clouds, Hideous Laughter, Strange Inversion, Psychic Puppetry, Blessed Breath, Into the Fray, Evermind, Everdream and Splicer's Skill. A splice cost that isn't mana (Veil of Secrecy, Horobi's Whisper, Roar of Jukai, Hundred-Talon Strike, Torrent of Stone) waits on #4055.
