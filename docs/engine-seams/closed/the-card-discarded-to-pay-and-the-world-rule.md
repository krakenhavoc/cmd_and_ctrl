**The card discarded to pay, and the world rule** (#1862, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §8, PR 7) — the activated-ability payer now records every card a discard component moved on `PaidCost.Discarded` (cycling's own card first), as the spell path does, and an effect reads it with `Context.DiscardedCard()` / `DiscardedManaValue()` (CR 400.7j). `copiedPaidCost` carries `Discarded`, `Exiled`, `Sacrificed` and `ReturnedAttacking`, so a CR 707.10 copy of an ability uses the objects the original paid with. The world rule (CR 704.5k) is a state-based action read off `Card.EntryOrdinal`, a game counter taken once per move onto the battlefield and shared by every card of one simultaneous entry: of two or more world permanents, all but the most recent entrant go to their owners' graveyards, and all of them on a tie. `EntryOrdinal` is an additive snapshot field; an older file orders its world permanents by entry stamp. **Cards:** Land's Edge, Hisoka, Minamo Sensei, Mercurial Chemister, Moria Scavenger, Necromancer's Stockpile, Slumbering Tora, Volrath the Fallen, and the world enchantments Living Plane, Gravity Sphere, Mystic Decree, Serra Aviary, Chaosphere, Nether Void, In the Eye of Chaos, Bazaar of Wonders, Eye of Singularity, Storm World, Pillar Tombs of Aku and Teferi's Realm (Full); Concordant Crossroads, Caverns of Despair and Forsaken Wastes lose their world-rule caveat. **Moved to other rows:** ten older world enchantments, each one shape short (#1997, `world-enchantment-leftovers`).
