**The energy payments and the may-pay-X prompts are keyed** (#4289, follow-up to #4264, amendments of 2026-10-24 to [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md) and [ADR 0041](decisions/0041-game-persistence.md)) — a table waiting on one is a restore point. `MayPayEnergy`, `PayEnergyUnless` and `MayPayX` were the last `pay_unless` family on the closure form. **Energy.** Energy is a counter on the player (CR 107.14: paying {E} removes one), so the payment was always plain data (`PayAction{Kind: PayActionEnergy, Count: n}`, `PendingChoice.PayActionClause`); only the consequence was a closure. `game.EnergyPayment` gains `Then` (registered branches), `Carry`, `Asked` and `MayPay`, and `QueueEnergyPaymentForEffect` routes a keyed payment through `QueuePayUnlessPromptKeyedForEffect` with the step it holds (`InThisStep`, `OwedInStep`). `MayPayEnergy{Then}` and `PayEnergyUnless{Else}` take a `game.PayUnlessElse` and no longer take a closure; the helpers (`whenThisAttacksMayPayEnergy`, `mayPayEnergyThen`, `anotherAttackerMayPayEnergy`) take the handle, built by `energyPaid(key, effect)` (the paid half) or `energyDeclined(key, effect)` (the decline), and the branch runs `effect(ctx.Game, ctx.Item)` against the asking item rebuilt from the prompt. **Shared keys:** the Thriving cycle and Scrapper Champion share `pay-unless/energy-plus-one-counter-on-this`, the five Aether Chasers `pay-unless/energy-create-servo`, Lathnu Hellion, Liberty Prime and Static Prison `pay-unless/energy-sacrifice-this`, and Robobrain War Mind reuses `pay-unless/draw-a-card`; every other card has its own. **May-pay-X.** The first prompt of "you may pay {X}{R}" is a `pay_amount` with no resource (CR 608.2d: X is announced as the effect is applied). It now holds no frame: `PendingChoice.MayPayX` (`game.MayPayXClause`: the printed cost or symbol, the label, what X buys and whether the payment holds the step) tells the answer how to ask the next question, `PickThen` names the card's branches, `PayAmount` is written to the restore point (it was `dropped`; now `carried`) and `Asked` and `OptionCarry` ride it. Answering it settles the cost with X and queues a keyed may-pay that carries X as data (`PendingChoice.PickNumber`, handed to the branch as `PayUnlessAnswer.Number`); an empty cost ({X} at X = 0, CR 118.5) runs the paid branch at once. The one frame is `chooseValueFrameOf`, read by the answer, by the departure rule (CR 800.4f: a chooser who left pays nothing and X settles at 0) and by the drop default, so a keyed prompt is answered, auto-answered and dropped as a framed one is. No new registry: the branches are the pay-unless registry's, so the ledger gains 29 `payunless` lines and a restore refuses a `pay_amount` naming a key this binary lacks. **Callers keyed:** all 8 direct (Assaultron Dominator, Behemoth of Vault 0, Blaster Hulk, Confiscation Coup, Electrozoa, Jolted Awake, Lightning Runner, Rush of Inspiration), the 19 `whenThisAttacksMayPayEnergy` cards and the three `anotherAttackerMayPayEnergy` ones, the 6 `mayPayEnergyThen` cards (Cyclops Superconductor, Glint-Sleeve Siphoner, Guide of Souls, Riddle Gate Gargoyle, Sentry Bot, T-45 Power Armor), the 3 `sacrificeThisUnlessYouPayEnergy` cards, and all three `MayPayX` cards (Flameblast Dragon, Leyline Tyrant, Tilonalli's Summoner). **Snapshot:** `pendingChoices[].payAmount` (now with JSON tags; the wire view keeps its own struct), `.mayPayX` and `.pickNumber`, additive in v9 (`-update-shape`, no fixture edited). **Still a closure:** `PayEnergyAmount` (Harnessed Lightning, Galvanic Discharge and the other "any amount of {E}" cards, and the assist helper's prompt, which share the framed `pay_amount`), `ChooseNumber` and the split prompt of `CreateTokensAttackingYourChoice` (Tilonalli's Summoner asks it after the payment), `MayChoice`, ward's life, sacrifice and discard confirms, and the multi-seat discard runs. **Tests** (`effects/keyed_energy_restore_test.go`, each a strict restore with the prompt open): Thriving Rhino (both answers, the held step), Lathnu Hellion (paid, declined, declined after a new object), Assaultron Dominator (the attacker off the carried event), Confiscation Coup (the target off the carried item), Electrozoa, Tilonalli's Summoner (both prompts, X = 2 and X = 0, a decline and a chooser who leaves), Flameblast Dragon and Leyline Tyrant (both prompts, X carried to the damage), and an unknown key refused for an energy prompt and for the X prompt. **Back-outs:** dropping `InThisStep` from the keyed route fails `TestThrivingRhinoEnergyPromptIsARestorePoint`; dropping `Asked` fails the Assaultron, Confiscation Coup, Electrozoa, Lathnu and Thriving tests; dropping `PayAmount`, `MayPayX` or `PickNumber` from the restore half of the snapshot fails the Tilonalli, Flameblast and Leyline tests; making the restore check skip a `pay_amount` key fails `TestPayXPromptWithAnUnknownKeyIsRefused`; answering through `choice.chooseValueResume` alone fails the same three X tests.
