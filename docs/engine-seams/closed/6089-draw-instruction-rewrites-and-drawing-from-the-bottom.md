**Rewriting an opponent's multi-card draw, and drawing from the bottom** (#6089, CR 121.2, 614.1a, 616.1) — two draw seams. `ReplacementEffect.DrawInstructionEachDrawOne` is Alms Collector's "If an opponent would draw two or more cards, instead you and that player each draw a card": it is asked once of the whole draw instruction, on the synthetic event Quantum Riddler's `DrawInstructionExtra` already used (`game/draw_instruction_rewrite.go`), and turns it into one draw for the drawing player and one for the effect's controller, made one at a time in APNAP order through the amount replacements' `drawPending` frame, so each still opens its own CR 614 window, a paused draw holds the other behind it, and the instruction's "then" runs after both. The rewrite is applied before `DrawInstructionExtra` (the order that leaves the drawing player the most cards). `CardDef.DrawsFromBottom` (`Spec.DrawsFromBottom`, `game/draw_from_bottom.go`) is River Song's "You draw cards from the bottom of your library rather than the top": the draw is unchanged except for which card `actuallyDrawCardLocked` takes. The other two cards the issue named needed no new seam: Abundance is a `DrawInstead` with an option pick and the chosen-order bottom placement, and Jace, Mirror Mage reads the card it drew from `Game.DrawnThisTurn` past a count carried on its `DrawThen`, as Mad Wizard's Lair does. Snapshot: no new field. **Cards:** Alms Collector, Abundance, River Song and Jace, Mirror Mage (Full).
