**Modal and multi-target clauses** (#764, [ADR 0065](decisions/0065-modal-and-multi-target-clauses.md)): a `game.TargetSpec` **is** a target clause and hangs the rest of a multi-clause statement off it (`Rest`), built with `Clauses(first, then…)` and `Distinct(…)`; each clause carries its own predicate and count, is enforced on its own at announce (CR 601.2c) and re-checked on its own at resolution (CR 608.2b). `TargetRef` gains `Slot` / `Mode`, so `item.Targets` stays one flat list in announce order and a positional card file is unchanged; `ctx.ClauseTarget(slot)` and `ctx.ModeTargets(occurrence)` are the new reads. One `game.ModeSpec` now serves three owners — `Spec.Modes`, `TriggeredAbility.Modes` and `ActivatedAbility.Modes` — with `Repeatable` for CR 700.2d and `ModeOption.Effect` (`ModeDoing`) for a bullet's body; the `Register` panic on "targeted modes with Max > 1" is gone. A trigger's modes are chosen as the ability is put on the stack (CR 603.3c) through the new `mode_pick` pending choice, before the CR 603.3d target pick, with unfillable bullets not offered; a spell's at CR 601.2b and an activated ability's at CR 602.2b, in the announcement itself. The client walks the clauses one prompt at a time, the bot enumerates modes-outermost under `MaxExpansionPerSource`, and the stack overlay prints the chosen bullets instead of raw indexes. **9 cards:** Bite Down, Soul's Fire and Aetheric Amplifier are now Full (the first two lost their per-slot caveats, the third gained its second bullet); Resourceful Defense's second slot is a Distinct clause; Gala Greeters trades "the engine picks" for a real mode prompt and keeps a narrower caveat; Kolaghan's Command, Mystic Confluence, Sublime Epiphany and Glissa Sunslayer are new. Still open: "choose one that hasn't been chosen this turn" (a per-source tally — Gala Greeters, Monument to Endurance, Silent Hallcreeper keep a caveat), modes chosen by another player (CR 700.2e), divided damage (CR 700.2i), set-level `Validate` on a clause list (#626), ~~targeting an ABILITY on the stack (Sublime Epiphany's second bullet)~~ — **closed by #1211**, and the Epiphany ships all five bullets — and per-clause retargeting of a spell copy.
