**A flashback cost that removes X counters and sets the spell's X** (#5679; CR 107.3a, 107.3b, 601.2b, 601.2h, 702.34a, 118.3) — a cast's counter removal (`AlternativeCost.RemoveCounters`, #5390) was a fixed count only, so "Flashback—{3}{R}, Remove X loyalty counters from among planeswalkers you control. If you cast this spell this way, X can't be 0." had no shape: the number removed is the caster's to announce and is the spell's X, while the printed {X}{R} is not paid. The component now takes the activation's `CounterRemovalCost.Variable` on a cast, with `N` the floor. Such an offer is an alternative cost that includes X (`AlternativeCost.CountersDefineX`, folded into `PaymentDefinesX` beside Shining Shoal's pitch, #3175), so `CastCost.AltIncludesX` keeps CR 107.3b from locking X at 0; `validateAltCostCountersLocked` holds the payment's total to the announced `x_value` after the activation's own validator has held it to the floor, the caster's own matching permanents and what each holds. Nothing new is stored: the X is the stack item's `XValue` and the total is `PaidCost.CountersRemoved`, both already in the snapshot. The wire gains no field: the offer's counter block ships `counter_cost_variable` with `counter_cost_n` as the floor and `counter_cost_max`, `x_locked_at_zero` stays false, and the client sends the counter picker's total as `x_value` instead of asking for X. The enumerator writes one payment (`castCounterChoice`): every counter but one from each permanent, so a bot's planeswalkers survive paying, or every counter when that would be under the floor; the total is the X it announces. `effects.FlashbackRemoveXCounters(mana, kind, label, preds...)` builds the offer; `Register` (`checkAltCostRemoveCounters`) allows the variable form for one printed kind with a floor of at least 1. **Cards now Full:** Light Up the Night.
