**Search your library and/or graveyard** (#3217, duplicate #3218, [ADR 0015](decisions/0015-zone-browser-library-search.md) 2026-10-13 amendment) — `SearchLibrarySpec.AlsoGraveyard` (`SearchLibrary.AlsoGraveyard` in the catalog) searches the searcher's graveyard beside the library, under one predicate, in one prompt. The prompt lists the graveyard matches after the library's in `PendingChoice.SearchCards` and marks them in `SearchGraveyardCards` (wire `search_graveyard`, additive snapshot field). A graveyard card is public, so CR 701.23b's "may fail to find" does not reach it: while one is on offer the floor is `min(limit, graveyard matches)` (`searchFloor(hiddenZone=false, …)`, its first real caller), recomputed from the live zones as the prompt is answered. A take that comes wholly out of the graveyard never looked in the library, so it neither shuffles nor fires `EventSearchLibrary` ("If you search your library this way, shuffle"); any library card taken, or nothing found, is a library search. The graveyard half enters the battlefield through the CR 614 pipeline with `OldZone` graveyard. **Cards:** Finale of Devastation, Tower Winder and Ajani's Aid lose their caveat (Full); Delivery Moogle, Goldmane Griffin, Chandra's Firemaw, Teferi's Wavecaster and Yanling's Harbinger ship on it. **Not covered:** Ajani's-Aid-style cards whose search also needs the hand ("graveyard, hand, and/or library"), a `LibraryOwner` search (the graveyard searched is the library owner's), and a `ZoneLibrary` destination.
