**Teamwork and blight as optional costs** (#1703, CR 702.194a / CR 701.68a at CR 601.2h, [ADR 0073 amendment 2026-09-28](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)). Two components of `game.AdditionalCost`, announced by index like a kicker. **Teamwork** (`AdditionalCost.Teamwork`, `effects.Teamwork(n)`) is crew's sentence on a spell: the caster names the creatures on `cast_spell.teamwork_ids`, and `validateTeamworkLocked` checks what crew checks — untapped creatures they control, total effective power at least N, no summoning-sickness check (CR 302.6 is about a creature's own `{T}`). The taps land with the spell on the stack and the creatures are out of the auto-tapper's reach. **Blight** (`AdditionalCost.Blight`, `effects.OptionalBlight(n)`) names one creature the caster controls on `cast_spell.blight_ids`; a creature that dies of the counters still pays. The counters go through the CR 614 counter window marked `ReplacementEvent.CounterFromCost` and settle without pausing: Doubling Season's "if an effect would put" skips them (CR 614.16), while Winding Constrictor and Vizier of Remedies, which name no effect, apply. The hand card's `optional_costs[i]` carries `teamwork` / `teamwork_options` and `blight` / `blight_options` (present-and-empty when the board cannot pay), the client opens the crew picker or a one-creature picker after the add-on toggle, and the enumerator makes one payment per announced set (`crewPayment` for teamwork, a survivor first for blight), checked for affordability with those creatures excluded from the plan. "Cast using teamwork" is `ctx.UsedTeamwork()` and the `TeamworkUsed` mode condition; "if this spell's additional cost was paid" is `ctx.BlightPaid()` and `BlightUsed`. **Cards:** HULK SMASH!, Go Nuts!, Atlantis Attacks, Murdock's Crusade, Widow's Bite, Pyrrhic Strike, Repulsor Blast, Cinder Strike and Requiting Hex, all `full`. **Still open:** "blight N or pay {M}" and "blight X" as mandatory costs (Wild Unraveling, Bogslither's Embrace, Soul Immolation), blight on an activated ability's cost (Dawnhand Dissident, Gristle Glutton, Sting-Slinger), "you may blight N. If you do" as an effect (Sourbread Auntie, Dream Seizer), "becomes tapped to pay a teamwork cost" (Agent Maria Hill) and "cast a spell using teamwork" (Virtual Assistant), which no event names. Devoted Druid's and the loyalty cost's counters still skip the window.
