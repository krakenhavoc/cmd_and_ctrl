**Explore twice, several explorers, and investigate as an action** (#5425, CR 701.44d, 614.5, 701.16a) — three additive pieces for Topography Tracker, Hakbal of the Surging Soul and Erdwal Illuminator.
- **"Instead it explores, then it explores again."** `game.RegisterExploreAgainKey(catalogKey)` (`game/explore.go`) is the CR 614 replacement, beside Twists and Turns' `RegisterExploreScryKey` and read the same way, through the catalog key of a permanent the explorer's controller controls. One copy makes an instruction two explores, two copies four (CR 614.5). The explores it adds ride the explore's tails and prompts as data (`exploreRest.Again`) and are not replaced again. With Twists and Turns the result is the scries and then every explore, in either order of applying them. Both replacements now leave alone a permanent that is on the battlefield and is no longer a creature.
- **Several permanents at once.** `Game.ExploreEachForEffect(source, explorers, controller)` (`game/explore_each.go`) is CR 701.44d for one controller: a pick prompt over the permanents still to explore, the picked one's whole explore, then the next pick, so the player chooses knowing what the last explore left on top. One left asks nothing. A permanent that has left still explores (CR 701.44c) and goes first. The permanents still owed are `exploreRest.Queue`, data on the same words, so every prompt in the chain is a restore point. New keys: `engine/explore-each-pick` (a run).
- **Investigate.** `Game.InvestigateForEffect(player, source, n)` (`game/investigate.go`) creates the Clue through the token window and then emits `EventInvestigate`, once per time the action is taken; "investigate twice" is two creations and two events. `PlayerTurnTally.Investigates` counts them (`Game.InvestigatedThisTurn`), an additive snapshot field. The Clue is the catalog's row, registered with `RegisterInvestigateToken`. New keys: `engine/investigated` (a tail).
- **Card side** (`cards/effects/investigate.go`): `Investigate{Player, N}`, `InvestigateForEach{Name, Count}` and `WheneverYouInvestigateForTheFirstTimeEachTurn`. `Investigate` declares the new `game.FootprintInvestigate` step (the Clue's creation plus the event). Every catalogued card that prints "investigate" uses it; a card that prints "create a Clue token" keeps `CreateToken` and does not investigate.
- **Cards:** Topography Tracker, Hakbal of the Surging Soul and Erdwal Illuminator, all Full.
- **Not built:** one instruction that tells several players' permanents to explore (no catalogued card does; it is one `ExploreEachForEffect` per player in APNAP order, each behind the last).
