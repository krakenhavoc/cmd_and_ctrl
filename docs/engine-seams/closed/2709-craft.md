**Craft with one or more materials, rules over the set, and graveyard-only materials** (#2709, CR 702.167, [ADR 0137](decisions/0137-craft.md)'s 2026-10-10 amendment) — the second half of the "Craft" row, which closes. COST: `game.ExilePermanentsCost` gained `OrMore` (Count is a floor: "one or more", "four or more"; the list's length is the announcement), `GraveyardOnly` (a material named with the word "cards" is a card in your graveyard), `CardTypes` ("instant and/or sorcery") and `Color`, and two set rules judged over the whole payment: `ShareCardType` (Eye of Ojer Taq's "two that share a card type") and `EachSubtype` (Throne of the Grim Captain's "a Dinosaur, a Merfolk, a Pirate, and a Vampire", one-to-one, the sacrifice set rule's matching, so a changeling fills one part and never two). `ExilePermanentsPaymentForEffect` finds one legal payment, which is what "payable" asks. WIRE: `exile_permanent_options` carries an open count as min N / max 0, the one-of-each rule as `each_of`, and the new `shares` (each candidate's card types); the client's exile picker reads all three, and skips itself only when there is nothing to choose. BOT: the enumerator offers the floor, every graveyard material and everything for an open count, one payment per shared card type, and the matching for each-of. BACK FACES: `game.PowerAnywhereForEffect` (a `*` material's own characteristic-defining ability, per the rulings), a per-turn entry tally by card type (`EnteredWithCardTypeThisTurn`), a free alternative cost on a next-spell promise (`NextSpellPromise.WithoutPayingManaCost`, `PermissionFilter.CardType`, key `granted-next-spell-free`), an "as becomes attached" clause (`Spec.AsAttached`, run by `AttachForEffect`) with a copy that lasts while attached (`WhileSourceAttachedToPinned`), and a granted triggered row's item now names its grantor (`StackItem.GrantedBy`). **Cards, all Full:** Saheeli's Lattice, Altar of the Wretched, Sunbird Standard, Paleontologist's Pick-Axe, Eye of Ojer Taq, Throne of the Grim Captain, Ore-Rich Stalactite, Master's Guide-Mural, Dire Flail and Tetzin, Gnome Champion. The Enigma Jewel moved to the "Having another card's activated abilities" row (#1557): its back face is that seam.
