**A loyalty cost of X** (#1944, [ADR 0032](decisions/0032-planeswalkers.md)'s amendment of 2026-10-10) — a loyalty ability whose cost is −X (CR 107.3a, 606.4) is `game.AbilityCost.LoyaltyX` beside a zero `Loyalty`, built by `effects.LoyaltyMinusX()`. `DemandsX` counts it, so the view (`demands_x`, and `loyalty_cost_x` beside `loyalty_cost: 0`), the enumerator and the client's X picker ask for X as for an {X} in a mana cost, and the effect reads it through `Context.X()`. `AbilityCost.LoyaltyDelta(x)` is the one reading of the cost: `ActivateCatalogAbility` refuses an X above the loyalty there with `ErrInsufficientLoyalty` (CR 606.6) and pays the delta through `payCostCounterLocked`, and `internal/legal` bounds X by the same number. The X is a real choice, so unlike a mana X the enumerator offers every X from the floor to the loyalty, one move per X with its own `MoveCost.Loyalty` and a per-source budget multiplied by the number of rungs. The bot prices each: a damage row declares `game.TargetPurpose.DamageIsX` (`effects.DamageXToTarget`, wire `damage_is_x`), so the heuristic reads the X the move names and takes the smallest lethal X, every larger X being loyalty spent for nothing. The X picker caps X at the planeswalker's live loyalty, opens one under it, and says so when X is all of it. `effects.Register` refuses `LoyaltyX` without a zero loyalty cost or beside another X claimant. No printed loyalty ability costs +X. **Cards:** Chandra, Awakened Inferno, Chandra Nalaar and Jeska, Thrice Reborn (new, Full); Chandra, Chill of Compliance (now Full) and Ugin, the Spirit Dragon (his −X added; the −10's caveat remains).
