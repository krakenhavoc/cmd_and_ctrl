**Forage as a mana ability's cost** (#5397, CR 605.1a, 605.3b, 701.61a, 118.3) — #5184 shipped forage as an activated ability's cost, but a mana ability had no either/or cost, so Thornvault Forager's "{T}, Forage: Add two mana in any combination of colors" waited. `effects.ManaAbilityCost.Forage` now builds `game.ManaAbilityShape.Either` over `game.ForageAbilityBranches` with `ManaAbilityShape.Forage`. The activator names the way as `activate_mana_ability.cost_branch` (0 exiles three graveyard cards named in `exile_ids`, 1 sacrifices the Food named in `sacrifice_ids`), and `game.ChosenManaAbilityShape` folds it into the plain `ExileCards` or `SacrificeOther` clause before any gate, so the validators and payers are unchanged and a missing or wrong branch is refused with nothing paid. Paying it records `PaidCost.Foraged` and emits `game.EventForage`, so "whenever you forage" sees it. The view ships the mana row's `cost_branches[]` (the activated row's shape, public), the enumerator offers one move per payable branch, the bot prices a forage float below a plain one, and the client runs the existing branch picker. The auto-tapper never plans a branched mana ability. No snapshot change. **Card now Full:** Thornvault Forager. **Still open:** Osteomancer Adept's "cast creature spells from your graveyard by foraging" (the Forage row).
