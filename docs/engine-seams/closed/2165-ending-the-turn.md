**Ending the turn** (#2165, CR 724.1, [ADR 0059 amendment 2026-10-05](decisions/0059-turn-machinery.md#amendment-2026-10-05--ending-the-turn-cr-7241-2165)) — `Game.EndTheTurnForEffect` (`game/end_turn.go`) is CR 724.1 in its printed order, inside the turn plan. Inside the resolution (or a prompt's answer) it drops the triggers not yet on the stack with the prompts still announcing them (724.1a, `dropUnstackedTriggersLocked`, now shared by `EndCombatPhaseForEffect` for 724.2a), exiles the stack through end_combat.go's `exileEntireStackLocked` (724.1b, not countering), parks priority and sets `Game.TurnEndPending`, because a turn never ends inside a resolution (ADR 0059 Decision 6). The resolution's CR 704.3 boundary consumes the flag (`finishEndingTheTurnLocked`): state-based actions with the trigger drain off (724.1c, `stateChecksLocked(false)`), every creature out of combat and the plan cut to one cleanup step and popped (724.1d; the end step never begins, so end-step triggers do not fire and a "next end step" delayed trigger waits for next turn's, 724.1e), then the cleanup step begins through `runStepEntryHooksLocked` and cleanup.go does the rest (724.1f: discard, damage and "until end of turn", the CR 514.3a window). From a cleanup window, a new cleanup step begins. `passPriorityLocked` no longer hands priority back after a resolution that moved the cursor (`cursorMovedSince`), and `SettleResolution` finishes a pending turn end. The flag is carried by Clone and the snapshot (`turnEndPending`, additive in schema v7). The log has one `turn_ended` line (`EventTurnEnded`). **Cards:** Sundial of the Infinite, Obeka, Brute Chronologist (asks the player whose turn it is, `effects.ActivePlayerMayEndTheTurn`), Time Stop, Glorious End and Ultima, all Full. **Still open:** none for this seam; Day's Undoing needs only a card file, and Discontinuity waits on a coloured cost reduction.
