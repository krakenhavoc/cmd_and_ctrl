**An activated ability's OWN cost clause, and a price that reads the ability's target** (#1296, CR 602.2b / 601.2c / 601.2f / 113.6, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) Decisions 41-44) — narrows the open "Cost modification for activated abilities" row. `ActivatedAbilityShape.CostModifiers` (card side `ActivatedAbility.CostModifiers`) is "This ability costs {N} less to activate …": the activation twin of a spell's `SelfCostModifiers`, joined to the same CR 601.2f pass beside the board's activation-scoped modifiers, bound to the ability's source with the ACTIVATOR as its controller, and read from the query rather than the battlefield — so it works wherever the ability does. A clause with `ReadsTargets` is priced after the targets (`Game.AbilityManaCostForTargetsForEffect`, which `ActivateCatalogAbility` pays through with the targets it has just validated); `Game.AbilityPriceReadsTargetsForEffect` tells the enumerator to price each target set on its own (the cast path's §14 rule) and the view to ship `activated_abilities[i].target_charged_mana_costs`. `effects.CostsLessForTheCardItTargets` / `ColorsOf` / `CountersOf` and `ChannelDiscountPerLegendaryCreature` are the card vocabulary; `Register` refuses a clause on an ability with no mana cost, a `CostFloor`, `SpecialActions` or a designation gate. **Cards:** Dragonfire Blade (the equip discount ships; hexproof-from-monocolored stays caveated), Ghostfire Blade and Warrior's Blades (new, `full`), and Takenuma, Otawara and Boseiju (`full` — Takenuma's discount was a board modifier that the battlefield scan never found from the hand, so its "applies when you pay" caveat was false). **Not this seam, fixed in the same PR:** the reported "equip costs were not paid" was the client never sending `strict` / `auto_tap` on `activate_ability`, so every activation took the paper path. **Still open:** Belt of Giant Strength ("{X} less, where X is the power of the creature it targets") is expressible with `CostsLessForTheCardItTargets` and was not added; Arm-Mounted Anchor, Crown of Gondor, Plate Armor and the rest of the non-target "costs {N} less to activate" family are plain `CostsLessEach` / `CostsLess` clauses on the ability and are ordinary card work now.
