**Exiling a card from your graveyard as an additional cost** (#1948, [ADR 0189](decisions/0189-the-rest-of-face-down-and-manifest-dread.md) §2 and its 2026-10-16 amendment, CR 601.2b / 601.2h / 107.3a / 107.3i / 118.3) — "As an additional cost to cast this spell, exile N cards from your graveyard", its X form and its place in an either/or cost.
- **The fixed count** (#3536, #3537). `game.AdditionalCost.ExileCards` is the activated ability's `ExileCost` on a cast's mandatory slot (`effects.ExileFromGraveyardCost(n, label, match)`), so the candidate walk, the validator and the payer are the activation's own. The caster names the cards on `cast_spell`'s `exile_ids`; they are exiled with the spell on the stack, recorded on `PaidCost.Exiled` (`ctx.Exiled()`), kept by a CR 707.10 copy and charged under every alternative cost. Abhorrent Oculus, Draconic Intervention and Relentless Skaabs.
- **The X form** (#1948). `game.ExileCost.CountFromX` (`effects.ExileXFromGraveyardCost(label, match)`): the count is the one X announced with the cast, so a card whose mana cost has no {X} still asks for it (`additional_cost.demands_x`), and Skeletal Scrying's {X} is the same number. Exactly X cards are named; X can't exceed what the graveyard pays (`ExileCardsXCeilingForEffect`, the one walk the validator, the view's `exile_cost_x` / `exile_cost_x_max` and the enumerator read). The client opens its X prompt, capped there, then the graveyard picker for exactly X; the enumerator offers the largest X it can pay with exactly X cards named and no open X range. The spell reads X through `ctx.X()`. Skeletal Scrying, Harvest Pyre, Chill Haunting and Haunting Misery.
- **The either/or branch** (#1948). A fixed count may sit on a branch of `EitherCost`, payable only with enough matching graveyard cards (`AdditionalCostBranchPayableLocked`), and the chosen branch is the plan's mandatory entry, so nothing else changed. Soaring Stoneglider.
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` refuses the hand form, a non-positive printed count, an optional cost, and the X form on an ability, on a branch, beside pay X life, blight X or a waterbend {X}, or with a printed count.
