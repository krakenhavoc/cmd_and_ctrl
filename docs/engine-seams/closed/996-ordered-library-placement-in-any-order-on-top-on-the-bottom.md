**Ordered library placement — "in any order" on top, on the bottom, or top-or-bottom** (#996, [ADR 0088](decisions/0088-ordered-library-placement.md)) — the placement half of the roadmap's "Library-top placement and ordered look" row (45 / 31 / 37 sole unlocks). One prompt kind, `put_in_library` (`game/library_order.go`), is the fourth member of the scry family: same `ScryCards`, same chooser-only projection, same client dialog, answered with scry's `{top_order, bottom}` keys, both TOP-FIRST, and a `LibraryPlacement` (`top` / `bottom` / `top_or_bottom`) that says which lanes are open. `Game.PutInLibraryInChosenOrderThenForEffect` takes the cards from anywhere — a card already in its owner's library is REORDERED (no window, no event), anything else goes through the tuck route one leg at a time in pile order, so the CR 614 window opens, a commander is offered CR 903.9 and a paused leg pauses the pile. `From` is CR 400.7: a card that left the zone it was in is skipped. Knowledge afterwards follows CR 401.4 — a card alone in its lane keeps its knowers, two or more are known by the chooser alone, because the order is not revealed. No prompt for a pile of one on a one-lane placement; `top_or_bottom` always asks. Gate: blocks; leave-game: dropped with the chooser; enumerator: leave-alone + each-to-front (+ bury-all and bury-each for top-or-bottom); bot: best own card on top, bury opponents' cards. Card side (`effects/library_order.go`): `PutInLibraryInAnyOrder`, `TakeRestOnBottomInAnyOrder` (the `Then` for the forty-odd "the rest on the bottom of your library in any order" cards), `LookAtTopThenTakeOneRestOnBottom` (Impulse's whole sentence), `PutFromHandOnTopInAnyOrder` (Brainstorm's put-back — now pick, THEN order, instead of the pick order standing in for the order), and `PutIntoLibrary{Depth}` for "second / third from the top", plus `SearchLibrary.Depth` for "then shuffle and put that card third from the top". **Cards:** Goblin Ringleader goes `full` (its random-order caveat is gone), Brainstorm moves onto the two-prompt shape, Aetherspouts stops borrowing Scry (it fired "whenever you scry" payoffs and opened the scry window), Vampiric Tutor's stale "goes straight to your hand" caveat is cleared, and Impulse, Oust and Long-Term Plans are new. The scry dialog's bottom lane is reorderable, which scry wanted too (CR 701.22a). **Closed by #1298** ([ADR 0088's 2026-09-23 amendment](decisions/0088-ordered-library-placement.md#amendment-2026-09-23-1298-the-library-positions-put_in_library-did-not-cover)), on the same prompt: a look at ANOTHER player's library (`Game.LookAtTopOfPlayersLibraryForEffect(looker, owner, n)` marks the looker alone; `effects.LookAtLibraryThenPlace` — Jace, the Mind Sculptor's +2, Portent); a top lane at a DEPTH chosen by the card's owner (`PutInLibrarySpec.TopDepth`, `effects.PutIntoLibraryAtDepthOrBottom` — Temporal Cleansing; a real `top_or_bottom` prompt rather than the `ConfirmPrompt` first guessed at, so the wire, enumerator and dialog carry the position); an EXACT top-lane count (`TopCount`, refused otherwise — Cream of the Crop); and a COUNTER to a position chosen by the counterer (`Counter`, each leg `Game.CounterSpellToLibraryThenForEffect` on the shared stack exit — Hinder, Spell Crumple — asked BEFORE the counter, so an uncounterable spell is never asked about and a commander's owner answers CR 903.9 knowing the end). A card placed alone in its lane from a public zone is known to the whole table afterwards (`publicKnowersOfLocked`, CR 400.2). Wire: `top_count` / `top_depth`, public. Still open: Library of Leng's batch discard order; Lost Days and Wan Shi Tong, All-Knowing are card work on `PutIntoLibraryAtDepthOrBottom` (Wan Shi Tong also needs a batched "one or more cards are put into a library" event).
