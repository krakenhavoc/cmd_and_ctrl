**Phasing** (#1199, CR 702.26 / CR 502.1, [ADR 0084](decisions/0084-phasing.md)) — the seam had no row of its own, only a mention in the player-protection entry below and one comment in the tree (`game/activation_tally.go`) saying phasing was not modelled at all. It is Teferi’s Protection’s larger half and the half the card is famous for. **The rule is one sentence and the surface is 125 walks.** CR 702.26b — *"a phased-out permanent is treated as though it does not exist. It can’t affect or be affected by anything else in the game"* — is a statement about every read of the battlefield, and this engine open-codes 125 of them in `internal/game`, `internal/legal`, `internal/protocol` and `internal/aiseat`, plus 189 in the card catalog and 144 callers of eight near-identical single-card lookups. #1199 proposed one predicate at each walk; the ADR takes the same rule and makes it structural instead, because a walk that forgets to ask is a silent rules bug (a phased-out creature that still blocks, a Wrath that still kills it) that no test can notice. **A phased-out permanent leaves the battlefield SLICE**, into `Game.PhasedOut` — a `*Zone` for the plumbing (`cloneZone`, `snapshotZone` / `restoreZone`, `viewOfZone` all work on it unchanged) and explicitly NOT a zone in the CR 400 sense, which `findCardZoneLocked` enforces by not looking there. Targeting, the layer pass, the trigger harvester, the SBA sweep, combat, the autotapper, the legal-move enumerator, the view builder and every bot heuristic are then correct with **no edit and no knowledge of phasing**; `leave_game.go`’s CR 800.4a sweep makes the same argument for the same reason. **It is still not a zone change** (CR 702.26d), and the code is written so that this is checkable: `phaseOutLocked` / `phaseInLocked` call neither `MoveCard` nor `battlefieldExitLocked`, emit no `EventZoneMove` / `EventLTB` / `EventETB`, and leave `Card.ObjectEpoch` alone — the promise `activation_tally.go` has been making since S38, which is what keeps an exhaust ability spent across a phase cycle where a flicker refreshes it. `MoveCard`’s battlefield-exit block is the negative image of the rule: every field it clears is one CR 702.26d says to keep. **Three fields and a rider on `Card`**, each answering a question the rules ask: `PhasedOutBy` (CR 702.26a’s *"the active player controlled when they phased out"*, which is not `Controller` — CR 702.26f lets a control-changing effect expire while the permanent is away), `PhasedOutIndirect` (CR 702.26g/h, decided once per batch by the transitive attachment walk so a permanent named AND attached phases out indirectly), `PhaseInLockedBy` (the *"phases out until ~ leaves the battlefield"* family — Oubliette, Out of Time — released by a sweep at the top of `stateBasedActionsLocked`, not by an untap step) and `TapOnPhaseIn` (Oubliette’s rider). **CR 502.1 is one statement** at the top of `performUntapStepLocked`, ahead of the CR 302.6 sickness clear and of CR 502.3’s determination, and inside that function rather than in the step-entry arm for the reason [ADR 0070](decisions/0070-untap-step-choices.md) gives about `consumeUntapSkipsLocked`: everything ahead of the pause runs exactly once. **`phasing` joins `canonicalKeywords`**, read off `Effective().Abilities`, so a granted phasing (Shimmer, an Aura) works like a printed one and the ~70 printed-phasing permanents of the Mirage/Visions cycle are correct with **no catalog entry at all**. **The wire** gets `GameView.phased_out`, a shared `ZoneView` beside `battlefield` / `stack` / `exile`, deliberately not merged into the battlefield: the positional stamps in `view.go` index against `g.Battlefield.Cards`, and `internal/aiseat` reads the view rather than the game. The client folds it back onto the controller’s row, dimmed and badged `PHASED`, with the click withheld. **4 cards** `full`: Vodalian Illusionist, Reality Ripple, Clever Concealment, Oubliette. **Teferi’s Protection** goes from two caveats to one. **Closed since, and exactly where the ADR’s closing section said it would be**: *"your life total can’t change"* (#1200, [ADR 0085](decisions/0085-life-total-cant-change.md)) went on `Player.Statics` and was consumed by the one CR 614 life window, so the duration on `TurnScopedReplacements` was never built — see the row at the top of this section. Tracker [#889](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/889).
