**Conditional castable zones** (#3105, CR 601.2, CR 702.178a, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)'s 2026-10-13 amendment) — a card could open its graveyard as a cast zone only unconditionally (`Spec.CastableZones`, whose hook takes an oracle ID and no game), so "Max speed — You may cast this card from your graveyard", "as long as you control a Zombie" and "as long as you control a black or green permanent" had no home. `Spec.CastableZonesIf []game.ConditionalCastZone{Zone, Cond}` is a second declaration with its own catalog hook; `Cond func(g, owner, card) bool` is pure and asked of the card's owner. `Game.cardOpensZoneLocked` is the one predicate behind the engine (`validateCastPathLocked`, `castUsesGrantLocked`), the legal-move enumerator and the view's `castable_here` (`CardCastableFromAnyFaceLocked`), so a bot is offered the cast exactly while the engine accepts it and the client's button appears and disappears as the condition flips. Unlike a `CastCondition` it gates only the card's own text: a permission (Snapcaster Mage, Underworld Breach) still opens the zone, and a one-spell permission is spent only when it was the reason. `effects.MaxSpeedCastableFrom` is the ADR 0138 wrapper. No snapshot change. **Cards (3, all Full):** Lightwheel Enhancements (the last Commander-legal speed card, so the `speed` row is Implemented), Gravecrawler and Marang River Prowler.
