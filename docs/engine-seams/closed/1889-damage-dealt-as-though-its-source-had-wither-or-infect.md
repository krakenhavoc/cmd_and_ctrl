**Damage dealt as though its source had wither or infect** (#1889, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §10, PR 10) — "All damage is dealt as though its source had wither" and "all damage is dealt to you as though its source had infect" are a battlefield static, `Spec.DamageAsThough` (`game.DamageAsThoughStatic{Wither, Infect, ToYou, WhileAtOrBelowZeroLife}`, `game/damage_as_though.go`), read live through `game.CatalogDamageAsThough` keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey`, so a permanent that has lost its abilities stops (CR 613.1f). It is a rules effect (CR 613.11) read where the rule it changes is used: one gate, `damageResultAsThoughLocked`, ORs it into the result the source's own infect, wither and toxic gave the event's tail (ADR 0056 Decision 2) as the damage lands, in the two landing functions and nowhere else. So the damage has been settled first (CR 120.4b before 120.4c): a prevention shield prevents it and no counter is put for a prevented point, "damage can't be prevented" (ADR 0107 §5) makes every point a counter and leaves the shield's charge alone, a multiplier doubles the counters with the damage, and §7's charged and half shields take their part off first. The counters go through the CR 614 counter window, put or given by the source's controller (CR 120.3b, 120.3d). The source gains no ability (CR 609.4): a "whenever a creature with infect deals damage" trigger does not see it. Phyrexian Unlife's "as long as you have 0 or less life" is read once per damage instance, against the life total its controller had as the instance began (owner decision 4, its ruling): `nextDamageInstanceLocked` records every seat's life total, transient like the stamp, cloned with the game and never captured, so two attackers that take you from 3 to -1 in one combat damage step are all life loss, and the next instance is poison. `Register` refuses a static that names neither keyword, and wither narrowed to damage dealt to a player. **Cards** (both Full): Everlasting Torment, Phyrexian Unlife (its "you don't lose the game for having 0 or less life" is ADR 0057's `LossLife`-narrowed gate, as on Transcendence).
