**Craft with a fixed number of materials of one type** (#2124, CR 702.167a–c, [ADR 0137](decisions/0137-craft.md)) — the first half of the "Craft" row. COST: `game.ExilePermanentsCost` (#1600) gained `FromGraveyard`, CR 702.167b's second zone: the candidate walk lists the activator's matching permanents and then the matching cards in the activator's OWN graveyard, the validator accepts either (a graveyard card judged on its front face, CR 712.8a), and the payer moves either through the one exit primitive. It also gained `Subtype` ("Craft with Island", "Craft with Cave"). No wire field changed: `exile_permanent_options` may now hold graveyard card IDs, and the client resolves them against the viewer's graveyard too (`exilePermanentOptions`). `effects.Register` refuses `FromGraveyard` on a mana ability and beside an `ExileCards` component. The heuristic prices a graveyard material as fuel. RETURN: `game.ReturnCraftedFromExileForEffect` sets the back face on the card in exile and runs the ordinary exile entry, so what enters is a new object under its owner's control; a token, a card no longer in exile, or a card that cannot transform stays where it is. LINK: `Card.CraftedWith []ObjectRef` is CR 702.167c's "the exiled cards used to craft it", delve's shape, stamped by the entry finisher before `EventETB`, cleared on leaving the battlefield, carried by clone, the snapshot (additive in v7) and `PermanentInfo.CraftedWith`, read with `CraftMaterialsForEffect` / `effects.CraftMaterials`. `effects.Craft` with `CraftWith` / `CraftWithN` / `CraftWithSubtype` is the keyword. **Cards, all Full:** Tithing Blade, Visage of Dread, Clay-Fired Bricks, Braided Net, Jade Seedstones, Idol of the Deep King, Oteclan Landmark, Inverted Iceberg, Kaslem's Stonetree, Waterlogged Hulk and Spring-Loaded Sawblades. **Still open** on the row (#2709): "one or more", rules over the chosen set, and graveyard-only materials.
