**The first spell you cast has assist, and the size of a starting deck** (#5948, CR 702.132a, 100.2a, 903.5a) — two small seams from Mystery Booster Commander's commanders. `CardDef.FirstSpellHasAssist` (`Spec.FirstSpellHasAssist`) is "The first spell you cast during each of your turns has assist" (Yume, Chronicler of Valor), a bool beside `SpellsYouCastHaveDelve`: `Game.SpellHasAssistLocked` is the one door the cast announcement and the cast surface ask, true for a spell that prints assist or for the first spell its controller casts on their own turn (the cast tally still empty, so the spell finds it so) while they control a permanent whose catalog row sets it. Nothing is stored. `Player.StartingDeckSize` is the library plus the commanders as the deck was seated (`AddPlayer`, `ReplaceDeck`), never a companion or a sideboard card; it is carried through the snapshot (`startingDeckSize`, additive within version 9) and a restart, and Whtz, the Bibliophile's "if you had 200 or more cards in your starting deck" reads it. The deck validator reads three "Rulebreaker" clauses off the commander's oracle text (`internal/deck/rulebreaker.go`): Angels of any colour identity and any basic land (Seluma), one off-identity colour for instants and sorceries and any basic land (Tolabow), and no maximum deck size, at least 100 cards (Whtz). **Cards:** Yume, Chronicler of Valor, Whtz, the Bibliophile, Tolabow, Loch Rascal, Seluma, Light of Aysen (Full).
