**Graveyard cards chosen after a sacrifice** (#2863, [ADR 0013 amendment of 2026-10-09](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)) — three cards that sacrifice and then return cards from your graveyard made one of the two choices at the wrong time, because when they were written a sacrifice prompt had no continuation (#1019 gave it one) and no card had put a graveyard pick inside one. Victimize sacrificed as a cast cost; Rise of the Witch-king and Lich-Knights' Conquest picked the returning cards as targets at cast, so opponents saw the pick and the creature you sacrificed could never be the card that came back. `effects.ReturnChosenFromGraveyard` (`cards/effects/graveyard_return_together.go`) is the shape: one `choose_cards` prompt over your graveyard as it is WHEN IT RUNS, bounded, with an optional "another" exclusion, the chosen cards entering together (#1867). Placed in a sacrifice's continuation, the just-sacrificed card is on offer. No engine change, no snapshot field, no closure route: the prompt is the existing `choose_cards` kind with its `Then`. **Cards:** Victimize (the sacrifice is a one-seat run on resolution, so it is castable with no creature and its death triggers wait for it), Rise of the Witch-king (APNAP edict, then "you may return another permanent card" chosen from the graveyard after the sacrifices) and Lich-Knights' Conquest ("sacrifice any number" is an own-permanents pick on resolution, "that many" is what really left the battlefield, and the creature cards are then chosen, so a sacrificed artifact creature card can come back and a sacrificed token cannot). All three are Full. **Still open:** Grapple with the Past picks its card at cast, before its mill, the same posture; it can move to the same helper once its mill's continuation is used.
