**Exalted given by an effect or a counter** (#2538, CR 702.83, CR 122.1b, [ADR 0101 amendment 2026-10-08](decisions/0101-keyword-counters.md)) — exalted is a canonical keyword now, the fourth whose trigger the engine derives from the ability list, after prowess, evolve and annihilator. `exalted` joins `canonicalKeywords` as a cumulative token (CR 113.2c), so a printed exalted, a layer-6 grant (Sublime Archangel's) and a token's each trigger, one trigger per instance, and the 15 Commander-legal cards whose only text is keywords need no card file. The trigger watches a declared attacker (CR 508.3a), applies when exactly one creature was declared (CR 506.5, `game.AttackedAlone`) and its controller is the source's, pins the attacker by instance and object epoch, and resolves through the new keyed body `exalted/pump`: +1/+1 until end of turn, nothing for an attacker that left and came back (CR 400.7). Exalted triggers commute, so a batch of them asks for no order. `CounterExalted` joins `keywordCounterKinds`, and each exalted counter is one instance (the Emissary of Soulfire ruling of 2024-06-07), in the layer pass and off the battlefield alike. Noble Hierarch and Ignoble Hierarch moved off the retired `effects.Exalted()` constructor to `PrintedKeywords`, with one trigger each; a restore point holding one of their old catalog triggers restores through a one-entry alias to `exalted/pump`, and the Hierarch is not flagged. The bot values exalted at 0.30 per instance and never prices a second exalted counter as redundant; the attack planner is unchanged. Snapshot: one new on-disk body key and a v7 corpus board with the trigger waiting. Decayed stays out (#2650). **Cards:** Emissary of Soulfire — Full.
