**Station (CR 702.184, 721)** (#759, [ADR 0071 addendum 2026-09-23](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)) — the station ABILITY, on #758's tap-another cost. The threshold half shipped with ADR 0071 (#969); what closed here is "Tap another untapped creature you control: Put charge counters equal to its power on this permanent. Activate only as a sorcery." `effects.Station()` is the whole keyword: `TapAnotherUntapped` (a `TapOthersCost` of one, a creature filter, `ExcludeSource` for "another"), `SorcerySpeed`, and no `ActiveWhen` gate (CR 721.4). **Which power** is CR 608.2h's answer, confirmed by the Edge of Eternities release notes: the tapped creature's power AS THE ABILITY RESOLVES, or as it last existed on the battlefield if it left — not the power it was tapped with, which is what ADR 0071 had assumed. So the payment record (`PaidCost.TappedOthers []PaidTap`) carries the tapped object's ID and epoch, `Context.TappedPower()` reads it live through `Game.PaidTapPowerForEffect`, and the one battlefield-exit choke point (`battlefieldExitLocked`) freezes the last-known power onto any stack item that tapped the leaving object. Zero or negative power puts nothing on and takes nothing off; a source that left or was replayed as a new object gets nothing (`AbilitySourceGoneForEffect`). The wire carries the cost's options (`tap_others_label` / `tap_others_options`), the enumerator offers one move per creature, the heuristic prices the tapped creature's attack before combat, and the client reuses the sacrifice picker with the verb "Tap". **Cards:** The Seriema (#337) goes Full; Galvanizing Sawship, Uthros Research Craft (a gated trigger) and Adagia, Windswept Bastion (a Planet — a land with station) ship Full. Still open, stated: CR 702.184c modifiers (Tapestry Warden) and CR 721.2c (no P/T outside the battlefield).
