**The first activation each turn that targets your creature, and becomes the target of an activated ability** (#3779, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md), [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — two small seams for Professor Hojo. **The record:** `ActivationTally.OwnCreatureTargeted` counts, per activating player and per turn, the activations whose announced targets included a creature that player controlled. It is written at the announce on the catalog and sandbox activation paths (a refused activation does not spend it, a countered one does) and read by `Game.OwnCreatureTargetedActivationsThisTurn`; the cost side is `effects.ActivationCostsLessIfItTargets` with `YouActivate`, `ActivatedDuringYourTurn` and `TheFirstOwnCreatureTargetingActivationThisTurn`, on the existing `ReadsTargets` pricing so the enumerator and the view price each legal target on its own. **The event:** `Event.TargeterKind` (`spell`, `activated` or `triggered`) is stamped by `emitBecameTargetLocked` from the stack item, so a trigger can tell an activated ability from a spell without walking the stack; "one or more … only once each turn" is the existing `b11TriggeredThisTurn` gate. Both fields are additive under snapshot v9. **Card:** Professor Hojo (Full).
