**Cumulative upkeep** (#567, CR 702.24): `CumulativeUpkeep(label, cost)` in `cards/effects/cumulative_upkeep.go` is the whole keyword in one constructor over primitives that already existed — an `AtYourUpkeep` trigger, `AddCounter` for the age counter (`game.CounterAge`, CR 122.1d) and `PayUnless` for "sacrifice it unless you pay". The cost is not fixed when the card is written, and the fix is `strings.Repeat(cost, age)` at resolution: three counters on a "cumulative upkeep {1}{U}" permanent is `{1}{U}{1}{U}{1}{U}`, which `ParseCost` accumulates, so hybrid and Phyrexian symbols stay separate symbols to pay rather than a multiplied number. The prompt is `pay_unless` through `effects.UpkeepPayUnless` — the shape of that kind that stops the table, because unlike Rhystic Study it asks the ACTIVE player during their own upkeep and the answer decides whether a permanent is still on the battlefield. Both narrowings on `(*game.Game).ChoicePromptBlocksTable` are now DERIVED from the board and read by the engine gate and the enumerator alike (ADR 0018 §6 is amended for the prompt, not the kind): `PendingChoice.GuardsStackItem` (#951, `game/counter_unless_paid.go`) blocks while the object a decline would counter is still on the stack, and `PendingChoice.OwedInStep` (#997, `game/upkeep_pay_unless.go`) blocks while the cursor is still in the step the prompt was raised in — which is Stasis's and Pact of Negation's rule as well as cumulative upkeep's. `PayUnless.Blocking` is gone: the per-card switch #567 shipped was missed by both of the cards written after it. "Cumulative upkeep" does NOT join `canonicalKeywords`, for ward's reason: it carries a cost and a bare token has nowhere to put one. Shipped on Mystic Remora, both halves. Still open: non-mana cumulative upkeep — "Pay 2 life" (Glacial Chasm) and "Sacrifice a creature" (Phyrexian Soulgorger) are the same trigger with a payment the pay-or-else prompt cannot parse.
