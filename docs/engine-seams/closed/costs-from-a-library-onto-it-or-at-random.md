**Activation costs from your library, onto it, or discarded at random** (#1902, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §7 and owner decision 3, PR 8) — three cost components, validated with the others and paid in `activateCatalogAbilityLocked` (`game/library_cost.go`). **"Put a card from your hand on top of your library"** (`AbilityCost.PutFromHandOnLibraryTop`) names its card at announce in a new `top_ids` field: a card in the activator's hand, never the source and never one the same payment discards or exiles (CR 118.3). It goes to the top of the library through the exit primitive, the activator keeps knowing it, and it is not a discard. **"Exile the top N cards of your library"** (`AbilityCost.ExileFromLibraryTop`) chooses nothing: a library of fewer than N cards refuses the activation, and the cards are exiled last (CR 601.2h) and recorded on `PaidCost.Exiled`, top first, for "the exiled card" (CR 400.7j). **"Discard N cards at random"** (`DiscardCost.Random`, CR 701.9b) is drawn by the engine from the hand the other components leave, once per announcement, so a commander drawn is offered CR 903.9a before anything is paid and the re-made announcement discards the same card; it is paid last through the one discard door and joins `PaidCost.Discarded` for ADR 0109 §8's readers. Each move is asked CR 903.9 like every other cost move (#1397). The view stamps `top_cost_n`, `top_cost_label`, `top_cost_options` (private to the activator), `library_exile_cost_n` and `discard_cost_random`; the enumerator offers one payment and gates the other two on the library and the hand; the client asks for the card to put on top with the discard picker and confirms the library exile and the random discard. **Cards** (24, all Full): Penance, Leashling, Seasoned Tactician, Arc-Slogger, Whirling Catapult, Royal Herbalist, Storm Elemental and Phyrexian Devourer, and the random discarders Pyromancy, Stormscale Anarch, Mage il-Vec, Frenetic Ogre, Canyon Drake, Coral Helm, Meteor Storm, Pardic Swordsmith, Stormbind, Amok, Pyromania, Hell-Bent Raider, Dwarven Strike Force, Draconian Cylix, Ogre Shaman and Pardic Lancer. **Moved to other rows:** Hidden Retreat (#1904, its spell-keyed shield), Thought Lash (`cumulative-upkeep`, its upkeep paid from the library) and Barbarian Bully (#1903, an offer to every player).
