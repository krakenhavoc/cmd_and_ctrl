**Untap N tapped creatures you control, as an activation cost** (#5669, CR 701.26b, 614.17b, 122.1d, 118.11, 601.2h, 302.6) — "{W}, {T}, Untap a tapped creature you control: ..." is the tap-others component turned over: `game.TapOthersCost.Untap`, built with `effects.UntapTapped(n, label, preds...)` and set on `AbilityCost.TapOthers`. The candidate walk, the validator, the view and the enumerator are the tap clause's, asking for a TAPPED permanent that can become untapped; a permanent that can't become untapped can't pay, and one with a stun counter can, because the counter is removed in place of the untap and the cost is still paid. The payment goes through `untapPermanentLocked` after the ability is on the stack, so "whenever a creature becomes untapped" sees each one, and writes nothing to `PaidCost.TappedOthers`. It is not the {Q} symbol, so summoning sickness is not asked. Where the same cost prints {T} and the source matches the clause, the source may be named for both in either order (`UntapOthersSourcePaysForEffect`): untapped, it is tapped and then untapped; tapped, it is untapped and then tapped, unless a stun counter would leave it tapped. `effects.Register` keeps the form to an activated ability with a fixed count. On the wire it rides `tap_others_options` and `tap_ids`, with `tap_others_untap` for the picker's verb and `tap_others_frees_source` for a tapped source its own clause untaps. No snapshot change. **Cards now Full:** Halo Fountain.
