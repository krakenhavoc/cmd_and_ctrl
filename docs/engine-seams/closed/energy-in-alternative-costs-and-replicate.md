**Energy as an alternative cost, replicate and a keyword's cost** (#1995, [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md) §5 and owner decision 4, PR 4) — `AlternativeCost.Energy` is checked with the offer's life and cards (`AlternativeCostPayableLocked`, so an offer the caster is short for is not listed, CR 118.3) and paid through `payEnergyLocked` with the spell on the stack; never waived. ADR 0118 §3's granted offer gained a spell filter (`GrantedAlternativeCost.Spells` and `MaxManaValue`), energy, and `AlternativeCost.AsThoughFlash`, CR 601.3c's "as though it had flash" for that claim only, read by `CastTimingForOfferOpenLocked` in CastSpell, the enumerator and the view. `CastPermission.EnergyEqualToManaValue` is Bolas's Citadel's price in energy (an additive snapshot field). Replicate (CR 702.56a) is an optional cost keyed `replicate` with `AdditionalCost.Energy`, summed per payment with the offer's energy (`game.CastEnergyOwed`), and the `effects.Replicate` cast trigger copying the spell once per payment. Equip and unearth take an `AbilityCost` with energy (`effects.EquipPayingAbility`, `UnearthPaying`). The wire names the energy on `alternative_costs[].energy`, `cast_prices[].energy` and `optional_costs[].energy` (with `max_times` capped at what the viewer can pay and `energy_short`), and the enumerator on `MoveCost.energy`. **Cards** (6, all Full): Nissa, Worldsoul Speaker; Primal Prayers; Amped Raptor; Reiterating Bolt; Inventor's Axe; Salvation Colossus. Razorfield Ripper waits on reconfigure (#2639). Closes `energy-alternative-costs`.
