**Put-from-hand onto the battlefield** (#654, PR #728) - `Game.PutFromHandOntoBattlefieldForEffect(cardID, HandEntryOptions{Controller, Tapped})`: the CR 614 entry pipeline runs BEFORE the card leaves the hand (a paused pipeline leaves it there rather than stranding it), then the move, `EventZoneMove`, `EventETB` and the catalog's `AsEnters` hook — the same four steps the search, reanimation and exile-return paths take. A land put this way is **not a land drop** (CR 305.4), and since #1322 the entry is resumable: the batch runs every card's pipeline against the pre-entry board one at a time, stops on a card's question, and moves them together once the last answer is in (`game/entry_batch.go`; `PutFromHandOntoBattlefieldThenForEffect` for a caller that reads the result). The card side is `effects.PutFromHandOntoBattlefield` on top of #552's pick-from-hand prompt, with `MayPutALandFromHand(card)` / `MayPutALandFromHandTapped(card)` for the sentence five cards share and a `Then` continuation for a rider on the result. Shipped on Eureka Moment, Broken Bond, Chulane, Insidious Fungus's third mode and Spelunking (its Cave life bonus included). Unblocked and still to be written: Growth Spiral (#294); Archaeomancer's Map (#299); Kodama of the East Tree (#301); Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant (#302); Sneak Attack (#306); Cultivator Colossus (#307); Terrain Generator (#310); Arboreal Grazer (#313); +7 more. Two catalog cards still declare the clause as a caveat and are a small edit each: Stoneforge Mystic (its `{1}{W}, {T}` is an activated ability) and Ugin, the Spirit Dragon (the -10 also searches a graveyard and a library). Uro (#653) and the Obstinate Baloth redirect (#650) wait on their own seams, not on this one.
