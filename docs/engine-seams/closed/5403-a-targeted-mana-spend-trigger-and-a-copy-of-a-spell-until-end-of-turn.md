**A targeted mana-spend trigger and a copy of a spell until end of turn** (#5403; CR 603.3d, 707.2, 608.2h) — a "when that mana is spent" rider (`game.ManaSpendTrigger`, #1547) could only queue an untargeted trigger, and nothing could make a permanent a copy of a *spell*. `ManaSpendTrigger.Targets` now gives a rider its own target clause, announced as the trigger is put on the stack (CR 603.3d) through the reflexive dispatch (`Game.dispatchTargetedManaSpendTriggerLocked`), so it gets the same pick prompt, the same removal when a required clause has nothing to choose, and the same restorable announcement frame. Its item is keyed under the new `mana-rider/dispatch-targeted` body, a `game.TargetedDelayedBody` that re-derives the clause from the rider's registry key (`Params.Name`) on restore; the spell paid for still rides as `Payload[0]`, and "other" is the permanent that made the mana. `Game.CopiableValuesOfSpellForEffect` reads a spell's copiable values (CR 707.2) on the stack, or from its last-known information once it was countered (CR 608.2h, `stack_lki.go`), and `effects.BecomeCopy{Values: …}` makes the duration copy. No snapshot change: the new body is one ledger line, and the frame and the item are the shapes the reflexive and delayed triggers already write. **Cards now Full:** The Everflowing Well // The Myriad Pools (mill two then draw two, and a descend 8 upkeep transform on the front).
