**A creature enters tapped and attacking, and its CR 508.4 defender** (#4973, Star Trek; CR 614.1d, CR 614.12a, CR 508.4, CR 508.4a) — "If a creature you control would enter while you control an attacking creature, instead it enters tapped and attacking" is a CR 614 entry replacement declared with `ReplacementEffect.EntryAttack` (`game.EntryAttackChoice`, data), built by `effects.YourCreaturesEnterTappedAndAttackingWhileYouAttack`. Its `AppliesTo` reads the creature as it would exist on the battlefield (`Game.EntryWouldBeCreatureForEffect`, the CR 614.12 look-ahead; a face-down entry is a creature) and its would-be controller (`Game.EntryWouldBeControllerForEffect`). The apply-loop sets `EntersTapped` and asks CR 508.4's question in the entry window (`game/entry_attack.go`), the shape of `entry_controller` and `entry_read_ahead`: the new mandatory prompt `entry_attack_target`, answered with `{option_index}`, offers every player, planeswalker and battle the chooser could attack with the one their attack already targets first, and the answer fills `ReplacementEvent.EntersAttacking`, which `stampEntryAttackerLocked` turns into an attacker that was never declared. One defender is not asked; an entry that cannot pause takes the first; an entry whose putting effect already names a defender (ninjutsu, a token created attacking) only gets tapped; a defender gone by the answer leaves the creature not attacking (CR 508.4a). The paused entry is a keyed resume frame, so the table is a restore point at the question. The bot sends the creature at the opponent with the least life. No snapshot change. **Cards:** Kira Nerys, Firebrand — Full.
