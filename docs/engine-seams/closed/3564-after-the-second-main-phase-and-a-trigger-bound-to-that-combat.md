**After the second main phase, and a trigger bound to that combat** (#3564; [ADR 0188](decisions/0188-skipped-turns-and-turn-bound-effects.md) Decisions 4 and 5, tracker #753) — "after the second main phase this turn, there's an additional combat phase followed by an additional main phase" is `PhaseAnchor{Kind: AnchorNthMainPhase, N: 2}`. `AddPhasesForEffect` finds the Nth main phase by walking the turn plan, because CR 505.1b counts every main phase of the turn, added ones included: it is the phase in progress when that is the Nth, a phase still to come when the Nth has not begun (a rebound cast in the upkeep, a spell cast in the precombat main phase), and nothing once the Nth has begun and ended (the World at War ruling: a copy in the third main phase adds no phases). Several copies splice ahead of one another, newest pair first (CR 500.8). "At the beginning of that combat" is `DelayedTrigger.OnPhaseID` with `OnSeq`: phase ids restart every turn, so the pair names one phase of one turn, `ScheduleDelayedTriggerForEffect` refuses an id that is neither the phase in progress nor in the plan, and `sweepUnreachableBoundTriggersLocked` drops a trigger whose turn has passed, so a combat that never began (the turn was ended, CR 724.1) leaves nothing to fire in the next turn's combat. The card side is `effects.ExtraCombatAndMainAfterNthMain(n)` and `AddPhases.AtThatCombat`, which binds the delayed trigger to the first combat the effect added and schedules nothing when none was added. World at War (untap all creatures that attacked this turn, in any earlier combat) and Moraug, Fury of Akoum (untap all creatures you control, at its own added combat and not the regular one) are Full. Phase-bound triggers use their own body keys, `extra-combat/untap-attackers-at-that-combat` and `extra-combat/untap-all-you-control`, and the two new fields are additive in snapshot v9 (`delayedTriggers[].onPhaseId`, `.onSeq`).
