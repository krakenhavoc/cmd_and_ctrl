**A card in exile as a target, and "exiled with this"** (#3259, CR 115.1, CR 607.2a, CR 608.2b) — "put target creature card exiled with The Darkness Crystal onto the battlefield", "put target face-up exiled card into its owner's graveyard". `effects.TargetCardInExile(label, preds…)` declares `Zones {ZoneExile}` with the new `card_in_exile` mode, and the zone browser's exile tab answers it exactly as the graveyard tab answers `card_in_graveyard`. `game.TargetSpec.ExiledWithSource` (built with `effects.ExiledWithThis(spec)`) is the source-relative half: the candidate's `Card.ExiledWith` must name the clause's own source object, `{id, epoch}`, judged in the one shared walk beside `RelativeToSource` and `CombatWithSource`, so the legal set, the announce check (CR 601.2c), the resolution re-check (CR 608.2b), the client's picker and the bot's moves are one answer. A second source's exile, a stale epoch and an unlinked card are out; a source that has left is read from its last-known record (CR 608.2h); a walk with no source admits nothing. A face-down exiled card its chooser has not seen is never offered. The heuristic bot now prices an exile target by the card's value. Data, not a closure: no snapshot shape, wire field or closure-ceiling change. The ability-exile `Card.ExiledWith` stamp (ADR 0155 PR 3) is separate and still open. ADR 0019 amendment 2026-10-13. **Cards:** The Darkness Crystal (its caveat — the card was chosen as the ability resolved — is gone) and Pull from Eternity, both `Full`.
