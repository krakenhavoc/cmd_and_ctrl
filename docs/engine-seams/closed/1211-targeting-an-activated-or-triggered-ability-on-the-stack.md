**Targeting an activated or triggered ability on the stack** (#1211, CR 115.4 / CR 701.5c, [ADR 0019 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md), [ADR 0043 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0043-copy-effects.md)) — the row three closed seams above left open in their "still open" clause (`Stack-item retarget`, `Ability-copy`, `Modal and multi-target clauses`) and ADR 0065 named first: every target clause walked ZONES, the stack zone holds spell cards, and an activated or triggered ability is a `StackMeta` entry with a synthetic id and no card anywhere — so "counter target activated or triggered ability" and the "or ability" half of "target spell or ability" were unwritable. **The gap was never the rule.** The engine has been able to COUNTER an ability since S13.1 (`counterAbilityLocked`); what it could not do was NAME one. #1223 built the half it needed for Strionic Resonator (`TargetSpec.Abilities` + `AbilityOK`, enumerating `StackMeta` by `Seq`), and generalising it to the whole catalog is one constructor: **`TargetSpellOrAbility` sets `Zones: {ZoneStack}` AND `Abilities: true` on one spec**, so the zone walk finds the spell cards, `abilityItemsBySeqLocked` finds the ability items, both append to `LegalTargets.Cards` and both answer an ordinary `TargetRef{Kind: TargetCard}` — no fifth `TargetRefKind`, because the wire, the picker, `internal/legal` and the CR 608.2b re-check all key on "a uuid that has to still be there", which is as true of an item as of a card. The two id spaces overlap by design (a spell item's id IS its card's instance id) and `specMatchLocked` resolves it by checking `StackMeta` first and excluding `StackItemSpell` there. **The predicate vocabulary is over the ITEM**, not the card: Bolt Bend's "with a single target" is a fact about the announcement and a spell and an ability have the same announcement record, so `StackItemPredicate` narrows both halves at once (`ItemHasASingleTarget`, `AnAbilityItem`, `ASpellItem(Legendary())`, `AnyStackItem`) — which is how Tale's End says "activated ability, triggered ability, or legendary spell" in one clause. **A mana ability is unreachable for free**: CR 605.3b keeps it off the stack, so the printed "(Mana abilities can't be targeted.)" needs no predicate, no check and no caveat. **The CR 702 keyword gate stays off** — an ability is neither a permanent nor a player, so nothing about it can have hexproof — and **CR 701.5c is a deletion, not a zone change**: no `routeCardToZoneLocked`, no CR 903.9 window, no destination to redirect, and the source permanent does not move. Two bugs on that path are fixed rather than worked around: `CounterAbility` (the public entry) was a second copy of the deletion, the #529 shape that let the two counter paths drift, and now delegates; and the emitted `EventCounterSpell` put the COUNTERED ability's own source in `Source`, which `events.go` reserves for the counter and the game log reads as "who countered it" — so the line came out as *"Llanowar Elves countered `<uuid>`"*, the victim in the attacker's place and a raw id in the victim's. The event now carries `Target`, `CardID` and `Label`, and the log renders the label, because a countered ability has no card to name. **What needed nothing**: `RetargetStackItemForEffect` and `OfferRetargetForEffect` (#1196) are written over `StackMeta` already, so the "or ability" half of the redirect family is one word in a clause constructor; `CounterTargetForEffect` has discriminated on `item.Kind` since S13.1; the bot's enumerator and the protocol's `stampLegalTargets` are id-agnostic; the stack overlay already lights any item in the legal set. The client's share is three strings — the `stack_ability` and `stack_item` target modes beside `stack_spell` (the banner's sentence, `isTargetingStack`) and the cast flow's mode allowlist, now `opensTargetPicker` in `targeting.ts` rather than a chain of `===` in `Board.svelte`, since a mode missing from it fires `cast_spell` with no targets and the server refuses it with nothing on screen to say why. **7 cards:** Stifle, Tale's End and Voidslime are new and `full`; **Disallow**, **Deflecting Swat**, **Bolt Bend** and **Sublime Epiphany** lost their caveats and are `full` — the Epiphany got its printed SECOND bullet back, kept as a bullet of its own rather than merged into the first with a wider clause, because CR 700.2 lets "choose one or more" take both. **Still open, and each a different shape**: **Trickbind**'s second sentence ("activated abilities of that permanent can't be activated this turn"), a per-permanent activation restriction with a turn duration that [ADR 0073](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md) already prices — the card is not shipped; **Spellskite** (#302), which changes a target TO a named object; and **"counter target ability unless its controller pays"**, whose guard (`game/counter_unless_paid.go:105`) is keyed to a spell's stack card and which no card in the gap needs yet.
