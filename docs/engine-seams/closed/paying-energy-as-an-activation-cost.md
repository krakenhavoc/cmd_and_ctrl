**Paying energy as an activation cost** (#1995, [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md) §2, §7 and §8, owner decisions 1, 4 and 5, PR 1) — energy stays a counter on the player (CR 107.14, CR 122.1). `AbilityCost.Energy` is "Pay N {E}" and `AbilityCost.EnergyX` is "Pay X {E}", which `DemandsX` counts; X may not exceed the activator's energy (CR 118.3). Both are checked with life before anything is paid and paid through `payEnergyLocked` (`game/energy_cost.go`), the one path that pays energy: the counter map, the legacy `Player.Energy` mirror, and a negative `EventPlayerCounterPlaced` naming the payer and the source, with no replacement window. Energy is never waived; strict, permissive and force_cast decide only the mana. A short activator is refused with `ErrInsufficientEnergy`, "Not enough energy (have 2, need 3)", the same text the row's `cant_activate` carries. The view stamps `energy_cost` and `energy_cost_x`, and the client draws {E} as an energy pip and caps an energy X at the seat's energy. The enumerator gates on the engine's predicate, ladders an energy X for a target bound by X and names the energy on `MoveCost.energy`; the heuristic prices energy at a flat `Weights.Energy` per counter, spent and gained (`Purpose.energy`); the board text prints each seat's player counters for the model tiers and the MCP seat. **Cards** (55, all Full): Consulate Surveillance, Gonti's Aether Heart, Aether Theorist, Aethergeode Miner, Aethersphere Harvester, Aethersquall Ancient, Aethertide Whale, Aethertorch Renegade, Aetherwind Basker, Architect of the Untamed, Automated Assembly Line, Bespoke Battlewagon, Bristling Hydra, Chthonian Nightmare, Consulate Turret, Deadlock Trap, Demon of Dark Schemes, Dr. Madison Li, Dynavolt Tower, Electrostatic Pummeler, Era of Innovation, HELIOS One, Janjeet Sentry, Longtusk Cub, Minister of Inquiries, Multiform Wonder, Peema Aether-Seer, Peema Trailblazer, Phyrexian Ironworks, Roil Cartographer, Scurry of Gremlins, Shielded Aether Thief, Shipwreck Moray, Solstice Zealot, Sphinx of the Revelation, Spontaneous Artist, Stone Idol Generator, Synth Eradicator, Tempest Harvester and Whirler Virtuoso, and the cards that only give energy: Aether Meltdown, Attune with Aether, Electrosiphon, Empyreal Voyager, Glassblower's Puzzleknot, Glimmer of Genius, Highspire Infusion, Inspired Inventor, Live Fast, Maximus, Knight Apparent, Reservoir Walker, Rogue Refiner, Sage of Shaila's Claim, Tune the Narrative and Woodweaver's Puzzleknot. **Still open:** mana abilities with an energy cost (Aether Hub and three more, `pay-energy-cost`). **Moved to other rows:** energy paid as an ability resolves (`energy-resolution-payments`), as an alternative cost or a keyword's cost (`energy-alternative-costs`), and "whenever you get {E}" and "paid or lost this turn" (`energy-paid-or-lost`); Aetherflux Conduit and Aetherworks Marvel (#2110), Plasma Caster (#1863), Hightide Hermit (#1882), Rex, Cyber-Hound (#1557), Emissary of Soulfire (#2538), Unstable Amulet (#2539) and Gonti's Machinations (#2540).
