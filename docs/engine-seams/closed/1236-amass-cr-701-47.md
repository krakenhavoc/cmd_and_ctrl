**Amass (CR 701.47)** (#1236, CR 701.47a-d, [ADR 0087](decisions/0087-amass.md)) — the seam had no row of its own: the roadmap's detector files it under "Keyword actions with no primitive (proliferate, surveil, explore, connive, amass…)", where it was the last unclosed member. **The rules number in the issue is wrong** — amass is CR **701.47** in the pinned edition (`MagicCompRules 20260819`); 701.35 is the number it had before the Alchemy and Attraction keyword actions were inserted ahead of it, and 701.51 is *Open an Attraction*. Corrected in the ADR and in the code, the way #1260 corrected storm's. **The verb is a counted keyword action**, not a card primitive: `KeywordActionAmass`, a count on `RepEventKeywordAction`, one arm of `applyResolvedKeywordActionLocked` — the three pieces proliferate, scry, surveil and earthbend have, and the answer to #1236's third bullet (a future "double the counters from amass" doubles the INSTRUCTION, one level above the placement, which stays a plain `AddCounterByForEffect` so Hardened Scales and Doubling Season keep working). It **acts at a count of zero**, earthbend's rule for earthbend's reason: the count is a number of COUNTERS and the other three sentences do not depend on it, so Summons of Saruman off an empty graveyard still leaves an Army — which then dies to CR 704.5f, which is why the template's 0/0 is `PrintedPTKnown`. **The Army token is DERIVED from the keyword, not tabled and not an ADR 0083 template.** Not the catalog, because the Army is vanilla and `checkTokenTemplate` refuses a template with no ability — which is also why this is ADR 0087 and not an amendment to 0083. Not `tokens_table.go` either: `TokenCard` panics on a missing key, four species have a printed Army token card today (Zombie, Orc, Goblin, Sliver) with four more named by amass cards outside tournament-legal sets, the next set can name a ninth, and CR 701.47a *derives* the characteristics from the keyword's parameter rather than reading them off a card. `effects.ArmyToken(subtype)` is that derivation, pinned against the real Scryfall dump for all four printed species by the #1127 guard. **Find-or-create** is `armiesControlledLocked` then ADR 0061's one token path (`CreateTokensThenForEffect`, because the creation can pause), with the board RE-READ in the continuation — so a Doubling Season really does make two Armies and the pick below becomes a real question, which is the ruling. **The pick** is `QueueChooseCardsForEffect` over the battlefield, Min 1 / Max 1 — `effects.SacrificeChoice`'s door, the one prompt kind that carries a continuation — asked only when the controller has MORE THAN ONE Army, because Orcish Bowmasters amasses on every opponent draw and a modal you cannot answer wrongly is a worse table than a silent one. The enumerator needed nothing (`PendingChoiceChooseCards` has been enumerated since #544); `legal/amass_choice_test.go` pins that every Army is offered, nothing else is, every move dispatches and none is marked always-legal. **The fourth sentence is the one #1236 did not see**, and it is why this sits on tracker [#889](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/889): *"if it isn't a [subtype], it becomes a [subtype] in addition to its other types"* is a layer-4 continuous effect with no stated duration, `PinnedTo` the object for CR 611.2a + CR 400.7 — `animateEarthbentLandLocked`'s layer-4 arm one subtype over — registered only when the Army does not already have the type, which is both the rule's "if it isn't" and the reason a wheel does not leave a hundred identical layer effects in the census. **CR 701.47c** ("the Army you amassed") is a second continuation on `keywordActionTail`, `func(*Game, uuid.UUID)`, run exactly once and run with `uuid.Nil` on the abandoned path because CR 701.47b says the player amassed anyway. **4 cards:** Orcish Bowmasters (#295, the card the issue was filed for — `caveats`, Notion Thief's draw-step caveat word for word), Dreadhorde Invasion (#306) and Eternal Skylord (#451) `full`, plus Widespread Brutality, which is not on a batch list and is here because it is the only printed card that exercises CR 701.47c and the "non-Army" read side. **Closed since** (#1282, [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md) §5ac): a continuation that survives a paused counter placement. `AddCounterByThenForEffect` carries CR 701.47c's clause, so it runs when the counters land. **Also closed** (#1289, [ADR 0007 §7](decisions/0007-stack-foundation.md) and [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md) §5ae): a fresh 0/0 Army whose counter placement pauses survives to get its counters, because state-based actions wait for a resolution paused on its own prompt. **Still open:** the other seventy-two amass cards, each waiting on its own row — Barad-dûr (#309) on the mana pipeline and a per-turn death tally, Lazotep Plating (#388) and Gleaming Overseer (#389) on protection / prevention, Sauron, the Dark Lord (#390) on the Ring, Saruman, the White Hand (#391) on cost modification. Trackers [#889](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/889) (the subtype half) and [#882](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/882) (the three CR 614 windows it rides: the keyword action, the token creation and the counter placement — none of them new).
