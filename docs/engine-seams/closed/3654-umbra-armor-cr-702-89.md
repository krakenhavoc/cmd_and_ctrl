**Umbra armor (CR 702.89, formerly totem armor)** (#3654, [ADR 0013 §5p](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)) — "If enchanted permanent would be destroyed, instead remove all damage marked on it and destroy this Aura." A catalog replacement on the Aura's Spec, `effects.UmbraArmor()`, backed by `game/umbra_armor.go`. It watches the same destroy `RepEventMove` regeneration does (`ev.Destruction`): a destroy spell, a wipe, and lethal or deathtouch damage (CR 704.5g/h) are replaced; sacrifice, zero toughness (CR 704.5f), exile and bounce are not. It cancels the move, clears the damage through `clearBattlefieldDamage` and destroys the Aura (an indestructible Aura survives, and the creature is still saved). It is not a regeneration, so "can't be regenerated" does not stop it. The enchanted permanent's controller orders it against a regeneration shield or another umbra (CR 616.1). Two copies of one umbra destroy different Auras, so the new `ReplacementEffect.ActsOnItsSource` flag takes it out of #792's shared identity, and two Bear Umbras prompt instead of collapsing. A wipe that destroys a creature and its umbra together keeps the creature: the destroy entry points move hosts before the Auras attached to them (`hostsBeforeAttachmentsLocked`), so the Aura is still there when its host's leg asks. **Cards:** Bear Umbra (Full). Unblocked and not yet written: Snake, Hyena, Lion, Eel, Spider, Felidar, Boar, Treefolk, Octopus, Crab, Mammoth, Drake, Indrik and Penumbra Umbra. Still open: granting umbra armor (Umbra Mystic, Estrid's Mask token) and Dog Umbra's conditional umbra armor.
