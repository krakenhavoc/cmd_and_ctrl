**A commander moved to pay a COST is offered CR 903.9** (#1397, CR 903.9 / 601.2h / 602.2b / 605.3a, [ADR 0013 §5af](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md#5af-amendment-2026-09-24-a-commander-paid-as-a-cost-is-asked-before-the-payment-not-during-it)) — not a catalog seam but an engine gap every cost component shared, in two opposite directions. The discard, return-to-hand and exile payers (Thrill of Possibility, cycling, ninjutsu, Quirion Ranger, Cadaverous Bloom, Grim Lavamancer, scavenge, the Spirit Guides) settled with `MustSettleNow` and never asked the owner; the sacrifice and alternative-cost payers (Viscera Seer, Ashnod's Altar, Village Rites, a Force of Will pitch, escape) paused half way through the payment, leaving the commander spendable a second time. **Now every announcement asks first**: `ActivateCatalogAbility`, `CastSpell` and `ActivateManaAbility` pass `askCostCommanderLocked` (`game/cost_commander_choice.go`) after validating and before paying; a commander among the cards the payment would move parks the announcement on the existing `optional_replacement` prompt, addressed to its OWNER (an opponent, for a stolen commander), and the answer re-makes the announcement with the answer carried onto each move (`zoneRoute.commanderAnswer` → `ReplacementEvent.commanderAnswer`, read by the gather). The payment still settles in one step. No wire change; the enumerator and bots answer the prompt as any other yes/no. **#1445** adds the other half. A card whose exit an EFFECT has paused (a destroyed commander waiting on its owner) is refused by every one of those announcements with `ErrChoicePending` (`refusePausedCostCardsLocked`), and the auto-tapper neither plans nor spends it (ADR 0013 §5af addendum). **#1427** extends that refusal to a cost that only TAPS the card — its own `{T}`, crew, tap-another, convoke and waterbend — through the same gate (second addendum). **#1474** closes the last two uses: casting or playing the paused card itself (CR 601.2a moves it before any cost, so it was in no cost list), and activating any ability of it or naming it as a counter-removal source. Both go through the same gate, and `castable_here` agrees (third addendum). Not closed: the auto-tapper's own cost moves (no commander carries a self-costed mana ability), and a CR 616 ordering between two OTHER replacements on a sacrificed or alternative-cost card, which can still pause those two payers ([#1420](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/1420)).
