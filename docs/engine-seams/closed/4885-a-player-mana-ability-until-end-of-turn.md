**A player mana ability until end of turn** (#4885, CR 605.3a, CR 119.4) — a mana ability a player has, bound to no card: Channel's "until end of turn, any time you could activate a mana ability, you may pay 1 life. If you do, add {C}." Every mana ability was a row on a card. The grant is now `PlayerStatic.ManaAbility` (`game.PlayerManaAbility{LifeCost, Produced}`), one more payload on the player's ability slice: plain data, swept at cleanup by the CR 611.2 duration machinery, cloned for undo and carried in the snapshot additively. `GrantPlayerManaAbilityForEffect` writes it, `PlayerManaAbilitiesLocked` lists it and `ActivatePlayerManaAbility` pays the life (refused when the player can't, CR 119.4) and adds the mana with no stack. **Wire:** it rides `activate_mana_ability` with `player_ability` (the granting object) in place of `card_id`, so it is accepted wherever a card's mana ability is: with priority, while a "pay {N}" prompt is open and on a controlled seat. `PlayerView.mana_abilities` lists it, and the client draws one button per ability beside the seat's mana pool ("Channel: pay 1 life, add {C}"), greyed when the life can't be paid. **Planning:** the auto-tapper never spends the life; mana floated with the button pays a cost from the pool (CR 601.2g). The enumerator offers one `KindMana` move per payable grant, with no Source and a life Cost, and the heuristic prices it below a free float, so a bot never takes it. **Cards:** Channel and Yavimaya Bloomsage // Channel (Full); the Bloomsage's power-7 check is the counter placement's keyed tail (`yavimaya-bloomsage/prepare`).
