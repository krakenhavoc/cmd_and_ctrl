**Trigger suppression** (#1735, CR 603.2 / CR 603.10, [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-10-01](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — closes item (4) of the "Stopping abilities" row: "Creatures entering don't cause abilities to trigger" and its family. It is neither a replacement effect (the event still happens) nor an ability removal (the permanents keep their abilities). It is a static that decides whether an event triggers an ability at all. `Spec.TriggerSuppressors []game.TriggerSuppressor` is the `TriggerDoubler`'s sibling, read from the same per-event candidate list (`scanTriggerModifiersLocked`), and `harvestMatchLocked` asks it **first**: before the once-per-batch guard, so a suppressed match does not spend a "whenever one or more" slot, and before the doublers, so a suppressed ability is never doubled. With an Elesh Norn on each side of the table, nothing triggers on entry at all. A suppressed ability makes nothing: no instance, no "you may" prompt, no target prompt, no `EventTrigger`. `harvestMatchLocked` now takes a `triggerOrigin` (a permanent, a spell, or neither) in place of `fromSpell`, which is what lets Elesh Norn's "abilities of permanents your opponents control" skip emblems and cards in other zones. **Which board is asked** follows CR 603.10: an enters trigger is judged after the event, so a suppressor that enters together with the creature applies (including Elesh Norn's own entry), and one that has already left does not, even as a member of an open simultaneous exit (`modifierCandidate.live`). A dies trigger looks back in time, so a Hushbringer that dies in the same wipe still stops every death in it, its own included. Every harvest site is covered, including **evoke's sacrifice**, which is the creature's own enters trigger, so an evoked creature stays under Torpor Orb. Event-conditioned **delayed triggers** are covered too, and a suppressed one stays queued: an earthbent land that dies under Hushbringer does not come back. Helpers: `SuppressesEntering(filter)`, `SuppressesDying(filter)`, `OfOpponentsPermanents(s)` and `CreaturesEnteringDontTrigger(label)` in `effects/trigger_suppression.go`. They share the doubler's `isEntering` / `isDying`. No wire, snapshot or bot change. **Cards:** Torpor Orb, Hushwing Gryff, Tocatli Honor Guard, Doorkeeper Thrull and Hushbringer (Full); Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines loses its caveat and goes Full. That is every card the row's waiting list named for this item. "Trigger suppression" joins the coverage table's mechanic probes. **Still open on the row:** a scoped "creatures lose all abilities" (Dress Down, Tishana's Tidebinder).
