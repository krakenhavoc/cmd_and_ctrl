**Replacing losing the game, and replacing planeswalking** (#6096) — "If you would lose the game, instead …" and "If you would planeswalk, instead …" (CR 614.1a). Both are statics a permanent prints as plain data, `Spec.GameActionReplacements` (`game.GameActionReplacement`), read off the battlefield through `CatalogAbilityKey` like a "can't lose" gate, so nothing is stored and no func is reachable from `Game` (`game/game_action_replacements.go`). The loss half is read at both doors a loss goes through, the state-based loss pass and `loseGameLocked`, after the gate (a player who can't lose is not one who would): `ReplaceLoseGameExileThisLifeBecomes` exiles the permanent and sets the life total by gaining or losing the difference (CR 119.5), emits `EventGameLossReplaced`, and replaces every loss one check would deal (CR 704.7). A concession is never replaced (CR 104.3a). The planeswalk half is read in `PlaneswalkForEffect`: `ReplacePlaneswalkOrderTopTwo` shows the planeswalking player the top two cards of the planar deck (the communal one, CR 901.15c) and asks, as a keyed option pick (`planechase/order-top-two`), which goes to the bottom; each source applies once (CR 614.5), then the planeswalk goes on. Lich's Mirror, Transcendence and other "if you would lose the game" cards with a different replacement need their own kind. **Cards:** The Golden Throne, Susan Foreman (Full).
