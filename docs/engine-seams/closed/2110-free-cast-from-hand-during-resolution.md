**Casting a spell from your hand for free as an ability resolves** (#3270, #2110, [ADR 0099](decisions/0099-discover.md) amendments 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-15, CR 608.2g) — `effects.offerFreeHandCast` queues a `choose_cards` pick tagged `game.ChooseCastFree` and grants `Game.FreeCastFromHandPermission`: `{0}` claimed under `AltCostKeyGrantedCost`, `TimingFlash`, `CastOnly` and `LapseOnPass: LapseStaysInHand`, so the holder's next priority pass ends the window. The client opens the cast chain the moment the pick is answered and the enumerator offers the same cast to a bot. Two more shapes sit on it. "Any number of spells from your hand" is `grantFreeHandCastsFrom`: one such grant per nonland card in hand, no prompt, so casting one leaves the rest open and one pass closes them all. "Look at the top N cards … you may cast a spell from among them" is `lookAtTopAndOfferFreeCast`: a private look, a pick tagged `game.ChooseCastFreeFromLibrary` (wire `cast_free_library`) over the nonland cards, the rest to the bottom in a random order, and `Game.FreeCastFromLibraryPermission` over the pick where it sits (`LapseToLibraryBottom`, so an uncast pick goes under with the rest); the client finds the card in the viewer's library pile and opens its cast chain. The cast is still an ordinary `cast_spell` taken right after the resolution (ADR 0066, ADR 0091, ADR 0099 §4). **Cards:** Buster Sword, Kari Zev's Expertise, Rishkar's Expertise, Surtland Elementalist, Great Intelligence's Plan, Cecily, Haunted Mage and Aetherworks Marvel (Full); Aetherflux Conduit (Caveats: its energy needs the mana spent recorded).
