**Putting the permanent itself on the bottom of its owner's library as a cost** (#2726, CR 602.2b, 601.2h, 118.3) — Timestream Navigator's "{2}{U}{U}, {T}, Put this creature on the bottom of its owner's library: Take an extra turn after this one" had no cost component to name the source, and shipping it without one would have given a free extra turn every time. `game.AbilityCost.BottomSelf` (`effects.PutThisOnTheBottomOfItsOwnersLibrary`, `game/bottom_self_cost.go`) is `ReturnSelf`'s sibling one zone over: paid at announce through the one zone-route exit with cause cost, so the permanent is in its OWNER's library before anyone can respond, the leaves-the-battlefield triggers see it go, and the ability resolves from the permanent's last-known information (CR 400.7, 608.2h). A commander is asked CR 903.9b first, with the library as its destination (ADR 0115). Paid from the battlefield or, since #4973's batch 24, from the graveyard for a card's own graveyard ability (`game.BottomSelfZoneSupported`: Daniels, Temporal Agent's "{2}, Put this card from your graveyard on the bottom of your library"), one component per object, and the source is out of the other components' picks and the auto-tapper's plan; the wire carries `bottom_self` and the heuristic prices the loss like a self-return. **Cards (2):** Timestream Navigator, `full`; Daniels, Temporal Agent, `full`. With it the ascend row no longer waits on a cost shape; Tilonalli's Summoner still waits on a "may pay {X}{R}" as its trigger resolves.
