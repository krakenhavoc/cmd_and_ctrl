**Putting the permanent itself on the bottom of its owner's library as a cost** (#2726, CR 602.2b, 601.2h, 118.3) — Timestream Navigator's "{2}{U}{U}, {T}, Put this creature on the bottom of its owner's library: Take an extra turn after this one" had no cost component to name the source, and shipping it without one would have given a free extra turn every time. `game.AbilityCost.BottomSelf` (`effects.PutThisOnTheBottomOfItsOwnersLibrary`, `game/bottom_self_cost.go`) is `ReturnSelf`'s sibling one zone over: paid at announce through the one zone-route exit with cause cost, so the permanent is in its OWNER's library before anyone can respond, the leaves-the-battlefield triggers see it go, and the ability resolves from the permanent's last-known information (CR 400.7, 608.2h). A commander is asked CR 903.9b first, with the library as its destination (ADR 0115). Battlefield only, one component per permanent, and the source is out of the other components' picks and the auto-tapper's plan; the wire carries `bottom_self` and the heuristic prices the loss like a self-return. **Cards (1):** Timestream Navigator, `full`. With it the ascend row no longer waits on a cost shape; Tilonalli's Summoner still waits on a "may pay {X}{R}" as its trigger resolves.
