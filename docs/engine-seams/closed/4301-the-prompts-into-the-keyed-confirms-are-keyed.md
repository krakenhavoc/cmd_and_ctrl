**The prompts that lead into the keyed confirms are keyed** (#4301, follow-up to #4298, amendment of 2026-10-24 to [ADR 0018](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md), [ADR 0168](decisions/0168-the-london-mulligan.md), [ADR 0088](decisions/0088-ordered-library-placement.md) and [ADR 0133](decisions/0133-opening-hand-actions.md)) — a table waiting on Ponder's look, Portent's order, Sylvan Library's or Lazav's pick, or a London-mulligan bottoming was not a restore point, though the confirm after each was. **Engine:** `LookAtTop.ThenTail` (a `look_at_top` prompt now carries `scryThen` to the snapshot), `PutInLibrarySpec.ThenTail` / `PutInLibraryInAnyOrder.ThenTail` / `LookAtLibraryThenPlace.ThenTail` (a `put_in_library` prompt carries `libraryOrderKeyed`, written as `pendingChoices[].libraryOrder`), and `seats[].mulliganBottoming` written. **Cards:** Ponder, Portent, Sylvan Library and Lazav, Familiar Stranger are saveable from their first prompt. **Mulligan:** the bottom pick is a keyed `choose_cards` prompt and its order a keyed `put_in_library` prompt; the pick stays private across a restore, and the view no longer sends a private pick's candidates to the other seats. Six keys appended to the ledger; closure counts unchanged. **Still a closure:** the search prompt itself (`searchResume`), so Tempt with Discovery's four searches are not restore points yet; the closure forms of the three `Then` fields; a commander tucked by Sylvan Library and Lazav's exile when they stop on a CR 903.9 or CR 616 prompt.
