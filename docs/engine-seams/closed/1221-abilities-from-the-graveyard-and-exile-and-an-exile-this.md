**Abilities from the GRAVEYARD and exile, and an exile-this cost component** (#1221, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)'s 2026-09-22 addendum and [ADR 0071](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)'s) — finishes the ACTIVATED half of "Ability activatable from a non-battlefield zone" that #660 started. The SHAPE was already there and had been since #660; what was missing was every consumer past the activation path. `internal/legal`'s enumerator grew `abilityZones()`, the activation sibling of #1014's five-zone cast walk and deliberately one pile shorter — hand, graveyard, command, exile, and NOT the library, because CR 401.2 makes it hidden and reading it would touch cards the seat is not entitled to see to answer a question whose answer is always "nothing". `protocol/view.go`'s `stampHandAbilities` became `stampZoneAbilities` over the same four, with the CR 108.4 "you" read off `Card.Owner` for exile (one shared pile, everybody's cards) and off the seat for the per-seat piles. **The wire field `hand_abilities` became `zone_abilities`**, one field rather than a second list beside it, and — the half that matters — it stopped riding the hand's own secrecy: a graveyard is PUBLIC, so writing one seat's rows to the exported field would have shipped their `legal_targets` to the whole table, which is #1055 and #1167's surface arriving one field over. The rows ride `castOffers` now and `publicIn` drops them, so the seat whose card it is gets the list and nobody else does (a merge rather than a `stampsFor`, because `stampLegalTargets` files the same map first and replacing it would blank a flashback card's announce surface). `AbilityCost.ExileSelf` is scavenge's and embalm's "Exile this card from your graveyard" — `DiscardSelf`'s sibling one zone over, a second bool rather than a zone on one component because discarding is a CR 701.8 keyword action with its own event and cause while exiling as a cost is a plain CR 406 move with neither; validated before anything is paid, paid last through the one exit primitive with `MustSettleNow`, and refused at BOOT on an ability that does not declare the graveyard. The effects read their own source back out of exile by instance ID rather than off a snapshot on the stack item: a card outside the battlefield has no layers, so the printed power scavenge wants is the same number the graveyard held. `effects.Unearth(cost)`, `Scavenge(cost)`, `Embalm(cost)` and `Eternalize(cost)` build the abilities; `ExileInsteadOfLeavingBattlefield` is factored out of Whip of Erebos and shared with unearth, and its two caveats (turn-scoped, and a bounce bypasses it) are inherited whole. The client's zone browser wires the pop-over it was already capable of rendering and had never been given. Shipped on Dregscape Zombie (unearth), Deadbridge Goliath (scavenge) and Sacred Cat (embalm). Still open on this family: nothing — the mana half closed with #1228, below. Ninjutsu (CR 702.49) shipped with #1227 — the return-an-unblocked-attacker cost and the attacking entry are both built, and the ZONE half of it needed nothing from this row; statics that function from a graveyard are the other row of the same issue.
