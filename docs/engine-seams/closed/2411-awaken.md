**Awaken** (#2411, [ADR 0135](decisions/0135-alternative-costs-that-tap-discard-awaken-and-emerge.md) §3, PR 4) — "Awaken N—[cost]" is an alternative cost (CR 118.9) whose target statement is the spell's own clauses followed by "target land you control", so the land is a target only when the awaken cost is paid (CR 702.113b, 601.2c) and every gate that already reads an alternative cost's rewrite judges it. `effects.Awaken` declares the offer, `effects.AwakenAfter` runs the printed instruction and then the awaken, and `game.AwakenForEffect` is the verb (owner decision 4): the N +1/+1 counters go on first, through the CR 614 placement window, so Doubling Season doubles them and Hardened Scales does not see a creature yet (CR 608.2c), and then the land becomes a 0/0 Elemental creature with haste that is still a land, in one record with earthbend's animation and no duration (CR 611.2a), pinned to the object (CR 400.7), with no return trigger. The offer view gained `clauses` for a multi-clause rewrite, which the client already walks. `Purpose.AwakenLand` (owner decision 6) lets the heuristic price the land as a hasty N/N creature beside the spell's own purpose. **Cards:** Boiling Earth, Clutch of Currents, Coastal Discovery, Earthen Arms, Encircling Fissure, Mire's Malice, Ondu Rising, Part the Waterveil, Planar Outburst, Rising Miasma, Roil Spout, Ruinous Path, Rush of Ice, Scatter to the Winds and Sheer Drop (Full).
