**Abilities any player may activate** (#1793, CR 602.2 / CR 602.1a / CR 602.1b / CR 109.5 / CR 113.8, [ADR 0106 §1](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md)) — "Any player may activate this ability", which CR 602.2 lets an object say and the engine could not: every gate assumed the activator was the controller. `ActivatedAbility.AnyPlayer` (mirrored on `game.ActivatedAbilityShape`) is the row's own CR 602.1b permission, so a copy or a layer-6 grant of the row carries it and losing the ability loses it; nothing is serialised. One predicate, `game.MayActivate(player, source, zone, ab)` — the controller, or anyone for an AnyPlayer row on the battlefield, the CR 108.4a owner off it — is asked by `ActivateCatalogAbility`, by the enumerator (`activatedMoves` now visits other players' permanents for their AnyPlayer rows only, behind a fast negative) and by the view. Everything after that question already read the activator: the board-wide gate, the timing read, the `Condition`, every cost validator and payer (CR 602.1a), and the stack item's controller (CR 602.2a, 113.8), so "you" in the effect is the activator (CR 109.5) and "this permanent's controller" is `ctx.SourcePermanent().Controller`, last-known if it has left (CR 608.2h). The once-per-turn and exhaust records stay keyed by object and label (CR 602.5b). Arrest still stops every player. Register refuses AnyPlayer beside a {T}, loyalty, crew or sacrifice-this component and on a non-battlefield zone. The view marks the row `any_player` and gives every other seat its own copy stamped with that seat as the activator — price, life, condition, timing, and the option lists out of that seat's own hand — through the #1369 per-seat carrier (`stampAnyPlayerOffers`). The ADR 0105 digest needed no change: built from the seat's own moves, it lists another player's Xantcha under its ID for the activator alone. The client opens the ability popover on another player's permanent for its `any_player` rows, lights the bolt pip from the digest, and smart autopass does not count such a move as a response (owner decision 1). `ActivatedAbility.Purpose` (`game.ActivationPurpose{Draws, ControllerLosesLife}`, wire `purpose`) is the bot's reason to reach across: the heuristic never chooses a row on another player's permanent without one (owner decision 2), and the enumerator's label names the controller for the model tiers. Xantcha, Sleeper Agent lands Full, with 27 more any-player cards in the follow-up batch (26 Full; Fan Favorite carries a caveat for assist). Nine more wait on their real blockers: Lethal Vapors (skipping a turn), Deadly Designs and Task Mage Assembly (state triggers), Glittering Lion and Glittering Lynx (losing one printed ability), Mercenaries (a shield against the next damage from one source), Nullhide Ferox (a discard caused by an opponent), Land's Edge (the card discarded to pay) and Wall of Vipers (a target relative to the source). Out of scope: any-player mana abilities (Mana Cache) and abilities of a spell on the stack (Lightning Storm).
