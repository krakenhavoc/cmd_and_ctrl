**Divided damage** (#1563, CR 601.2d / CR 700.2i, [ADR 0065 amendment 2026-09-28](decisions/0065-modal-and-multi-target-clauses.md)). "N damage divided as you choose among …" is now a clause property: `TargetSpec.Divide` (`game.DivideSpec`: a fixed `Total`, or `FromX`, with `DoubleFromX` for Shatterskull Smashing's "twice X if X is 6 or more"), declared with `.Dividing(Divide(4))`, `DivideX()` or `DivideXDoublingFrom(6)`. It is data, not a func, so the ADR 0041 closure ratchet gains no route. The caster announces the split with the targets, keyed by target id, on `cast_spell`, `activate_ability` or the `pick_target` answer to `resolve_choice`. One gate, `settleDistribution`, runs after the target gate at all three points (CR 601.2d / 602.2b / 603.3d): each target at least 1, the shares summing to the amount under the announced X, never more targets than the amount, and a lone target handed the whole amount unasked. The settled map is `StackItem.Distribution`, which undo, snapshot, copy (CR 707.10) and retarget (CR 115.7c) already carried and which tests now pin. `effects.DealDividedDamage` / `PutDividedCounters` deal each surviving target exactly its share; a departed target's share is lost, not redistributed (CR 608.2b). The view ships the amount as `LegalTargetsView.divide`, the client asks for a share per target once a divided step has two or more picks, and the bot announces `game.EvenDistribution` (remainder to the earliest targets), never offering a set the gate refuses (#544). **Cards:** Fury, Dragonlord Atarka, Shatterskull Smashing and Abzan Charm are now `full`; Inferno Titan, Bogardan Hellkite, Fire Covenant and Mogg Mob are new. **Still open, and a different shape:** an amount read off the board or a paid cost rather than a constant or the announced X (Lathiel's "up to that many", Ureni, Orca, Avacyn's Judgment's madness X), and divided prevention (Remedy).
