**Planeswalker statics and eminence** (#2797, [ADR 0140](decisions/0140-eminence-statics-from-the-command-zone.md) and dated amendments to ADRs [0093](decisions/0093-abilities-granted-to-other-permanents.md), [0032](decisions/0032-planeswalkers.md), [0018](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md) and [0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md); CR 606.3 / 606.6 / 704.5i / 113.6; Reality Fracture tracker #2795) — five pieces the set's planeswalker cards share. (1) **A loyalty ability granted to a class** ("Planeswalkers you control have '[−8]: …'"): `effects.GrantAbilitiesToYourPlaneswalkers(keys…)` over an ADR 0093 bundle whose row has a `LoyaltyCost`. The row is the walker's, so CR 606.3's one activation a turn is shared with its printed rows and with every other grantor's, in either order; the recipients are read live, so a walker that arrives later has the row and one that leaves takes it. (2) **A static that stops CR 704.5i applying** (`Spec.ZeroLoyaltyExemptions`, `game/zero_loyalty_exemption.go`): the LegendRuleExemption pattern, read live by the state-based pass and keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey`; every other state-based action still applies, and a walker at 0 can use a plus ability and no minus. (3) **"Whenever you put one or more loyalty counters on a planeswalker"** (`effects.WheneverYouPutLoyaltyCountersOnAPlaneswalker`): one trigger per `EventCounterPlaced`, so a +2, a doubled placement and a bulk placement are each one trigger, a removal is none, and "each planeswalker" is one per walker. (4) **The stored half of an activation-timing statement** (`PlayerStatic.ActivationTiming`, `game/activation_timing_grant.go`): "until end of turn, you may activate loyalty abilities of Jace planeswalkers you control … any time you could cast an instant" is plain data with a duration, read by `activationTimingVerdictLocked` beside the derived Teferi statements, so the engine, the bot's list and the wire's `timing_closed` agree; additive snapshot data. (5) **Eminence** (`game.CostModifier.Eminence`, `effects.Eminence(...)`): a cost modifier that also works while its card is in its owner's command zone, bound with that player as "you" and gathered nowhere else. **Cards**, both Full: Kiora of Salt and Sand and The Ur-Sphinx. Sanctum Lurker, Inspired Tethermage, Jace's Machinations, Avatar of Burgeoning Echoes and the "Way of the …" cycle also need `empower Jace` (#2796) and wait on that row.
