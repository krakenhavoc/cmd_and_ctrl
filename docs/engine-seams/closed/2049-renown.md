**The renowned designation** (#2049, CR 702.112, [ADR 0071 amendment 2026-10-09](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)) — an eighth designation with Monstrous's lifecycle, and renown as a keyword the engine runs. `Card.Renowned` is set as a renown trigger resolves and kept until the permanent leaves the battlefield: not copiable, kept through a control change, cleared at both CR 400.7 sites, carried by clone, the snapshot (additive to the v7 shape) and `PermanentInfo` for last-known information. `DesignationRenowned` gates "as long as this creature is renowned", `EventBecameRenowned` (Amount = N) is what "becomes renowned" watches, and `g.IsRenowned(id)` answers "if it's renowned". `renown` joins `canonicalKeywords` as a numbered family key like `annihilator`: the tokens are `renown N`, the deck importer reads N off the oracle line, and the token is cumulative (CR 702.112c). Each instance is one engine trigger on combat damage to a player (never a planeswalker, a battle or a creature) with the intervening "if it isn't renowned" read as it triggers and again as it resolves (CR 603.4), resolving through the keyed body `renown/grow`: N +1/+1 counters through the CR 614 window, then the designation. A v7 corpus board freezes a renown trigger on the stack beside a renowned creature. `CardView.renowned` and a RENOWNED badge carry it to the table. **Cards** (8, all Full): Enshrouding Mist, Goblin Glory Chaser, Valeron Wardens, Kytheon's Irregulars, Undercity Troll, Outland Colossus, Consul's Lieutenant and Scab-Clan Berserker; and nine creatures whose only text is keywords and renown need no card file (Akroan Sergeant, Citadel Castellan, Firefiend Elemental, Knight of the Pilgrim's Road, Pharika's Disciple, Rhox Maulers, Stalwart Aven, Topan Freeblade, War Oracle). **Not yet catalogued:** Aragorn, Hornburg Hero, Relic Seeker, Constable of the Realm, Acolyte of the Inferno and Honored Hierarch.
