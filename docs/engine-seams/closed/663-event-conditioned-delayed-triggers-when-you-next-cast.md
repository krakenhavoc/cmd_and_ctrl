**Event-conditioned delayed triggers, "when you next cast"** (#663, CR 603.7b, [ADR 0026 amendment 2026-09-18](decisions/0026-delayed-triggers.md)) — closes the "When you next cast" delayed-trigger row (count 7). `DelayedTrigger` gains an event condition beside its step: `On []EventKind` plus an `AppliesTo` predicate, checked by ONE hook at the end of `triggerHarvester.OnEvent` after the zone walks, where the first matching event fires the trigger and removes it. It fires once (CR 603.7b) and ends with the turn whether or not it fired — carrying [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md)'s `Duration` rather than a private int, so `durationExpiredLocked` stays the one place the engine decides a duration is over, and swept by `sweepTurnEndLocked` beside the scoped statics and the turn-scoped replacements (CR 514.2). It never sees the cast that created it, because that spell's `EventCast` was emitted before the resolution that scheduled it — the ordering does the work and nothing has to remember the exclusion. A fired trigger goes through `dispatchTriggerLocked`, the harvester's own dispatch, so the CR 603.5 "you may", the CR 603.3d drop, the CR 603.3b ordering prompt and the APNAP drain are one implementation rather than two; the triggering event's object rides on the item as `Payload`, which is how "copy THAT spell" names the spell without the `Effect` closing over it. This REVERSES ADR 0026 §1-2, which is why the amendment is dated and says what was rejected (a second `TurnScopedTriggers` registry) and why. The catalog side is `effects.WhenYouNextCast(label, pred, effect)` and the general `DelayedOnEvent{…}`, over the existing `CopySpell`. Shipped on Doublecast and Galvanic Iteration, both full. Unblocked and still to be written: Summon: Fenrir (#312), Thunderclap Drake (#313), Sword of Wealth and Power (#313), Yuna, Grand Summoner (#397), Ral, Storm Conduit and Chandra, the Firebrand (#397, both also want emblem-free minus abilities on a planeswalker), Teach by Example, Complete the Circuit and Rimefire Torque — each still needs its own check for other gaps.
