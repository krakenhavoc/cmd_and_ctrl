**Controlling another player** (#2164, CR 723, [ADR 0166](decisions/0166-controlling-another-player-word-of-command.md), [ADR 0167](decisions/0167-controlling-another-players-turn-emrakul-the-promised-end.md)) — `Game.PlayerControls` records who controls whom (`game/player_control.go`) and `ControllerOfPlayerLocked` is the one question (CR 723.5). Prompts keep their real chooser and show the controller `on_behalf_of`; `actions.routeControlledPlayer` re-addresses a controller's action to the controlled seat and refuses the controlled player everything but `concede` (CR 723.6); `FilterViewFor` shows a controller what the controlled player can see (CR 723.4). Word of Command is CR 723.2's limited form, a `controlled_play` prompt. Emrakul, the Promised End is CR 723.1's whole turn: `Game.BeginTurnControlForEffect` (`game/turn_control.go`) schedules a record that begins as the controlled player's next turn begins (CR 723.1b) and ends at the rotation seam, queuing the extra turn directly after that turn (`queueAnchoredExtraTurnLocked`, CR 500.7). The controller may send every rules verb as that player, the sandbox is closed to both, the controller's `legal_moves` and `controlled_legal_actions` carry the controlled seat's moves, and a bot plays such a turn passively (`aiseat.controlledTurnPolicy`). The client's "Playing as Bob" board swap (`lib/playingAs.ts`, `PlayingAsBanner.svelte`) puts the controlled seat at the bottom of the controller's board, acts for it in the dock, combat and click to act, and stays on the board the controller chose, offering a jump chip by the dock (#4434) when the other seat owes the decision; `FilterViewFor` hands the controller that seat's cast stamps and ability rows. **Cards:** Word of Command and Emrakul, the Promised End, both Full; Mindslaver, Worst Fears, Sorin Markov's −7, Construct a Cosmic Cube and Cruel Entertainment (#3857) use `TurnControl.NoExtraTurn`, the same record with no extra turn. Opposition Agent (#3998, ADR 0166's 2026-10-16 amendment, Full) controls a player only while their library search prompt is open (`Until "search"`, `game/search_control.go`), and the search primitive exiles what they find with a play permission for the Agent's controller; a bot answers such a search with `aiseat.controlledSearchPolicy`. Secret of Bloodbending (#3999, ADR 0167's 2026-10-19 amendment, Full) controls a player for one combat phase (`Until "combat"`, `game/combat_control.go`): the record begins as their next combat phase begins, an additional one included, ends as the cursor leaves that phase, and is played like a turn (`ControlUntil.PlaysForThem`) except that `end_turn` and the cleanup discard are closed; its optional "waterbend {10}" is `effects.OptionalWaterbend`, an optional additional cost that becomes the cast's tap cost while it is chosen (`game/optional_waterbend.go`). The Dominion Bracelet (#4000, ADR 0093's 2026-10-19 amendment, Full) grants the equipped creature an ability whose cost exiles the Bracelet (`AbilityCost.ExileGrantor`, `game/exile_grantor_cost.go`) and whose own cost clause reads the creature's power.
