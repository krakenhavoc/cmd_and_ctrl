**Controlling another player** (#2164, CR 723, [ADR 0166](decisions/0166-controlling-another-player-word-of-command.md), [ADR 0167](decisions/0167-controlling-another-players-turn-emrakul-the-promised-end.md)) — `Game.PlayerControls` records who controls whom (`game/player_control.go`) and `ControllerOfPlayerLocked` is the one question (CR 723.5). Prompts keep their real chooser and show the controller `on_behalf_of`; `actions.routeControlledPlayer` re-addresses a controller's action to the controlled seat and refuses the controlled player everything but `concede` (CR 723.6); `FilterViewFor` shows a controller what the controlled player can see (CR 723.4). Word of Command is CR 723.2's limited form, a `controlled_play` prompt. Emrakul, the Promised End is CR 723.1's whole turn: `Game.BeginTurnControlForEffect` (`game/turn_control.go`) schedules a record that begins as the controlled player's next turn begins (CR 723.1b) and ends at the rotation seam, queuing the extra turn directly after that turn (`queueAnchoredExtraTurnLocked`, CR 500.7). The controller may send every rules verb as that player, the sandbox is closed to both, the controller's `legal_moves` and `controlled_legal_actions` carry the controlled seat's moves, and a bot plays such a turn passively (`aiseat.controlledTurnPolicy`). The client's "Playing as Bob" board swap (`lib/playingAs.ts`, `PlayingAsBanner.svelte`) puts the controlled seat at the bottom of the controller's board, acts for it in the dock, combat and click to act, and switches between the two seats only at a handoff; `FilterViewFor` hands the controller that seat's cast stamps and ability rows. **Cards:** Word of Command and Emrakul, the Promised End, both Full. **Still open:** none requested. Mindslaver and Sorin Markov's −7 need a `TurnControl` that owes no extra turn, then a card file each; Opposition Agent (control while searching) is not built.
