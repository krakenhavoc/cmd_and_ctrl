**Carry the amount of mana a mana ability produced on its event** (#5644) — `EventManaAbilityActivated` is emitted when the cost is paid, before any mana exists, and `EventManaAdded` is one event per mana with no ability around it, so nothing could answer "whenever a mana ability of this creature resolves, put a number of +1/+1 counters on it equal to the amount of mana this creature produced". `game.EventManaAbilityResolved` is one event per mana ability that finished resolving, with `Amount` the mana that ability produced after the CR 106.12b window and without the mana another ability added because of it. Every route emits it: the hand click, the last answered colour pick, both arms of the auto-tapper and each triggered mana ability (CR 605.1b), whose `Source` is the trigger's own permanent. A pick that is a mana ability's output carries `PendingChoice.ManaAbilityRun` and `ManaAbilityAmount` (additive, in the snapshot), so the answer announces the total after a restore; answering it also brings the layers up to date and puts the triggers it queued on the stack. It is written only while a permanent on the battlefield has an ability watching it (`battlefieldWatchesLocked`), so every other game's event log is unchanged, and the public log is silent on it. **Cards:** Tyvar the Bellicose (Full).
