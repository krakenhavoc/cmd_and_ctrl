**Behold N as part of a flashback cost** (#4689, [ADR 0025](decisions/0025-alternative-costs.md)'s 2026-11-03 amendment, CR 701.4a, CR 702.34a, CR 601.2b, CR 601.2f–h, CR 118.9) — "Flashback—{1}{R}, Behold three Elementals" had no shape: `game.AlternativeCost` carried no reveal or behold, and behold named one card, as an additional cost. `AlternativeCost.Behold` (the additional cost's `RevealCost{Behold}`) with `BeholdCount` is the offer's card component, built with `effects.FlashbackBehold(mana, n, subtype, plural)`. The caster names N distinct objects on `alt_cost_ids`, each from the reveal walk the additional cost's behold reads (`RevealCostOptionsForEffect`: a matching hand card or a matching permanent they control, a changeling included, never the spell). `validateBeholdPaymentLocked` holds the picks to exactly N with no repeat, `payBeholdLocked` reveals the hand cards with the spell on the stack and moves nothing, and `AlternativeCostPayableLocked` withholds the offer with fewer than N candidates. The view ships `pay_options` (min == max == N) and `behold: true` (additive); the client's pay picker reads "Behold", the enumerator offers the permanents first, and the bot charges nothing for the picks. No snapshot field: the offer is catalog data and `StackItem.AltCost` and `PaidCost.AltCostObjects` already carry the cast. **Cards:** Kindle the Inner Flame (`caveats`: a copy of the token isn't sacrificed).
