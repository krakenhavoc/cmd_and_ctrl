**A creature that can't be regenerated this turn** (#1887, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §2, PR 1) — "<that creature> can't be regenerated this turn" (CR 701.19c) is a `ScopedEffect` rules-gate kind, `cantBeRegenerated` (`game.ModCantBeRegenerated`, reader `rule`), pinned to the permanent until cleanup by `Game.CantBeRegeneratedThisTurnForEffect` (CR 400.7, 514.2). There is one regeneration gate, `regenerationRefusedLocked` (`game/regeneration.go`), and it reads both the destroy instruction's own rider (`DestroyOptions.CantBeRegenerated`, Wrath of God) and the turn's mark. The shield built-in asks it in its AppliesTo, so a refused shield is not applied and not spent; a catalog static regeneration ("If this creature would be destroyed, regenerate it", CR 701.19b) is `effects.RegenerateIfThisWouldBeDestroyed`, which asks it through `Game.RegenerationAllowedForEffect`, and `TestEveryRegenerationAsksTheGate` reads every replacement in the catalog and the engine and fails one that regenerates without asking. The card side is `CantBeRegeneratedThisTurn` and the dealt-damage continuation `CantBeRegeneratedIfDealtDamage` ("A creature dealt damage this way", Incinerate, Flamebreak). Whippoorwill's "When the creature dies this turn, exile the creature" is an event-delayed trigger pinned to the object (`effects.ExileWhenItDiesThisTurn`, keys `dies/the-listed-object` and `dies/exile-from-graveyard`), so the creature dies, its dies triggers see it, and the card is then exiled from the graveyard if it is still there (CR 603.7c). The view shows a NO REGEN chip (`CardView.cant_be_regenerated`). **Cards** (all Full): Whippoorwill, Incinerate, Disintegrate, Carbonize, Scorching Lava, Engulfing Flames, Rage of Purphoros, Gravebind, Hurr Jackal, Furnace Brood, Flamebreak, Jaya Ballard, Task Mage, Orcish Healer, Lim-Dûl's Cohort. **Moved to other rows:** Bone Shaman and Runesword (#1946), Clergy of the Holy Nimbus and Knight of the Holy Nimbus (#1947).
