**Until end of combat on your next turn, timed cost reductions, and a timed enters-tapped replacement** (#6093, CR 511.3, 601.2f, 611.2a, 611.2b, 614.1c, [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md), [ADR 0041](decisions/0041-game-persistence.md)) — three shapes a resolving effect needed and the engine did not have. **The duration** is the fifteenth kind, `game.UntilEndOfCombatOnYourNextTurn` (persisted as 14, appended before the sentinel), stamped by `Game.UntilEndOfCombatOnYourNextTurnDuration(player)` with the player's next seat-turn (`ExpiresAtTurnsBegun`, as "until your next turn"); in that turn it is over once a combat phase has ended (`aCombatPhaseEndedThisTurnLocked`: the cursor has left the first combat phase, a second has begun, or the turn has reached a second main phase or its ending phase with a skipped combat behind it), read off the turn's phase tally and never during the turn's first beginning phase, where the turn-start sweeps run before the tally is emptied; any later turn of the player's ends it too (CR 800.4m). Card side: `effects.DurationUntilEndOfCombatOnYourNextTurn(ctx, player)`, Brazen Cannonade's impulse window. **The cost reductions** are two scoped rule mods read by the cast pricer beside Tax Collector's increase (`game/scoped_cost_reduction.go`): `spellsCostLessForPlayer` ("spells <player> casts [of these card types] cost {N} less", `game.SpellsCostLessForPlayerMod`) and `exiledSpellsCostLessForPlayer` (the same for spells cast from exile, `game.ExiledSpellsCostLessForPlayerMod`), each a CR 601.2f reduction of generic mana only, lasting for the record's duration; a kind for exile rather than a zone field, because Mod's one zone field is the damage-source shield's. **The replacement** is the scoped mod `creaturesEnterTappedForOpponents` (`Game.CreaturesEnterTappedForOpponentsForEffect`, `game/scoped_enters_tapped.go`): Authority of the Consuls' "creatures your opponents control enter tapped" as a record with a duration, gathered with the other scoped replacements. Snapshot: no new field; an older binary refuses a restore point holding any of them through `DurationKind.Known` and the unknown mod kinds. **Cards:** Brazen Cannonade, Commander Liara Portyr, Will Kenrith, Cone of Cold (Full).
