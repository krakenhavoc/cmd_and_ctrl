**A prevention effect's additional effect that asks a question at once** (#2040, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) amendment of 2026-10-16) — a prevention follow-up (CR 615.5) that asks a player something is answered as the damage is prevented. The body runs with the resolution open, so its prompt holds the CR 704.3 boundary. After combat damage, that means before the state-based actions (CR 510.2). While the prompt is open, no other follow-up runs. They wait in the queue and are asked after the answer, of whoever controls the object then. The damage instruction that asked parks its continuation (a `...ThenForEffect` caller, an Each walk's total) until the answer, ahead of a modal spell's next bullet. `choose_cards` gains a keyed continuation (`game.RegisterChooseCardsThen`, ledger lines `choose <key>`), and choose-a-player gains a keyed form, so a table waiting on such a question stays a restore point. Not covered: a catalog effect written as plain sequential steps runs its later steps at once, as it does after a CR 616 ordering prompt. **Cards:** Khârn the Betrayer and Channel Harm (Full). Immortal Coil loses its caveat.
