**Shuffle after a replacement move** (#1735, [ADR 0013 §5ah](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md#5ah-amendment-2026-09-29-a-redirected-library-destination-can-ask-for-a-shuffle)): Blightsteel Colossus's "reveal it and shuffle it into its owner's library instead" landed the card on TOP of the library, because the CR 614 pipeline could redirect a move's destination but had no hook to shuffle once the card actually got there — a real information leak (everyone at the table knows the top card). **`ReplacementEvent.ShuffleDestinationLibrary`** is the fix: a bool a replacement's `Replace` sets alongside the redirected `NewZone`, consumed once by whichever of the two exit primitives performs the physical landing — `executeBattlefieldLeaveLocked` (die/sacrifice/SBA) or `executeZoneRouteLocked` (discard, mill, exile, bounce, tuck, counter) — both of which now call `ShuffleLibraryForEffect` on the settled destination. A mill's redirect lands the card back in the SAME library it started in (`dstZone == src`, previously treated as a pure no-op); that branch shuffles too, since there is nowhere to move the card FROM but it still has to end up at a random position rather than exactly where it was. Blightsteel's own `AppliesTo` also grows `RepEventDiscard` alongside `RepEventMove` — a discard is its own `ReplacementEventKind` (§5g) under a different `Watches` key, so its "from anywhere" covered only move-shaped exits before this, and a discarded Blightsteel fell straight into the graveyard. Blightsteel moves to `CompletenessFull`. Checked the Eldrazi titans named alongside it in #1735 and found a different mechanic, not this seam: their printed clause triggers a shuffle of the whole graveyard AFTER the titan has already landed in it, rather than redirecting the titan's own move — recorded in the ADR rather than built, and gated on Annihilator (not implemented) regardless.
