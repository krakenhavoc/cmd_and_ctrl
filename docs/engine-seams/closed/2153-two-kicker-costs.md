**Two kicker costs on one card** (#2153, CR 702.33b / 702.33d / 702.33f, [ADR 0073 amendment 2026-10-05](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)) — "Kicker {R} and/or {W}" is `effects.Kickers("{R}", "{W}")`: two `Kicker` entries in printed order, both keyed `game.KickerKey`, which `Register` now allows for that key alone (never three kickers, and the two must differ in mana). Each is a once-only toggle announced by index, so payment, price, the view's two offers, the client's toggles and the record needed no change, and every "was it kicked" reader counts both: both paid is "kicked twice". CR 702.33f's "if it was kicked with its [A] kicker" is `game.KickedWithPaid` / `game.CardKickedWith` / `ctx.KickedWith` / the `effects.ThisKickedWith` trigger condition, keyed by the printed cost; a CR 707.10 copy keeps which kicker was paid. The bot enumerator offers the pair together as one extra announcement. No wire change. **Cards:** Urborg Lhurgoyf drops its relabelled one-payment multikicker for the real shape; Archangel of Wrath, Thornscape Battlemage and Sunscape Battlemage ship, all Full. **Not catalogued here:** the other two-kicker cards (Ana, Nightscape, Stormscape, Thunderscape and Wastescape Battlemage, the five Volvers, Stronghold Arena, Temporal Firestorm, Vodalian Mindsinger, Illuminate) are card work on this shape plus whatever else each prints. Wastescape's "when you cast this spell, if it was kicked with its {G} kicker" asks `game.KickedWithPaid` of the spell's record on the stack (`StackItemPaidForEffect`), not of a permanent's.
