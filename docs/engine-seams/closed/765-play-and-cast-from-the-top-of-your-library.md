**Play and cast from the top of your library** (#765, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)) - `castZoneFromWire` and `castSourceZoneLocked` gained `library`, resolved to the caller's own; the top-card restriction rides `CastPermission.TopOfLibraryOnly` rather than the zone, and lands go through `cast_spell` so a land on top reaches the same land branch, spends the same land drop (CR 305.2) and runs the same CR 614 entry pipeline. Visibility (CR 401.5) is per PLAYER and kept on the POSITION, not on the card — `Card.KnownBy` cannot express "the top card", which changes identity on every draw, mill, shuffle and scry — so `LibraryTopVisibility` (`game/library_top.go`, `Spec.LibraryTopVisible`) is derived from the battlefield and resolved LAZILY at the only two places that need an answer: the view projection and `CastPermissionForLocked`. Nothing has to be invalidated and CR 401.6 falls out. The per-viewer filter is widened by exactly one case: an opponent's library keeps its top card, and only when that viewer knows it AND the standing rule put it there — a one-shot "reveal the top two" does not open the zone. Shipped on Bolas's Citadel (caveat: its ten-permanent sacrifice ability), Oracle of Mul Daya, Courser of Kruphix and Realmwalker. **#1035 made the position rule follow the CARD**: it used to read the permission HOLDER's own library, which scoped every printed clause by accident ("your library", all four cards) and made a permission over ANOTHER seat's library top open nothing at all — silently, before the cast path, the enumerator or the view could ask. Three pieces came with it and are the shape a cross-seat card needs: `CastPermission.ZoneOwner` names the pile a STANDING permission is over (no seat named still means the holder's own, so two Coursers do not read each other's library); `CastPermission.SeesLibraryTop` carries CR 401.5's LOOK, because the per-library derivation cannot express "you may look at the top card of THEIR library" — it is per (library, viewer) and granted by a resolution rather than printed as a static ability; and `LibraryTopVisibleToLocked` is the one predicate both the cast path and the knower stamp read. Xanathar, Guild Kingpin is the printed shape and is not in the catalog; nothing catalogued needs any of it, which is why the hole went unnoticed. Still waiting on something else: Conspicuous Snoop (#394); Korlessa, Scale Singer (#398); Emperor Mihail II (#403); Sigarda, Font of Blessings (#394); Augur of Autumn (#303); Thundermane Dragon (#397, cast provenance #653); Mystic Forge and Vizier of the Menagerie (spend-mana-as-though restrictions); Fortune Teller's Talent (#333, Class levels).
