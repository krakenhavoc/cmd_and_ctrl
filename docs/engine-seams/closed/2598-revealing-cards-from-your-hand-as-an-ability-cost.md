**Revealing cards from your hand as an ability's cost** (#2598, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)'s 2026-10-08 amendment, CR 602.2b / 701.20) — `AbilityCost` had no reveal component; the only reveal cost was a spell's either/or branch (one card, a creature type), so the Martyr cycle's "{1}, Reveal X black cards from your hand, Sacrifice this creature" could not be written. `game.RevealCardsCost{RevealCost, N, CountFromX, Label}` (`effects.RevealX` / `effects.RevealN`) embeds that branch's `RevealCost`, so the candidate walk and the quality test are shared, and `RevealCost` gained a `Color`. The count is a printed `N` or the X announced with the activation (`DemandsX` is true; no `{X}` symbol, so the mana cost is the printed one whatever X is; X may be zero). The activator names the cards in `reveal_ids`; revealing moves nothing (CR 701.20b), so the cards stay in the hand, every seat learns them, and no other component's pick is excluded. `Register` refuses it beside another claim on the announced X, a zero count, an unknown colour, a clause with no label and behold. The wire carries `reveal_cost_label`, `reveal_cost_n`, `reveal_cost_count_from_x` (public) and `reveal_cost_options` (the controller's hand, stripped from every other viewer); the client reuses its discard picker with the verb "Reveal" and skips the X stepper; the enumerator offers the first 1, 2 and 3 matching cards, holds an "up to X" target count to each payment's X, never offers the X = 0 no-op for a card that declares `XMatters`, and offers nothing when no card in hand matches. **Cards:** Martyr of Bones, Martyr of Sands, Martyr of Spores, Martyr of Frost, Martyr of Ashes (all `full`). **Still open:** "Reveal this card from your hand" (forecast, augment, Tetzimoc), which reveals the source rather than naming cards, and Illuminated Folio's "two cards that share a color".
