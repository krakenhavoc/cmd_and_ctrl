**Exhaust ("Activate each exhaust ability only once")** (#1181, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) exhaust addendum 2026-09-22) — 41 printed cards, and the shape is ONE record plus ONE bit. **`Game.Activations`** (`game/activation_tally.go`) counts ANNOUNCEMENTS — CR 602.2b, not resolution, because an exhaust ability countered on the stack is still spent — in two scopes at once: `Ever`, never reset, which exhaust reads, and `Turn`, emptied on the turn advance beside `TurnTally`, which the per-source activations row above will read. Both are keyed by `ObjectTallyKey(source, Card.ObjectEpoch, label)`, the key `ResolvedThisTurn` already uses, so CR 400.7 falls out rather than being coded: a flicker refreshes an exhaust ability, a phase-out would not (phasing is not a zone change and must not bump the epoch when it is built), and a copy has its own (CR 707.2). The LABEL and not the ability's index, because an ADR 0071 designation switching an ability on renumbers the list. The card side is `ActivatedAbilityShape.Exhaust bool` and no per-card logic; `effects.Register` panics at boot on a blank label, on two exhaust abilities of one card sharing a label, and on a label that prints the keyword without the bit or the bit without the keyword. **One reader, `Game.AbilityExhausted`**, for the three places the rule has to hold: `ActivateCatalogAbility` refuses with `ErrAbilityExhausted` before anything is paid, `internal/legal` does not enumerate the move, and `ActivatedAbilityView` ships `exhausted` so the client greys the row — #544's rule as a property of there being one reader. It is neither `Condition` (which may hold again tomorrow) nor `ActiveWhen` (which makes the ability absent); Bitter Work prints both and the order is timing → exhaust → condition. `Game.Activations` is classified `carried`, so #1020's round-trip test enforces it, and it rewinds with `Clone` / `RestoreFrom`. **Cards:** Prowcatcher Specialist, Greenbelt Guardian (an exhaust ability beside a repeatable one), Bitter Work and Ba Sing Se (whose earthbend is deliberately NOT exhaust), all four `full`. **The MANA half landed with #1183** ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) exhaust addendum, note of 2026-09-22): `ManaAbilityShape.Exhaust` / `effects.ManaAbility.Exhaust` are the twin bit, `Game.ManaAbilityExhausted` the twin reader over the SAME record and the SAME key, and `ActivateManaAbility` (CR 605.3b — no stack, no priority) writes it at the point every gate has passed and the first payment is about to be made. TWO write sites, which the CR 602 path does not need: the hand click and the AUTO-TAPPER's executor (`materializePlanLocked`), which taps a permanent and mints its mana directly. FIVE readers: the activation path, `legal.manaMoves`, `ManaAbilityView.exhausted`, the auto-tapper's one shared picker (`autoTapAbilityFor`, so the planner and the executor cannot disagree), and CR 106.7's `ProducibleManaLocked` — the one activation restriction that reader asks about, declared as a weaker-than-printed narrowing so a Reflecting Pool is not priced on mana a spent Loot can never make again. `effects.Register`'s label checks run over BOTH ability lists with ONE `seen` set, because the two kinds share one key space on one object. **Card:** Loot, the Pathfinder, `full`. **The cards that read the record from OUTSIDE landed with #1184** ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) exhaust addendum, note of 2026-09-22), and they were never one gap — three mechanisms wear one keyword. (1) **The activation event**: `EventActivateAbility`, carrying the ability's `Label` (the record's own key) and an `Exhaust` bit stamped at the announce, because by the time a watcher runs a `SacrificeSelf` cost may have ended the object; `EventManaAbilityActivated` carries the same two at both of its write sites. Built wider than its two cards so the "whenever an OPPONENT activates an ability" row above closes on it. (2) **The permission**: `AbilityExhausted` / `ManaAbilityExhausted` take the ASKING PLAYER, because "as though they haven't been activated" is not a fact about the object — the record still says spent, and every opponent still reads it that way. Not an entry point that clears the record: the permission is continuous while its conditions hold, and a clear would be visible to everyone and survive its source dying, which "as though" never does (CR 609.4). Activating under it writes the record again, which is what makes Elvish Refueler self-limiting with no code. `Spec.ExhaustPermissions` is the card side, gated by ADR 0071 and CR 613.1f like every other static. (3) **The priced activation**: `CostQuery.Ability` is the ability being priced and `CostModifier.Activations` partitions the board's modifiers into cast-pricers and activation-pricers — without the partition, Sphere of Resistance's nil `AppliesTo` ("every spell") would have started taxing every `{T}` ability in the game. One `AbilityManaCostForEffect`, paid by the activation path and read by `internal/legal`, which is #544's rule with the sign reversed: an enumerator pricing at the printed cost would HIDE legal moves. **Cards:** Rangers' Refueler, Afterburner Expert and Elvish Refueler `full`; Boom Scholar `caveats` (the ability's menu row still lists the printed cost, and mana abilities are not priced through the pass). Still open: those two narrowings, and Avatar Kuruk, which wants extra turns (#753).
