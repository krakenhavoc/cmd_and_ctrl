**Two counters of any kinds from the source** (#3987, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) amendment 2026-10-16) - "Remove two counters from Ghost-Spider" is `RemoveCountersOfAnyKindsFromThis(2)`: #943's any-kind among payment with the source as its one candidate (`CounterRemovalCost.Among` with no `From`). The activator names how many of each kind come off, so a +1/+1 and a shield counter is a legal payment; the validator, the enumerator, the protocol view and the client's per-kind picker are the among form's, and the client pays straight from the menu when the source holds one kind. Shipped on Gwen Stacy // Ghost-Spider, Gnarlbark Elm, Loch Mare and Reaping Willow.
