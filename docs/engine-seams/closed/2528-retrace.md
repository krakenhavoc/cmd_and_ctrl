**Retrace, and granting it from a graveyard rule** (#2528, CR 702.81, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-10-07](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)) — retrace is "cast this from your graveyard by discarding a land card in addition to paying its other costs", and #652's last open piece. It rides a graveyard-bound priced offer with a new card component, `AlternativeCost.DiscardFromHand` (a land from hand discarded as a COST through `discardCardsLocked`, so a discard payoff sees it and a countered spell does not give it back), whose mana is the card's printed cost; `effects.Retrace("{cost}")` bundles it with `Spec.CastableZones`, and a granted retrace is `CastPermission{AltCostKey: AltCostKeyRetrace, DiscardLandCard: true}`, which synthesises the same offer from the same `RetraceDiscardSpec`. The model bends in one stated place: retrace is claimed as an alternative cost, so it cannot combine with another on one cast. Two permission shapes ship on it: Six's "during your turn, nonland permanent cards in your graveyard have retrace" (a `Spec.CastPermissions` entry with the new `PermissionFilter.NonLandPermanentOnly` and `TimingYourTurnOnly`) and Wrenn and Six's emblem (`EmblemSpec.CastPermissions`, which `standingCastPermissionsLocked` reads off the seat's `Player.Emblems`). **Eleven printed-retrace cards and both granters ship Full**: Flame Jab, Raven's Crime, Syphon Life, Oona's Grace, Monstrify, Savage Conception, Waves of Aggression, Embrace the Unknown, Spitting Image, Throes of Chaos and Decaying Time Loop, plus Six and Wrenn and Six (whose −7 is no longer omitted). [#2550](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/2550) added the printed ones that needed only data: Deeproot Historian (a union grant filter, `PermissionFilter.CreatureTypesAny`) and the four token makers (Cenn's Enlistment, Call the Skybreaker, Worm Harvest, Formless Genesis, on three new token-table rows and a hand-built X/X Shapeshifter), all Full. Reality Scramble shipped afterwards on the existing reveal-until primitive (#2781). Glamerdye waits on text-changing effects (CR 612, #2779), which is not retrace machinery.
