**The total mana spent, read as the permanent enters** (#1735, CR 601.2h, [ADR 0068 amendment 2026-10-02](decisions/0068-the-mana-spent-on-a-spell.md)) — closes the "total spent at entry" item of the "Mana that does something when it's spent" row. The record was never missing: `StackItem.Paid.Mana` already held every mana the payment spent (generic, coloured, X, kicker and other additional mana, the commander tax and other increases, after reductions; not Phyrexian life, convoke, waterbend or delve, and nothing for a free cast), and `ManaSpent.Total()` counts it. What was missing was the read inside the CR 614 window. `game.CastCounts.ManaSpent` is that total, set by `castCountsFor` from the one record, so a waived payment (strict mana off) reads 0 and a CR 707.10 copy reads a real 0. `Game.EntryCastCountsForEffect(ev)` hands the whole `CastCounts` to a hook that runs inside the window and is given the event: it reads the resolving item the event already carries across a paused entry, and it answers the zero value for an entry that was not a cast (reanimation, flicker, token). The counter-clause seeder and the accessor share `castCountsLocked`. `CopySelector.Candidates` keeps its signature; `effects.EntersAsCopyOfFromCast` hands the counts to a candidate filter. No wire, snapshot or bot change: the figure is read from the payment record, which already survived a clone and a snapshot round trip. **Cards:** Mockingbird copies a creature with mana value up to the amount spent, as a flying Bird, and keeps only the strict-mana caveat every mana-spent reader carries. Mana Sculpt's caveat claimed the engine kept no record of the mana spent; it now refunds that amount with a Wizard. **Now one `EntryCountersFromCast` clause away, not catalogued:** Verazol, the Split Current, Kurbis, Harvest Celebrant, Gyrus, Waker of Corpses, Marath, Will of the Wild, Dyadrine, Synthesis Amalgam. **Still open on the row:** granted readers (#1552), where this accessor is the read half of Lux Artillery's granted sunburst.
