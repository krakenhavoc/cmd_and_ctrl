**The monarch from a card effect** (#1722, CR 725, [ADR 0096](decisions/0096-the-monarch-from-a-card-effect.md)) — `Game.SetMonarch` took `g.mu` itself, so "you become the monarch" deadlocked from any `OnResolve` or trigger `Effect`, and no catalog card could hand out the crown. `becomeMonarchLocked` is now the one write to `Game.Monarch` — the card effect (`SetMonarchForEffect`, the `effect_api.go` convention), the CR 725.2 combat-damage steal, the CR 725.4 hand-on and the sandbox's manual `set_monarch` all go through it — and it emits **`EventMonarchChanged`** (Actor = new monarch, Target = previous) only on a real change of holder (CR 725.3), so "whenever you become the monarch" fires once per becoming. `layerVersionBump` invalidates on it, for "as long as you're the monarch" statics; `TurnTally.MonarchAtStart` (read through `Game.MonarchAsTurnBegan`) stamps the crown as each turn begins. Card side (`cards/effects/monarch.go`): `BecomeTheMonarch`, `WhenThisEntersYouBecomeTheMonarch`, `WheneverYouBecomeTheMonarch`, `AnOpponentBecameTheMonarch`, `YoureTheMonarch`, `AnOpponentIsTheMonarch`. Palace Jailer's "until an opponent becomes the monarch" is not an ADR 0063 duration — it is an event-conditioned delayed trigger (#663's shape) on `EventMonarchChanged` with the keyed condition `monarch/an-opponent-became` and an Indefinite duration. A test for CR 725.4's "next player in turn order" clause was added beside the existing active-player one. No wire change; the log gate lists the event as silent. **Cards:** Court of Grace, Court of Ambition, Court of Cunning, Court of Bounty, Court of Ire, Queen Marchesa, Entourage of Trest, Custodi Lich, Knights of the Black Rose, Regal Behemoth and Throne of the High City (all `full`), and Palace Jailer (`caveats`: if its controller leaves the game, CR 800.4a drops the release trigger before the CR 725.4 hand-on, so the creature stays exiled). **Still open:** Court of Locthwain — its monarch half is a single-use free cast from among cards exiled with a source, and a `{0}` cast permission over that set would let every card be cast free. Tracker [#883](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/883).
