**You choose which creatures attack and block** (#4154, CR 508.1, CR 509.1, [ADR 0167](decisions/0167-controlling-another-players-turn-emrakul-the-promised-end.md) second amendment 2026-10-19) — `game.DeclarationControl` is a "you choose which creatures attack / block this turn (or this combat)" effect, made with `Game.BeginDeclarationControlForEffect`. It is not player control: it is projected onto the player-control seam (stage `ControlDeclaring`, until `declarations`) only while its declarer is making one player's declaration, and it takes precedence over any other control of that player (the ADR 0167 ruling). Attacks have two halves (`Game.AttackHandoff`): the declarer stages the attacking creatures with the ordinary verbs, paying nothing and exerting nothing, and finishes with the active player's pass; then the active player points each chosen attacker and decides the costs (the Master Warcraft ruling, CR 508.1b), confirming with `declare_attackers` (the whole attack tax paid at once) or passing (declining every cost; a declined creature is taken out and the declarer chooses again, the Odric ruling). The lock-in waits for both. Blocks: the declarer declares and finishes every other pending defender's declaration. Every restriction, limit and requirement applies throughout. The enumerator offers the declarer the declaration as that player, the bots choose no attackers and no blocks (or the required ones) and accept the targets, and the client's "Playing as" board is how the declarer picks the creatures. **Cards:** Master Warcraft, Odric, Master Tactician, Brutal Hordechief and Melee (all Full; Melee's "this combat" delayed trigger is dropped as its combat phase ends).
