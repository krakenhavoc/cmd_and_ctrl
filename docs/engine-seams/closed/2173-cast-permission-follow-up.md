**A cast permission with an "if you do" follow-up** (#2173, CR 118.12 / CR 400.7h / CR 608.2g) — a cast permission can now make something happen because the spell was cast through it, which "you may cast that card. If you do, you can't cast additional spells this turn" needs: written beside the grant it would bind a player who declined, and written ahead of it it would stop the cast itself. `game.CastPermission.FollowUp` is a key into a registry of bodies (`game.RegisterCastFollowUp`, the `RegisterDrawThen` shape from #2388), so the permission stays plain data and the closure-field ratchet does not move. `CastSpell` runs the body once the spell is on the stack and `EventCast` is out, when the cast was made because of the permission (`castUsesGrantLocked`, the question `CastsLeft` already asks); a lapsed, expired or declined offer runs nothing. `CastPermission.RequiresNoSpellsCast` ends the permission once its holder has cast any spell this turn, which holds "if you haven't cast a spell this turn" across the window the stored permission stands in for the resolution. Snapshot: two additive `castPermissions[]` keys, `followUp` and `requiresNoSpellsCast`. **1 card:** Conduit of Worlds (`full`) — its standing graveyard land play is Crucible of Worlds' permission; the tap ability grants one flash-timed, pass-closed cast of the target card for its mana cost, and the cast alone is followed by a `CastBanOutright` until end of turn.
