**Computed life cost on an activated ability** (#1594, CR 601.2f–g via CR 602.2b, CR 119.4, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-09-28](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md), Decisions 47–48). "Pay life equal to the number of colors in your commanders' color identity" (War Room) and "Pay half your life, rounded up" (Murderous Betrayal, Lurking Evil) are now expressible. The new component is `AbilityCost.LifeFrom`, a `game.LifeCostCount`: a KEY into a registry that `game.LifeCount(key, fn)` fills at init, the same shape as `ModeCountCondition`. It is not a func, so the ADR 0041 closure ratchet gains no route, and the cost survives clone and snapshot as data. The card side is `effects.PayLifeCount(effects.LifeEqualToCommanderColors)` or `(effects.LifeHalfYoursRoundedUp)`, composed with `Plus`. `(*Game).AbilityLifeCostLocked` is the single reader, and three callers use it: the engine reads the count ONCE at announce, checks it against CR 119.4 / 119.8, charges exactly that amount and records it on `PaidCost.LifePaid`, so a commander that changes afterwards changes nothing; the legal enumerator drops an activation whose computed amount the seat cannot pay and puts that amount on `Move.Cost.Life` for the bot; and the view stamps it onto `activated_abilities[i].life_cost`, re-priced per snapshot. War Room's count is `CommanderIdentityColorCountForEffect`, the union over every commander the activator owns (partners and backgrounds), with 0 for no commander or a colourless one. **3 cards**, all `full`: War Room (caveat removed), Murderous Betrayal, Lurking Evil. **Still open:** "Pay X life" on an activated ability (Krumar Initiate, Decision 48). Its life total is a ceiling on X, which the ability enumerator's X search would have to solve, as the cast path does for Toxic Deluge. The card also needs endure. Tracker [#887](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/887).
