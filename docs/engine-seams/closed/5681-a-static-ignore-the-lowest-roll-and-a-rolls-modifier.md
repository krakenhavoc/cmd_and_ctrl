**A static 'ignore the lowest roll' and a roll's modifier beside its natural result** (#5681, CR 706.2 / 706.3a / 706.6 / 706.7 / 614.1a; follows [ADR 0054](decisions/0054-dice-rolls-and-coin-flips.md), amended 2026-11-12) — two gaps in the dice door (`game/dice_roll.go`). A permanent's replacement could add a die only with a chooser (`RollOneMoreAndIgnoreOne`, Ichor Elixir), and the announcement of a roll carried the face alone, so a card that adds to its roll did it inside its tail where nothing else could see it.
- **Ignore the lowest.** `ReplacementEvent.RollOneMoreAndIgnoreLowest()` is "instead roll that many dice plus one and ignore the lowest roll" applied to the roll's window: one more die and one more of the lowest ignored, which joins the card's own `DiceRoll.IgnoreLowest`. Nobody is asked, because rolls tied for the lowest show the same number. Each replacement applies once per roll (CR 614.5), so two roll two more dice and ignore the two lowest. A roll a player chose to ignore goes first, then the lowest of the rest. A roll of planar dice is left alone: the planar die has no numerical result, so none of its rolls is the lowest (CR 706.7). Card side, `effects.RollOneMoreDieAndIgnoreTheLowest(name)`.
- **The modifier.** `DiceRoll.Modifier` (`effects.RollDice.Modifier`) is what the instruction adds to or subtracts from the natural result. Each kept die's `EventRollDie` carries it beside the natural result (`Event.Amount` the face, `Event.Modifier`, `Event.RollResult()` the sum), and the tail is told it (`TailOutcome.RollModifier`, `effects.DiceResult`). So "a natural 20" and "a die's highest natural result" read the face (`effects.YouRolledADiesHighestNaturalResult`), and "if any of those results was 10 or higher" reads the sum (`effects.RolledDice`). `WheneverYouRollDice` now totals results, which changes Vexing Puzzlebox only for a roll that has a modifier. Diviner's Portent's hand moved from its tail to its roll's modifier.
- **Snapshot.** Additive: `modifier` on an event and on a paused roll (`diceRollPauseSnapshot`); a file without it restores as a roll with no modifier. New effect keys: `dice/netherese-puzzle-ward-scry`, `dice/revivify-result`, `dice/song-of-inspiration-result`, `dice/danse-macabre-result`, `prompt-run/danse-macabre-roll`, `choose-cards/danse-macabre-return`, `dice/the-deck-of-many-things-result`, `choose-cards/the-deck-of-many-things-reanimate`, `the-deck-of-many-things/curse` and `the-deck-of-many-things/owner-loses`. No closure field changes.
- **The wire.** Unchanged: a `roll` log entry still shows the natural result of each die.

**Cards:** Pixie Guide, Barbarian Class, Farideh, Devil's Chosen, Netherese Puzzle-Ward, Critical Hit, The Deck of Many Things, Danse Macabre, Revivify and Song of Inspiration — Full.
