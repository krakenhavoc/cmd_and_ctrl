**Gift (CR 702.174)** (#1267, CR 702.174a-m — gift is **702.174** in the pinned August 2026 edition, not the 702.170 the issue cites, [ADR 0089](decisions/0089-gift.md)) — the seam had no row of its own; it was filed while re-triaging roadmap batch 02 (#295), where **Dawn's Truce** (`edhrec_rank` ~360) was waiting, and six catalog cards carried "the gift can't be promised" caveats. **Gift is an ADR 0073 optional cost** whose payment names a player: `AdditionalCost.ChoosesOpponent`, keyed `game.GiftKey`, announced as its index in `optional_costs` plus `CastSpellParams.GiftOpponent` (`cast_spell.gift_opponent`), validated before the targets against `GiftOpponentsLocked` (other seats still in the game), and recorded as `PaidCost.GiftOpponent` — copied by CR 707.10, carried onto the permanent as `CastProvenance.GiftOpponent` by CR 400.7d, shown as `StackItemView.gift_to`. **A paid optional cost may now rewrite the target clause** (`AdditionalCost.Targets`, `game.TargetSpecUnderOptionalCosts`, applied after the alternative-cost rewrite at announce, resolution, the enumerator and the view) — CR 702.174m's "instead … target …", including a clause only the promise has. **Cards declare `Spec.Gift`** (`GiftACard` / `GiftAFood` / `GiftATappedFish` / `GiftATreasure`, `.Instead(clause)`), and `buildDef` grows the cost, the before-`OnResolve` gift for an instant or sorcery (CR 702.174j) and the intervening-if entry trigger for a permanent (CR 702.174b); the card reads `ctx.GiftPromised()`. The bot is offered the unpromised cast and one promise per opponent. Adopted on Dawn's Truce, Into the Flood Maw, Long River's Pull, Wear Down and Peerless Recycling (all now `full`), plus Scrapshooter (the permanent half) and Valley Rally (a Food, and a target only the promise has). **Still open:** Starfall Invocation (its promised branch needs a pick among the cards its own wipe moved — pinned in `coverage/caveats_test.go` under the new `gift` probe), gift an extra turn (no extra-turn primitive), gift an Octopus, and CR 702.174c's "whenever a player gives a gift" (no reader).
