**An optional life payment a permanent offers, with its linked coloured discount** (#5659, #5487, [ADR 0048](decisions/0048-cost-modification.md) amended 2026-11-12; CR 601.2b, CR 601.2f, CR 118.7b, CR 118.7c, CR 119.4, CR 110.4b) — the Defiler cycle prints "As an additional cost to cast blue permanent spells, you may pay 2 life. Those spells cost {U} less to cast if you paid life this way. This effect reduces only the amount of blue mana you pay." The offer needed nothing new: it is a `GrantedOptionalCost` (#3245) whose cost is the fixed `PayLife` component, `EachSource`, keyed `paid_life_for_discount`, so it is refused by the one life reader (`CanPayLifeLocked`: CR 119.4 and "can't pay life"). Two things were missing. **A reduction that can see a granted cost's announcement:** `CostQuery.GrantedOptionalTimesFromSource(key)` reads the announced indices against the same "printed, then granted" list every cast-time reader uses and counts only the entry the modifier's own permanent granted, so each Defiler's discount is linked to its own offer and a second Defiler whose offer was declined reduces nothing. **A coloured reduction with no generic fallback:** `CostModifier.UnitColorsOnly` takes one mana of the unit's colour off the cost and nothing when the cost has none, where a plain coloured `Unit` follows CR 118.7b and 118.7c into the generic part. A grant's `Spells` filter also reads the spell through the granting permanent's changed words now (CR 612.2). Card side: `effects.MayPayLifeToCast`, `effects.CostsColorLessIfLifePaid` and `effects.WheneverYouCastAPermanentSpellOfColor` (`pay_life_for_discount.go`). No snapshot or wire field: the payment rides `PaidCost.GrantedOptional`, which was already recorded. **Cards** (5, all Full): Defiler of Dreams, Defiler of Faith, Defiler of Flesh, Defiler of Instinct and Defiler of Vigor.
