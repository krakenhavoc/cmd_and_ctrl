**An opponent's non-mana choice at resolution** (#568, same ADR amendment) - `PendingChoiceOptionPick` (`option_pick`, `game/option_pick.go`): "choose one of the following", addressed to ANY seat, answered with `{option_index: N}` (routed by kind — zero is the commonest answer). An option is a label, a declared life cost, and optionally the cards it is about; what it MEANS lives in the frame the queuing effect supplies. **A pile split needs no kind at all** — it is two chained prompts to two different seats (#552): `choose_cards` to the splitter with a floor of zero, then an `option_pick` back to the controller whose two options each carry a pile (`Game.QueuePileSplitForEffect` / `effects.PileSplit`). Legality is enforced at QUEUE time (CR 608.2 "as much as possible"): an option the chooser cannot take is never offered, and the first option must be one that always works, because that is the answer `legal.choiceMoves` marks always-legal. **Redaction is one function**, `protocol.redactChoiceCards`, applied to `Options` and to every option's cards: a non-knower loses the card entirely (dropped, not a back — an instance ID out of a hidden zone is the correlation handle PR #513 closed), and the CHOOSER keeps unknown cards as answerable backs only over their own material or another player's HAND, whose size is public (CR 400.2). So a cross-seat prompt over a library must reveal first (#549), which every printed card of the family does. **A dropped option pick still finishes the card** (#1006): the departure table's `dropDefault` action runs the frame with `game.NoChoiceIndex`, from one performer (`runChoiceDropActionLocked`) reached both by the departure sweep and by `dropChoiceLocked`, the door every prune uses — before it, a Torment of Hailfire stopped at the victim who conceded and a pile split put neither pile anywhere. **And a dropped mid-card `choose_cards` does too** ([#1225](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/1225), [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)): the third shape a dropped prompt can have is a `chooseCardsFrame` with no run link and no option frame — `effects.SacrificeChoice`, the sacrifice in the MIDDLE of an effect — and `dropDefault` ran nothing for it, so Torment of Hailfire stopped at the victim whose BOARD emptied, one prompt further in than the drop #1006 fixed. `defaultDroppedChoiceLocked` now runs that frame with nothing picked, which is the same closure and the same empty answer every one of those callers already runs when the candidate list was empty at queue time. **5 cards converted:** Fact or Fiction (its "which opponent separates" caveat was cleared by #929's choose-a-player prompt and it is now `CompletenessFull`); Torment of Hailfire (X repetitions x each opponent, asked strictly one at a time so a later repetition sees an earlier one's answer); Combustible Gearhulk; Charismatic Conqueror; Painful Quandary. **Chain of Vapor shipped in #920**, whose resolving-item slot is what its copy leg was waiting on (see the row below). Braids, Arisen Nightmare, Tempt with Bunnies and Druid of Purification are unblocked by this seam and still need their own files and their own gap check.
