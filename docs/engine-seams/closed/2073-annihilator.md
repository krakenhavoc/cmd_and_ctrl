**Annihilator** (#2073, CR 702.86, [ADR 0113](decisions/0113-small-seams-for-the-s58-deck-requests.md) §2) — the third keyword trigger the engine derives from the ability list, after prowess and evolve, and the second numbered keyword, after toxic. `annihilator` joins `canonicalKeywords` as a family key: the tokens are `annihilator N` (minted by `CanonicalAnnihilatorToken`), a bare `annihilator` is refused, and the token is cumulative (CR 702.86b), so a printed annihilator 4 and a granted annihilator 2 are two triggers. The deck importer reads the number off the oracle line, as it does for toxic, and no longer counts a keyword sitting between two commas inside a sentence (Flayer of Loyalties' cast trigger), which would have stamped a second, unprinted instance. Each trigger watches its creature being declared as an attacker (CR 508.3a, so a creature put onto the battlefield attacking does not trigger), records the defending player (CR 508.5, per attacker), and resolves through the new keyed body `annihilator/sacrifice`: the defending player, read again if the creature is still attacking and otherwise the recorded one, chooses N permanents they control in one prompt and sacrifices them together (fewer if they have fewer; nothing if they have left the game). Ulamog, the Defiler's "annihilator X" is the catalog row `AnnihilatorCounted`, which reads X as it resolves. The Eldrazi titans' "When ~ is put into a graveyard from anywhere" is the new `WhenThisIsPutIntoAGraveyardFromAnywhere`, one trigger per arrival whether the card died, was sacrificed, discarded, milled or countered. Snapshot: one new on-disk body key and a v7 corpus board with the trigger on the stack; while the defender is choosing, the table holds the same pick prompt Lotus Field's does. **Cards:** Kozilek, Butcher of Truth; Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre; Ulamog, the Defiler — all Full.
