**Layers 4 and 5 off the battlefield, an effect that outlives a removal in its own layer, a granted can't-lose, and Ixidron's entry** (#3862) — four layer gaps, one card or three each.

- **Off the battlefield** ([ADR 0039 amendment of 2026-10-15](decisions/0039-layer-4-authoritative.md)). `StaticAbility.AffectsZones` declares a layer-4 or layer-5 battlefield static over spells (`ZoneStack`) or over cards in a hand, library, graveyard, exile or the command zone. `game/zone_layers.go` applies these statics after layer 2 and stamps `Card.zoneLayered`, which the six type and colour readers consult before the printed card. Mycosynth Lattice's colourless cards and spells, Maskwood Nexus' every-type creature cards and spells (so Cavern of Souls pays for them), and Secret Arcade's enchantment permanent spells (so Dusty Parlor sees them) are complete. Colour identity reads printed colours (CR 903.4).
- **In the removal's own layer** ([ADR 0067 amendment of 2026-10-15](decisions/0067-layer-dependency-ordering.md)). An effect that started in an earlier layer and declares `ContinuesAfterRemoval` carries on in the removal's own bucket in timestamp order (CR 613.6), and the layer-6 pre-sort no longer treats it as a removal's victim. `StaticAbility.StartsAtCounters` dates an "as long as it has N or more counters" effect from the moment the Nth counter arrived (recorded in `Card.CounterStampedAt`). Idol of False Gods follows its ruling: it has annihilator 2 if it lost its abilities before its eighth counter, and not otherwise.
- **A granted can't-lose** ([ADR 0093 amendment of 2026-10-15](decisions/0093-abilities-granted-to-other-permanents.md)). `AbilityGrant.GameEndGates` lets a bundle carry "you can't lose the game and your opponents can't win the game". Cloudsteel Kirin gives it to the equipped creature at its attach timestamp, so an earlier removal on the creature no longer stops it.
- **Ixidron's entry** ([ADR 0082 amendment of 2026-10-15](decisions/0082-casting-face-down-and-turning-face-up.md)). `Spec.AsEntersOthers` runs an "as this enters" clause over other objects after the landing and before any event announces it, with the permanents of the same entry marked (`Game.ArrivingForEffect`). Ixidron's creatures are face down before anything sees it arrive, and a creature entering with it stays face up.
