**Enlist** (#5658, CR 702.154 / 508.1g / 607.2h) — enlist is a canonical keyword now, and the second optional cost to attack after exert ([ADR 0130](../../decisions/0130-exert.md) §2, whose shape it follows). Both declaration verbs take the creature an attacker enlists (`AttackDeclaration.Enlist`, `enlist` on `declare_attacker` and on each `declare_attackers` entry), validate it before anything is paid or staged (`validateEnlistChoicesLocked`: an untapped creature the attacker's controller controls, other than the attacker, not attacking, with haste or under their control since the turn began, and tapped for one enlist only; `ErrCantEnlist`, `ErrEnlistedCantAttack`, `ErrDeclarerCantEnlist`) and stage it on `Card.EnlistOnAttack` (snapshot `enlistOnAttack`, additive). `commitAttackDeclarationLocked` pays it as the declaration locks in (CR 508.1j): the enlisted creature is tapped and `EventEnlist` joins the declaration's batch ahead of the `EventAttack`s, so a staged attack taken back never tapped anything and the triggers go on the stack with the attack triggers. The linked "when you do" is derived from the ability list (`keywordTriggersFor`, keyed body `enlist/pump`): +X/+0 until end of turn on the pinned attacker, X the enlisted creature's power as the trigger resolves, last-known if it has left (CR 608.2h), nothing for a negative power (CR 107.1b). `effects.WheneverThisEnlists` is "Whenever this creature enlists a creature". The enumerator offers one twin attack move per creature that may be enlisted, the digest lists them as `enlist_on_attack`, and the log has an `enlist` line. The client asks on every path, as it does for exert: the dock's "Attack" / "Enlist <creature>" row for a clicked attacker, a "Declare attacker and enlist <creature>" row in the card menu, and a per-row choice in "Choose attackers…", which "Attack with all" opens when an attacker could enlist. Limits: the token is not cumulative, so an attacker enlists at most one creature (CR 702.154d is unreachable until a card grants enlist), and the heuristic bot never picks an enlist move. **Cards:** Argivian Cavalier, Balduvian Berserker, Barkweave Crusher, Benalish Faithbonder, Coalition Skyknight, Coalition Warbrute, Guardian of New Benalia, Hexbane Tortoise, Keldon Flamesage, Linebreaker Baloth, Yavimaya Steelcrusher — Full.
