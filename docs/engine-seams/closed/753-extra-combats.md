**Extra combat and main phases** (#753, [ADR 0059](decisions/0059-turn-machinery.md) Decisions 3, 4 and 8 and its sub-PR 2b amendment): the rest of the turn is data. `Game.TurnPlan` is the steps still to come, `advanceCursorLocked` pops it, and an effect splices phases into it with `Game.AddPhasesForEffect` ("after this phase", "after this main phase") or a step with `Game.AddStepAfterCurrentForEffect` (CR 500.8 / 500.9), newest first. An added main phase is a postcombat main phase (CR 505.1a), so the Saga lore action and "at the beginning of your precombat main phase" happen once. #717's first-strike damage step is a plan entry of every combat and is still walked through when nobody has first or double strike. `Turn.PhaseID`, `PhaseOrdinal` and `StepOrdinal` answer "the first combat phase of the turn" and "the first end step of the turn", and `TurnTally.Attacks` answers "attacked this turn" and "attacks for the first time each turn" per object. A step-conditioned delayed trigger with a duration fires at every matching step ("at the beginning of each combat this turn", CR 603.7b). The plan clones and snapshots as plain data; the wire has `TurnView.phase_id` / `phase_ordinal` / `upcoming` and an `extra_phase` log line. Relentless Assault, Seize the Day, Aggravated Assault, Full Throttle, Karlach, Fury of Avernus, Hellkite Charger, Aurelia, the Warleader, Sphinx of the Second Sun and Éomer, Marshal of Rohan joined the catalog, and Y'shtola Rhul's additional end step is wired. Still open: an additional phase after "the second main phase" and a trigger bound to one added combat (World at War, Moraug, Fury of Akoum).
