**Several cards returned from a graveyard enter together** (#1867, CR 603.6a / CR 614.12 / CR 101.4) — every catalog card that put more than one card from a graveyard onto the battlefield did it with a loop over the single-card reanimation door, so the first card was announced before the second moved: of two returned Soul Wardens only the first saw the other enter. `Game.PutOntoBattlefieldTogetherThenForEffect` (#1322, #1324) already ran one simultaneous entry, but refused `ZoneGraveyard`; it takes graveyard cards now, each keeping its instance ID as the single-card door does. `effects.ReturnFromGraveyardTogether{Targets, Controller, Tapped, Then}` is the catalog's door over it: it skips a card no longer in a graveyard, a token or a nonpermanent instead of refusing the batch, carries the effect's own "tapped" on each entry event, and runs the rest of the effect in `Then` once the last entry question is answered. No snapshot field and no closure route was added. **22 cards routed through it:** Reveillark, Pull, Lich-Knights' Conquest, Smile at Death, Victimize, Eerie Ultimatum, Agadeem's Awakening, Behold the Sinister Six!, Mantle of the Ancients, Exhume (whose players now choose one at a time in APNAP order, CR 101.4b, before anything enters), Replenish, Brilliant Restoration, Open the Vaults, Raise the Past, Funeral Room // Awakening Hall, Rise of the Dark Realms, Splendid Reclamation, Will of the Sultai, Aftermath Analyst, World Shaper, Lumra, Bellow of the Woods and Zombie Apocalypse. Pull goes `full`, and Reveillark is `full` honestly. The batch's tapped clause also closed the "enters untapped and is tapped a moment later" caveat on Aftermath Analyst, World Shaper, Lumra and Zombie Apocalypse, which go `full`. **Still open:** loops that return several cards from EXILE one at a time (Living Death, the multi-card flickers), which the same door already accepts.
