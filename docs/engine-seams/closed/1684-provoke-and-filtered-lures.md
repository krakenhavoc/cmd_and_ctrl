**Provoke-style "blocks it" and filtered Lures** (#1684, CR 509.1c and CR 702.39, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-28 (#1684)](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) Decision 59). Closes two of the "still open" items #1597 left. A fifth requirement kind, **`blocksAttacker`**, sits on the would-be blocker and names ONE attacking object — instance and epoch, carried on the `addBlockRequirement` record in the new `Mod.Objects` and on the characteristic as `BlockRequirement.Attacker` — so only blocking that object obeys it, and a creature that left and came back is not it (CR 400.7). A Lure may carry a registered blocker **filter key** (`BlockRequirement.Filter`; `wall`, `flying`, `game.RegisterBlockerFilter`), never a closure, so the characteristic stays plain comparable data (ADR 0041's ratchet); a blocker the filter does not match carries no requirement from it. Both ride the existing search unchanged in shape: each blocker → attacker arc is now weighted by `blockPairWeight`, the requirements that one pair obeys, and the witness is still counted exactly and re-checked by the ordinary validator, so a provoked creature that can't legally block the provoker owes nothing. Card side: `effects.Provoke()` (a triggered ability, not a `PrintedKeywords` token, for Exalted's reason), `BlocksAttackerUntilEOT`, `TargetBlocksTheAttacker`, `TargetBlocksThisCreature` and `FilteredLure(key)`. **Goblin Grappler**, **Grappling Hook**, **Turntimber Basilisk**, **Marble Priest**, **Talruum Piper**, **Alluring Scent**, **Bloodscent**, **Elvish Bard**, **Taunting Elf**, **Canopy Stalker**, **Watchdog** (with its "creatures attacking you get −1/−0" static) and **Nacatl War-Pride** (with its token-copy attack trigger) are new and `full`. **Still open:** blocking costs (CR 509.1d) and "blocks each attacking creature". Crested Craghorn, Lowland Tracker and Brontotherium (printed keywords plus `Provoke()`) and Hunt Down's "target creature blocks target creature" (`BlocksAttackerUntilEOT`) need no new machinery and wait only for a card batch.
