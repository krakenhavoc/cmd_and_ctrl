**Detain, a cast ban on a name, an uncopyable spell, and a required pick per opponent** (#4128, [ADR 0045](../../decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md), [ADR 0066](../../decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md), [ADR 0043](../../decisions/0043-copy-effects.md) and [ADR 0019](../../decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md), each with a 2026-10-19 amendment) — four shapes the Roon of the Hidden Realm deck request needed. **Detain** (CR 701.35) is `effects.Detain`: the Arrest bundle of restriction bits (attack, block, activated abilities, mana abilities included) over a set pinned at resolution, ending as the detaining player's next turn begins. **A cast ban on a name** (Reflector Mage) is `PermissionFilter.Named` read by the existing granted cast ban, under a kind of its own (`CastBanNamed`) so a binary from before it reads no ban rather than an outright one. **"This spell can't be copied"** is `Spec.CantBeCopied`, refused at `copySpellFromLocked`, `createSpellCopyLocked` and `CopySpellKeepingTargetsForEffect` before the retarget prompt opens. **"For each opponent, … target … that player controls"** with a required pick is `TargetSpec.OpponentSlot`: the candidate must be controlled by the live player in that slot, and the clause is required only while that seat holds a live opponent (`Game.ClauseMinForEffect`, read by the announce gate, the cast offer, the wire view and the enumerator). The snapshot grows `filter.named` on every `PermissionFilter`, additive within v9. **Cards** (5 new, all Full): Beguiler of Wills, Blatant Thievery, Lavinia of the Tenth, Reflector Mage, See Double; Glasspool Mimic is the sixth card of the slice and uses the existing copy-effect seam.
