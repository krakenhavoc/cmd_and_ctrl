**Sacrifice cost of more than one permanent** (#747) - a sacrifice clause's count is its `TargetSpec`'s `Min == Max`, built with `SacrificeN(n, label, preds…)` for an activated or mana ability (`.SacrificeOther` for the mana one) and `SacrificeNCost(n, label, preds…)` for an additional cost to cast ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) and [ADR 0021](decisions/0021-additional-costs.md) addenda). One validator serves all three sites: exactly N distinct permanents the payer controls, each matching the clause, never the source when the cost also sacrifices it. One payment leaves as one simultaneous exit, so a dies-watcher paid in with the rest sees every death. The enumerator offers one payment per move for N ≥ 2, tokens first, then lower mana value, then the source last; the view ships `sacrifice_options.min`/`max` as N with the options in that order, and the client's picker is a multi-select that confirms at exactly N with a "Choose for me" button. `effects.Register` refuses variable counts. Shipped on Priest of Forgotten Gods, Kuldotha Forgemaster, Tamiyo's Journal, Teysa, Orzhov Scion, Hedron Detonator, Olivia, Opulent Outlaw, Breya, Etherium Shaper and Whisper, Blood Liturgist, and it cleared the caveats on Savvy Hunter, Samwise Gamgee, Sai, Master Thopterist, Magda, the Hoardmaster and Magda, Brazen Outlaw. Still open: Transmutation Font's "with different names" (the set-level row above) and variable counts (the row above). Not this seam: Liliana, Dreadhorde General (a resolution-time edict), Phyrexian Soulgorger (cumulative upkeep's sacrifice payment — the trigger shipped with #567, the payment shape did not), Jolene, the Plunder Queen (a token-creation replacement), Nemata, Primeval Warden (a reflexive "when you do" trigger off a replacement) and Ulamog, the Defiler (ward's sacrifice cost, `WardCost.Sacrifice`, is a separate shape). Unblocked by the cost and still to be written, each with another gap the audit named: Pippin, Warden of Isengard (#448, partner with) and Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus (#303).
