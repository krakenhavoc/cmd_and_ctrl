**Cost changes aimed at part of a cost** (#6206, CR 601.2f, 118.7a, 118.8d, 702.27a, 119.4) — two clauses that name a PART of a cast's cost instead of the whole of it. (1) "Buyback costs cost {2} less" (Memory Crystal) is `game.CostModifier.PricesOptionalCost`, the optional-cost key a modifier prices instead of the announcement's total (`effects.BuybackCostsLess`), matched against the new `CostQuery.PricingOptionalCost` in `pricesAnnouncement` — the same partition `Activations` and `SpecialActions` are, a third door over, so a Sphere of Resistance never taxes a buyback cost and the Crystal never discounts a spell. The one pricer (`printedCostLocked`) prices each announced component on its own through `pricedComponentsLocked` (`game/component_cost_modifier.go`) before `AdditionalCostMana` folds its mana into the total, so the reduction floors at the component's own generic mana ({2} less off a buyback {1} leaves {0} and takes nothing off the spell), an unbought cast is not discounted at all, and the spell's mana cost is untouched (CR 118.8d). The component pass is the board's modifiers alone — a self modifier (affinity, "this spell costs {1} less") and a "next spell you cast" promise are written against the spell's own cost, and `effects.Register` refuses the field in the self slot; a component whose cost carries {X}, and a modifier that returns a negative amount, refuse the cast rather than guess (#289). A non-mana buyback ("Buyback—Sacrifice a land", "Buyback—Pay 4 life") is left exactly as printed. (2) "This spell costs 3 life more to cast for each target" (Phyrexian Purge) is `Spec.LifePerTarget` (`game.LifePerTargetCost`, `effects.CostsLifePerTarget`, read through `game.LifePerTargetFor` under `CatalogKey`): the target tax (#3860) one owner over, so it is one more line in `castTargetTaxLinesLocked` marked `Self`, and all four of that seam's askers learn it at once — the cast's CR 119.4 check against every other life cost of the announcement and the payment with the spell on the stack, the enumerator's per-target-set pricing (which drops the sets the seat can't pay and names the life in the move), the picker's `life_taxes` beside each candidate, and the bot's `MoveCost`. It counts TARGETS, not targeted permanents, so a player or a spell named as a target pays too. No wire or snapshot shape changed: the buyback toggle still shows the clause as printed and the price badge shows the modified total, as every other cost modifier is shown. **Cards:** Memory Crystal and Phyrexian Purge are built by their OLD batches (b745 and b809).
