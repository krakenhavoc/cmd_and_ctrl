**Spending mana as though it were any color** (#1600, CR 609.4b, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)'s 2026-10-02 amendment) — the one-spell form (a cast permission's `AnyColor` / `AnyType`, folded in the cast pricer, #1573) now has a player form: `Spec.AnyColorSpend`, a `game.AnyColorSpendStatic` for "you" or "players", optionally narrowed by `Covers` over the payment's spend context, read live off the battlefield. `costAsPaidByLocked` (`game/spend_any_color.go`) reads it at every PAYMENT — a cast and its auto-tap, a CR 602 activation / special action / attack tax (`payAbilityManaCostLocked`), a mana ability's own mana cost, a pay-unless (`payCostLocked`) — and `internal/legal`'s one affordability probe and the auto-tap preview read it through the same function, so the view's `legal_actions`, the bot's moves and the payment agree. No pricer is touched, so the price shown stays printed. The cost is WIDENED rather than folded: coloured requirements become `ColorRequirement.AnyMana` with their printed options kept, `{C}` is left alone (CR 106.1b), and both solvers pay a widened symbol with its printed colour when they can and as generic otherwise, after convoke / waterbend / delve and before the Phyrexian strike. Mana restrictions still bind. **Cards:** Chromatic Orrery and Oath of Nissa (Full); Mycosynth Lattice (its spend line; caveat left for "everything is colorless"). **Not covered:** "spend WHITE mana as though it were any color" with "other mana only as colorless" (Celestial Dawn), and grants over one permanent's own abilities (Manascape Refractor); no catalogued card waits on either.
