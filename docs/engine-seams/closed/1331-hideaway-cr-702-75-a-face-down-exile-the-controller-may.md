**Hideaway (CR 702.75): a face-down exile the controller may look at, linked to its source, with a free play** (#1331, CR 702.75a / 406.3 / 406.3a / 607.2a, [ADR 0091](decisions/0091-hideaway.md)) — the last named hideaway item on the face-down row. **A seventh exile-side kind**, `FaceDownHidden` (`"hideaway"`), whose viewer is the one rule no other kind has: the controller of ANOTHER object, the permanent that exiled the card. `faceDownViewersLocked` reads it through `Card.HiddenBy`, and `hideawayKnowersSweepLocked` (with the state checks, never "fired") adds the current controller when the permanent changes hands — CR 406.3 keeps anyone who has already looked. **The link** is `Card.HiddenBy`, the permanent OBJECT {instance, epoch}, stamped by the exile route before the knowers are computed (`zoneRoute.HiddenBy`, `ExileHiddenForEffect`) and cleared by `MoveCard` on every move; `HiddenCardsForEffect(ref)` is CR 607.2a's "the exiled card". Card-carried rather than the effects package's event-log exiled-with record (`b27ExiledWith`) because the viewer rule is answered inside the engine about one `Card`; it is exact in the same way that record is since #1239 — the epoch pins the incarnation, so a hideaway land bounced and replayed has no claim on its old card. **The free play of a hidden spell** is an ADR 0066 grant over the linked card written by the card's own ability (`effects.PlayHiddenCard`): `{0}`, not cast-only (hideaway says *play*), `TimingFlash`, until end of turn — the cascade / Malcolm grant-not-inline posture, declared. The link is read off the activation's `SourceEpoch` (`HiddenRefOfActivation`) or captured in a trigger's Build, so a hideaway land destroyed in response still plays its card. `effects.Hideaway(name, n)` is CR 702.75a whole: look (controller only), a mandatory one-of-N `choose_cards` pick, the face-down exile, the rest to the bottom in a random order. **4 cards**, all `full`: Rabble Rousing (#1306's card), Mosswort Bridge (#294), Windbrisk Heights (#298), Spinerock Knoll (#299). **A hidden land is played during the resolution** (ADR 0091 decision 5): `Game.PlayLandDuringResolutionForEffect` is a land play through the one land entry finisher — it spends a land drop (CR 305.2a), needs no main phase or empty stack, and is ignored with no drop left or on another player's turn (CR 305.2b / 305.3) — offered through cascade's `may_cast` prompt; cascade and Malcolm can reuse it for a land. **Still open:** Evercoat Ursine's two hideaways and "one of them"; Watcher for Tomorrow's leaves-the-battlefield read of the link (the LTB needs the departed object's epoch captured at trigger time); and the other hideaway cards, each on its own condition. Trackers [#886](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/886) and [#889](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/889).
