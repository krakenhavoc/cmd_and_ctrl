**Duration copy effects — "becomes a copy of X until end of turn"** (#1593, CR 707.2, CR 611.2, CR 613.1a, CR 613.7, [ADR 0043 amendment 2026-09-28 (#1593)](decisions/0043-copy-effects.md) Decisions 21-25). A copy effect with a duration and a timestamp of its own, on a permanent already on the battlefield, is an ADR 0041 `ScopedEffect` carrying one **`becomeCopy`** mod, and the mod carries the copied `PrintedValues` BY VALUE (`Mod.Copy`) — never a reference, because the copied card may be in a graveyard and may leave it. It is layer 1 by materialisation, not by a layer-pass effect: `materialiseDurationCopiesLocked` (`game/duration_copy.go`) writes the winning copy — the latest timestamp, entry copy underneath — onto the flat printed fields at the top of every recompute, when the sweep drops a record, and when one is registered, so the oracle ID every catalog hook keys on switches with the copy and switches back. A new carried field, `Card.DurationCopyBase`, is the baseline under the duration copies: the entry copy for a Clone (a Cytoshaped Clone of Grizzly Bears is Grizzly Bears again at cleanup, not Clone), the card's own values otherwise. "As this enters … until end of turn" (Cursed Mirror) lands as an entry copy before any event and is re-filed as a duration copy once the permanent has its entry stamp. Counters, damage and status are untouched (CR 707.2); a snapshot or undo carries the record and the baseline; a restore refuses a copy mod with no values or a granted bundle it does not know. Card side: `effects.BecomeCopy`, `EntersAsCopyOfUntilEndOfTurn`, `PrintedValues.AddKeyword`, `game.OwnPrintedValues`. **Shifting Woodland** and **Cursed Mirror** drop their caveats; **Mirage Mirror**, **Cytoshape**, **Mirrorweave**, **Lazav, Dimir Mastermind** and **Dimir Doppelganger** are new and `full`; **Unstable Shapeshifter** is new with one caveat (a copy of a creature that was itself a copy and left before the trigger resolved copies the card, because the engine keeps no last-known copiable values for a permanent). **Still open:** Shapesharer (a two-target clause with different specs), Mizzium Transreliquat's second ability and the other Lazavs need no new machinery and wait only for a card batch; last-known copiable values of a permanent that has left the battlefield.
