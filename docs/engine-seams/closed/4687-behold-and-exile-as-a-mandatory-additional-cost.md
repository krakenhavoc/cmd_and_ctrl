**Behold and exile as a mandatory additional cost** (#4687, [ADR 0100](decisions/0100-delve-either-or-and-variable-sacrifice-costs.md)'s 2026-11-05 amendment, CR 701.4a, CR 601.2h, CR 118.3, CR 607.2q) — the Lorwyn Eclipsed Champions' "As an additional cost to cast this spell, behold a Kithkin and exile it" and "When this creature leaves the battlefield, return the exiled card to its owner's hand" had no shape: behold existed only as a branch or an optional cost, and it moved nothing. `RevealCost.Exile` (`effects.BeholdAndExileCost(article, subtype)`) is the mandatory slot's one component: the caster names one card on `reveal_ids` from the behold walk (`RevealCostOptionsForEffect`: a matching permanent they control or a matching card in their hand, a changeling included), it may not pay another cost of the same cast, and `payBeholdExileLocked` exiles it with the spell on the stack, linked to the spell object (`Card.ExiledWith`). `relinkCostExilesLocked` moves the link to the permanent as the spell resolves, so `effects.ReturnTheExiledCardToHand` returns that card and no other: a countered spell's card stays in exile, a second Champion's card stays with the second Champion, and a card that has left exile is not pulled back. No snapshot field: `Card.ExiledWith` already carries the link. `AnyAdditionalCostBranchPayableLocked` makes a card with nothing to behold uncastable. The view adds `additional_cost.behold_exile` (additive); the client's one-pick sheet reads "Exile"; the enumerator exiles a hand card first, then the weakest token, then the cheapest permanent, and the bot prices the card like the other exile costs. **Cards:** Champion of the Clachan, Champions of the Perfect (both `full`). Champions of the Shoal and Champion of the Path need only their other abilities; Champion of the Weird also waits on "Pay 1 life, Blight 2" as an ability cost.
