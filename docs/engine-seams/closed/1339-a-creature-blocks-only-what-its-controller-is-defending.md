**A creature blocks only what its controller is defending against** (#1339, CR 802.4a / 509.1a, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md), Decision 34) — no row of its own: a rules bug found while testing #1329, not a missing mechanic. `DeclareBlockers` took any pairing whose blocker the caller controlled, so at a four-seat table seat 1 could block the creature attacking seat 2; the option generator (`blockOptionsLocked`) already offered each seat only its own attackers, so bots were right and a human client could send what no bot was offered. **The check** is `blockDefenderRefusalLocked` (`game/block_declaration.go`), one per-entry line in Decision 13's validator reading the same `defendingPlayerForAttackLocked` the generator reads — the player attacked, a planeswalker's controller, a battle's protector — and refusing with the reserved token `not_defending`. A pairing already standing is not re-judged, so an attacker reselected after it was blocked (CR 508.7a, #1329) keeps its blockers. **The wire** stamps `defending_player` on every attacker (`protocol/view.go` `stampCombatTargets`), and the client's four block pickers read it through `attackersDefendedBy` (`client/src/lib/attackTargets.ts`); the click paths now also offer the attacks on a planeswalker the viewer controls or a battle they protect. **Closed since (#1364, Decision 35):** CR 506.4c's "it may be blocked" for an attacker whose planeswalker or battle has left. `Game.attackDefenders` records the defending player whenever an attack is pointed (`setAttackTargetLocked` in `game/attack_target.go` — the two declaration verbs, an entry "attacking", a CR 508.7 reselect), and the block path — generator, verb, refusal sentence, landwalk, the enumerator's pre-filter and the wire's `defending_player` — reads `defendingPlayerForAttackerLocked`, which falls back to it once the live target is gone. Damage still goes nowhere (CR 510.1b). **Closed since (#1376, Decision 36):** an attacked planeswalker or battle removed from combat WITHOUT leaving the battlefield — a control change (`materialiseControlLocked`) or phasing out (`phaseOutLocked`) — takes its attackers with it: `removeAttackedFromCombatLocked` (`game/attack_target.go`) re-points every announced attacker at the reserved `game.AttackingNothing` id, so they still attack, deal no damage to it or its new controller, and are blocked by the recorded defender. **Closed since (#1387, Decision 37):** the type clause — an attacked permanent that stops being a planeswalker or battle is removed from combat for good. `recomputeLayersLocked` (`game/layers.go`) calls `removeTypeLostAttackTargetsLocked` (`game/attack_target.go`) after each layer pass; it checks only the permanents announced attackers name and re-points the attackers of any that is no longer a planeswalker or battle at `AttackingNothing`, so a type that comes back later in the same combat does not bring the attack back. No proof cards: this is a legality gate every block crosses, not a card's missing shape. Tracker [#880](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/880).
