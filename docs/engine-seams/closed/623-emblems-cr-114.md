**Emblems (CR 114)** (#623, [ADR 0064](decisions/0064-emblems.md)): an emblem is a `game.Card` with a synthetic `"emblem:<source key>"` catalog key, living in `Player.Emblems` — a second command-zone slice beside the commander pile. Its statics join the layer pass's existing source gather (`activeStaticAbilitiesLocked` → `emblemContinuousEffectsLocked`) and its triggers join the harvester's existing zone walk (`triggerHarvester.OnEvent` → `harvestFromEmblemsLocked`), so a card author writes an emblem's abilities in exactly the vocabulary a permanent's are written in — one new `Spec.Emblem` slot, `CreateEmblem{}` as the ability's whole effect. The split slice is the enforcement of CR 114: `ZoneRef` is `{Kind, Owner}`, so every cast, move, route and lookup resolves `{command, owner}` to the commander pile and can never name an emblem. Snapshot carries it (schema 1 → 2, so a pre-emblem binary refuses a post-emblem restore point rather than silently dropping them), clone carries it, and CR 800.4a is the only exit. `GameView.seats[i].emblems[]` is public and unredacted; the board shows chips beside the player identity with the printed text on hover. **2 of the row's 4 cards converted**: Elspeth, Sun's Champion (−7, static) and Teferi, Hero of Dominaria (−8, targeted trigger). Wrenn and Six's −7 stays omitted — its emblem's only text is a retrace grant, which needs retrace and #652; its caveat now names #652 rather than emblems. Teferi Akosa of Zhalfir's −2 joined them in #626 — the third card, and the first emblem carrying a static AND a trigger in one printed text. "Knights you control get +1/+0 and have ward {1}" is an anthem plus a WARD, and ward is a triggered ability carrying a cost (CR 702.21a), so the emblem carries the trigger rather than writing a keyword onto the Knight: `effects.WardGranted`, of which `Ward` and `WardAttached` are now the two narrow cases. One predicate value serves both halves, so the layer half and the trigger half cannot drift. **#1315** ([ADR 0064 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0064-emblems.md)) gave `EmblemSpec` two more slots, `UntapStep` and `DrawStep`, for the two clauses that widen a turn-based action (CR 502.3, CR 504.1) instead of triggering — the emblem-side mirror of `Spec.UntapStep` (#74) and the new `game.DrawStepPermission`. The untap-step and draw-step gathers now walk every seat's `p.Emblems`, the third and fourth walks alongside the statics and triggers gathers above. Teferi, Who Slows the Sunset's −7 is the fourth card converted, closing the row. **#1275** added a fifth slot and a fifth walk: `EmblemSpec.ActivationTimings`, read by the per-player activation timing walk over `p.Emblems` (Teferi, Temporal Archmage's −10 — see the entry at the top of this list).
