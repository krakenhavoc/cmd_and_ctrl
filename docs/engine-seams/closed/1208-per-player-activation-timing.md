**Per-player ACTIVATION timing** (#1208, CR 602.5d / CR 606.3 / CR 101.1, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md), [ADR 0073 note 2026-09-23](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)) — "you may activate [these abilities] any time you could cast an instant". Before this, `ActivatedAbilityShape.SorcerySpeed` and `AbilityCost.Loyalty != nil` were the engine's whole opinion about when an ability may be activated, written out in three places that each kept their own copy of it, and NOTHING could open that window or narrow it. **The cards are the grant, not the restriction**, and that is the one thing the row above got wrong when it predicted this one: every printed restriction on somebody's activations says "can't be activated" (Cursed Totem, Linvala, Collector Ouphe, Pithing Needle, Karn, Grand Abolisher) and goes through #1210's `ActivationGateLocked`, so the eight printed cards on THIS row all print a grant. **`game.ActivationTiming`** is a struct of hooks mirroring `ActivationRestriction` field for field — a printed `Label`, a `Covers func(ActivationQuery) bool`, an `ActiveWhen` designation gate — and it is a SECOND type rather than `CastTimingRule` with a scope field, because a cast statement has a stored home and must be pure data while this one has only a derived home and can carry a predicate. It needs one: what the cards narrow on is the ability's SOURCE ("loyalty abilities of Teferi") and the ABILITY ("equip abilities"), two things a filter over *the object being cast* says neither of. So `ActivationAbility` grew `SorcerySpeed`, `Loyalty` (the bool ADR 0073's #1210 scope note predicted, derived from the loyalty cost component so a catalog entry cannot forget CR 606.3) and `Equip` (the CR 702.6 keyword, NAMED rather than made a kind — `EquipOnlyAbility` and `TestEveryEquipAbilityIsMarked` exist because three cards wrote a narrowed equip out by hand). **ONE HOME, not two:** every card that prints one of these is a permanent whose static says it for as long as it is there, so `Spec.ActivationTimings` is derived through `CatalogAbilityKey` and nothing is stored — no `PlayerStatic` payload, no sweep, no clone, no snapshot field. The Wandering Emperor's "as long as she entered this turn" rides that derivation as `game.EnteredThisTurn` (#1009's entry tally), deliberately NOT `Card.SummonedThisTurn`, which survives until your untap step and would have given a flashed-in Emperor instant-speed loyalty abilities for a whole turn cycle. **ONE read** — `game.ActivationTimingOpenLocked` — folding, in this order: a mana ability (never asked, CR 605.3a gives it its own window, so a card that stops mana abilities is a BAN and belongs to the gate, where #1210 already put the decision on the card), the ability's own clause, the per-player grants, and LAST the restrictions, because CR 101.2 says "can't" beats "can". **A grant DOES reach loyalty abilities**, against what the issue proposed: CR 101.1 lets a card beat a rule, and both planeswalkers here print exactly that sentence — refusing would have made the only two printed users unwritable. **Four callers:** `ActivateCatalogAbility`, `legal.abilityMovesForSource` (which dropped the `speed` parameter it threaded through two functions to keep a copy of the rule), `protocol.viewOfActivatedAbilities`, and `ActivateLoyalty` — the sandbox manual loyalty verb, which `internal/legal` skips by design but which a statement about "planeswalkers you control" reaches on a walker the catalog has never heard of. **The wire grows one field**, where the cast side needed none: `activated_abilities[i].timing_closed`, negative and `omitempty`, because the rows carried only `sorcery_speed` (the PRINTED clause) and `client/src/lib/timing.ts` re-derived CR 307.1 to grey them — its own comment called that "the LAST rules derivation left in this file". The client now takes the BIT from the server and still writes the SENTENCE from the snapshot. **Cards:** Leonin Shikari (`full`) and Teferi, Master of Time (`caveats`, for the −10's extra turns) are new; **The Wandering Emperor goes from `caveats` to `full`** — and her three loyalty abilities were WRONG and are corrected in the same PR (S27 shipped a permuted, half-invented set; the printed +1 puts a +1/+1 counter and grants first strike, the −1 makes the Samurai, the −2 exiles a tapped creature and gains 2 life). ~~**Still open:** the EMBLEM home — Teferi, Temporal Archmage's −10 and Teferi's Talent both make an emblem that says this, which is the derived home one zone over (`Player.Emblems` beside the battlefield, plus a slot on `EmblemSpec`); not built because the one card behind it also needs a library-look prompt that does not exist.~~ — **closed by #1275**, the entry at the top of this list. **Also still open and a different seam:** Forge Anew's `{0}` equip cost (cost modification for activated abilities) and Thousand-Year Elixir's "as though those creatures had haste" (CR 302.6's tap-symbol restriction, not CR 602.5's window).
