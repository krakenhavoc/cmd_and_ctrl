**Effects that last while a permanent stays tapped, and while it has a counter** (#1894, #1604, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §3 and §2's condition half, PR 2) — a `Duration` can now be a conjunction: `Duration.Also []DurationCondition` lists conditions that must hold as well as `Condition`, so Seasinger's "for as long as you control this creature and this creature remains tapped" ends the moment either stops (CR 611.2b). Three conditions were appended, all read in the one sweep (`durationConditionHoldsLocked`): `WhilePinnedHasCounter` with `Duration.CounterKind` ("for as long as it has a flood counter on it"), `WhilePinnedRemainsTapped` (Zygon Infiltrator's "for as long as that creature remains tapped") and `WhilePinnedPowerAtMostSource` (Old Man of the Sea's power comparison, re-checked after every layer pass). Every constructor answers CR 611.2b's "never starts". The restore check covers every stored duration (ADR 0109 Shared machinery 2, `game/snapshot_durations.go`): the routes to a `Duration` are reflected from the snapshot types, and an unknown key or a duration this binary cannot read (`Duration.Problem`) is refused with `ErrUnknownEffectKey` on a cast permission, a player static and an untap hold, not only on a scoped effect and a delayed trigger. Card side: `effects/remains_tapped.go` (`WhileYouControlThisAndItRemainsTapped`, `DurationWhileThisRemainsTapped`, `TargetGetsWhileThisRemainsTapped`, `GainControlOfTargetFor`, `TapTargetsAndHoldWhileThisRemainsTapped`) and `effects/counter_held.go` (`CounterThenWhileItHasIt`, `GrantWhileItHasCounter`, `FloodTargetLand`). A v7 corpus fixture holds a conjunction and a counter-held duration. **Cards**: Seasinger, Helm of Possession, Hivis of the Scale, Rubinia Soulsinger, Willow Satyr and Zygon Infiltrator; the counter-held Aquitect's Will, Xolatoyac, Aven Mimeomancer, Liege of the Tangle, Minas Morgul, Mathas and Obsidian Fireheart, and Makeshift Mannequin (with a caveat); and the single "for as long as this remains tapped" cards (owner decision 4) Ashnod's Battle Gear, Deserter's Quarters, Endoskeleton, Everglove, Flamestick, Frightshroud, Ghosthelm and Pearlspear Courier, Flowstone Armor, Hisoka's Guard, Ice Floe, Kill Switch, Mana Leech, Mole Worms, Phyrexian Gremlins, Sand Squid, Spirit Shield, Tawnos's Weaponry, Thalakos Dreamsower, The Blackstaff of Waterdeep, Vedalken Shackles and Zelyon Sword.
