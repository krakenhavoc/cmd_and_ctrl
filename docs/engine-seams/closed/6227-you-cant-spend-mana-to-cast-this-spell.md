**"You can't spend mana to cast this spell"** (#6227, CR 106.1, CR 601.2g, CR 601.2h, CR 702.51a, CR 702.66a) — Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis has a total cost like any other spell and still has to pay it in full ("Partial payments are not allowed. Unpayable costs can't be paid"), but CR 106.1's one way of paying it is taken away, so the mana half has to be covered by the convoke taps and the delve exiles the announcement makes and nothing else. `Spec.CantSpendMana` (`CardDef.CantSpendMana`, read through `game.CantSpendManaFor`) is that clause, a bit beside `Delve`. It adds no arithmetic: the one pricer already subtracts the taps and then the exiles from the total (`costAfterModifiersLocked`), and the clause only makes the REMAINDER unpayable, through `ManaSpendContext.NoMana` — `SourceOnly`'s sibling, refused by the pool's one admission gate (`allowsToken`), so every reader that has to agree with the payment agrees by construction: the cast (`applyCastCostLocked`), its auto-tap (`applyAutoTapLocked`, which plans nothing under `NoMana` rather than tapping a land for mana the cast cannot spend), `internal/legal`'s affordability probe and the auto-tap preview. A cast whose taps and exiles leave anything owed is refused with `InsufficientManaError` and the symbols it could not pay; one that covers the whole of it is paid with no mana at all. Mana abilities are untouched (CR 601.2g's window is about the mana a payment may use, not about adding it), so a Swamp tapped beside the cast keeps its `{B}` floating, and a non-mana cost — an additional cost, a Phyrexian symbol paid with life — is paid as it always was. `effects.Register` refuses the clause on a card with neither a tap cost nor delve, which nothing could ever cast. One limit is inherited rather than added: `internal/legal` enumerates no TAP payments for a cast at all (`castMoves`), so it offers such a spell a cast only when the delve exiles can cover the whole of the mana — conservative in the direction that never advertises a move the engine would refuse, and the bot convoking is a seam of its own. No wire change: the client's convoke and delve pickers and the preview's affordability already carry everything a cast of such a card needs. The snapshot's recorded shape gains the one additive field (`AssistPrompt.Spend`). **Cards:** none here — Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis is built by its own OLD batch (b161), which this seam unblocks.
