**Discarding your hand as a cost** (#1600, CR 118.3 / CR 602.2b / CR 602.5e / CR 605.3a, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)'s 2026-10-02 amendment) — `game.DiscardCost` demanded a fixed count of at least one, so "Discard your hand" had no shape. It is now the component's `Hand` form, built by `effects.DiscardYourHand()` and owned by both `AbilityCost.DiscardCards` and `ManaAbilityCost.DiscardCards`: `validateDiscardCostLocked` reads the activator's whole hand at payment, refuses `discard_ids` for it, and pays an empty hand as nothing (CR 118.3). The cards leave through the one discard door with cause cost, so discard triggers and madness see them, Library of Leng (effects only) does not, and on a mana ability the triggers go on the stack after the mana is in the pool. No options are stamped, so the client opens no picker. `effects.Register` refuses a hand clause with a count, a predicate or "at random", and beside any other component that spends a hand card. "Activate only as an instant" on a mana ability is `effects.OnlyAsAnInstant()`, a Condition over `Game.InstantWindowOpenForEffect` (holds priority, owes no prompt, no prompt stopping the table, mulligans shut) — the legal enumerator's own reading of "may act now", so a mana ability that may otherwise be activated mid-cast or mid-resolution (CR 605.3a) cannot be; the view greys it through `condition_unmet`, and the auto-tapper, which runs mid-cast, refuses every discard component. `legal.MoveCost.Hand` tells a policy how many cards the move throws away, and the heuristic prices it at `Weights.Hand` per card. **Cards:** Lion's Eye Diamond, Diamond Lion, Null Brooch and Slate of Ancestry (Full). **Not covered:** "As an additional cost to cast this spell, … discard your hand" (Kaervek's Spite, which also sacrifices every permanent you control); Bomat Courier, Kyren Archive, Connecting the Dots, Reverberating Summons, Subira, Tulzidi Caravanner, Tarrian's Journal and Flamewar print the cost beside other mechanics and are not catalogued.
