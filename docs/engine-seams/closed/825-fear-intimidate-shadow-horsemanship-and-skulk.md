**Fear, intimidate, shadow, horsemanship and skulk** (#825) — canonical keywords enforced by the shared block-pair check, from effective colors/types/power. Importing a card with one of these printed abilities now retains it; keyword-only creatures need no effect file. Each refusal carries a server-built explanation; the enumerator and block-decision signal read the same check, and the bot's attack estimate reads the public characteristics. First catalog consumers: Shizo, Death's Storehouse; Bladetusk Boar; Looter il-Kor; Lu Xun, Scholar General; Furtive Homunculus. Cover of Darkness and Bellowing Tanglewurm are enabled by the mechanic but still need card entries. Parameterized blocking rules and block-count bounds remain #750.
