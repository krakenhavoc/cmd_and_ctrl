**Replace a life total being reduced to 0 or less** (#5678) — "If your life total would be reduced to 0 or less, instead transform this creature and your life total becomes 3. Then if this creature didn't transform this way, you lose the game." The planner expected a new player-level replacement beside the Angel's Grace floor. It is an ordinary `ReplacementEffect` on the `RepEventLife` window instead, because every falling life total already runs that window as a negative `LifeDelta`: a loss, a set-life effect (CR 119.5), a life payment (CR 119.4) and the life that damage costs (CR 120.3a, ADR 0169). `game/life_instead.go` adds what the body needs: `LifeWouldBeReducedToZeroOrLessForEffect` (the test, on the event as it stands), `LifeTotalBecomesInsteadForEffect` (the event becomes a gain or loss of the difference, so "life lost this way" and later replacements see what really moves, and a difference of zero lands as no event) and `ReplacementEvent.LifeThenLoses` (the loss of the game, carried by the event across a CR 616 prompt and a snapshot, and carried out where the event lands, CR 104.3e, behind the "can't lose" gate). The damage path now lands a loss the window rewrote into a gain (5 damage at 2 life gains 1) and the carried loss, after the damage (ADR 0169 and ADR 0057, amendments 2026-11-12). "Did it transform" is `CanTransformForEffect` asked before `TransformPermanentForEffect`. The auto-tapper's read-only preview of the window is told apart with `ReplacementIsPreviewForEffect`. The field is additive in snapshot v9. Serra Avatar's life-total sizing became the shared `powerAndToughnessEqualToYourLifeTotal()`, which now declares `DependsOnLifeTotal` (it was one life change stale). **Cards:** Enduring Angel // Angelic Enforcer (Full).
