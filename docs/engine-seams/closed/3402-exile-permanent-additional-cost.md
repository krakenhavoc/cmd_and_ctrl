**Exiling a permanent you control as an additional cost** (#3402, CR 601.2b / 601.2h / 118.3 / 608.2h) — activated abilities could exile a permanent as a cost (`AbilityCost.ExilePermanents`) but a spell could not, so Soul Exchange ("As an additional cost to cast this spell, exile a creature you control") had no way to be cast. `game.AdditionalCost.ExilePermanents` is that same `ExilePermanentsCost` on a cast's mandatory slot, built with `effects.ExileCreatureYouControlCost()` (or `ExilePermanentCost(label, cardType)`), so the candidate walk (`ExilePermanentsOptionsForEffect`), the clause check and the payer are the activation's own.
- **The wire.** The caster names the permanent on `cast_spell`'s `exile_permanent_ids` (`CastSpellParams.ExilePermanentIDs`, the name an activation already uses). It is validated at announce with the rest of the cost: exactly the clause's count, each once, the caster's own, of the clause's type, and not also named to a sacrifice or an alternative cost (one permanent pays one component). An `exile_permanent_ids` on a spell with no such cost is refused rather than ignored.
- **The payment.** Paid with the spell already on the stack, beside the sacrifices: a leaves-the-battlefield trigger lands above the spell, a commander is asked CR 903.9 first, nothing dies and nothing is sacrificed.
- **What the spell reads.** `PaidCost.Exiled` (the instance IDs) and the new `PaidCost.ExiledObjects` (each as the battlefield object it was, instance ID and CR 400.7 epoch), read at resolution through `ctx.ExiledPermanents()` as last-known information (CR 608.2h), the exile twin of `ctx.SacrificedPermanents()`. A CR 707.10 copy keeps the record. Soul Exchange's "if the exiled creature was a Thrull" is that read, and a changeling counts.
- **Views.** `additional_cost.exile_permanent_label` / `exile_permanent_options` (the activation's picker view, off the same walk), the client's cast chain opens the sacrifice picker with the verb "Exile" after the sacrifice step, the cast gate greys a spell with nothing to exile ("Nothing to exile"), and the auto-tap preview and `CastSpell`'s auto-tap never tap the exiled creature for mana. The legal-move enumerator offers one payment per cast (the policy's cheapest fuel first), names it in the move label ("exiling Blood Pet") and in `exile_permanent_ids`, and offers nothing when the seat has no creature to exile; the bot prices the lost permanent like a sacrificed one.
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` refuses the component on an optional cost, on an either/or branch, and in its craft forms (a graveyard, an open count, a set rule).
- **Cards:** Soul Exchange (Full).
