**Roll dice as an activation cost and as a permanent enters** (#5682, CR 601.2h / 614.1c / 614.12a / 107.3m / 706; follows [ADR 0054](decisions/0054-dice-rolls-and-coin-flips.md)) — every roll went through `RollDiceThenForEffect` from a resolving effect, with the rest of the effect as its tail. Two printed rolls are nobody's effect, and each now reaches the same door through an engine tail that writes the kept results where its reader looks (`game/dice_cost.go`), so both are replaceable rolls (Squid Fire Knight's sticker), an ignored roll never happened (CR 706.6), and "whenever you roll one or more dice" sees the rest.

**A roll as a cost.** `AbilityCost.RollDie` (`effects.RollDieCost(sides)`) is "Roll a d8" in an activation cost. A cost with a random element is paid after every other (CR 601.2h), so it is paid once the ability is on the stack, where the tap-another costs are already paid, and the result is written to that item's `PaidCost.Rolled` (`effects.CostRollOf`). A replacement that asks which roll to ignore leaves its question open with the ability on the stack; the prompt blocks the table, and the answer writes the result. A copy of the ability keeps the result with the rest of what was paid (CR 707.10). Not paid by the mana-ability path: no printed mana ability rolls.

**A roll as a permanent enters.** `EntryCountersFromCast.RollXSides` (`effects.RollXDiceCounters(kind, sides)`) is "As this creature enters, roll X d6. It enters with a number of +1/+1 counters on it equal to the total of those results." X is the spell's X (CR 107.3m), so only a resolving permanent spell rolls. The dice are rolled as the spell resolves, at the point its entry is about to be built and before the CR 614 window opens, and kept on the item (`StackItem.EntryRolls`); their total (`CastCounts.EntryRoll`) is seeded onto the entry event with every other cast-read clause, so Doubling Season and Hardened Scales apply and the permanent is never on the battlefield without them. A roll that asks a question is one of the resolution's own prompts, and the entry waits for it with the rest of the resolution's tail (CR 614.12a). A copy of the spell copies X and rolls its own dice. Like `XCounters`, the clause is read off the card that was cast: a different spell entering as a copy of the Hydra does not roll.

On disk, additive: `PaidCost.rolled`, the stack item's `entryRolls`, and the tails `dice/roll-cost` and `dice/entry-roll`. No wire change: neither roll asks its roller anything. **Cards:** Clay Golem, Neverwinter Hydra (both Full).
