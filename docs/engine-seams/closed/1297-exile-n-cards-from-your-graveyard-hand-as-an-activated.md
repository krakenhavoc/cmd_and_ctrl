**"Exile N cards from your graveyard / hand" as an activated ability's cost** (#1297, CR 602.2b / 118.3 / 406, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) Decisions 35-37, [ADR 0073 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md) Decision 7) — the open remainder the #1242 / #1283 / #1285 entry below named. `AbilityCost.ExileCards` is #1283's `game.ExileCost` with a SECOND OWNER, not a second component: the candidate walk (`ExileCostOptionsForEffect`), the validator (refusing overlap with the discards and the source) and the payer (the one exit primitive with `MustSettleNow`) are the mana ability's, now called from `ActivateCatalogAbility`. The component grew `From` (`ZoneHand` / `ZoneGraveyard`; zero reads as the hand, so every #1283 declaration is unchanged), because the printed CR 602 form is overwhelmingly the graveyard and the two piles are one rule read against two of the activator's own zones; `effects.Register` refuses any other zone and a zero count on both owners through one check. `PaidCost.Exiled` records the exiled cards, which is how "the card exiled this way" (Holistic Wisdom, Dread Defiler) is answered at resolution. The hand form joins `AbilityAutoTapExclusions`, so a Spirit Guide named to the cost is never also exiled for the mana. Wire: `activate_ability`'s `exile_ids`, and `exile_cost_n` / `_label` / `_options` / `_zone` on the ability view (the mana view gained `_zone`). The enumerator offers ONE payment out of the engine's own walk, cheapest fuel first when the policy has an opinion (`Options.OrderCostFuel`, escape's price); the heuristic prices the exiled cards with the same `fuelValue`. Client: Board's announce chain asks after the discard, through `DiscardCostModal` with the verb changed and the pile named (`exileCost.ts`). Proof cards, all `full`: **Mines of Moria** (its caveat named this seam), **Grim Lavamancer**, **Moorland Haunt**, **Tome Shredder** (the "instant or sorcery card" predicate) and **Holistic Wisdom** (the hand form and the paid record). Still open: the variable forms — "Exile X cards" (Necropolis Fiend, Taigam), "one or more" (Corpseweft) and "any number … with total mana value 30 or greater" (The Capitoline Triad) — which are an ANNOUNCED count, the #1213 variable-sacrifice question one component over; craft's "exile a creature you control or a creature card from your graveyard", which spans two zones in one clause; and a MANA ability that exiles from the graveyard (Molt Tender, Titans' Nest), which the component now expresses (`ExileCardsFromGraveyard`) but which no catalog card declares yet.
