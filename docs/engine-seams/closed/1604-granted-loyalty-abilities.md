**Granted loyalty abilities** (#1604, CR 606.3 / 606.6 / 114.2 / 707.10, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §2, owner decision 2, PR 4) — ADR 0093 Decision 10's loyalty exclusion is lifted. A grant bundle's row with a `LoyaltyCost` is an ordinary granted activated row on the planeswalker it lands on, so CR 606.3's "no player has previously activated a loyalty ability of that permanent that turn" and CR 606.6's counter check are the walker's: the granted row and the walker's own rows share one activation a turn, in either order, and a granted −N needs N loyalty counters. `ActivateCatalogAbility` stamps the row's grantor on the stack item (`StackItem.GrantedBy`, read by `effects.Context.Grantor`), and `effects.CreateEmblem` files the emblem under the grantor's catalog key, because "you get an emblem with …" in a granted ability is the grantor's text (CR 114.2). The grantor is carried by a snapshot (`grantedBy`, omitted when there is none, so an older binary refuses only a file holding a granted ability on the stack) and by a CR 707.10 copy, so a copied −12 is still the Talent's emblem. A restore point names the ability by its `grant:` ref, as every granted activated ability. `EventActivateAbility` now carries a `Loyalty` bit, read by `effects.WheneverYouActivateALoyaltyAbilityOfEnchanted` ("whenever you activate a loyalty ability of enchanted planeswalker"). **Cards**, all Full: Teferi's Talent (its −12 makes the Talent's own emblem, Teferi, Temporal Archmage's text under the Talent's name), Elspeth's Talent, Liliana's Talent, Rowan's Talent (Rings of Brighthearth's copy, without the {2}) and Vivien's Talent.
