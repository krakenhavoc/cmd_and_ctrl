**Which mana paid {X}** (#3941, [ADR 0068](decisions/0068-the-mana-spent-on-a-spell.md)'s 2026-10-16 amendment) — the payment record now says which spent mana paid the restricted `{X}` of a "Spend only <colours> mana on X" cost. The pool solver (`colouredPassX`) keeps the match it already made between mana and requirement: the folded X run is marked (`reqRun.x`, kept apart from an identical printed symbol), and `ManaPool.split` writes the share onto the spent runs as `ManaToken.ForX` (additive snapshot field, v9). `ManaSpent.OnX()` is the view a resolving spell reads ("the amount of {B} spent on X" is `OnX().Count("B")`). CR 601.2h: the caster chooses which mana pays what. The auto-pay solver chooses in the card's favour, `Spec.WantsColors` giving the X part that colour first (the printed `{B}` is paid before X, so it cannot take X's black), and a declared paper payment (#3855) states its own allocation: the picker marks the slots that are the X part (`PaperSlot.X`) and the mana declared for them (`PaperMana.X`); an unmarked declaration leaves the matching to the engine. **Cards:** Soul Burn is Full, its caveat dropped. Drain Life and Consume Spirit read nothing of the record (Drain Life caps its gain by the target, Consume Spirit gains X), so they were exact already. **Not covered:** an unrestricted `{X}` is generic mana that any mana pays, and the record does not tell it from the rest of the cost; no printed card reads it.
