**A land that becomes a basic land type for a while** (#1881, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §1, PR 1) — CR 305.7 from a resolved effect is a `ScopedEffect` kind, `setBasicLandTypes` (`game.ModSetBasicLandTypes`, layer 4, a CR 613.1f removal, reads `Mod.Subtypes`, one or more basic land types; registration and restore refuse anything else). The land's land types are replaced and its other subtypes stay (CR 205.1a, `Characteristic.SetLandSubtypes`, over `game.LandTypes`, CR 205.3i's seventeen), it loses the abilities its rules text gives it and keeps every layer-6 grant, and it taps for the new colour through the intrinsic CR 305.6 path. "In addition to its other types" stays `addSubtypes` (CR 205.1b). The static form `effects.SetsBasicLandType` now replaces land types only, so Magus of the Moon leaves Dryad Arbor its Dryad. Card side (`effects/land_types.go`): `LandBecomes`, `ChooseBasicLandTypeThen` (an option pick at resolution over the five basic land types, a narrower list, or all seventeen), the ability bodies `TargetLandBecomesUntilEOT`, `…WhileSourceRemains`, `…Indefinitely`, `…UntilItsControllersNextTurn` and `TargetLandYouControlGainsChosenTypeUntilEOT`, and the Aura form `ChooseBasicLandTypeAsEnters` / `EnchantedLandIsTheChosenType`. A group's set is fixed as it begins (CR 611.2c). The view stamps `CardView.land_type_effects` and the client draws a land-type badge ("Island until end of turn — Tidal Warrior"). A v7 corpus fixture holds the kind in four durations. **Cards** (all Full): Deepwood Elder, Dream Thrush, Dreamwinder, Floodchaser, Grixis Illusionist, Jinx, Kavu Recluse, Kukemssa Serpent, Moonbow Illusionist, Mystic Compass, Pixie Illusionist, Reef Shaman, Sea Snidd, Shimmering Mirage, Slimy Kavu, Streambed Aquitects, Tidal Warrior, Tideshaper Mystic, Tundra Kavu, Unstable Frontier, Zombie Trailblazer, Elsewhere Flask, Terraformer, Nightcreep, Vision Charm, Navigator's Compass, Energybending, Gaea's Liege, Graceful Antelope, Tide Shaper, Orcish Farmer, Cyclopean Giant, Thelonite Monk; and the static land-type cards (owner decision 4) Spreading Seas, Sea's Claim, Evil Presence, Tainted Well, Lingering Mirage, Contaminated Ground, Lush Growth, Convincing Mirage, Phantasmal Terrain, Blood Moon, Harbinger of the Seas, Illusionary Terrain.
