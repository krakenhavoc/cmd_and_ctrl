**Disturb** (#1855, CR 702.146, [ADR 0107 §4](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md#4-disturb-1855)) — an alternative cost that casts a card's back face out of its owner's graveyard. `AlternativeCost.CastsFace` names the face, and `effects.Disturb(cost)` is the graveyard-bound offer with `CastsFace: 1`. The claim settles the face (`faceForClaimLocked`): the card goes on the stack back face up (CR 712.11a) and enters that way (CR 702.146b), a caller may send face 0 or 1, and the claim is refused on any card that is not a `transform` card with that face. The offer and the zone it opens are judged off the front face, which prints them, and every other announce gate off the back (CR 712.11d, 712.8c), so a disturbed Aura targets with its own enchant clause; the mana value is the front face's, on the stack and the battlefield (CR 712.8c, 712.8e). The back face's "If [this] would be put into a graveyard from anywhere, exile it instead" is `DisturbedExile(name)` (`GraveyardBecomesExile{SelfOnly: true}`) on the `"<oracle_id>#1"` entry, read only while that face is up (CR 712.8a). `disturb` joins `canonicalKeywords`. The bot enumerator and the view turn the card over through `AlternativeCost.CastFaceOf` before reading the target clause, the timing and the cast gate, so the graveyard menu needs no client change. A double-faced card's keywords are now per face (`game.Face.Keywords`, stamped by the importer from Scryfall's union and materialised by `SetFace`), so a back face no longer keeps its front face's keywords; the field is additive in snapshot v7. No other snapshot change. **Cards:** Malevolent Hermit // Benevolent Geist (the row's Waiting card), Baithook Angler, Beloved Beggar, Binding Geist, Brine Comber, Chaplain of Alms, Covert Cutpurse, Covetous Castaway, Devoted Grafkeeper, Distracting Geist, Drogskol Infantry, Faithbound Judge, Galedrifter, Gutter Skulker, Katilda, Dawnhart Martyr, Kindly Ancestor, Lantern Bearer, Lunarch Veteran, Mirrorhall Mimic, Mischievous Catgeist, Mourning Patrol, Overwhelmed Archivist and Twinblade Geist, all Full; Dorothea, Vengeful Victim, Full on the front and with a caveat on the back. **Still open:** Dennick, Pious Apprentice waits on "cards in graveyards can't be targeted" (#1885).
