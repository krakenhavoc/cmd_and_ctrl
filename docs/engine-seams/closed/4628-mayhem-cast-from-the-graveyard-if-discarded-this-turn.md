**Mayhem: cast from the graveyard if discarded this turn** (#4628, [ADR 0025 amendment 2026-10-30 (#4628)](decisions/0025-alternative-costs.md), CR 702.187) — `effects.Mayhem(cost)` is a graveyard-bound alternative cost with no exile clause, and `effects.MayhemCastableZone()` opens the owner's graveyard through `Spec.CastableZonesIf` while `Game.DiscardedThisTurn(card)` holds. The record is a per-object cell of the turn tally (`TurnTally.Resolved`, label `discarded`, keyed by the object's epoch), written off `EventDiscardCard` for a card that landed in a graveyard and flushed with the turn, so a milled card, a card discarded last turn and a card that left the graveyard and came back all answer no (CR 400.7). `AlternativeCost.RequiresDiscardedThisTurn`, read by `validateCastPathLocked`, keeps another permission that opens the graveyard from handing out the mayhem price to a card that was not discarded. The view's `castable_here` and `alternative_costs`, and the bot's cast moves, follow the same predicates with no change. Mayhem without a cost (CR 702.187c) has no card yet. **Cards:** Abomination, World Ravager; Chameleon, Master of Disguise; Green Goblin, Back for More; Rocket-Powered Goblin Glider; Sadistic Slash; Swarm, Being of Bees; The Squadron Sinister (Full).
