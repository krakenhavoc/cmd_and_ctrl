**Emblems that change when you can activate** (#1275, CR 114.3 / 602.5d / 606.3 / 101.1, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md), [ADR 0064 note 2026-09-24](decisions/0064-emblems.md)) — #1208's per-player activation timing had one home, derived from the BATTLEFIELD, and an emblem lives in the command zone, so "you may activate loyalty abilities of planeswalkers you control on any player's turn any time you could cast an instant" had nowhere to be said. It is now the derived home one zone over, and nothing is stored: an emblem leaves only with its owner (CR 800.4a), so its presence is the duration. **Two lines, both the ones the issue named.** `effects.EmblemSpec.ActivationTimings` is the `Spec.ActivationTimings` slot on an emblem, projected by `buildEmblemDef` into the emblem's `CardDef` and checked at boot by the same guard (`checkActivationTimings`, now shared by both homes). `Game.activationTimingVerdictLocked` (`game/activation_timing.go`) walks every seat's `Player.Emblems` after the battlefield — the fifth emblem walk, beside the layer pass's, the trigger harvest's and the two turn-based-action gathers. `Covers` receives the emblem as `q.Source`, so "you" is its owner. **No reader changed**: the engine (`ActivateCatalogAbility`), the bot enumerator, the view's `timing_closed` and the sandbox `ActivateLoyalty` verb all read `ActivationTimingOpenLocked` already, which is the test that they did not have to learn the walk. CR 606.3's other half is untouched: the emblem opens the window and each planeswalker still activates one loyalty ability per turn. **Constructor:** `effects.LoyaltyAbilitiesOfYourPlaneswalkersAtInstantSpeed` — Teferi, Master of Time's clause with the self-reference widened to a control test. **Cards:** Teferi, Temporal Archmage ships `full` (its +1 is `LookAtTopThenTakeOneRestOnBottom(2)`, which #952 had built in the meantime; its −1 untaps up to four target permanents). Teferi's Talent ships `caveats`: Enchant planeswalker (new `EnchantPlaneswalker`) and the draw trigger work, and the granted −12 is the "Abilities granted to other permanents" row above, not this one. Trackers [#883](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/883), [#885](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/885).
