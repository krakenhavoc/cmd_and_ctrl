**A spell exiled from the stack takes its stack record with it; "exile target spell"; plot's effect half; and the cause of a zone change** (#1318, #1320, CR 400.7 / CR 701.6 / CR 702.170c-d / CR 603.2c, [ADR 0013 §5ad](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md), [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)) — no row of its own: both were filed straight from the *Aang is so flashy* deck triage (#1306). **#1318** was a live bug on the deck's commander. Aang, Swift Savior airbends "a creature or SPELL" through the plain exile route, which moved the card and retired the `StackMeta` record only when the route said `DropStackMeta` — and only the counter / return-to-hand body and the sandbox move did. The spell's record stayed behind and the table wedged. The flag is gone: `executeZoneRouteLocked` retires the record, and `routeCardToZoneLocked` takes the CR 608.2h last-known record, whenever the card came FROM the stack, whatever verb asked. `Game.ExileSpellForEffect` / `ExileSpellThenForEffect` (`effects.ExileTargetSpell`) is the third verb over `exitSpellFromStackLocked` — not a counter, refuses a non-spell, and its `Then` hears whether the card really reached exile. `Game.PlotExiledCardForEffect` (`effects.PlotExiled`, `game/plot.go`) is CR 702.170d as an ADR 0066 permission with a new `TimingPlot` that no flash grant widens; the plot KEYWORD's hand special action followed in #1342 (see "Special actions from the hand" below). **#1320:** a routed zone change now carries `Event.Cause` / `CauseController` / `CauseItem` — `effect` inferred from the resolving item (`Game.resolving`), and `cost`, `special_action`, `rule` and `manual` named by the routes that know it, which is what keeps a stale resolving slot from claiming a cost. `game.ExiledBySpellOrAbilityOf` reads it. **#1341, closed:** two foretells in one priority window used to share one event batch, so `OncePerBatch` counted one occurrence where CR 603.2c counts two (each special action is its own event); `PerformSpecialAction` now opens its own batch (`event_batch.go`), the same boundary a resolution or a step entry opens. Proof cards: Aang, Swift Savior airbending a spell (still caveated for waterbend, #1310); Aven Interrupter (`full`); **Ranar the Ever-Watchful is now `full`** — the foretell discount (#1319) and the one-event-batch gap (#1341) were its last two caveats and both are closed. Still open, as a general engine limitation rather than a per-card caveat: the destroy / SBA exit carries no cause, so a Rest in Peace exile is not "a spell you control exiled it"; a cost is recorded but not counted as "a spell or ability exiles".
