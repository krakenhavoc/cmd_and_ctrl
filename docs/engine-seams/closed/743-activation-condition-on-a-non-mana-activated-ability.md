**Activation condition on a non-mana activated ability** (#743) - `ActivatedAbilityShape.Condition` / `effects.ActivatedAbility.Condition`, the same `func(g, controller, source) bool` mana abilities carry ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) activation-condition addendum). Checked once in `ActivateCatalogAbility` right after the timing check, before X, targets or any cost (`ErrConditionNotMet`, nothing paid), never at resolution (CR 602.1b), and mirrored in the legal enumerator. `ActivatedAbilityView` and `ManaAbilityView` both carry `condition_unmet`, and the client greys the row. Builders in `cards/effects/activation_conditions.go`: `DuringYourTurn`, `OpponentControlsAtLeast`, `OpponentControlsMore`, `GraveyardAtLeast`, `NoCardsInHand`, `SourceHasCountersAtLeast`, `LifeAtLeastAboveStarting`, beside the existing `ControlsAtLeast`. Sanctum of Eternity lost its sorcery-speed caveat. Shipped on Tectonic Edge, Weathered Wayfarer, Bonders' Enclave, Cryptic Caves, Endless Atlas, Kelpie Guide, Barbarian Ring, Cephalid Coliseum, Rivendell, Sea Gate Wreckage, Minas Tirith, Idol of Oblivion, Speaker of the Heavens, Luminarch Ascension, Sliver Hive, Inventors' Fair, Leechridden Swamp, Mistveil Plains, Lagomos, Hand of Hatred, Hall of Oracles, The Seedcore, Goro-Goro, Disciple of Ryusei, Lilypad Village and Arch of Orazca (the city's blessing read live, with Tendershoot Dryad's caveat). Unblocked and still to be written: Blitzball (#397), Stella Lee, Wild Card (#455). Still waiting on other rows: Kitsa, Otterball Elite (prowess, #706), Glint-Horn Buccaneer (whose discard-a-card cost closed with #660, so it is now unblocked and still to be written), Barad-dûr (amass), Varragoth, Bloodsky Sire and Broadside Bombardiers (per-source activation count), Hydra Broodmaster and Alpha Deathclaw (monstrosity), Metallurgic Summonings (exile-this cost), Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim (reading the sacrificed creature), and Desert (the engine clears combat as the end of combat step begins, so no creature is attacking in the only step Desert can be activated; CR 511.3 keeps them in combat until the step ends, #785). Goro-Goro declares the same gap as a caveat. `SourceIsAttacking`, the step-window builders and `AllOf` from the addendum's table wait for their first card.
