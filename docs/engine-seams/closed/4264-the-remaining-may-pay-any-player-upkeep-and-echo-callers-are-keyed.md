**The remaining may-pay, any-player, upkeep and echo callers are keyed, and a keyed prompt carries the item that asked it** (#4264, the second PR, amendments of 2026-10-24 to [ADR 0018](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md), [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md), [ADR 0150](decisions/0150-other-players-pay-any-player-offers-and-assist.md), [ADR 0187](decisions/0187-any-player-pays-per-object-graveyard-and-either-payments.md), [ADR 0198](decisions/0198-outside-the-game-is-the-card-database-without-a-sideboard.md) and [ADR 0041](decisions/0041-game-persistence.md)) — a keyed branch ran against a stand-in item that had a controller and a source card and nothing else, so every caller whose branch read the trigger's event, its targets, the source object's epoch (CR 400.7) or a Go value stayed a closure. **The asking item is plain data:** `game.AskedItem` rides `PendingChoice.Asked` (an additive snapshot field), `PayUnlessAnswer.Item` / `AnyPlayerAnswer.Item` hand a branch the item rebuilt from it, and `PendingChoice.OptionObjects` carries named objects with their epochs. **Callers keyed:** the remaining 34 `MayPay` callers (Mana Vault and Dance of the Dead among them), the remaining 17 `AnyPlayerPays` callers, echo (both answers under one key), Heroism, Jace Multiverse Architect, Pillar Tombs of Aku, the cumulative upkeep that flips coins, Springheart Nantuko, Assimilate Essence, the waterbend clause of a ward (`PendingChoice.PayTap`, `RegisterTapCost`, ledger kind `tapcost`), the per-object any-player offer (`RegisterAnyPlayerPaysEach`; Cleansing) and Extrapolate the Impossible's opponent and reveal picks (`RevealPickPrompt.ThenKey`). 59 keys appended to the ledger and an older binary refuses a file naming one. Closure counts unchanged. **Still a closure:** `MayPayX`, the energy payments, `MayChoice`, ward's life, sacrifice and discard confirms, and the multi-seat discard runs.
