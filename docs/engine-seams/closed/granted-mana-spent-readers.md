**Granted mana-spent readers, and sunburst as a keyword** (#1552, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §11, PR 10) — closes the "Mana that does something when it's spent" row. Four pieces, all reading the one payment record (`StackItem.Paid.Mana`, CR 400.7d). `Game.EntrySpentForEffect(ev)` hands the entering spell's whole spend, as a `game.ManaSpent`, to a replacement on ANY permanent inside the CR 614 entry window: the known zero for an entry that was not a cast, unknown for a waived payment. Sunburst (CR 702.44) joins `canonicalKeywords` as a cumulative keyword, and `applySunburstLocked` counts its instances on the resolving stack card (printed, deck-imported, or given to the spell), seeding a +1/+1 counter per colour on an object entering as a printed creature and a charge counter otherwise; `effects.SunburstCounters` is gone and Etched Oracle moved to the keyword. `GrantKeywordsToSpellForEffect` takes a `Duration`, and the re-pin to the permanent (`repinSpellControlLocked`, `adoptSpellControlLocked`) keeps it, so "it gains haste until end of turn" on a spell ends at cleanup on the creature (CR 400.7a, 611.2a). Spend riders gained `spell_gains` (`effects.SpentSpellGains`: a keyword grant to the spell made at the spend, one per production) and a counted enters-with-counters rider (`ManaSpendRider.Count`, `RegisterManaRiderCount`, read as the creature enters). Restore refuses a rider kind or count key the binary cannot read, wherever a token is carried (`snapshot_mana_riders.go`). Satoru's condition reads the whole event batch as a CR 603.4 intervening if. **Cards:** Lux Artillery, Coin of Mastery, Satoru, the Infiltrator and Opal Palace keep only the strict-mana caveat; new are Kalain, Reclusive Painter, Generator Servant, Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind, Tyvar Kell (Full), Freestrider Commando, Solar Array and the printed sunburst cards Suntouched Myr, Skyreach Manta, Pentad Prism, Clearwater Goblet, Opaline Bracers, Sawtooth Thresher, Lunar Avenger, Heliophial, Engineered Explosives, Suncrusher, Solarion, Baton of Courage, Infused Arrows, Spinal Parasite and Arcbound Wanderer (modular's dies trigger with its last-known counters). **Still waiting:** Domri, Chaos Bringer on riot (ADR 0109 PR 9, #1556), and Primeval Spawn on free casts with a total mana value budget (#2017).
