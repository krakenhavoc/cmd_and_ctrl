**Skips by choice: skip a turn, skip main phases, and "you may skip that step instead"** (#6226, CR 500.11, 614.10, 614.10a) — Fatespinner's "the player skips each instance of the chosen step or phase this turn" is `Game.SkipThisTurnForEffect(player, source, part, label)` with `part` one of `SkipThisTurnDraw`, `SkipThisTurnMain` or `SkipThisTurnCombat`: one `ScopedEffect` with a `ModSkipThisTurn` mod (`skipThisTurn`) naming the player, the turn (`Amount`, the `Turn.Seq`) and the part (`Text`). It is read at step entry, so every draw step, main phase (an added one included) or combat phase of that turn that would begin is walked past, and it goes as the next turn begins. Time Vault's "If you would begin your turn while this artifact is tapped, you may skip that turn instead. If you do, untap this artifact" is a `game.TurnSkipOffer` on `CardDef.TurnSkipOffers` (plain data: `WhileTapped`, `UntapSource`). The rotation seam pauses the turn change (`TurnChangePending`) on a keyed confirm (`confirm/turn-skip-offer`) for a normal or an extra turn. Yes adds one ADR 0188 skip charge that the same turn spends at once, and untaps the source. No resumes with that source declined. Fasting's "If you would begin your draw step, you may skip that step instead. If you do, you gain 2 life" needed nothing new: it is an `Optional` step-transition replacement whose `Replace` cancels and gains (pinned by `TestOptionalDrawStepSkipGainsLife`). **Cards**: Fatespinner (OLD b602), Fasting (OLD b861), Island Sanctuary (OLD b902) and Time Vault (OLD b906) are built by their OLD batches. Island Sanctuary's "you can't be attacked except by creatures with flying and/or islandwalk" still needs a live creature-filtered can't-attack-player restriction.
