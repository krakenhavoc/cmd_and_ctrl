**Until your next upkeep** (#5737, CR 500.10, 503.1a, 800.4m, [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md)) — "gains forestwalk until your next upkeep" ends as its player's next upkeep step begins, which is neither "until your next turn" (that ends a step earlier, as the turn begins) nor a turn count: a turn can have a second upkeep (CR 500.10), and a skipped turn or upkeep never begins one. The thirteenth duration kind, `game.UntilYourNextUpkeep` (persisted as 12, appended before the sentinel), is stamped by `Game.UntilYourNextUpkeepDuration(player)` with the player's `Player.UpkeepsBegun` and is over once that count has grown, a pure read in `durationExpiredLocked`. The upkeep's step-entry hook counts the step, sweeps the durations and only then announces the step, so the effect is gone before the upkeep's own triggers are put on the stack (CR 503.1a) — the order the end step already uses for "until your next end step". A player who has left the game takes no more upkeeps, so the stamp also carries the seat-turn count of the turn that upkeep falls in, and the effect ends as that turn would have begun (CR 800.4m). Card side: `effects.DurationUntilYourNextUpkeep(ctx, player)`. Snapshot: no new field; a restore point holding one is refused by an older binary through `DurationKind.Known`. **Cards:** Erhnam Djinn and Xenic Poltergeist (Full).
