**Attack requirements** (#1571, CR 508.1d / 701.15b, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-24 (#1571)](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) Decisions 48–52). "Attacks each combat if able", "creatures your opponents control attack this turn if able" and goad's two requirements are enforced over the whole declaration, which must obey the most requirements obeyable without breaking a restriction or paying a cost. `Characteristic.AttackRequirements` carries the requirements layers write, and `Card.GoadedBy` carries goad's pair. `attackRequirementReachLocked` is the requirements a staged declaration obeys plus the best free addition, solved exactly under attack-limit capacities by a small min-cost flow. `DeclareAttackerWith` / `DeclareAttackersWith` refuse a declaration that lowers reach. The active player's pass in declare_attackers (and `AdvanceStep`) is the checkpoint, run once per combat (`Game.attacksDeclared`): it is refused while a free addition would obey another requirement, so declaring nothing is refused only when a requirement can be met. The enumerator offers only what the verbs accept, withholds the pass while it would be refused, and marks the answering attacks `AlwaysLegal`; a declining bot takes that answer. Refusals are `illegal_attack` / `attack_requirement`, and the wire adds `CardView.must_attack`. Floating requirements are ADR 0041 data: the `addAttackRequirement` mod, with `ScopedEffect.Scope` `opponentsCreatures` for CR 611.2c's live set. **Bident of Thassa** and **The Akroan War** ship `full`; **Alela, Cunning Conqueror**'s goad is enforced, with one caveat left (one goader per creature).
