**Clash and "whenever you win a clash"** (#5424, CR 701.30a, 701.30b, 701.30c, 701.30d) — the engine had no clash, so Hoarder's Greed and Marvo, Deep Operative waited. `Game.ClashForEffect(source, player, opponent, then)` and `Game.ClashWithAnOpponentForEffect(source, player, then)` (`game/clash.go`) are the keyword action: both clashers reveal their top card at once, the winner is settled by the reveal (a higher mana value than every other card revealed, so a player with an empty library cannot win and the other wins with any card; a tie has no winner), each clasher who revealed a card is asked top or bottom in APNAP order through a keyed `option_pick` (`option-pick/engine-clash`; "which opponent" is `option-pick/engine-clash-opponent`), and the cards move once every answer is in. The run rides the prompt as plain data, so a table waiting on a decision is a restore point; a clasher who leaves with the question open leaves the card on top and the clash still finishes. `EventClashed` (one per clasher) and `EventClashWon` follow the move and are the log's new `clash` and `clash_won` lines (`protocol/log.go`, `client/src/lib/protocol.ts`, `gameLog.ts`). The card's rest is a `KeyedTail` told 1 when its controller won. Card side: `effects.Clash`, `effects.ClashWithAnOpponent`, `wonTheClash`, `youWonAClash`. No snapshot field and no closure route added. **Cards now Full:** Hoarder's Greed, Marvo, Deep Operative. **Not built:** a CR 614 window on the action.
