**Supertypes and controller in the leaves-the-battlefield last-known information** (#1682, CR 603.10a / 108.4, [ADR 0027 amendment 2026-09-28, #1682](decisions/0027-attack-triggers.md)). Rakdos Joins Up asked `isLegendary` of the card in the graveyard, so a Clone that died a copy of a legend was not counted; and every "a creature you control dies" clause read `dead.Controller` off the moved card, which names no one in the rules (a card in a graveyard has no controller) and is right today only because the move leaves the field alone. `EventLTB` now also carries `Event.LastKnownSupertypes` and `Event.LastKnownController`, stamped by the same `exitLKI` as the #1675 types and #1679 subtypes; `Event.WasSupertype` and `Event.LeftUnderControlOf` read them. Card side, `leftAsSupertype(ev, c, supertype)` and `leftUnderControlOf(ev, c)` are the questions: Rakdos Joins Up asks the first, and 43 "you control" / "an opponent controls" dies and leaves-the-battlefield conditions (Zulaport Cutthroat, Midnight Reaper, Massacre Wurm, Death Tyrant, The Ozolith, Scrap Trawler and the rest) ask the second; the per-turn `CreaturesDied` tally credits the stamped controller too. Owner clauses are unchanged. A stolen creature sacrificed by its thief triggers the thief's Zulaport and not the owner's. **Still open:** colour as it last existed (Teysa, Orzhov Scion's "black creature").
