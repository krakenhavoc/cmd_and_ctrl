**Granted cast and play permissions** (#652, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)) - `game.CastPermission` (`game/cast_permission.go`) is the ONE model of "an effect lets you cast or play a card from a zone its own text does not open", and it replaced `ExilePlayPermission` rather than sitting beside it: impulse exile, airbend, warp, cascade and a defeated Siege's face grant are all `CastPermission` values now. Two scopes — `ScopeCards` names card OBJECTS (one card, or a set locked at resolution under CR 611.2c), `ScopeStanding` is a rule over a zone narrowed by a pure-data `PermissionFilter`. Three homes, each chosen so a duration falls out: `Player.CastPermissions` for stored grants (a `game.Duration` since #945 — ADR 0063's one duration vocabulary, `UntilEndOfTurn` for impulse exile and Snapcaster, `UntilEndOfYourNextTurnDuration` for Reckless Impulse, the `WhileInZone` kind for airbend, warp and foretell, plus a `NotBeforeTurn` FLOOR that is not a duration at all: CR 702.185a's and CR 702.143a's "on a later turn"), the battlefield for standing ones (`Spec.CastPermissions` → `CardDef` → `CatalogCastPermissions`, derived per query so two Underworld Breaches compose), and nothing at all for the rest. CR 400.7 is an identity check rather than a sweep: `Card.ObjectEpoch` counts how many times a card has become a new object and a permission names `{instance, epoch}`, which replaced six hand-written "zero the grant on the way out" sites (one of which was missing). One query — `Game.CastPermissionForLocked` — is read by the cast path, the view (`castable_here`, and since #978 every announce-time stamp on an exiled or library-top card) and the bot enumerator, so none of them can disagree; the price rides the same object, synthesising the `AlternativeCost` the cast is judged under, so CR 702.34a's "exile it if the flashback cost was paid" (now on `StackItem.AltCostExiles`) and CR 702.138b's "escaped" work however the permission arrived. A card that both prints and is granted the same key keeps its PRINTED cost. The PRICE question itself has one answer since #673 — `Game.CastOffersForLocked`, the list of CR 118.9 costs a cast may claim out of a zone, with a nil entry for the printed one — and since #1012 the view reads it too, so `alternative_costs` on the wire is that list verbatim and the client's picker and the bot's move list are the same set by construction. Two wire facts are derived from it and from nowhere else: `castable_here` is "no `cant_cast` and at least one claimable price" (#1015 — an escape card in a graveyard too small to pay for it is no longer a cast surface), and the new `alternative_cost_required` is "the printed cost is not among them", which the cost picker reads instead of inferring. Still open here: a permission over a card in somebody ELSE's graveyard reaches the view as nothing at all (#1022). `CastPermission.Timing` is where a grant says it opens the sorcery-speed gate, and no card in this batch does. Card side: `effects.GrantFlashbackToCard`, `effects.GrantCastFromYourGraveyard`, `Spec.CastPermissions`. Shipped on Snapcaster Mage, Past in Flames, The Grim Captain's Locker and Underworld Breach. Queued behind the same model, each card work plus a key: madness (#657), foretell (#658) and suspend's free cast (#659) have all shipped on it, and madness is `CastPermission.Timing`'s first user — the `TimingFlash` value ADR 0066 reserved for it by name. Out of scope and stated in the ADR: Muldrotha's per-type per-turn allowance, the Descendants' Path "reveal and cast it free" family, and #760's announce-time cast gate (hooked, not built).
