**An Aura put onto the battlefield chooses what it enchants** (#3158, CR 303.4f / 303.4g / 303.4i, [ADR 0154](decisions/0154-auras-that-enchant-a-card-in-a-graveyard.md)'s 2026-10-13 amendment) — an Aura that entered other than by resolving as an Aura spell landed unattached and CR 704.5m buried it, so Animate Dead carried a caveat for Sun Titan and Replenish, and eleven more cards declared the same gap. The choice is now the LAST step of the entry's CR 614 window (`settleEnteringAuraHostLocked`, `game/aura_host.go`), called from the apply-loop's settled branch: every entry door that can pause — reanimation, library search, exile return, the hand / library / graveyard / exile put batch, created tokens — asks with no per-door code, and a simultaneous entry (Replenish) asks once per Aura. The candidates are the Aura's catalogued enchant clause read without the targeting gate (hexproof and shroud don't stop it; protection does, CR 702.16c), ADR 0154's graveyard clauses included, minus the Aura itself and the other cards of the same entry. One candidate is a forced choice and asks nothing; none leaves the Aura where it was (CR 303.4g, a token isn't created). The answer rides `ReplacementEvent.auraHost` to the one finisher, which attaches between the zone move and `EventETB` (`attachEnteringAuraLocked`), where a resolving Aura spell is attached, so Animate Dead's enters trigger finds its graveyard host. An effect that NAMES the host — `SearchLibrarySpec.EntersAttachedTo` (Light-Paws), `ZoneEntryOptions.AttachedTo` (Mantle of the Ancients, Gift of Immortality), `TokenEntryOptions.AttachedTo` (a copy of an Aura spell) — is not asked; its host is checked against the same predicate and an illegal one leaves the Aura where it was (CR 303.4i). The prompt is the new kind `aura_host`: the `pick_target` projection and `{target}` payload, board-answered on the client (a graveyard card through the zone browser), one enumerated move per host, and a heuristic that takes the best creature card in any graveyard for a graveyard clause and reads a battlefield host the way it reads an Aura spell's target. No snapshot change: the paused entry rides `replacementResume` like every entry question and is counted by the census. The sandbox `move_card` verb, the one entry that can't pause, keeps its old behaviour. **Cards (12):** Animate Dead, Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice, Brilliant Restoration, Ondu Spiritdancer, Zur the Enchanter, Carmen, Cruel Skymarcher, Open the Vaults, Guardian Sunmare, Armored Skyhunter, It That Betrays and Replenish are Full; Nazgûl Battle-Mace loses its Aura caveat and keeps its Rest in Peace one.
