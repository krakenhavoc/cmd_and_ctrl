**Triggered mana abilities, and the colour they read** (#763, [ADR 0074](decisions/0074-triggered-mana-abilities.md)) — closes BOTH the "Triggered mana abilities (CR 605.1b)" row (audit 15 / 19) and the "Mana-ability trigger carrying produced colour" row (count 2, audit 1 / 4), which were always one pipeline. `Spec.ManaTriggers []game.ManaTrigger` is a slot of its own, read through `CatalogManaTriggers` / `game.ManaTriggersForCard` (`CatalogAbilityKey` + the ADR 0071 `ActiveWhen` gate, like `TriggersForCard`) — NOT a flag on `TriggeredAbility`, because none of that struct's nine stack-shaped fields survive an ability with no item, no targets and no batch. Each entry is `{Label, AppliesTo(prod, source, g) bool, Produced(prod, source, g) string, ActiveWhen}`, and `Produced` returns the ordinary `ParseProducedMana` grammar so "{G}", "{G}{G}" and a pipe all work. ONE firing function, `fireManaTriggersLocked(ManaProduced{Source, Controller, Colors}, pending)`, called from the three production sites and never from `AddMana` (CR 106.12a): `ActivateManaAbility` after the rider when it minted directly, `ResolveManaChoice` when the answered pick came from a tap (`PendingChoice.ManaTapped` — the only place a Birds-style colour is known), and `materializePlanLocked` per planned source. Each real card takes exactly one of the first two branches, so it fires once. A colour choice INSIDE a trigger reuses the pipeline's two existing modes through the shared `addManaSlotsLocked`: a `mana_pick` by hand, a greedy `pickColorForSlot` against the cast under the auto-tapper. `EventManaAdded` now carries the colour it added in `Event.Colors`. Triggered mana does not re-trigger (the adder never calls the firing function, which is also the rule). Shipped on Wild Growth, Overgrowth, Utopia Sprawl (with #742's chosen colour), Fertile Ground and Mana Flare. Out of scope and still open: mana-production REPLACEMENT (CR 106.12b, Nyxbloom Ancient — its own row below), turn-scoped mana triggers (High Tide, Bubbling Muck — #663's registry question), counting triggered mana in the auto-tap PLAN (declared: the planner may tap one land too many and the surplus floats), and production without a tap.
