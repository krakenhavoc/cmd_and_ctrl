**Can't become suspected, passing the suspicion on, and goads that last** (#2733, CR 701.60, 701.15, 603.2c, [ADR 0071 amendment 2026-10-10](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)) — the four suspect cards #2698 left out. "Can't become suspected" is a sixth restriction bit, `game.CantBecomeSuspected` (wire token `cant_become_suspected`), written by an ordinary `RestrictAttached` static and read by the one suspect mutator: `Game.CanBecomeSuspected` (a creature on the battlefield, not suspected, not restricted, after a stale-layer recompute) is what `SuspectForEffect` asks, and what a card offering a choice of creatures to suspect asks to build the offer. "One of the other creatures" in a "one or more … enter" ability is `effects.enteredInTriggeringBatch`: every `EventETB` stamped with the triggering event's `Batch`, read back out of the event log the snapshot carries, keeping only the objects still on the battlefield (CR 400.7) with the controller each entered under (the loser of its first later control change, else its controller now). A goad can now be a continuous effect that cards see: `game.GoadRequirements` is goad's CR 701.15b pair with `GoadedBy` set, written by both `effects.GoadAttached` and the new scoped mod kind `ModGoad` (`game.GoadMod(by)`, an on-disk identity an older binary refuses), and `Card.Goaded()` / `StaticGoaders()` / `AllGoaders()` read the marker and the continuous goads together while `IsGoaded` / `Goaders` / `LatestGoader` stay marker-only for the snapshot. Vengeful Ancestor and Puppet Master read `Goaded()`, and the wire's `goaders` list includes continuous goaders ahead of the marker ones, so its last entry is still `goaded_by`. **Cards:** Airtight Alibi, Frantic Scapegoat, Nelly Borca, Impulsive Accuser and Hot Pursuit (Full); Shiny Impetus and Incriminating Impetus lose their "isn't counted as goaded" caveat.
