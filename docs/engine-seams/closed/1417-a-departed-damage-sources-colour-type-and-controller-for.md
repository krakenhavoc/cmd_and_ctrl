**A departed damage source's colour, type and controller for protection and "a red source you control"** (#1417, CR 608.2h / 400.7 / 702.16e, [ADR 0056 amendment 2026-09-24 (follow-up)](decisions/0056-infect-wither-toxic.md) Decision 12, [ADR 0072 amendment 2026-09-24](decisions/0072-protection.md)) — the last named piece of the #1396 entry below. The damage event's `SourceLKI`, which CR 702.16e protection reads, was snapshotted from the source's current zone, so a creature that had left before its damage event (a dies trigger, a creature killed in response to its own ability) was judged by its graveyard card's printed colour. `effectDamageTailLocked` (`game/damage_tail.go`) now takes it from the same `lastKnownPermanents` record `departedDamageSourceLocked` already returns for lifelink and deathtouch, by object when a ref is given and by the "current epoch = recorded + 1" rule for an instance ID. Spells on the stack, live permanents and cards that have moved on keep the current-zone read (#662). Torbran, Thane of Red Fell, Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might and Mechanized Warfare read the same snapshot through the new `effects.damageSourceCharacteristics` instead of looking the card up. No caveat named the gap. The proof is Murderous Redcap turned blue by Cerulean Wisps and then killed: protection from blue prevents its damage, and none of the three red-source cards raise it. ~~Still open: the CR 608.2b target re-check reads a departed ability source's graveyard card (#1429)~~ — **closed by #1429**, the entry above. **#1430 closed the other half**, [ADR 0056 amendment 2026-09-24 (follow-up 2)](decisions/0056-infect-wither-toxic.md) Decision 13: the four catalog damage replacements that test only the source's controller or type (Angrath's Marauders and its three siblings, Fated Firepower, Gratuitous Violence, Inquisitor's Flail) now read `effects.damageSourceCharacteristics` too, instead of a fresh `LookupCardForEffect` at apply time. Proved directly against each registered `Spec`'s `AppliesTo` closure (`departed_source_matcher_test.go`), the same technique `TestOjerAxonilLeavesCombatDamageAlone` uses. Still open: `EventDealDamage` **triggers** still look the source up — `game.Event` carries no snapshot at all, so that half needs a `SourceLKI` on the event first (#1430).
