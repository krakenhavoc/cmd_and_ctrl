**Abilities of a spell on the stack** (#1865, CR 113.6 / CR 602.1b / CR 602.2 / CR 112.2 / CR 109.5 / CR 115.7d / CR 400.7, [ADR 0106 §1 amendment 2026-10-16](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md)) — an activated ability that works while its card is a spell on the stack, which ADR 0106 §1 had left out of scope. Lightning Storm is the only printed card. A row declares `Zones: []game.ZoneKind{game.ZoneStack}`, as cycling declares the hand, and `effects.Register` now lets an `AnyPlayer` row declare it. `game.MayActivate` answers for a spell as for a permanent: its controller (every spell has one, CR 112.2), or anyone for an any-player row. `ActivateCatalogAbility` already found the source in any zone; it gains a stack arm beside the battlefield's, and the costs, timing, the item and the "you" of the effect are the activator's (CR 602.1a, 109.5), so the player who discards the land is the one who may choose the storm's new target (CR 115.7d, through `effects.ChangeTargets`, judged for the spell). `game.AbilitySourceSpellForEffect` is the spell only while it is still the object the ability came from (CR 400.7): countered in response, the ability puts nothing on the card in the graveyard and asks nothing. The enumerator walks the stack behind `game.HasStackAbility` and labels another player's spell with its controller; the view files each seat's own copy of the row on the stack card's `zone_abilities` (`stampStackAbilities`, through `castOffers`, promoted for the viewer on the stack as in exile); the client draws a button per row on every stack surface beside Counter and hands it to the announce chain, and shows the spell's counters as a chip. The row declares `Answers: prevent`, threat-gated, so smart autopass stops the player the storm is aimed at; the heuristic bot never pumps another player's storm (no priced purpose, decision 8). Nothing is serialised: the item restores from its catalog row and the counters ride the spell card. Lightning Storm lands Full.
