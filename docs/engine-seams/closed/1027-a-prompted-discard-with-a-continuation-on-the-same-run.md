**A prompted discard with a continuation, on the same run** (#1027 / #1026, [ADR 0013 §5y](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)): `QueueDiscardChoiceForEffect` discards nothing either — it queues a question over the player's own hand and returns the PROMPT'S ID — and since #853 a discard is a real exit, so a discarded commander's CR 903.9 prompt holds the batch for an action after the answer. Archon of Cruelty is the card the hole was filed on: *"target opponent sacrifices …, discards a card, and loses 3 life. You draw a card and gain 3 life"* ran its last three clauses beside two open prompts. **The run is #1019's, and the body is now shared** (`game/prompt_run.go`): `promptRun`, `runPromptsLocked` with the per-verb prompt builder injected, one `settleRunLegLocked`, one `clonePromptRuns`, one `SeatCards`, and `PromptedSacrifices` / `PromptedDiscards` as two defined types over it spelling their own verb. `Game.sacrificeRuns` became `Game.promptRuns` (ONE registry) and `PendingChoice.sacrificeRun` became `PendingChoice.promptRun`; `defaultDroppedChoiceLocked` branches on that LINK rather than on the prompt kind, because a discard prompt is a `PendingChoiceChooseCards` and Thoughtseize's revealed-hand pick is the same kind and no run's leg. Three entry points plus a fire-and-forget fan-out: `PlayerDiscardsThenForEffect`, `PlayersDiscardThenForEffect`, `EachPlayerDiscardsThenForEffect`, `EachPlayerDiscardsForEffect`. **"Discarded this way" is CR 701.8a's move OUT of the hand** (`discardedThisWayLocked`, `sacrificedThisWayLocked`'s shape with the hand in place of the battlefield), so a madness card exiled instead of binned counts, a Library of Leng card on top of the library counts, a CR 903.9 commander counts, and a leg the CR 614 window cancelled does not; the landed list is accumulated by `discardBatchLocked` and carried forward by value across a paused leg. `DiscardPrompt.Then` stays as the LEG's own sentence and takes `(g, seat, discarded)` now, because a fan-out copies one template per seat. `DiscardRandomThenForEffect` is the random discard's answer to the same lint row. **4 cards converted:** Archon of Cruelty and Vicious Rumors for the ORDER, Syphon Mind and the `b39MayDiscardThenDraw` pair (Thrilling Discovery, Cathartic Pyre) for the COUNT — Syphon Mind's declared "one card per answer" simplification and its hand-rolled empty-hand skip are both gone. **Springbloom Druid** (#1026) rides #1019's sacrifice run at resolution instead of picking its land as a trigger target, and lost its caveat. Still open: `graveyardVerb` is the last lint row with no continuation form (`PutIntoGraveyardForEffect`), and a discard prompt whose candidates all leave the hand before it is answered has no prune beside `pruneSacrificeChoicesLocked`, so it wedges rather than settling its leg — unreachable from the catalog today, filed as #1045.
