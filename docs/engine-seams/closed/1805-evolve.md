**Evolve** (#1805, CR 702.100, [ADR 0106 §3](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md#3-evolve-1805)) — the second engine-derived keyword trigger, built on prowess's model. `evolve` joins `canonicalKeywords` and is cumulative (CR 702.100d), and `keywordTriggersFor` hands `TriggersForCard` one evolve trigger per instance on the effective ability list (`game/evolve.go`), so a printed evolve (stamped by the deck importer), a token's, and a layer-6 grant (Tyranid Prime's "other creatures you control", Propagator Drone's "creature tokens you control") all work with no catalog entry. The comparison is **CR 702.100a**'s — the old registry note credited it to 702.100b — power against power and toughness against toughness, false if either side is not a creature (702.100c), and made twice under the intervening-"if" rule (CR 603.4): on `EventETB`, with the layer cache already caught up, and again when the keyed body `evolve/grow` resolves, reading the entered creature through the item's carried trigger object, live or as it last existed (CR 608.2h), and the evolving creature live only (CR 400.7). The +1/+1 counter goes through the CR 614 counter window, and the new `EventEvolved` is emitted behind it only when a counter actually landed — CR 702.100b's "evolves", which Renegade Krasis and Watchful Radstag watch. Evolve triggers keep the CR 603.3b ordering prompt (they change each other's comparisons), and each label names the creature that entered, so two triggers on one creature from creatures entering together are told apart. `evolve/grow` is a new on-disk body key; nothing else in the snapshot changed. **Cards:** Dinosaur Egg drops its caveat and goes Full, plus Tyranid Prime, Propagator Drone, Renegade Krasis, Watchful Radstag, Darwin, Adaptive Mutant, Elusive Krasis, Experiment One, Gyre Sage, Lonis, Genetics Expert, Pollywog Prodigy, Ray Fillet, Wave Warrior, Scurry Oak and Simic Fluxmage, all Full. Adaptive Snapjaw, Battering Krasis, Clinging Anemones, Cloudfin Raptor, Crocanura, Gluttonous Slug and Shambleshark need no card file. **Still open:** Fathom Mage waits on a trigger that fires once per counter (#1841) and Simic Manipulator on a target bounded by the counters removed to pay (#1842).
