**Cards that can't enter the battlefield** (#1985, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) amendment of 2026-10-15) — "Lands can't enter the battlefield" (Worms of the Earth) is a prohibition on a zone change, not a land play (CR 305.4), so the land-play gate never reached a fetched, reanimated or flickered land. `Spec.EntryRestrictions` (`game.EntryRestriction{Label, Forbids(EntryQuery), ActiveWhen}`, constructors `LandsCantEnter` and `CardsInZonesCantEnter`) is read from the battlefield and emblems through `CatalogAbilityKey` and asked by `Game.refuseForbiddenEntryLocked` (`game/entry_gate.go`) at the top of every pass of the CR 614 window, before any replacement is gathered — so it reaches every battlefield entry (a land play, a resolving permanent spell, a search, a reanimation, an exile return, a hand put, an entry batch, a created token, and each of those resumed after a prompt), asks no shockland or riot question about an entry that cannot happen, and catches a creature told to enter as a copy of a land on the next pass. `EntryQuery.Card` is the card in the zone it is leaving; `EntryQuery.WouldBe()` is the CR 614.12 look-ahead's permanent, so a land manifested face down enters. A refused object stays where it was (CR 101.2) and a search still shuffles; a refused token is never created (CR 111.1); a refused permanent spell goes to its owner's graveyard (CR 608.3e). The land-play gate asks the same restrictions, so the enumerator and the view never offer a land that can't enter. It is not a replacement, so it takes no part in CR 616 ordering: Grafdigger's Cage moved onto it from its cancelling replacement. **Cards:** Kunoros, Hound of Athreos, Soulless Jailer and Weathered Runestone (Full); Grafdigger's Cage moved. Worms of the Earth (#3576) now needs only its card file: the either payment shipped in PR #3754.
