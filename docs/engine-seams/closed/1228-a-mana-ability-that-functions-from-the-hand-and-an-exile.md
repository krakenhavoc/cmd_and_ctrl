**A MANA ability that functions from the HAND, and an exile-this cost on it** (#1228, [ADR 0011](decisions/0011-mana-pool-and-auto-tapper.md)'s and [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)'s 2026-09-23 addenda) — finishes "Ability activatable from a non-battlefield zone" down to its one remaining non-zone item, and closes the two cards #1221 could not take off the row. CR 605.1a makes "Exile this card from your hand: Add {R}" a mana ability, so it takes the OTHER entry point: `ActivateManaAbility` found its source on the battlefield and nowhere else, and `ManaAbilityShape` had neither a zone field nor a cost component, which is exactly what [ADR 0071](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md) Decision 1 note 3 predicted ("the field plus its accessor is the same two lines on the day one does"). `ManaAbilityShape.Zones` is `ActivatedAbilityShape.Zones`' sibling with the same nil default and the same posture — a declared zone is not an ADDITION to the battlefield, so a Spirit Guide that got cast is a 2/2 Ape with no abilities — and `game.supportedManaAbilityZones` is the HAND alone, refused at boot for the rest, the shape `supportedStaticZones` took for the graveyard. `ManaAbilityCost.ExileSelf` is #1221's clause with a SECOND OWNER rather than a second component (`exile_cost.go`'s validator and payer now take the zone and the bit), paid last through the one exit primitive with `MustSettleNow`, so a commander spent this way gets its CR 903.9 window and settles without pausing. The zone and the cost imply each other at boot in both directions: a hand zone with no exile cost is a FREE REPEATABLE mana source, and the auto-tapper's picker states the same demand a second time rather than trusting a check two packages away. **The reader with no CR 602 counterpart is the auto-tapper**, because nothing plans a cycling activation for the player and a mana source is what the planner exists to find: `gatherManaZoneSources` walks the supported piles beside the battlefield loop, `autoManaExileAbilityFor` is `autoTapAbilityFor`'s sibling (two pickers rather than a zone parameter — the battlefield one excludes payments the planner may not decide, this one demands a payment that exists at all), and `tapSource.LeavesHand` is a third tier BELOW #1215's sacrifice tier: a Treasure is a resource already on the table and a card in hand is a spell not yet played, so the order reads `Wanted → LeavesHand → Sacrifices → Frozen`. The executor re-finds each planned card in whatever zone holds it rather than carrying a zone on the plan, mints with `fromTap: false` (CR 106.12a — nothing was tapped, so Mana Reflection does not double it and no triggered mana ability fires), and the undo stack's pre-mutation clone now covers a HAND exit (`internal/ws/undo_exiled_mana_source_test.go`). On the wire, `zone_mana_abilities` is `zone_abilities`' twin on the same per-seat carrier, and **`mana_abilities` is now filtered to the battlefield** — it always meant "what does this permanent do" and only now has a card that would make the difference visible. Shipped on Simian Spirit Guide and Elvish Spirit Guide, both `full`, and they are the entire printed family: a Scryfall scan for "exile this card from your hand" finds nine other cards and all nine are CR 602 abilities that GRANT a mana ability to a land. Still open on this family: a mana ability from a graveyard, exile or the command zone (one line in `supportedManaAbilityZones`, no card asking); ~~`ManaAbilityCost.ExileCards`, "Exile A CARD from your hand" as a battlefield mana ability's cost (Cadaverous Bloom)~~ (closed by #1283, above); and REPLACEMENT effects, which still have no zone field.
