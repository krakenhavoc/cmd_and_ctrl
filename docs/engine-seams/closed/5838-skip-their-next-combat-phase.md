**That player skips their next combat phase** (#5838, CR 500.11, 614.10, 614.10a) — "Whenever this creature deals combat damage to a player, that player skips their next combat phase" skips one combat phase, the next one that player would begin, in whatever turn it falls, and is used up by it; #5075's `skipCombatPhases` takes every combat phase of one turn instead. It is one `ScopedEffect` (`ScopeGame`, `IndefiniteDuration`) with a `ModSkipNextCombatPhase` mod (`skipNextCombatPhase`) naming the player, written by `Game.SkipNextCombatPhaseForEffect` and spent at step entry (`spendNextCombatSkipLocked`): as a combat step of that player's turn is about to begin and its combat phase has not begun, the oldest record goes and the rest of that phase instance leaves the plan, so nothing in it happens. A turn with two combat phases loses only the first, two records skip two, a combat phase that has begun is never skipped (the record waits for the next), and a combat phase another effect skips does not use it up. A waiting record of a player who has left goes as the next turn begins. An older binary refuses a restore point that carries one. `Game.NextCombatPhaseSkipsForLocked(player)` counts what is owed. The card side is `effects.DamagedPlayerSkipsNextCombatPhase`. **Cards** (1, Full): Blinding Angel.
