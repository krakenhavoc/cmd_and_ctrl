**Innistrad werewolves and "can't transform"** (#4071, [ADR 0132](decisions/0132-day-and-night.md) amendment 2026-10-16) — the previous turn's spells are now recorded for every player as each turn closes (`DayNightState.PrevTurnAllSpells`, `PrevTurnMostSpells`, `PrevTurnAllKnown`, beside CR 502.2's active-player count), and `Game.NoSpellsWereCastLastTurnForEffect` / `APlayerCastTwoOrMoreSpellsLastTurnForEffect` are the werewolves' two intervening-if conditions (CR 603.4), false on the first turn. `effects.TransformAtUpkeepIfNoSpellsLastTurn` and `TransformAtUpkeepIfAPlayerCastTwoSpellsLastTurn` are the upkeep triggers; `ThisTransformedInto(face)` is "whenever this transforms into <face>" (CR 701.27e). "Can't transform" is `Characteristic.CantTransform`, set by a layer static from the restricting permanent and read in both in-place turn-over routes, an instruction to transform or convert (CR 101.2, 701.28f) and the daybound / nightbound turn-over, which also settles when a permanent leaves the battlefield. `Game.CanTransformForEffect` gates a "you may transform it" prompt. The same-controller target relation takes a player anchor as "that player". **Cards:** Afflicted Deserter, Daybreak Ranger, Duskwatch Recruiter, Geier Reach Bandit, Huntmaster of the Fells, Immerwolf, Instigator Gang, Kruin Outlaw, Mayor of Avabruck, Mondronen Shaman, Sage of Ancient Lore and Scorned Villager (Full).
