**A permanent that becomes an Equipment, with a granted equip and "equipped creature gets"** (#2562, [ADR 0093 amendment of 2026-10-10](decisions/0093-abilities-granted-to-other-permanents.md)) — Decision 10 is lifted for a granted layer-7c static: once layer 6 has run, `game.grantedStaticEffectsLocked` gathers each "+N/+N" static of every bundle a permanent was granted, with the host as its source, so "Equipped creature gets +3/+3" follows whatever the host is attached to, and a later removal on the host takes it while an earlier one does not (CR 613.6). `game.GrantedStaticProblem` is the one statement of what a layer-6 bundle may carry. Two new `ScopedEffect` mods: `setName` (layer 3, CR 612.8), read by the legend rule and every "named" check through the effective name, and `setTypes` (layer 4, CR 205.1a's set, with supertypes gained per CR 205.4b, carried in the new additive `Mod.Supertypes`). A granted equip row is the host's own activated ability (CR 702.6a), and the attach checks already read the effective Equipment subtype. `effects.AreAlsoEquipment` is the layer-4 "are Equipment in addition to their other types". **Cards:** The Irencrag (Full); Gemcutter Buccaneer and Puresteel Paladin lose their caveats (Full).
