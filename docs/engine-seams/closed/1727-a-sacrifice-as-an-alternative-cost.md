**A sacrifice as an alternative cost** (#1727, CR 118.9 / CR 601.2h / CR 701.21a / CR 702.34a, [ADR 0025 amendment 2026-10-01](decisions/0025-alternative-costs.md)) — Dread Return's "Flashback—Sacrifice three creatures" had no shape: `game.AlternativeCost` could charge mana, life, a pitched card, a bounced permanent and escape's graveyard exile, but not a sacrifice of the caster's own permanents. **`AlternativeCost.Sacrifice`** is the additional cost's clause verbatim — a `TargetSpec` over permanents with the count on `Min` == `Max`, built by `sacrificeSpec` — read through `cardComponent` like the other card components, validated by the one `validateSacrificeCostLocked` (exactly N, each once, each the caster's own, each matching, nothing moved on a refusal) and paid by the one `payCostSacrificesLocked` (an `EventSacrifice` per permanent, one simultaneous exit, with the spell already on the stack so the dies triggers resolve first and a counter gives nothing back). The picks ride `alt_cost_ids`, not `sacrifice_ids`: they pay the offer, and `PaidCost.Sacrificed`, the per-sacrifice discount and the additional-cost validator all read the other list; a permanent named to both is refused (CR 118.3). The view ships the offer's options as **`sacrifice_options`** (via `sacrificeCostOptions`, payment order, per viewer) instead of `pay_options`, so the client opens `SacrificeCostModal`; an offer the board cannot fill is not stamped, so the card is not `castable_here`. The enumerator reads `CardPaymentCount` / `AltCostCandidatesLocked` as for escape, and `CastAutoTapExclusions` now holds `alt_cost_ids` (a Thalia tax on a flashed-back Dread Return cannot be paid by cracking an Eldrazi Spawn the sacrifice names), with the enumerator checking affordability under the same exclusion. Constructors `effects.FlashbackSacrifice` and `effects.SacrificeInstead`; `Register` refuses a variable count and an offer with two card components. **4 cards, all `full`:** Dread Return (adopted; its `adoptableGaps` pin is gone), Lava Dart, Fireblast, Demon of Death's Gate. **Still open:** emerge (CR 702.119 — the sacrificed creature's mana value reduces the price), Firecat Blitz's "Sacrifice X Mountains" on a flashback, a tap price on flashback (Battle Screech, Prismatic Strands), and Cabal Therapy, whose front half needs a card-name choice at resolution.
