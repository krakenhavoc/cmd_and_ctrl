**Targets described relative to the source** (#2146, CR 115.1, CR 608.2b, CR 608.2h) — "target creature with lesser power", "with power less than this creature's power". `game.TargetSpec.SourceOK` is a predicate that sees the source as a `TargetSource` (`PowerToughness()`: layer-computed, counters included, unclamped). It runs inside the one shared walk, beside `CardOK`, so the legal set, the announce check (CR 601.2c), the resolution re-check (CR 608.2b), the client's `legal_targets` and the bot's enumerated moves are one answer. A source that has left the battlefield is read from its last-known record (`PermanentInfo.Power` / `Toughness`, CR 608.2h), and a walk with no source at all admits nothing rather than everything. A `TargetsFrom` that read `source.CurrentPower()` froze the bound when the trigger was built, so a source pumped or shrunk in response left a stale clause; that is gone from Legion Warboss and Vodalian Mindsinger. Card side: `effects.RelativeToSource(spec, preds...)` with `LesserPower`, `PowerNoGreater`, `GreaterPower`, `LesserToughness` and `GreaterToughness`, and the shared `effects.Mentor(key)` trigger (CR 702.136). No wire change. **Cards:** Rangers of Ithilien, Hammer Dropper, Blade Instructor, Barging Sergeant and Unliving Psychopath, all `Full`; Legion Warboss and Vodalian Mindsinger moved onto it.
