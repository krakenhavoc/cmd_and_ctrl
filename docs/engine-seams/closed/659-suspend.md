**Suspend** (#659, [ADR 0062](decisions/0062-abilities-and-special-actions-from-the-hand.md) Decision 4) — the special-action verb's second kind, and the one that closes the "Suspend (CR 702.62)" row outright. The special action pays the suspend cost and exiles the card FACE UP with N time counters, at the card's own casting speed and never under split second (CR 702.62c). Having time counters in exile IS being suspended — no flag, which is what makes a Path to Exile'd copy of the same card inert. The countdown is an exile-zone triggered ability (#925's `TriggeredAbility.Zones = {ZoneExile}`, the dimension that issue built for this), grown by `buildDef` from the suspend declaration so no card file writes it; it fires only on its OWNER's upkeep and only while counters remain. The last tick offers the cast through the same `PendingChoiceMayCast` prompt cascade uses, and a "yes" stamps a per-instance `CastPermission` with `Cost: "{0}"`, `TimingFlash` (the trigger resolves in an upkeep and Rift Bolt is a sorcery, CR 608.2g) and `GrantsHaste` (CR 702.62e). `{0}` rather than an empty override is also what makes Lotus Bloom and Ancestral Vision castable at all: their printed cost is CR 118.6's unpayable one, and CR 118.6a's exit is exactly an alternative price. X is locked at 0 by #873's shared rule, with nothing suspend-specific. `effects.Suspend(n, cost)` is the card-file line; "suspend" joined `canonicalKeywords`. **The declared-`ZoneExile` shape is retired**: S29 allowed a card-level `CastableZones: exile` "for the shape suspend and foretell will use", `TestExileCastAllowedByCardLevelDeclaration` pinned it, and it was the wrong shape — it opens exile for every copy of the card at any time, so a Path to Exile'd Rift Bolt would have been castable for free. `CastSpell` and `validateCastPathLocked` now take a per-instance permission as the only way in, `effects.Register` refuses the declaration at boot, and the test is replaced by one asserting the declaration buys nothing. Shipped on **Rift Bolt**, **Lotus Bloom** and **Ancestral Vision**. **#990 retired both of #659's declared simplifications.** CR 702.62b's two triggered abilities are two `TriggeredAbility` entries — `game.SuspendUpkeepTrigger()` removes a counter and stops, and `game.SuspendLastCounterTrigger()` watches `EventCounterPlaced` with a post-change count of zero and makes the offer — so a removal from Jhoira's Timebug, Clockspinning or Vampire Hexmage ends the countdown exactly as the upkeep does, each ability is separately counterable, and they cannot double-fire because the offer lives in one of them. CR 702.62e's haste is now a `ScopedStatic` with a `ForAsLongAs` duration — `game.UntilYouLoseControlOfDuration`, whose new `WhileYouControlSourceOnceItLands` condition holds while the object is still a spell on the stack and is false the moment the named player stops controlling it — so the grant outlives the turn, ends for good on a control change (CR 400.7: getting the creature back does not get the haste back), and is swept the moment a non-permanent spell leaves the stack. The layer listener bumps on `EventControlChanged` for the same fix: the pass that MOVES control writes `Card.Controller` after the layer walk has read it, so without the bump a stale "you control" resolution survived the theft. Still open on this family: Greater Gargadon (activating from exile while suspended needs the per-instance ACTIVATION grant, ADR 0062 open question 2) and Hypergenesis (#654).
