**Regeneration (CR 701.19)** (#667, [ADR 0013 §5p](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)): a regeneration shield is a count on the permanent (`Card.RegenerationShields`), created by `Game.RegenerateForEffect` / `effects.Regenerate{Target}`, cleared at the cleanup step beside the marked damage and on the way off the battlefield (CR 400.7). One engine built-in owns the rule — `regenerationShieldReplacement` in `game/builtin_replacements.go`, beside CR 903.9's — watching the DESTROY `RepEventMove`: it spends one shield, cancels the move, taps the permanent, removes all damage from it (through the shared `clearBattlefieldDamage`, which is also what stops the CR 704.5g check re-firing) and removes it from combat (#921's `removeFromCombatLocked`), then emits `EventRegenerated`. Nothing leaves the battlefield, so no dies-trigger fires and `destroyedThisWayLocked` counts no destruction. Two shields never prompt (a built-in is one registration, so two shields are one applicable effect); a shielded COMMANDER does, because CR 903.9 applies to the same event and CR 616.1 gives its controller the order — both orders leave it alive on the battlefield. **"Destruction" is declared on the event, never derived**: every exit off the battlefield shares one primitive, so `destroyRoute` sets `ReplacementEvent.Destruction` and `battlefieldExitRoute` does not, and the state-based-action sweep tags each doomed permanent with its own rule (`doomedPermanent`) so CR 704.5g/h are destructions and CR 704.5f/i/v are not while all of them still leave as one event. **"It can't be regenerated"** (CR 701.19c) is a variadic `DestroyOptions{CantBeRegenerated}` rider on the destroy verbs that gates the built-in's `AppliesTo`, so an ignored shield is not spent (CR 701.19d); it is wired onto the **11 catalog cards that print it** (Terminate, Mortify, Putrefy, Pongify, Rapid Hybridization, Snuff Out, Damn — both halves, Damnation, Wrath of God, Winds of Rath, Shatterstorm) and deliberately left off the printings that don't (Day of Judgment, Supreme Verdict, Vanquish the Horde), which is why Wrath of God and Day of Judgment no longer share one helper. The board shows a REGEN badge with the shield count. **4 cards converted**: Asceticism (full — the hexproof half has worked since #353), Wrap in Vigor, Welding Jar and Goblin Chirurgeon. Still open and NOT closed by this: Golgari Charm and Dawn Charm need the modal-clause seam (#764) before their regenerate modes can be written; totem armor (CR 702.111) and CR 701.19b's static "if this would be destroyed, regenerate it" are unmodelled, and no catalog card needs either yet. The rest of the row's cards are unblocked by this and still need their own card files and their own gap check.
