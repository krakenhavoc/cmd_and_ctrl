**Companion** (#3401, CR 702.139, 116.2g, [ADR 0172](decisions/0172-companion-a-card-outside-the-game.md)) — a deck names its companion in a `Companion` section, on Moxfield's `companions` board or in an Archidekt `Companion` category; a lone companion card in the sideboard is read as one with a `companion_from_sideboard` warning, and a pasted text with no commander marked reads a last headerless block of one or two rows as its commanders (Moxfield's and MTGO's text exports). `deck.Validate` judges the companion outside the 100: a condition per card from the registry in `deck/companion.go` (keyed by oracle ID; a companion with no entry is `unsupported_mechanic`), the commander's colour identity, Commander legality, and never the commander's own card. The card seats in `Player.Companion`, a `ZoneOutside` container built like emblems that no `ZoneRef` or card lookup reaches (CR 400.11c), and every seat's is revealed in turn order from the starting player as the game begins (`EventCompanionRevealed`, the `companion_revealed` log line). Paying {3} to put it into hand is the sixth `special_action` kind, `companion`, at sorcery speed and once per game because the card never goes back. `PlayerView.companion` shows it to everyone, with the {3} row for its owner; the heuristic prices it as a known card drawn. Snapshot: additive in v8. **Cards:** Jegantha, the Wellspring (Full). The other nine companions each need a condition function and a card file.
