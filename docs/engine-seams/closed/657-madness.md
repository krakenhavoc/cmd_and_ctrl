**Madness** (#657, CR 702.35, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md) amendment 2026-09-19) — the last keyword on the discard row, and the one that needed no new model at all. A card file declares one string, `Spec.Madness: "{R}"`, and `buildDef` grows both printed abilities from it (`game.MadnessReplacement`, `game.MadnessTrigger`) so no card writes either and none can forget half the keyword. The CR 702.35a replacement watches `RepEventDiscard` (#650) and reads NO cause — a discard as a cost, an effect's instruction and the cleanup step's hand-size trim all work, which is what the Fiery Temper ruling (2022-12-08) says — and it is found by the SELF-replacement pass in `gatherActiveReplacementsLocked`, the one that consults a card that is not on the battlefield, which is what lets a card in a hand replace its own exit. The exile is FACE UP with no marker (the #94 body said otherwise and was wrong). The "when you do" half is an exile-zone trigger (#925) watching the same `EventDiscardCard`, which fires wherever the card LANDED (CR 701.8a), so `ev.NewZone == ZoneExile` is what distinguishes a madness discard from one that reached a graveyard, a library (Library of Leng) or the command zone (CR 903.9). Its "may" is the `PendingChoiceMayCast` prompt cascade and suspend use; a "yes" stamps a per-instance `CastPermission` priced at the madness cost, keyed `"madness"` (so #653's "if its madness cost was paid" will read it off `StackItem.AltCost`) and `TimingFlash` — CR 608.2g, and the first use of the value ADR 0066 reserved for this keyword by name. A "no" puts the card into its owner's graveyard inside the trigger's resolution (CR 702.35b), through the exit primitive so a discarded commander is still offered the command zone. "madness" joined `canonicalKeywords`. Shipped on **Fiery Temper** and **Big Game Hunter**, with **Olivia's Dragoon** as the enabler that proves the COST-discard road (#660's `DiscardACard()`, which settles without pausing per CR 601.2h). Declared simplifications, both cascade's: the accepted cast is a GRANT rather than an inline cast, so there is a CR 117.3b response window paper does not have, and a card accepted but never cast goes to its owner's graveyard at the next end step rather than at the trigger's resolution. One declared looseness: if another replacement (Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void) was the one that exiled the discarded card, the trigger cannot tell and offers the cast anyway — unreachable by a player acting in their own interest, since CR 616.1 asks the discarding player and madness is strictly better for them. Still open behind it: Avacyn's Judgment (divided damage plus #653's cast provenance) and Asylum Visitor.
