**Per-player "cast as though it had flash"** (#1195, CR 307.1, CR 702.8, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-09-22](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)) — the primitive, and its inverse. ADR 0066 had put a timing rule on a PERMISSION (`CastPermission.Timing`), which is right for a grant that opens one cast of one object — madness, suspend's free cast — and wrong for the sentence Vedalken Orrery prints: "you may cast spells as though they had flash" names no card, names no zone and outlives no particular object. **`game.CastTiming`** is that sentence, and it invents no vocabulary: `GrantTiming` is ADR 0066's own enum gaining `TimingYourTurnOnly` (Dosan the Falling Leaf, which is **not** sorcery speed — it leaves every instant-speed window on your own turn open), `PermissionFilter` is the zone filter gaining `NoncreatureOnly` and `SorceryOnly`, and the window is [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md)'s `game.Duration` swept by the one `durationExpiredLocked`. **Two homes, ADR 0066's two:** DERIVED from the battlefield through `CatalogAbilityKey` (`Spec.CastTimings` — so an Orrery that has lost its abilities stops granting, two of them compose, and nothing is stored to expire), and STORED on the player (`Player.CastTimings`, `GrantCastTimingForEffect`) for a statement that outlives its source — Emergence Zone sacrifices itself to pay for its own ability, Teferi's +1 resolves and the planeswalker may die before your next turn. A catalog entry cannot name a seat, so `CastTimingAffects` (`you` / `each opponent` / `each player`) is expanded by the derivation. **ONE read** — `game.CastTimingOpenLocked` — folding the card's own timing, the permission's ADR 0066 override, the per-player grants and, LAST, the per-player restrictions, because CR 101.2 says "can't" beats "can": an opponent's Orrery does not get them past your Teferi. Its three callers are ADR 0073 §7's three, and the read sits BESIDE `CastGateLocked` rather than inside it, because "banned" and "not yet" are different answers and `cant_cast` means the first: `CastSpell`, `legal.castMovesFromZone` (which drops the `speed` parameter it threaded through two functions to keep a copy of the rule), and `protocol.castStampsFor`, where `castable_here` gains its third input — a fix in both directions, since a flashback SORCERY in a graveyard used to carry the bit in an opponent's end step where the announce path refuses the cast. A land PLAY keeps its own branch: CR 116.2a makes it a special action, so no timing statement reaches it either way. **Cards:** Vedalken Orrery and Emergence Zone (both `full`, both off this row), Leyline of Anticipation (`caveats`, the opening-hand clause every Leyline in this catalog defers), and **Teferi, Time Raveler goes from `caveats` to `full`** — its static and its +1 were the two homes, and both of its caveats were this seam. **Still open and ordinary card work now**: the other seven on this row each need only a `Spec` — Borne Upon a Wind, High Fae Trickster, Alchemist's Refuge, Liberator (colourless and artifact spells), Shimmer Myr (artifact spells), Gandalf the White (legendary and artifact spells) and Winding Canyons; the colour- and supertype-shaped filters (Yeva's "green creature spells", Gandalf's "legendary spells") are `PermissionFilter` fields nothing has needed yet. **The activated-ability twin was built by #1208** (below), and this row's prediction about it was wrong in both halves: Grand Abolisher's "or activate abilities of artifacts, creatures, or enchantments" is a BAN and landed with #1210's activation gate (the card ships `full`), and the twin wanted ONE home rather than two. What it did share is the shape — one read, folded in CR 101.2 order, with three callers.
