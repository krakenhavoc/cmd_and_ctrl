**Optional additional costs** (#664, [ADR 0073](decisions/0073-optional-additional-costs-and-the-cast-gate.md)) — closes the "Optional additional costs (kicker, buyback)" row (count 15, audit 32 / 47). `AdditionalCost` gains `Optional`, `Key`, `ManaCost` and `Repeat`, so kicker (CR 702.33), multikicker (CR 702.33d) and buyback (CR 702.27) are the components ADR 0021 already shipped with one bool set — Constant Mists' "Buyback—Sacrifice a land" IS the S21 sacrifice clause. `ManaCost` is the one new component and joins the total at CR 601.2f through `AddOptionalCostMana`, which the cast path, the auto-tapper and the bot enumerator all call, so a kicked cast is never offered at the unkicked price. The choice is announced as `CastSpellParams.OptionalCosts []int` (indices, repeated once per payment) at CR 601.2b with the modes; the card-shaped halves ride the flat `discard_ids` / `sacrifice_ids` lists against ONE ordered payment plan, so there is still one validator and one payer. It is recorded on `PaidCost.OptionalCosts`, read at resolution through `ctx.WasKicked()` / `ctx.KickedTimes()`, and carried onto an entering permanent as `Card.PaidOptionalCosts` (CR 400.7d) — the half the stack item cannot answer, because the item is out of `StackMeta` before the ETB event is emitted. Cleared on the way out with the other per-instance state; carried by clone and snapshot; a copy of a kicked spell is kicked (CR 707.10). Buyback replaces the RESOLUTION destination only (CR 702.27b) through #529's shared stack-exit route, so a bought-back commander still gets its CR 903.9 choice and one countered by game rules still goes to the graveyard. **Cards:** Constant Mists, Capsize, Burst Lightning, Rite of Replication, Gatekeeper of Malakir and Wolfbriar Elemental, all Full. Still to be written on the row's other waiters: Everflowing Chalice, Comet Storm and Marshal's Anthem each read the kick count at ENTRY rather than at resolution, which wants the counters-per-payment seam this ADR deliberately left out; Tear Asunder, Maddening Cacophony, Galadriel's Dismissal and Orim's Chant are ordinary catalog work now. Escalate and entwine are explicitly NOT covered — their cost is per extra mode, which the index-list announcement cannot express.
