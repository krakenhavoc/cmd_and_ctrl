**Boast and Birgi's second boast activation** (#2697, CR 702.142, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-10-08](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)) — boast is "[cost]: [effect]. Activate only if this creature attacked this turn and only once each turn", about twenty Commander-legal cards, and the engine already held both halves (`TurnTally.Attacks` and the `Activations.Turn` count) with nothing to join them; Birgi, God of Storytelling's "creatures you control can boast twice during each of your turns rather than once" had nothing to modify. **`ActivatedAbilityShape.Boast`**, built with `effects.Boast` / `BoastTargeting`, is the whole keyword, and **`Game.BoastBlockLocked`** (`game/boast.go`) is the one gate that answers which half of the instruction fails: `ActivateCatalogAbility` refuses with `ErrBoastNotAttacked` or `ErrBoastSpent` before anything is paid, `internal/legal` drops the move, and `ActivatedAbilityView.boast_blocked` (`not_attacked` or `used`) greys the row with a sentence the client owns. The once-each-turn count is the announcement, so a countered or fizzled boast is still spent. The limit is read through **`CardDef.BoastLimits`** (`game.BoastLimit`, built with `effects.YourCreaturesBoastTimes`): the largest applicable limit wins, a replacement of the printed one rather than an addition, so two Birgis make two and Birgi leaving mid-turn leaves a twice-boasted creature simply spent. `Event.Boast` is stamped at the announce (Frenzied Raider's "whenever you activate a boast ability"), and `AbilityCostSubject.Boast` lets a board cost modifier price boast abilities (Dragonkin Berserker, through the new `ActivationCostsLessEach`). **15 cards:** Birgi (caveat cleared, Sami Whammy #2190), Varragoth, Broadside Bombardiers, Eradicator Valkyrie (caveat: hexproof from planeswalkers), Dragonkin Berserker, Fearless Liberator, Usher of the Fallen, Fearless Pup, Duskwielder, Draugr Recruiter, Horizon Seeker, Tuskeri Firewalker, Frenzied Raider, Axgard Braggart and Battershield Warrior. **Still waiting:** Sigurd, Jarl of Ravensthorpe, Baron Helmut Zemo and Arni Brokenbrow each need something other than boast; Goldmaw Champion and Hagi Mob have no blocker and were left for a later slice.
