**The outside-the-game picks, the discard-then prompts and pay-unless are keyed** (#4256, [ADR 0198](decisions/0198-outside-the-game-is-the-card-database-without-a-sideboard.md), [ADR 0013 §5y](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md), [ADR 0018 §6](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md), amendments of 2026-10-23) — three prompt families that resumed through a closure, so a table waiting on one could be undone but not saved and restored. **Outside picks:** `game.RegisterOutsidePick(key, filter, then)` registers a pick's filter (a function of the card and one word, `OutsidePickPrompt.MatchArg`, for a text-changed subtype) and its continuation under one key; the sideboard branch is a keyed `choose_cards` pick, and the database branch carries the key (`pickThen`), the word (`pickMatchArg`, additive in v9) and the carry and finds the filter by the key when the typed names come in. Card side: `registerOutsideTake` / `registerOutsideTakeOfWord` (every wish, learn's Lesson, Karn's and Coax's outside half), `registerOutsideShuffle` (Research), `registerPlayFromOutside` (Spawnsire of Ulamog). **Discard-then:** `game.RegisterDiscardThen` and `PlayerDiscardsThenKeyedForEffect`, one seat and one prompt, so no run: a keyed `choose_cards` pick whose continuation discards and then runs the rest (learn's discard, Abandon Attachments, Charming Scoundrel). **Pay-unless:** `game.RegisterPayUnlessElse` and `QueuePayUnlessKeyedForEffect`; the decline is a key on `pickThen` with its data on the carry, and `payUnlessFrameOf` builds the frame every reader goes through. Card side `PayUnless{Else, Carry}` with `effects.PayUnlessElse` (Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Smothering Tithe, Esper Sentinel, Kazuul, Elesh Norn's chapter, Wandering Archaic, Arcum's Whistle). 24 keys appended to the ledger (`choose` and the new `payunless`), and an older binary refuses a file naming one (`checkEffectKeys`). Closure counts unchanged. **Still a closure:** Extrapolate the Impossible's pick (a set rule), Karn's and Coax's exile half, the multi-seat discard runs and every other `PlayerDiscardsThenForEffect` caller, the upkeep, guarded, any-player and non-mana pay-unless shapes, and the CR 616 optional-replacement prompt (#4253).
