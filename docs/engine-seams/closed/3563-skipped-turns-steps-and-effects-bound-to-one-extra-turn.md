**Skipped turns, a skipped step, and an effect during one extra turn** (#3559, #3560, #3563; [ADR 0188](decisions/0188-skipped-turns-and-turn-bound-effects.md) Decisions 1 to 3, tracker #753) — "skip your next turn" is a `skipTurns` ScopedEffect record the rotation seam spends as the player's next turn, extra or normal, would begin (CR 614.10, 614.10a; the Magosi ruling), and "skip every extra turn" is `effects.SkipExtraTurns()` plus `Game.TakeExtraTurnAfterReplacedMoveForEffect` for Ugin's Nexus. The last slice ties two things to ONE extra turn. `TakeExtraTurn{SkipSteps}` writes a `skipExtraTurnStep` record bound to the queued turn's `Ref` (`Game.SkipStepOfExtraTurnForEffect`), spent by the first matching step entry of that turn in `runStepEntryHooksLocked`, before the CR 614 window opens: nothing in the step happens (Savor the Moment's extra turn has no phasing, no day/night check, no untap), an untap step in a beginning phase added later in the same turn still does, and the next turn's day/night check reads Savor's turn. `TakeExtraTurn{DuringThatTurn}` queues rules effects on the `ExtraTurn` itself (`ExtraTurn.Effects`, `Game.DuringExtraTurnRuleEffectForEffect`, with `buildScopedEffectLocked` split out of `appendScopedEffectLocked` so a record is validated now and registered later), which `startTurnLocked` registers as the turn begins with an until-end-of-turn duration and its original timestamp: Alchemist's Gambit's "damage can't be prevented" covers none of the turn that cast it, ends at the extra turn's cleanup, and cleaved there is no loss. A skipped turn takes its step record and its queued effects with it (CR 614.10a). Both ride the existing `scopedEffects[]` / `extraTurns[]` snapshot keys, additive within the version, and an older binary refuses a file naming the new mod kind. **Cards:** Magosi, the Waterveil, Lethal Vapors, Ugin's Nexus, Gerrard's Hourglass Pendant, Savor the Moment and Alchemist's Gambit (Full). **Still open on ADR 0188:** the which-skip-first question when a player owes a skip and an extra-turn skip also applies (slices 3 and 4), the bot and client work (slices 8 and 9), and the extra-combat row (slice 6).
