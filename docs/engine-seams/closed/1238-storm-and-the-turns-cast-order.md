**Storm, and the turn's cast order** (#1238, CR 702.40 — storm is **702.40** in the pinned August 2026 edition; 702.39 is provoke — plus CR 113.7a and CR 608.2h, [ADR 0086](decisions/0086-storm-and-the-turns-cast-order.md)) — the seam had no row of its own, because it was filed while re-triaging roadmap batch 02 (#295) rather than by a batch PR appending a skip; **Flusterstorm** (`edhrec_rank` 307) is the card that was waiting, and every other storm card in the format was waiting on the same thing. The gap was never the copies (`CopySpell{Count, ChooseNewTargets}` has made N independent copies with N independent CR 707.10c prompts since S30) nor the trigger (`FromStack`, the shape `Cascade()` uses) — it was the NUMBER. `Game.SpellsCastThisTurn` is `{Total, Noncreature}` **per player**, and `TurnTally`'s `EventCast` arm bumped no counter at all, so nothing could answer "how many spells were cast **before this one**, by **anyone**". `TurnTally.Casts` is that: the turn's spell casts, table-wide, **in order**, appended by the existing `turnTallyListener` arm and read through `Game.SpellsCastBeforeThisTurn(spellID)`, which returns the spell's own index. Three readings of CR 702.40a drove the shape and each is a way the obvious implementation goes wrong — **"each other spell"** is every player's (a per-player count makes Brain Freeze a blank), **"was cast"** means a spell that was cast and then COUNTERED still counts (which is why the record is an ID taken at the cast rather than a log scan that looks the card up — Thousand-Year Storm's `b12InstantsAndSorceriesCastBeforeThisTurn` keeps its scan, because that card needs the type test and can afford to be weaker), and **"before it"** is an index, which no scalar can produce. The count is read as the **trigger resolves** (CR 608.2h), not captured at announce: the list is append-only and a spell's index is frozen by its own cast, so a spell cast in response provably cannot move it — a property a test pins rather than a coincidence of when a closure ran. This is why #1238's own sketch (capture the pre-increment `SpellsCastThisTurn` at `Build`) was not taken: it is per player, it is a scalar a second instance of storm would have to re-derive, and "pre-increment" is a fact about the order of two statements in `CastSpell`. **Card-side it is a declaration**: `Triggered: []game.TriggeredAbility{Storm()}`, no new `Spec` slot, two entries for CR 702.40b. **The log gained one kind**, `storm` / `EventStorm`, rendering "Grapeshot — storm count 3": a spell copy is created and not cast so it emits no event, and an ability's `resolve` entry names the ability but not the number (it named nothing at all until #1257), so without it the table watches N copies appear unexplained. **Cards:** Grapeshot, Brain Freeze, Empty the Warrens (whose token half needed nothing new — each copy is its own CR 701.7b creation instruction, #762), Flusterstorm (over the `CounterUnlessPaid` half that has existed since #951) and Tendrils of Agony, all `full`. **Closed since (#1255, [ADR 0086](decisions/0086-storm-and-the-turns-cast-order.md) and [ADR 0043](decisions/0043-copy-effects.md) 2026-09-23 amendments):** a storm spell COUNTERED in response to its own trigger keeps the trigger (CR 113.7a) **and is copied** from last-known information (CR 608.2h). `Game.lastKnownStack` records a spell's card and stack item at the one choke point every non-resolving exit from the stack passes (`routeCardToZoneLocked` with a stack source — #1318 retired the `DropStackMeta` flag), and `CopyLastKnownSpellForEffect` reads it after the stack and the #920 resolving slot. The lookup is **opt-in** (`CopySpell.FromLastKnown`): storm, Thousand-Year Storm and the Doublecast / Galvanic Iteration delayed trigger name the spell without targeting it and take it; Reverberate, Twincast, Dualcaster Mage and Lithoform Engine target the spell, are governed by CR 608.2b, and keep the strict `CopySpellForEffect`. **Closed since (#1288 and #1340, the later 2026-09-23 amendments to [ADR 0043](decisions/0043-copy-effects.md) and [ADR 0086](decisions/0086-storm-and-the-turns-cast-order.md)):** Breeches, the Blastmaker's reflexive "copy that spell" opted in (`FromLastKnown`) — the Bolt countered after the flip is won still gets its copy. And the same choke point now ends a COPY that leaves the stack without resolving (#1340, CR 707.10a / 704.5e): a countered, bounced or exiled Twincast or storm copy ceases to exist instead of landing as a card, a countered one still announcing `EventCounterSpell`, and its last-known record is taken first.
