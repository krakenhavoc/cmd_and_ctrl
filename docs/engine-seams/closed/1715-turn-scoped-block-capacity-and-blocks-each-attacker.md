**Turn-scoped block capacity, "blocks each attacking creature" and "blocks two or more"** (#1715, CR 509.1a–c, 509.3e and 802.2, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-28 (#1715)](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) Decisions 64–67). Closes the three items #1706 left open. "Can block an additional creature this turn" and "can block any number of creatures this turn" from a resolving spell or ability are two ADR 0041 layer-6 mod kinds, `addBlockCapacity` (`Amount`, at least 1) and `blockAnyNumber`, writing the same `Characteristic.AdditionalBlocks` / `BlocksAnyNumber` the statics write, so they add up and `game.BlockCapacity` stays the one reader; the record is pinned, ends at cleanup and rides undo and the snapshot (card side: `effects.BlockCapacityUntilEOT`). "It blocks each attacking creature this turn if able" is a sixth CR 509.1c kind, `blocksEach`, on the blocker and counted per attacker — Lure from the blocker's side — so the existing search makes the creature block every attacker it can within its capacity. "Target creature defending player controls" on a spell reads `Game.IsDefendingPlayerForEffect`: every opponent of the active player during the combat phase (CR 802.2). "Whenever this creature blocks two or more creatures" reads the numbered `EventBlock` (`Amount == 2`), so it fires once. **Coastline Chimera**, **Mounted Archers**, **Anurid Swarmsnapper**, **Luminous Guardian**, **Give No Ground**, **Valor Made Real**, **Act of Heroism**, **Yare**, **Blaze of Glory** and **Lairwatch Giant** are new and `full`. **Still open:** re-pointing a multi-blocker away from one of its attackers (a new action), and the bot's attack-side estimates that still assume one attacker per blocker.
