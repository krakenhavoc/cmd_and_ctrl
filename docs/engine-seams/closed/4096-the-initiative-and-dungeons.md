**The initiative and venturing into the Undercity** (#4096, CR 726, CR 309, CR 701.49, [ADR 0203](../../decisions/0203-the-initiative-and-dungeons.md)) — `Game.Initiative` was a sandbox marker nothing read. The engine now runs CR 726.2's three inherent triggered abilities (the upkeep venture, the once-per-batch combat-damage steal, and the venture on every take, CR 726.5) and CR 726.4's hand-on from a listener, each a sourceless keyed stack item (`initiative/venture`, `initiative/take`); `effects.TakeTheInitiative` is the card verb and `set_initiative` makes the same take.
- **Dungeons.** A dungeon is data (`game.DungeonDef`, registered by `effects.registerDungeon`) and its room abilities are catalog trigger rows under `dungeon:<key>` watching `EventVentureRoom`, so a targeted room uses the trigger targeting and restores. `Player.Dungeon` is the marker, `Player.CompletedDungeons` the completed keys; `VentureForEffect` enters, advances (a keyed option pick, `venture/room`, when two arrows lead on) or completes; CR 704.5t completes a dungeon once its last room's ability has left the stack (`EventDungeonCompleted`). Snapshot and wire fields are additive (`seats[].dungeon`, `PlayerView.dungeon` / `completed_dungeons`, `PickOptionView.room` / `dungeon`).
- **Cards (18, all Full):** From the Catacombs, Aarakocra Sneak, Avenging Hunter, Goliath Paladin, Underdark Explorer, Stirring Bard, Vicious Battlerager, Passageway Seer, Feywild Caretaker, Imoen, Mystic Trickster, Safana, Calimport Cutthroat, Bloodboil Sorcerer, Undermountain Adventurer, Dungeoneer's Pack, Loot Dispute, Explore the Underdark, White Plume Adventurer and Trailblazer's Torch. **Still open:** the three Adventures in the Forgotten Realms dungeons and the "venture into the dungeon" cards that choose among them (#4100).
