**Up to N target cards from a target player's graveyard** (#4862, [ADR 0065](decisions/0065-modal-and-multi-target-clauses.md)'s 2026-11-03 (b) amendment, CR 601.2c, 608.2b, 115.7d, 707.10c) — a dependent target clause may now pick several cards, each of which must be in the graveyard of the player an earlier clause picked: `game.TargetInTargetPlayersGraveyard` (`players_graveyard`), built with `effects.FromTargetPlayersGraveyard`, and the one relation `TargetRelationKind.ManyPicks` admits. `effects.Register` keeps the anchor a player-only clause and the dependent clause graveyard-only with no set rule. Each pick is judged on its own as the spell is cast and again as it resolves, so a card that has left the graveyard is dropped and the rest stay. The copy and retarget walks (`relatedTargetsCanFinishLocked`) refill a many-pick clause's open picks when the player changes, and a copy no longer offers a dependent original its new anchor refuses. `effects.TargetPlayerShufflesTheirCards` is the shared body: an illegal player does nothing, and a legal one shuffles even with no card left. The wire, the client picker and the bot needed nothing new. **Cards:** Quandrix Command (Full). Gaea's Blessing, Loaming Shaman, Stream of Consciousness, Dwell on the Past, Witness the Future and Rite of Renewal need nothing more from it; Memory's Journey and Krosan Reclamation wait on #4869, a flashback cast that can target itself.
