**Ending the combat phase, and reselecting an attack** (#1317, #1329, CR 724.2 / CR 508.7, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-23](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md), Decisions 32-33) — two effects that reach into a combat already under way, neither with a row of its own (each had one waiting card before the *Aang is so flashy* triage, #1306). **`EndCombatPhaseForEffect`** (`game/end_combat.go`) is CR 724.2 as its printed procedure: a no-op outside combat (724.2g); `PendingTriggers` emptied (724.2a); every stack object exiled, the resolving one included, spells through the shared stack-exit door `exitSpellFromStackLocked` so each `StackMeta` record leaves with its card (the #1318 wedge — this uses the unexported door because #1318's exported verb had not merged), copies ceasing to exist, ability items deleted rather than countered (724.2b); `clearCombatLocked` and a cursor walk through `advanceCursorLocked` that enters no skipped step, so the end of combat step is never announced and "at end of combat" triggers never fire (724.2d/e). 724.2c's SBA check is folded into the resolution frame's own check, which runs next with nobody having had priority. **`ReselectAttackTargetForEffect`** (`game/attack_reselect.go`) writes `Card.AttackingTarget` and nothing else — no `EventAttack`, no removal from combat, the announcement and blocked records untouched (508.7a), no restriction, tap or tax (508.7b) — validated by `canAttackTargetLocked` against the attacking creature's controller (508.7c); 508.7d does not apply in Commander (CR 903.2). Its prompt, **`QueueReselectAttackForEffect`**, is an `option_pick` with "keep" first (the always-legal branch) and an answer read off the picked option's subject through a new `optionPickFrame.thenSubject`, #994's `thenSeat` generalised to seats-and-permanents, so a seat pruned mid-question cannot shift the answer. No wire change. **4 cards:** Misleading Signpost (the deck card), Portal Mage and Windshaper Planetar `full`; Mandate of Peace `caveats` — its "your opponents can't cast spells this turn" is #1316, a separate PR. **Still open:** Capricopian, whose reselect is activated by "the player this creature is attacking" — an activator that is not the controller, which `ActivatedAbility` cannot say; and extra combat phases (the Open row "Extra combat and main phases"), without which "the next phase" is always the postcombat main phase. Trackers [#880](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/880) and [#884](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/884).
