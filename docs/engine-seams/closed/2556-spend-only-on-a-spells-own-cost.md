**"Spend only …" on a spell's own cost** (#2556, [ADR 0040](decisions/0040-mana-pipeline.md)'s 2026-10-07 amendment) — the cast half of the "Costs that only some mana can pay" row. COLOUR clause: `Spec.SpendOnly` (built with `effects.SpellSpendOnlyOnX(colors...)`, the "on X" form only), resolved against the card and stamped on the `ParsedCost` by the cast pricer (`printedCostLocked`) and FOLDED by `costAsPaidByLocked` exactly as an ability's, so the cast, its auto-tap, `internal/legal` and the auto-tap preview pay it identically and the shown price stays printed; refused at boot beside delve or convoke / waterbend, and delve granted by a Teval leaves the restricted X alone. SOURCE clause: `Spec.SpendOnlySources` (`game.ManaSourceKinds`, new `ManaSourceBasicLand`) is read into `ManaSpendContext.SourceOnly` by `ManaSpendForCast`; the pool refuses mana whose recorded source does not qualify (`allowsToken`) and `autoTapTopUpLocked` excludes every source that could not make a qualifying mana. **Cards:** Drain Life, Consume Spirit, Imperiosaur, Myr Superion (Full) and Soul Burn (Caveats: the black "spent on X" is counted as the least it could have been). **Not covered** (the row stays open as partial): Emblazoned Golem's one-mana-of-each-colour cap, and Security Rhox's Treasure-only alternative cost.
