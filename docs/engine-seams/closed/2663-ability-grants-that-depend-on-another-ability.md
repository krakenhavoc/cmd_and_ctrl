**Ability grants that depend on another ability** (#2663, [ADR 0067](decisions/0067-layer-dependency-ordering.md) amendment of 2026-10-23, CR 613.8a–c) — a layer-6 static that reads an ability, such as "Other creatures you control with flying have indestructible", declares `StaticAbility.ReadsAbilities`. Before each application in the layer-6 bucket, a reader waits for every remaining effect whose trial application would change what it applies to or what it does. That is ADR 0067's trial on a fixed base, run for the reader's rows only. The question is asked again after each application, and the effect the reader last waited on is tried first. So a creature that gains flying from a later Equipment or spell has indestructible, and one that loses flying to a later Gravity Sphere does not. A loop among readers falls back to timestamp order for the effects in the loop, and an effect that only waits on a loop keeps waiting. `game.HasKeywordItCanHave` lets a reader see a "can't have" before the strip at the end of the bucket. A bucket with no reader pays one walk to count them (`BenchmarkLayerRecomputeLayer6Readers_80perms` prices the worst case). `effects.WithKeywordHave` builds the clause. `effects.Register` refuses a reader outside layer 6, beside a removal or a borrow, or over spells, and a granted bundle can't carry one. **Cards:** Sephara, Sky's Blade (with her ADR 0135 tap cost) and Kwende, Pride of Femeref (Full).
