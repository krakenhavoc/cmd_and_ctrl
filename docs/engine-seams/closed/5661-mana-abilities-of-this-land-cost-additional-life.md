**Mana abilities of this land cost an additional 1 life** (#5661, CR 118.8 / 601.2f / 602.2b / 119.4 / 305.6) — Thran Portal is a basic land type of its controller's choice in addition to its other types, so the mana ability it taps with is the intrinsic one of that type, which no card file declares and so none could price. The surcharge is a number on the permanent's layered view, `Characteristic.ManaAbilityLifeTax`, written by the static `effects.ManaAbilitiesOfThisPermanentCostLife(n)` and summed the way a restriction is OR'd. `manaAbilityRows`, the one accessor behind every reader of a permanent's mana abilities, adds it to each row's `LifeCost`: the printed rows, the intrinsic ones and the ones another effect grants (Chromatic Lantern). From there it is an ordinary life cost, checked before anything is paid and paid with the tap, shown on the wire's `life_cost`, planned by the auto-tapper in its pain tier and offered to a bot only when it can be paid. A land that lost its abilities stops charging (CR 305.7 under Blood Moon, CR 613.1f in layer 6 whichever timestamp is later), and a row a borrow lends from the land to another permanent is not charged, because the text prices abilities of this land. No wire change; the characteristic field is additive in the snapshot shape. "In addition to its other types" for a chosen basic land type is the card's own layer-4 static, beside Multiversal Passage's "is the chosen type". **Cards:** Thran Portal (Full).
