**The confirm, the one-seat discard callers, the exile half, a set rule and the other pay-unless shapes are keyed** (#4264, follow-up to #4256, amendments of 2026-10-24 to [ADR 0018](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md), [ADR 0013 §5y](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md), [ADR 0150](decisions/0150-other-players-pay-any-player-offers-and-assist.md) and [ADR 0198](decisions/0198-outside-the-game-is-the-card-database-without-a-sideboard.md)) — prompts that still resumed through a closure, so a table waiting on one could be undone but not saved and restored. **The confirm:** `game.RegisterConfirmThen` and `QueueConfirmKeyedForEffect` (`effects.MayChoice{Then}`, `effects.MayAnswer`): Rhystic Study's, Mystic Remora's and Wandering Archaic's second question. **Discard callers:** Last Rites, Pia, Eriette's Whisper, Mishra's Command, Malcolm, Occult Epiphany, Banon, Jiang Yanggu, Archon of Cruelty's discard, Tomb of Annihilation's Veils of Fear and the "discard, then draw" family (five shared draw rules) move their state to the carried IDs; `RegisterDiscardThenRule` keeps Thrilling Discovery's "two or none". **A string carry and a set rule:** `ChooseCardsPrompt.CarryWord` rides `pickMatchArg`, `RegisterChooseCardsThenRule` and `RegisterOutsidePickRule` register a set rule with the continuation under its key; Karn's and Coax's exile half (`registerExileOrOutside`) and Extrapolate the Impossible's first pick use them. **Pay-unless shapes:** `RegisterPayUnlessBranches` (a decline and a payment under one key) and `QueuePayUnlessPromptKeyedForEffect` cover the upkeep anchor, may-pay, the guarded and counter-unless-pays prompts (the engine keys the plain "counter that spell" itself) and the non-mana payments (`PendingChoice.PayActionClause`, a new additive snapshot field); `RegisterAnyPlayerPays` and `QueueAnyPlayerPaysKeyedForEffect` cover the any-player offer, every link rebuilt from the offer on the prompt. 30 keys appended to the ledger (`choose`, `payunless`, `confirm` and the new `anyplayer`), and an older binary refuses a file naming one (`checkEffectKeys`). Closure counts unchanged. **Still a closure:** the multi-seat discard runs, the remaining `MayPay` / `PayUnless` / `AnyPlayerPays` callers whose branch reads the stack item or a Go value (and every may-pay whose branch reads the source object's epoch: Mana Vault), echo, a cumulative upkeep that flips coins, the waterbend clause, the per-object any-player offer, Extrapolate the Impossible's second and third prompts, and the CR 616 optional-replacement prompt (#4253).
