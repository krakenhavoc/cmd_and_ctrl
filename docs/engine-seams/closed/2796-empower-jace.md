**Empower Jace and the Jace planeswalker token** (#2796, CR 701.71, CR 704.5i, [ADR 0139](decisions/0139-empower-jace.md)) — the engine had loyalty abilities but no planeswalker token, and nothing could put counters on "a Jace token you control", so no Reality Fracture Empower Jace card could be added. `effects.JaceToken` is a catalog token template (ADR 0083, slug `jace`): a blue, non-legendary `Token Planeswalker — Jace` with printed loyalty 0 and its `[−1]: Surveil 1` and `[−3]: Draw a card` as ordinary `LoyaltyCost` rows, so the view (`activated_abilities` with `loyalty_cost`, `loyalty_activated`, `token_text`), the legal-move enumerator, the bots and `ActivateCatalogAbility` reach them through the token key exactly as they reach a printed planeswalker's; nothing in `internal/game` changed for the token. `effects.EmpowerJace{N | Count, Then}` is CR 701.71a as one instruction: with no Jace planeswalker token (`IsJacePlaneswalkerToken`: a token, a planeswalker and a Jace, read live) it creates one through `CreateTokensThenForEffect`, so Doubling Season makes two; one candidate then gets the counters, two or more ask the existing own-permanents pick; the counters go through `AddCounterByThenForEffect` with the controller as placer, so CR 614 replacements apply. The 0-loyalty token is never swept first, because state-based actions are held for the whole resolution, prompt included (CR 704.3, #1289). `Count` is "Empower Jace X" counted as it resolves, and `Then` is anything printed after the keyword. `JaceLoyaltyAmong` is "loyalty counters among Jaces you control". The turn-began hook now also sweeps delayed triggers whose duration has ended (`clearExpiredDelayedTriggersLocked(false)` in `rotation.go`), so an "until your next turn, whenever" trigger ends as its controller's turn begins rather than at the following cleanup. **Cards (15, all Full):** No Admittance, Countersculpt, Rewrite Regrets, Campus Crier, Solve for Disappointment, Plan for All Outcomes, Mindseeker Oculus, Jace, Reality Sculptor, Academic Ascent, Protege's Awakening, Tam's Resistance, Vraska's Final Mercy, Arcane Amphisbaena, Keeper of the Quiet Hour and Repurposed Enforcer. **Still open:** the other 20 Empower Jace cards are on the `empower-jace` row's Waiting list (#2795); fourteen of them also need #2797 (granted loyalty abilities, surviving 0 loyalty, the loyalty-counter trigger, instant-speed loyalty activation).
