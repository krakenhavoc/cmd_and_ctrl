**Chosen-color evasion** (#4126, [ADR 0045 amendment](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md)) — a turn-scoped block rule reads blockers' current colors while preserving the affected object's identity across snapshots. Skrelv's toxic, colored hexproof and evasion share a duration; Umezawa's Jitte uses the existing modal, counter-cost and Equipment support, including last-known attachments. Both cards and their reskinned printings are automated.
