**Alternative costs that tap creatures you control** (#2030, [ADR 0135](decisions/0135-alternative-costs-that-tap-discard-awaken-and-emerge.md) §1, PR 3) — "If you control a Plains, you may tap an untapped creature you control rather than pay this spell's mana cost" reuses the activated abilities' tap-others cost (owner decision 1): `AlternativeCost.TapOthers` is a `TapOthersCost` (#758), so the options walk, the validator and the payer are the ones station and Springleaf Drum use. The permanents must be untapped and yours (CR 118.3, 701.26a), are not targeted, may have arrived this turn (CR 302.6), and are tapped with the spell already on the stack, so a "becomes tapped" trigger goes above it. They ride `alt_cost_ids`, which the auto-tapper already leaves alone, and one permanent can't pay the tap and convoke, waterbend or teamwork on the same cast. `effects.TapInstead`, `TapInsteadPaying` (a mana half, as Zahid prints) and `FlashbackTap` declare it; `effects.Register` refuses "another" and an X count. The offer view carries `tap_options`, the client opens the tap picker, the enumerator orders the candidates by what tapping each costs the bot, and the heuristic charges a tapped creature as tapped, not as lost. **Cards:** Orim's Cure, Sivvi's Valor, Angelic Favor, Ramosian Rally, Lashknife, Prismatic Strands, Battle Screech, Group Project and Zahid, Djinn of the Lamp (Full). Sephara, Sky's Blade waits on layer-6 dependencies (#2663) and The Lady of Otaria on a land-to-graveyard tally (#2664).
