**Day and night** (#2561, CR 731, CR 502.2, CR 702.145, [ADR 0132](decisions/0132-day-and-night.md)) — the game can be day or night, and nothing could say so. `Game.DayNight` (`game/daynight.go`) is the designation plus the one number its turn-based check needs: the previous turn's spell count, copied out by `recordPrevTurnSpellsLocked` as the turn ends because `SpellsCastThisTurn` has already reset when CR 502.2 asks. `dayNightTurnCheckLocked` is the check, the second action of `performUntapStepLocked` after phasing: day becomes night after a turn with no spells, night becomes day after a turn with two or more, and neither stays neither. `becomeDayNightLocked` is the one write and emits `EventDayNightChanged` (`Label` the new designation, `Amount` 1 for a flip, which is all "whenever day becomes night or night becomes day" waits for; the first designation a game gains is not one). `daybound` and `nightbound` are canonical keywords stamped per face by the deck importer: `settleDayNightLocked` is CR 702.145c/d/f/g, `dayNightListener` makes a daybound permanent that enters at night enter on its back face with no transform event, and `TransformPermanentForEffect` now refuses a permanent with either keyword (CR 702.145b/e) while the day/night rules turn it over through `transformInPlaceLocked`. The snapshot gains an optional `dayNight` key, `GameView` a `day_night` string, the log a `day_night` line, and the dock a Day / Night chip. Card-side: `effects.BecomesDayAsEnters`, `WheneverDayBecomesNightOrNightBecomesDay`, `BecomeDay` / `BecomeNight` / `ToggleDayNight`, `ItsNightCost`. **Cards (13):** The Celestus, Brimstone Vandal, Firmament Sage, Sunrise Cavalier, Obsessive Astronomer, Into the Night, Moonrager's Slash, Olivia's Midnight Ambush, Fearful Villager // Fearsome Werewolf, Harvesttide Infiltrator // Harvesttide Assailant, Bird Admirer // Wing Shredder, Village Watch // Village Reavers and Infestation Expert // Infested Werewolf, all `full`. **Still open, each filed where it belongs:** the other 37 day and night cards (#2586), which are card slices rather than engine work; a keyword granted to a permanent that did not print it, or a copy of a werewolf, is only noticed at the next entry or flip rather than "any time"; the shared-team-turns variant (CR 502.2a); and "it stops being day or night", which no Commander-legal card prints.
