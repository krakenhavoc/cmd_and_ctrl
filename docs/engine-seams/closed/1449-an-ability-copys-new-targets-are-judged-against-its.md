**An ability copy's new targets are judged against its departed source as it last existed** (#1449, CR 707.10c / 115.7c / 608.2h / 400.7 / 702.16b, [ADR 0072 amendment 2026-09-24 (#1449)](decisions/0072-protection.md)) — the "still open" clause of the #1429 entry below. The CR 707.10c "you may choose new targets for the copy" prompt judged the new targets against `abilitySourceCardLocked`, the source's current-zone card. Once the source had left, that was its graveyard card, so the copy of a painted-red source's ability could be pointed past protection from red, and the copy of a bleached red source's ability could not take a pro-red target it was allowed. `copyTargetSourceLocked` (`game/spell_copy.go`) is now the `TargetSource` for the offer, the #809 refresh of the open prompt (`game/trigger_target_timing.go`) and the answer. For an ability whose source `departedAbilitySourceLocked` finds a record for, it snapshots that record, which is the read #1429 gave the original, from the `SourceObject` the copy carries (#1418). Spell copies and live sources are unchanged. Also fixed: the refresh withdrew an ability copy's emptied prompt through the spell builder, which put a spell copy of the source permanent's card on the stack. It now uses `createCopyLocked`. No caveat named the gap. The proof is #1429's Murderous Redcap turned blue by Cerulean Wisps and killed, then copied with Strionic Resonator: the copy may now take a pro-red creature and may no longer take a pro-blue one. Trackers [#881](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/881), [#883](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/883).
