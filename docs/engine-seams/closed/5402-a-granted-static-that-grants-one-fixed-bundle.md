**A granted static that grants one fixed bundle** (#5402, [ADR 0093 amendment 2026-11-10](decisions/0093-abilities-granted-to-other-permanents.md), CR 611.2a, 613.6, 613.7a, 613.8a) — Roar of the Fifth People's chapter II, 'This Saga gains "Creatures you control have '{T}: Add {R}, {G}, or {W}.'"', is a granted static that grants, which ADR 0093 Decision 10 refused. The owner approved a narrow exception: a bundle static may grant when it opts in with `StaticAbility.GrantsFixedBundle` (`effects.GrantedFixedBundle`), and `game.GrantedStaticProblem` accepts it only as a layer-6 static naming exactly one registered bundle with no static of its own, so the inner grant can grant, remove or borrow nothing and the cascade ends after two steps. Removal, borrow, two bundles, an unregistered or static-bearing inner bundle and a grant without the opt-in stay refused, at `Register` (`checkFixedBundleGrants`: the inner bundle must be the same card's), at resolution (`checkGrantMods`) and in the build-time key scans. The engine's one change is `grantedStaticEffect.Apply` writing the inner bundle in the static's own slot with the host as grantor; the existing 2026-10-15 insertion orders it (CR 613.7a, 613.8a), so the Saga losing all abilities ends the creatures' mana ability, a creature that enters later gets it, and the Saga leaving ends it. No snapshot, wire or client change. **Cards now Full:** Huatli, Poet of Unity // Roar of the Fifth People.
