**A planeswalker that becomes a creature** (#2046, [ADR 0032](decisions/0032-planeswalkers.md) amendment of 2026-10-07) — a permanent that is both a creature and a planeswalker takes both damage results (marked damage, CR 120.3e, and loyalty counters off, CR 120.3c; `applyDamageToPermanentLocked` was already additive) and now answers to both state-based actions: `stateBasedActionsLocked` judges every permanent against every rule its current types call for (CR 704.5f/g/h as a creature, 704.5i as a planeswalker, 704.5v/w as a battle) instead of skipping the planeswalker rule for any creature, and dooms it *destroyed* only if every rule that doomed it destroys, so an indestructible creature planeswalker with no loyalty counters is still put into the graveyard. A creature whose toughness the engine doesn't know skips the creature rules and keeps the loyalty one. The client shows loyalty and power/toughness together on such a card. `effects.animateGideon` is the shared body of "becomes a P/T creature that's still a planeswalker. Prevent all damage that would be dealt to him this turn" (one layered record plus the ADR 0108 §7 source shield pinned to him); the shield is a prevention effect, so damage that can't be prevented (CR 615.12) still lands, which is the case Gideon of the Trials, shipped Full on the shield alone, now holds. **Cards:** Gideon, Ally of Zendikar, Gideon, Martial Paragon, Gideon, the Oathsworn and Gideon Blackblade (Full), with the new 2/2 white Knight Ally token. **Moved to other rows:** Gideon Jura and Kytheon, Hero of Akros // Gideon, Battle-Forged (#2567, creatures that must attack one particular permanent during one particular player's next turn) and Gideon, Champion of Justice (#2569, power and toughness set to a count).
