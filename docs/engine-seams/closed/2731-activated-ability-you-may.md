**A resolution-time "you may" on an activated ability** (#2731, CR 608.2d, [ADR 0197](decisions/0197-optional-triggers-ask-as-they-resolve.md) §10) — an activated ability whose whole effect is "you may …" had no way to ask as it resolved, so Arni Brokenbrow's boast ("{1}: You may have Arni's base power become 1 plus the greatest power among other creatures you control until end of turn", a layer 7b set that can shrink it) was left out. The row now declares its question (`ActivatedAbilityShape.MayQuestion`, written `effects.YouMay(row, MayAtResolution{…})`) and the activation stamps `StackItem.Optional`, so the item takes the optional-trigger path: a `trigger_prompt` asked as it resolves, "yes" runs the effect, an ADR 0127 standing answer keyed `<catalog key>|activated|<label>` answers it, and the open question is a restore point. A row may declare `MayAtResolution.Helps` (read through `game.CatalogActivatedMayHelps`); when it answers false the prompt carries `no_gain`, the client warns and the bot declines. A copied ability (CR 707.10) now carries the original's "you may", so a copied optional trigger asks too instead of resolving as a "yes". **Card:** Arni Brokenbrow (Full). The catalog sweep found no other activated row whose whole effect is a simplified "you may".
