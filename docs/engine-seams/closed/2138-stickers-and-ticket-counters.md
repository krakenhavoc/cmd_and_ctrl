**Stickers and ticket counters** (#2138, CR 123, CR 107.17, CR 103.2d, [ADR 0208](../../decisions/0208-stickers-and-ticket-counters.md)) — nothing in the engine knew Unfinity's tickets or sticker sheets, so the two sheets that print persist and undying behind a ticket cost could not be played. Tickets are a player counter (`game.CounterTicket`), given by `effects.GetTickets` and spent only by a sticker's ticket cost (`payTicketsLocked`). A deck's sheets (every record typed `Stickers`, under a `Stickers:` heading, Moxfield's `stickers` board or none) are `deck.List.StickerSheets`, not one of the 100; fewer than ten, or one twice, is warned (`sticker_sheets_illegal`, CR 123.2a) and none is seated.
- **The sheets are data.** `Player.StickerSheetPool` (every sheet the deck revealed) and `Player.StickerSheets` (three chosen at random as the game begins, CR 103.2d), never cards in a zone. A placed sticker is `Card.Stickers`, kept on a move between public zones and dropped on a move to a hidden one (CR 123.5), never copied. Snapshot keys are additive; no closure ceiling moved.
- **The layers.** `stickerContinuousEffectsLocked` is one more layer source: a name sticker in layer 3, an ability sticker in layer 6 (the catalog's `Spec.StickerSheet`: keywords or a grant bundle) and a power and toughness sticker in layer 7b, each at its CR 613.7k timestamp.
- **Putting one on.** `Game.PutStickerForEffect` asks three keyed questions (which object, which sticker, where a name sticker goes), each a restore point, and announces `EventStickerPlaced`.
- **Wire:** `PlayerView.sticker_sheets`, `CardView.stickers`, tickets in `counters.ticket`, the `sticker_placed` log line.
- **Cards (29, all Full):** Night Brushwagg Ringmaster, Phyrexian Midway Bamboozle, Ticketomaton, Aerialephant, Minotaur de Force, Stiltstrider, Carnival Carnivore, Glitterflitter, Grabby Tabby, Scared Stiff, Sanguine Sipper, Croakid Amphibonaut, Big Winner, Ticket Turbotubes, Blorbian Buddy, Prize Wall, Bioluminary, Costume Shop, Wee Champion, Goblin Airbrusher, Tusk and Whiskers, Robo-Piñata, Command Performance, Finishing Move, Baaallerina, Sword-Swallowing Seraph, Proficient Pyrodancer, Lineprancers and Park Bleater. **Still open:** the other sheets' ability stickers, sticker kicker, "all abilities of ability stickers", the name-sticker letter counts and stickers in a graveyard.
