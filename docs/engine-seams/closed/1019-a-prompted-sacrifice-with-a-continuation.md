**A prompted sacrifice with a continuation** (#1019, [ADR 0013 §5x](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)): `PlayerSacrificesForEffect` and `EachPlayerSacrificesForEffect` do not sacrifice anything — they queue a QUESTION per seat and return how many seats were ASKED — so every clause a card wrote on the next line paid out before anybody had chosen. It is the #911 / #993 payout shape arriving through a prompt rather than through a fire-and-forget exit, and it is why ADR 0013 §5v left both entry points out of the lint’s tables: there was no continuation form to name. A **RUN** is that form (`game/sacrifice_run.go`): one run is one printed instruction, however many prompts it takes to ask it, and its continuation runs ONCE — after the last prompt has settled AND the permanents those seats named have finished moving — handed `game.PromptedSacrifices`, one entry per ASKED seat in ask order with what really left the battlefield. Four entry points over one body: `EachPlayerSacrificesThenForEffect` (the APNAP fan-out), `PlayersSacrificeThenForEffect` (a named set of seats), `PlayerSacrificesThenForEffect` (one seat asked N times) and `PlayerSacrificesNForEffect` (the same ask with nothing waiting), plus `effects.EachPlayerSacrifices.Then` card-side. `ResolveSacrificeChoice` routes the picked permanent through `SacrificeThenForEffect`, so the leg rides `sacrificeRoute` and a sacrificed commander’s CR 903.9 prompt holds the payout — and a commander that takes the command zone still counts (CR 701.17a). A WITHDRAWN prompt settles its leg with "sacrificed nothing" rather than stranding the run: `PendingChoiceSacrifice` is the second kind to declare `dropDefault` (#1016), so both `pruneSacrificeChoicesLocked` and the departure sweep reach it through the one action. Run state lives on `Game.sacrificeRuns` keyed by a plain uuid on the choice, not in a frame shared by pointer, because the prompts of one run share MUTABLE state and `cloneLocked` copies each `PendingChoice` by value — `Game.replacementsAppliedThisEvent`’s shape, for #808’s reason. **6 cards converted:** Rise of the Witch-king (the only changed outcome — "if you sacrificed a creature this way" was "was the controller handed a prompt"), Lich-Knights’ Conquest (the COUNT), and Planar Engineering, Cornered by Black Mages, Will of the Abzan and Priest of Forgotten Gods for the ORDER. Archfiend of Depravity, Phyrexian Obliterator and Lotus Field lost their hand-written "ask N times" loops to `PlayerSacrificesNForEffect`. Both of the things it left open closed with #1027 / #1026 (next bullet): Archon of Cruelty, whose printed order needed a DISCARD continuation as well, and Springbloom Druid, whose caveat is gone. Its machinery now lives in `game/prompt_run.go`, shared with the discard run — `sacrifice_run.go` keeps the four entry points and the verb.
