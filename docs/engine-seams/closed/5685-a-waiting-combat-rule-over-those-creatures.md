**A waiting combat rule over "those creatures"** (#5685, [ADR 0188](decisions/0188-skipped-turns-and-turn-bound-effects.md) Decision 14, CR 500.8, 508.1c, 508.1d, 608.2h, 611.2c) — "Each of those creatures attacks that combat if able. They can't attack you or planeswalkers you control that combat" is `Game.DuringCombatPhaseObjectsEffectForEffect` (`game/phase_effects.go`), used through `effects.AddPhases.ThoseCreaturesThatCombat`. Decision 13 let a rule wait for the one combat a card adds, but only over a live scope. This one names the creatures the spell fixed as it resolved, each pinned as the object it is under its controller of that moment, and carries only attack requirements and restrictions, which change no characteristic (CR 611.2c, second sentence). The record waits on `Game.PhaseEffects`, is registered as that combat begins and ends with it, so a combat another effect adds later, which runs first (CR 500.8), is not bound. A creature whose controller changes while the rule waits, or that has left the battlefield by the time the combat begins, is dropped (owner decision 2026-11-11). The enumerator, the bot and the attacker chips read the requirement and the restriction through the paths they already use. The snapshot needed no new field: `phaseEffects[].effect.affected[]` was already in the v9 shape. **Cards:** Illusionist's Gambit (Full).
