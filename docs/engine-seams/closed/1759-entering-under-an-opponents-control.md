**Entering under an opponent's control** (#1759, [ADR 0102](decisions/0102-entering-under-another-players-control.md)) — "this enters under the control of an opponent of your choice" is a CR 614.1d replacement effect asked inside the entry window (`entry_controller`, CR 614.12a), ordered before every other entry effect (CR 616.1b), so the permanent lands under the chosen player and its enters triggers, "enters under your control" readers and summoning sickness are theirs. Captive Audience, Pendant of Prosperity and Abby, Merciless Soldier shipped with it. Xantcha, Sleeper Agent still waits on #1793 (any player may activate) and #1794 (can't attack its owner).
