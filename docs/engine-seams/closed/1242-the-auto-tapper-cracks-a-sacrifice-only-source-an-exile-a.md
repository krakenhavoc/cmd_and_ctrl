**The auto-tapper cracks a sacrifice-ONLY source, an exile-a-card cost on a mana ability, and a preview that names every source** (#1242, #1283, #1285, [ADR 0011](decisions/0011-mana-pool-and-auto-tapper.md) amendment 2026-09-23) — closes the "Still open" item both earlier ADR 0011 amendments carried (#1215's and #1228's) and the `ManaAbilityCost.ExileCards` item #1228's entry below named. **#1242:** a Gold token, an Eldrazi Spawn and an Eldrazi Scion print "Sacrifice this: Add …" with no `{T}`, and `autoTapAbilityFor` demanded a `{T}`, so a board of them read as "missing {3}" to the cast preview, the strict gate and every bot. The picker now demands that the ability cost the source SOMETHING (a `{T}` or itself); the tapped check moved after the pick and asks only of a `{T}` (`manaSourceTappedOut`, shared by planner and executor), so a tapped Gold is still a source; and the executor pays the tap as a component — a cracked Spawn is never tapped, emits no `EventTapCard`, is not "tapped for mana" (CR 106.12a) and fires no triggered mana ability. Nothing new rides the plan: both halves read it off the picked ability. The creature question is answered as an ORDER — `tapSource.SacrificesCreature`, a sub-tier inside the sacrifice tier, `Wanted → LeavesHand → Sacrifices → SacrificesCreature → Frozen` — not as #758's exclusion, because a Spawn's own text names it a mana source. Making Spawn plannable made a CR 118.3 gap common: `game.CastAutoTapExclusions` / `AbilityAutoTapExclusions` keep the planner off anything the announcement already named (a Spawn offered to Village Rites, a Spirit Guide offered to Thrill of Possibility), read by `CastSpell`, `ActivateCatalogAbility`, the `/autotap` preview and the enumerator's per-payment re-check. **#1283:** `ManaAbilityShape.ExileCards` / `game.ExileCost` is "Exile a card from your hand" — `DiscardCost`'s shape, NOT a discard (one exit primitive, no discard event, madness does not see it) — with its own wire triple (`exile_cost_*`, answered with `exile_ids`), the discard modal with the verb changed, and the enumerator's one-payment arm; the auto-tapper never plans it. **#1285:** the preview's `plan` IDs gain `sources`, each `{card_id, name, zone, tap, sacrifice, exile}` read through the executor's own pickers, and the client names every card leaving the hand ("Taps 2 permanents and exiles Simian Spirit Guide from your hand"). Proof cards: Gold, Eldrazi Spawn and Eldrazi Scion paying a cast through auto-tap; Deadly Dispute refusing to crack the Spawn it sacrifices; Cadaverous Bloom (`full`); the Simian Spirit Guide preview. ~~Still open: `AbilityCost.ExileCards`, the CR 602 owner, mostly the GRAVEYARD form (Grim Lavamancer, Scarblade Elite, Moorland Haunt — #1297).~~ (closed by #1297, above)
