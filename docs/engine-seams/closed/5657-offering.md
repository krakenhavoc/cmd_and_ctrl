**Offering** (#5657, CR 702.48a-c, 601.2b, 601.2f, 601.3c, 118.7) — "[Quality] offering" is an optional additional cost that sacrifices one permanent, reduces the spell's total cost by that permanent's mana cost, and lets the cast be made any time its controller could cast an instant. Two of the three halves were already costs the engine pays: the sacrifice is an optional cost's `Sacrifice` clause (ADR 0073) and the timing is `AdditionalCost.AsThoughFlash` (#5207), which opens the window only for the cast that announces the cost. The new half is the price: **`AdditionalCost.ReducedBySacrificedManaCost`** makes the one pricer read the mana cost of the permanent that cost names in `sacrifice_ids`, while it is still on the battlefield (`Game.SacrificedManaCostForEffect`, `game/offering.go`), into `CostQuery.SacrificedManaCost`, and `applyCostModifiersLocked` takes it off with the other reductions through `reduceByManaCost`, power-up's CR 118.7 rule: generic off generic, a coloured or colourless symbol off mana of the same type, the excess off generic, after every increase and never below {0}. A token that isn't a copy, a face-down permanent and a transformed back face have no mana cost; an {X} in it is 0. The bot enumerator prices each sacrifice on its own, as it does a variable sacrifice, so a Hellkite that is out of reach at nine mana is offered over the artifact that makes it affordable and over no other; the view counts an offering as opening the window from a non-hand zone only while there is a permanent to sacrifice. The card side is **`effects.Offering(quality, preds...)`** (`cards/effects/offering.go`); `Register` holds the flag to an optional cost that sacrifices exactly one permanent. The flag is catalog data, so there is no snapshot change (the payment is recorded on the existing `PaidCost.OptionalCosts` and `SacrificedObjects`), no new effect key and no wire change: the auto-tap preview already prices the announced `optional_costs` and `sacrifice_ids`.

**Cards:** Blast-Furnace Hellkite (Full).
