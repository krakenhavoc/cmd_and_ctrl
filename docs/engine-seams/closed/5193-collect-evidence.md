**Collect evidence** (#5193, [ADR 0020 amendment 2026-11-06](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md), CR 701.59) — "exile any number of cards from your graveyard with total mana value N or greater" is the graveyard `game.ExileCost` in its any-number form with a new `ManaValueAtLeast` floor (`game.CollectEvidenceCost`), so the walk, the payable check (CR 701.59b), the validator, the payer and the bot's pick are the existing exile cost's, and `ExileCost.FloorMet` is the one gate. Three forms: an optional additional cost (`effects.OptionalCollectEvidence`, key `collect-evidence`, picks on `exile_ids`, recorded on `PaidCost.Exiled` and `PaidCost.OptionalCosts`; read with `ctx.EvidenceCollected()` and `ThisEvidenceCollected`, and by a spell's own cost modifier through `CostQuery.OptionalCosts`), an activated ability's cost (`effects.CollectEvidenceN`), and the effect form (`effects.RegisterCollectEvidence`, a choose_cards prompt whose set rule is `game.CollectEvidenceMet`). The view ships `collect_evidence` / `evidence_options` on the offer and `exile_cost_mana_value_min` on an ability row, and the client's exile picker shows the running total. No snapshot change. **Cards:** Analyze the Pollen, Behind the Mask, Bite Down on Crime, Crimestopper Sprite, Extract a Confession, Forensic Researcher, Hedge Whisperer, Izoni, Center of the Web, Polygraph Orb, Sample Collector, Vitu-Ghazi Inspector (all Full).
