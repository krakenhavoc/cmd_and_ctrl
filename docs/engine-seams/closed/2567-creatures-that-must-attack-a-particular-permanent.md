**Creatures that must attack a particular permanent** (#2567, CR 508.1d, [ADR 0045](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) amendment of 2026-10-22) — `game.AttackRequirement.MustAttackPermanent` names a permanent as the object it was when the effect began (CR 400.7) and is obeyed only by an attack on it; the CR 508.1d search, the verb refusals, the checkpoint, `MustAttackForEffect` and the enumerator all count it like every other requirement, so a creature that cannot attack it for free owes nothing. The refusal reads "Grizzly Bears must attack Gideon Jura if able." It is written by the mod kind `attackPermanentIfAble` (`game.AttackPermanentIfAbleMod`, layer 6), whose `AfterTurn` stamp holds it back until a turn after the one it was made in. "During target opponent's next turn, creatures that player controls …" is `ScopeYourCreatures` with that opponent as its "you", read live (CR 611.2c), until the end of their next turn, so another opponent's creatures are never asked in four seats; "during its controller's next turn" is the new duration kind `UntilEndOfItsControllersNextTurn`, pinned to the creature, which follows a new controller (the ruling). `Game.AttackersThisCombatForEffect` reads this combat phase's declared attackers. **Cards:** Gideon Jura and Kytheon, Hero of Akros // Gideon, Battle-Forged (Full).
