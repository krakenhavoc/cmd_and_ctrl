**Granted miracle at a price, over a card filter, priced by its trigger** (#4685, CR 702.94a, CR 113.7a, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-11-03](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md); follows [granted miracle](engine-seams/closed/granted-miracle.md)) — "Each instant and sorcery card in your hand has miracle {2}" (Lorehold, the Historian) is `effects.MiracleForInstantAndSorceryCardsInYourHand(cost)`, built with Molecule Man's on one declaration, `grantedMiracleInYourHand(cost, spells, text)`: a `GrantedAlternativeCost` under `granted-miracle` limited to the hand by `Zones` and to the cards it reaches by a `PermissionFilter`. That is the general shape of a keyword with a cost given to the cards in a zone that match a filter, which #4680's granted sneak reuses over the graveyard. `game.GrantedMiracleTrigger` now fires only for the cards its own permanent's static reaches (`firstGrantedMiracleSourceLocked`), once for two permanents granting the same price, and its price rides the item onto the hand permission (`CastPermission.Cost`), from which `AlternativeCostFor` synthesises the offer, so the cast survives the granting permanent leaving once the card is revealed. The view, the bot enumerator and the reveal prompt all read it through `CastOffersForLocked`. **Cards:** Lorehold, the Historian — Full. Molecule Man's revealed cast now outlives it too.
