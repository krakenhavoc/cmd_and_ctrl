**Rebound** (#1854, CR 702.88, [ADR 0107 §3](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md#3-rebound-1854), Delivery PR 3) — printed rebound. `rebound` joins `canonicalKeywords`, and the stack's graveyard route (`routeStackCardToGraveyardLocked`) gains a fourth arm after flashback: a spell that RESOLVED, has rebound (`HasKeyword`, so a deck-imported card works with no catalog entry; several instances are one, CR 702.88c), is not a copy, and was cast from its controller's hand (`StackItem.CastFromZone`) is exiled instead of going to the graveyard. A countered or fizzled rebound spell, or one cast from anywhere but a hand, goes to the graveyard as usual (CR 608.2n). The route's continuation schedules a delayed trigger for the spell's controller (CR 603.7d) at their next upkeep (`At: StepUpkeep`, `ControllerTurnOnly`), naming the exiled object in `Params.Object` so a card that left exile in the meantime is offered nothing (CR 603.7c, 400.7). Its body is the new on-disk key `rebound/cast`: as it resolves it asks suspend's `may_cast` prompt (`may_cast_keyword: "rebound"`, CR 603.5), and "yes" grants a per-object `{0}` cast with flash timing (CR 608.2g, 702.88b) whose window closes on its holder's next pass, leaving the card in exile (`CastPermission.LapseOnPass: "exile"`, a new lapse value). The bot needs nothing new: the enumerator offers both answers and then the free cast, and the heuristic takes the offer. Three v7 corpus boards hold it on disk: the trigger queued, the trigger on the stack, and the grant. **Cards:** Artful Maneuver, Blessed Reincarnation, Blossoming Calm, Center Soul, Consuming Vapors, Distortion Strike, Emerge Unscathed, Ephemerate, Faithless Salvaging, Fantastic Elasticity, Fevered Suspicion, Flame On!, Great Teacher's Decree, Into the Time Vortex, Invisible Force Field, It's Clobberin' Time!, Nomads' Assembly, Ojutai's Breath, Ojutai's Summons, Prey's Vengeance, Profound Journey, Quantum Misalignment, Recurring Insight, Sight Beyond Sight, Staggershock, Surreal Memoir, Survival Cache, Taigam's Strike, Terramorph, Transpose, Trumpeting Herd, Virulent Swipe and Void Squall, all Full. **Still open:** World at War waits on the extra-combats row (`AnchorNthMainPhase`, `DelayedTrigger.OnPhaseID`), and a spell GIVEN rebound (Taigam, Ojutai Master; Narset Transcendent; Ojer Pakpatiq; Cast Through Time) waits on the new granted-rebound row, ADR 0107 PR 4.
