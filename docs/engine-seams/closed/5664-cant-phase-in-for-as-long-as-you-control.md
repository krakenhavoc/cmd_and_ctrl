**It can't phase in for as long as you control this** (#5664, [ADR 0084](decisions/0084-phasing.md), 2026-11-12 amendment) — a phased-out permanent can carry a prohibition tied to a source and its controller: `Card.PhaseInBarredBy`, `PhaseInBarredEpoch` and `PhaseInBarredFor`, written by `Game.CantPhaseInWhileControlledForEffect` and, in a card file, `CantPhaseInWhileYouControl{Targets, Source}` after the `PhaseOut`. While that player controls the source as the same object (CR 400.7), the untap step's phase-in (CR 502.1) skips the permanent and `phaseInLocked` refuses it; its indirectly phased attachments stay out with it (CR 702.26g). When the source leaves, phases out (CR 702.26f) or changes controller, `sweepPhaseInBarsLocked` clears the record and the permanent phases in during the next untap step of the player it phased out under, not at once; a duration that ended does not start again (CR 611.2b), and one that would begin without control of the source never starts. This is not the "phases out until ~ leaves the battlefield" lock (`PhaseInLockedBy`), which phases the permanent in the moment its source leaves. The three fields are additive in the snapshot. New token row: `2/2 black Phyrexian`. New effect key: `mass/phasing-of-zhalfir-phyrexians`. **Cards:** The Phasing of Zhalfir (Full).
