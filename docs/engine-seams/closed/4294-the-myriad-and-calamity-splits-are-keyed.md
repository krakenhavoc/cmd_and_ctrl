**The myriad split and Calamity's split are keyed** (#4294, follow-up to #4305, amendments of 2026-10-26 to [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md) and [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md)) — a table waiting on any of their questions is a restore point. They were the last two number or pick prompts of the #4294 family on a Go closure. **Myriad:** the split of a token creation (CR 702.116a, 508.4) is asked after the CR 701.7b window has settled, so it is not part of the CR 614 pipeline; it is the engine's `AttackSplit` with the settled creation as its data (`AttackSplit.settled`: the groups the replacements left, the one being split, the ones placed), under its own number key `token/settled-split` and with a keyed tail, `attacking-tokens/myriad-exile-the-copies`, that carries the granting card's name in the new `KeyedTail.Word`. A creation that is split refuses a closure tail. **Calamity, Galloping Inferno:** the pick is a keyed `choose_cards` (`choose-cards/calamity-galloping-inferno-copy`) that carries the trigger's item (`ChooseCardsPrompt.Asked`) and the rounds left (`CarryNums`), keeps the battlefield re-check (`LiveZone`), and the copy's tail (`attacking-tokens/calamity-galloping-inferno-sacrifice-the-token-and-repeat`, built with `ctx.ItemTail`) schedules the sacrifice and starts the next round against that item. `CreateTokensAttackingYourChoice.Then` and its closure path are removed. No non-test caller of `QueueChooseNumberForEffect`, `QueuePayEnergyAmountForEffect` or `QueueMayPayXForEffect` passes a closure. **Snapshot:** `pendingChoices[].attackSplit.settled` and `keyedTail.word`, additive in v9 (`-update-shape`, no fixture edited). **Tests** (`effects/keyed_split_restore_test.go`, `game/settled_split_test.go`): a two-opponent myriad attack restored at each question, a doubled myriad split, a three-group settled creation restored at each question, Calamity restored at the pick, the split and the second round's pick, and an unknown number key, tail and pick key refused. **Back-outs:** see ADR 0129's amendment.
