**Shields against a chosen source that are not one-use** (#1904, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §7, owner decision 1, Delivery PR 6) — `ModPreventFromSource` is a scoped prevention kind of plain data that reads the next-damage shield's source fields (a chosen or targeted source pinned as an object, CR 400.7 / 609.7a, and the CR 615.9 recheck), protects a player, a permanent or anything, can be combat-only, carries a CR 615.5 follow-up run once per damage instance (CR 615.13), and has an `Amount`: zero is all damage this turn and the shield is never spent, N is CR 615.7's charge. A charged shield with no source stays `preventDamage`. `Mod.Half` is Dark Sphere's "prevent half that damage, rounded down" (CR 107.1a); a shield that would prevent nothing is not used up (CR 609.7b). **`divide_shield`**: when a charged shield — `preventDamage` included — meets two or more events of one damage instance for more than its charge, the protected player divides the charge before any of them is dealt (CR 615.7, the Refraction Trap and Harm's Way rulings), on resolve_choice's `distribution` payload; nothing is asked when the charge covers them, and an event whose damage can't be prevented is not offered. The instance's events are collected first: an Each walk is asked about before its first leg, a damage scope stages the events a charged shield could meet and deals them as it ends, and a combat damage step stages its damage while a charged shield is live and deals it all at once. The division is plain data, spent as the events land; the prompt blocks the table, a departed chooser's prompt settles in the engine's order, the heuristic shields the player first and then the creature closest to dying, and the client answers it in the action dock. A banner line names the live source shields. **Cards** (12): Pay No Heed, Auriok Replica, Burrenton Forge-Tender, Prahv, Spires of Order, Rith's Charm, Dark Sphere, Healing Grace, Samite Ministration, Shieldmage Advocate, Mourner's Shield and Hidden Retreat (a targeted spell as the source, with ADR 0109 PR 8's library cost) (Full), and Protective Sphere (Caveats: with strict mana off it never sees the mana spent). **Moved to other rows:** Consulate Surveillance (#1995, a new row: paying energy as a cost) and Refraction Trap (#1905: its follow-up deals damage to the spell's target). **The recipient and to-and-by families** (Delivery PR 7): "prevent all [combat] damage that would be dealt to and dealt by that creature" is one prevention effect, so it is one record — `Mod.AndDealtBy` makes the record's pinned objects both what it protects and the sources it stops, so an event between two of them meets one effect (CR 614.5) and an instance is one application (CR 615.13) — and one record can protect several permanents (`DamageShield.ProtectPermanents`: "up to two target creatures", "those creatures"). **Cards** (39): Indestructible Aura, Shielded Passage, Djeru's Resolve, Leap of Faith, Fleeting Flight, Harvestguard Alseids, Favored Hoplite (heroic, read off the spell's targets as it is cast), Brace for Impact, Redeem, Kitsune Healer, Oriss, Samite Guardian, Godtoucher, Wellgabber Apothecary, Riot Control, Morningtide's Light, Ultimate Magic: Holy, Endure, Safe Passage, Take the Bait, Trained Pronghorn, Avacyn, Guardian Angel, Oko, the Trickster, Maze of Ith, Maze of Shadows, Ebony Horse, Elvish Scout, Foxfire, Ith, High Arcanist, Cephalid Illusionist, Deftblade Elite, Moonlight Geist, Urborg Phantom, Soratami Cloud Chariot, Energy Arc, Delirium, Dovin, Hand of Control and Kiora, the Crashing Wave (Full), and Kurbis, Harvest Celebrant and Mutational Advantage (Caveats: mana spent with strict mana off; the game chooses what to proliferate). **Moved to other rows:** Gideon Jura and the other Gideons that become creatures (#2046), Selfless Squire (#2047), Oketra's Avenger (#2048), Enshrouding Mist (#2049), You Look Upon the Tarrasque (#2050), Goblin Snowman (#1863), Glyph of Destruction (#2027) and the recipient sets the record can't name (#2045). The object-source and property families are Delivery PR 7b.
