**Infect, wither and toxic** (#748, CR 702.90 / 702.80 / 702.164 / 120.3b / 120.3d / 120.3g, [ADR 0056](decisions/0056-infect-wither-toxic.md) PR 2) — the damage tail (`game/damage_tail.go`) now gives damage its CR 120.3 result. The tail snapshots `result` (infect, wither, the CR 702.164b toxic total) and `controller` beside deathtouch and lifelink, through one reader (`damageSourceTraitsOfCard`, and `damageSourceTraitsOfAbilities` over a departed object's #1396 record); a spell on the stack is read too (CR 702.80c / 702.90e — Puncture Blast), and the CR 510.1c frame caches the three as `SourceInfect` / `SourceWither` / `SourceToxic`. Damage to a creature from an infect or wither source is -1/-1 counters instead of marked damage; infect damage to a player is poison instead of life loss; toxic adds its total in poison on combat damage to a player; infect plus toxic is ONE placement. The counters are a RESULT, placed through the CR 614 counter window as a separate event (`placeDamageResultCountersLocked`) by the source's controller (`CounterPlacer`) and flagged `CounterFromCombatDamage` in combat, so Doubling Season leaves combat counters alone and a Vizier-of-Remedies-shaped replacement sees them; the damage tail does not wait on that window. `infect`, `wither` and the `toxic` family joined `canonicalKeywords`, so the ~30 keyword-only infect / wither / toxic creatures import fully automated; toxic is minted as `toxic N` from the oracle line, and the catalog's keyword grants append through `game.AppendKeywordAbility`, which keeps every toxic instance. Shipped on Plague Myr, Blighted Agent, Ichor Rats, Puncture Blast, Tainted Strike, Triumph of the Hordes, Karumonix, the Rat King and Bloated Contaminator. Still to come under #748, and not rules seams: ADR 0056 PR 3 (the "as poison" / "as -1/-1 counters" log suffixes, a poison log line, the `N/10` chip, one `TOX N` badge) and PR 4 (the bots valuing poison). Grafted Exoskeleton, once listed here, waits on a "whenever this becomes unattached" trigger, not on this seam.
