**Until a player casts a creature spell, and until the end of your next upkeep** (#6229, CR 611.2a) — two `DurationKind`s appended after `UntilEndOfCombatOnYourNextTurn` (15 and 16), so every persisted int keeps its meaning. `UntilACreatureSpellIsCast` (Soul Sculptor; `game.UntilACreatureSpellIsCastDuration()`, `effects.DurationUntilACreatureSpellIsCast()`) ends on an event, not a turn boundary: once any player's creature spell is on the stack, `CastSpell` marks every such duration `Ended` and sweeps it, before the spell's cast triggers. A copy is not cast, and putting a creature onto the battlefield casts nothing. `UntilEndOfYourNextUpkeep` (Halfdane; `Game.UntilEndOfYourNextUpkeepDuration(player)`, `effects.DurationUntilEndOfYourNextUpkeep`) carries `UntilYourNextUpkeep`'s stamps. Made during that player's upkeep, it lasts through the rest of that upkeep and the whole of the next, and ends as the cursor leaves the next one (a sweep now runs as an upkeep step ends). A skipped upkeep ends nothing, and the CR 800.4m backstop is the same. An older binary refuses a file carrying either kind. **Cards**: Soul Sculptor (OLD b726) and Halfdane (OLD b878) are built by their OLD batches. Titania's Song (OLD b888) is not covered: its "If this enchantment leaves the battlefield, this effect continues until end of turn" needs a static that outlives its source, and an owner ruling on whether the affected set is locked when it leaves.
