**Variable activation and additional costs** (#6121, CR 601.2b / 601.2f / 601.2h / 602.2b / 702.24a / 118.3 / 401.4) — four cost shapes the engine had the parts for but no card could declare.
- **Sacrifice up to N, or any number, on an activated ability.** `effects.SacrificeUpTo(n, label, preds…)` (Baba Lysaga, Night Witch's "{T}, Sacrifice up to three permanents") and `effects.SacrificeAnyNumberOf(label, preds…)` (Sword of the Ages' "{T}, Sacrifice this artifact and any number of creatures you control") are the sacrifice clause at bounds 0..N and 0..open. `game.SacrificeCostBounds` reads a printed range as a range (it read it as its ceiling before, which no card declared), and `effects.Register` allows the zero floor on an ability only beside a component that always costs something (`effects.AbilityCostPaysBesideSacrifice`: {T}, {Q}, sacrificing the source, or mana). The effect reads what was paid off `ctx.SacrificedPermanents()`, each permanent as it last existed on the battlefield (CR 608.2h), so a card-type count or a total power is the paid objects'.
- **Put N cards from your graveyard on the bottom of your library.** `effects.PutFromGraveyardOnLibraryBottom(n, label, match)` (Battlefield Scrounger's threshold ability) is the graveyard exile cost (`game.ExileCost`) with `ToLibraryBottom`: the same pick on `exile_ids`, paid to the bottom of the library in the order named (each card beneath the one before it) instead of to exile, so nothing is recorded as exiled. The view stamps `exile_cost_to_library_bottom` and the client's picker says "Put on library bottom". A CR 602 ability's printed count from the graveyard only.
- **Remove any number of counters as an additional cost, for a discount.** `game.AdditionalCost.RemoveCounters`, built with `effects.RemoveAnyNumberOfCountersAmongCost(kind, label, preds…)`, is the activation's counter component on a cast's mandatory slot (Hierophant Bio-Titan's Frenzied Metabolism), validated and paid by the alternative cost's counter path (#5390) with the spell as the source; none is a payment. The counters ride `counter_source_ids` / `counter_counts`; their total is `CostQuery.RemovingCounters`, which `effects.CostsLessPerCounterRemoved("{2}", label)` reads at CR 601.2f, and `PaidCost.CountersRemoved` records it. The view ships the counter block on `additional_cost`, and the client opens the cast counter picker before the card costs. The enumerator offers the cast with no counters removed. `Register` refuses it on an optional cost, an either/or branch, beside an alternative cost that removes counters, or in any other counter shape.
- **Cards from a single graveyard as a cumulative upkeep payment.** `game.PayActionGraveyardToLibraryBottom` with `GroupSize`, built with `effects.GraveyardToLibraryBottomPayment(n, group)` (Jötun Grunt's "Put two cards from a single graveyard on the bottom of their owner's library"): the options are every graveyard's cards, the picks from each graveyard number a multiple of the group (CR 702.24a: one graveyard per age counter), and payability counts whole groups only (CR 118.3). A cumulative upkeep multiplies the count and keeps the group. The view ships `pay_cards.group_size`, the client lists every seat's graveyard, and the enumerator offers one payment, the opponents' graveyards first.
- **Cards** (0 here): Baba Lysaga, Battlefield Scrounger and Hierophant Bio-Titan are the GAP3 row's, Sword of the Ages OLD batch 867's and Jötun Grunt OLD batch 454's, none of them pushed yet; each batch builds its cards on these seams.
