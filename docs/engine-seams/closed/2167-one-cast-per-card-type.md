**One spell of each card type** (#2167, [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md) amendment of 2026-10-09) — `game.CastPermission` gains a per-type budget: `PerType`, the card types it opens one play or cast of each, and `PerTypeUsed`, the ones spent. `CoversCard` reads both, so a permission whose every type a card could use is spent no longer opens that card, and the cast path, the bot enumerator and the view agree without a second check. The caster names the type a multi-type card spends as `cast_spell.permission_type`, judged against the card as it is played or cast (its chosen face, or its face-down 2/2); a card with one type left needs no answer, and one with two is refused without one. A land is played and can only spend "land", beside the turn's land drop. A stored permission keeps its spent types on itself; a derived one writes them to `TurnTally.PermissionTypes` per holder and granting object, so a new Muldrotha grants a fresh set and the budget renews each turn. The view stamps `permission_types` (ranked so the type the permission's other cards need least comes first), the client asks with a type picker when there are two, and the enumerator offers one move per type in that order. A permission that charges the printed cost now also lists the card's unbound alternative costs (evoke, bestow, overload) out of the zone it opens, so a bestow card can be cast from Muldrotha's graveyard as the enchantment. **Cards:** Muldrotha, the Gravetide (Full) and Aminatou's Augury (Full).
