**Rooms, and casting either half of a split card** (#1756, [ADR 0103](decisions/0103-rooms.md)): a Room's two doors are `Card.Unlocked`, the CR 709.5c designations. A Room spell enters with the door of the half that was cast unlocked (CR 709.5d) and "when you unlock this door" fires as it enters (CR 709.5h). A locked half has no name, mana cost or rules text on the battlefield, so a fully locked Room is nameless, colourless and mana value 0. The `unlock` special action (CR 709.5e, 116.2m) pays a locked door's cost at sorcery speed; its offer comes from the card, so an uncatalogued Room can be unlocked too. `UnlockDoorForEffect` and `LockDoorForEffect` are the instructions (CR 709.5f-g), and `EventRoomFullyUnlocked` is the full unlock (CR 709.5i). The designation gate reaches replacements, untap permissions and "no maximum hand size" as well, and `effects.Room` gates every ability of a door. Every split card now casts either half (CR 709.3) and is both halves combined off the stack (CR 709.4); an aftermath half casts only from a graveyard (CR 702.127a), and fuse casts both halves from hand (CR 702.102). Still open: the Room card files themselves (ADR 0103 PRs 3 and 4), manifest dread (four Rooms wait on it), and a duration copy of a Room keeping the whole card's name.
