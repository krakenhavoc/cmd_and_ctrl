**Ascend and the city's blessing: the remaining cards** (#2706, CR 702.131, [ADR 0096 amendment 2026-10-08](decisions/0096-the-monarch-from-a-card-effect.md)) — the `ascend` row closes with every Commander-legal ascend card built. Nine needed nothing new (#2728: Snubhorn Sentry, Dusk Charger, Spire Winder, Skymarcher Aspirant, Storm Fleet Swashbuckler, Resplendent Griffin, Mausoleum Harpy, Deadeye Brawler, Pride of Conquerors). Timestream Navigator waited on a put-this-on-the-bottom-of-its-owner's-library cost (#2726), and Tilonalli's Summoner on paying {X}{R} as its trigger resolves (#2727, `pay-x-on-resolution`); its end-step exile reads the blessing as the delayed trigger resolves (`effects.ExileUnlessCitysBlessing`). The engine side is unchanged since #2696.
