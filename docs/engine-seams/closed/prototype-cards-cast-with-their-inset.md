**Prototype: cast a card with its inset mana cost, colour, power and toughness** (#5656, [ADR 0034](decisions/0034-multi-face-cards.md)'s 2026-11-12 amendment, CR 702.160, 718) — a prototype card is now a card with two faces. Scryfall's `prototype` layout is one face whose inset is only in the oracle text, so `deck.printedFaces` builds the second with `game.PrototypeFaces`: the same card with the inset's mana cost, power and toughness and the colours of that cost (CR 718.3b). `game.LayoutPrototype` makes both faces castable (CR 718.3), keeps the cast face on the permanent, and keys both on the bare oracle ID (CR 718.5); the existing face rules do the rest: the choice is made before every announce gate, so the cost, a permission's mana-value filter and another alternative cost all read the prototyped spell (CR 718.3a), every zone but the stack and the battlefield turns the card back (CR 718.4), and a copy takes the faces and the one that is up (CR 718.2a, 718.3c, 718.3d). Prototype is not an `AlternativeCost` (CR 702.160a). No snapshot or wire field: the cast sends `face: 1`, and the client's face picker asks "not prototyped or prototyped" for the layout. A card file declares nothing for the keyword. **Cards:** Blitz Automaton, Goring Warplow, Rust Goliath, Boulderbranch Golem, Combat Thresher, Cradle Clearcutter, Depth Charge Colossus, Fallaji Dragon Engine, Spotter Thopter, Iron-Craw Crusher and Autonomous Assembler (all Full). The seven Brothers' War prototype cards whose other text is rules-heavy need nothing more from it.
