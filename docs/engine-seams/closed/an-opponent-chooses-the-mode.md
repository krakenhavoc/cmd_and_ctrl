**An opponent chooses the mode** (#5771, CR 700.2e, [ADR 0065](../../decisions/0065-modal-and-multi-target-clauses.md)'s 2026-11-19 amendment) — "An opponent chooses one —" is `effects.OpponentChoosesOne`, data on the spell's `ModeSpec` (`ChosenByOpponent`). CR 700.2e says the opponent chooses when the caster otherwise would, as the spell is cast (CR 601.2b, before its targets), and that the caster decides which opponent when more than one could. The cast is parked before anything is paid, on the same frame "target … of an opponent's choice" uses: the caster names an opponent (asked only when more than one could choose), that opponent answers an ordinary `mode_pick` (`mode_chosen_for` and `mode_spell_name` on the wire), and the caster then picks the chosen bullet's targets with an ordinary `pick_target`; each answer re-runs the announcement. `StackItem.ModeChooser` records the opponent, read by the card as "that player" (`ctx.ModeChooser()`) and by the CR 608.2b re-check of `effects.ThatPlayerControls`. The opponent's prompt passes to another opponent if they leave (CR 800.4g) and is withdrawn if the caster does; all three prompts are restore points. A bot choosing for somebody else's spell takes the bullet that does the caster least. **Cards:** Fatal Lore, Library of Lat-Nam and Misfortune (Full).
