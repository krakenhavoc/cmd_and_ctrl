**Transform a permanent** (#343 / #1112, CR 701.27, CR 712.18, [ADR 0079](decisions/0079-transforming-a-permanent.md)) — the verb, and it is deliberately **two** verbs. `Game.TransformPermanentForEffect` turns a battlefield permanent over IN PLACE and never goes near `MoveCard`: CR 712.18 says the permanent doesn't become a new object, so `InstanceID`, `ObjectEpoch`, counters, marked damage, tap state, attachments, the CR 613.7 timestamp, summoning sickness, the controller and every `Game`-side per-object registry all survive, and no `EventZoneMove` / `EventETB` / `EventLTB` is emitted — a back face's "when this enters" trigger does **not** fire off a transform, because nothing entered. `Game.ExileAndReturnTransformedForEffect` is the OTHER shape ("exile this Saga, then return it to the battlefield transformed", CR 712.14a): two zone changes, CR 400.7 in full, a genuinely new object. Conflating them gives Fable of the Mirror-Breaker a Saga that kept its lore counters and Storm the Vault a permanent that re-triggers its own ETBs — wrong in opposite directions, which is why they are two functions and not a flag. Card-side: `effects.Transform{Target}`, `effects.TransformThis{}`, `effects.ExileAndReturnTransformed{Target, Controller}`. **What made it small** is that ADR 0034 had already built the representation — `Card.Faces` / `ActiveFace` / `SetFace`, the `"<oracle_id>#1"` catalog key that hands the back face its whole `Spec` for free, `MoveCard`'s CR 712.8a reset, and `layout` / `faces[]` / `active_face` already on the wire, in `cardSnapshot` and in `cloneCard`. **No new `game.Card` field**: "this permanent is transformed" is `ActiveFace != 0`, which is how CR 701.27g reads and which was already on disk and already on every snapshot. **`EventTransform`** carries the permanent, its controller, the face it turned to and the name of the face it turned from; `layerVersionBump` gains an arm for it, which is not optional — a face change is a *printed-value* change and `Effective()` serves a warm cache, so without it a transformed Storm the Vault keeps reporting "Legendary Enchantment". `LogTransform` narrates it, per #984's gate. **CR 701.27c/d is `nil` and no event**, not an error and not `EventEffectError`: an instruction that legally does nothing is a resolution, not a card that threw, and the catalog soak fails a game on the latter. The layout allowlist in `CanTransform` is the load-bearing line — an `adventure` card also has two `Faces`. One rider came with it: **CR 712.8e**, a nonmodal back face's mana value is computed from its FRONT face's cost, which `ParsedManaValue` was getting wrong as 0 for every transformed permanent. **Transforming Sagas needed no change at all**: `IsSaga` reads the effective subtypes and `SagaFinalChapter` derives from the back face's (absent) chapter triggers, so CR 714.4 finds nothing to sacrifice whichever shape the card uses. **Cards:** Storm the Vault // Vault of Catlacan (`full`) and Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki (`caveats`); the four Avatar: The Last Airbender transforming Sagas (`#343` — The Legend of Kuruk // Avatar Kuruk, The Legend of Kyoshi // Avatar Kyoshi and The Legend of Roku // Avatar Roku, `caveats`, and The Legend of Yangchen // Avatar Yangchen, `caveats`), all using `ExileAndReturnTransformed` exactly as Fable does; and Aang, Swift Savior // Aang and La, Ocean's Fury (`caveats`), the first card to reach a back face through `TransformThis` on an ACTIVATED ability rather than a Saga's chapter III — its Waterbend {8} pays a flat mana cost instead of the printed discount, which is the tap-another-permanent seam below, not this one. Still open on this row, each now filed where it belongs rather than here: **Incubator and other double-faced TOKENS** (the same object model as "Triggered and static abilities on a token that is not a copy", #521 and #1248 — a token can carry a trigger since ADR 0083, so Fable's Goblin Shaman has its attack trigger; what a token still cannot have is a second FACE); **meld** (CR 701.42, CR 712.4c explicitly forbids melded permanents from transforming, so the verb is already right about them); **day and night** (CR 730 — a turn-machinery designation, which is what the werewolves actually wait on); **CR 701.27f**'s "hasn't transformed since the ability was put onto the stack" anti-loop clause; **"as this permanent transforms"** (CR 712.20); and **entering the battlefield transformed from a zone other than the stack** beyond the one exile-return use (Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might is the card waiting on that last one, and ships without its dies trigger rather than returning as a 4/4 — #1107). **Brass's Tunnel-Grinder** is off this row entirely: it also needs discover (CR 701.57) and descend, neither of which exists.
