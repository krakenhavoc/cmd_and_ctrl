**Can't be enchanted, and other players can't gain control** (#6203, CR 303.4, 303.4c, 303.4j, 701.12b) — Tetravus's Tetravites ("This token can't be enchanted"), Consecrate Land and Anti-Magic Aura ("can't be enchanted by other Auras"), and Guardian Beast ("noncreature artifacts you control can't be enchanted … other players can't gain control of them. This effect doesn't remove Auras already attached"). Four `game.ExtraRestriction` bits: `CantBeEnchanted` and `CantBecomeEnchanted` (new attachments only) on the host, `EnchantedCantBeEnchantedByOthers` carried by the Aura on itself, and `OthersCantGainControl`. `auraBarredFromHostLocked` is asked by the Aura spell's target (`canBeTargetedBy`), an Aura entering without a target (`auraHostCandidatesLocked`), `AttachForEffect` and the CR 704.5m state-based check, which skips the new-only bit. `controlChangeBarredLocked` refuses a scoped `setController` record over a barred permanent at registration, and `ExchangeControlForEffect` refuses the whole exchange when either half is barred (CR 701.12b). Card helpers: `effects.ThisCantBeEnchanted`, `EnchantedCantBeEnchantedByOtherAuras`, `CantBecomeEnchantedAndOthersCantGainControl`. **Cards:** Anti-Magic Aura (b869), Consecrate Land (b895), Guardian Beast (b892) and Tetravus (b888) are built by their OLD batches.
