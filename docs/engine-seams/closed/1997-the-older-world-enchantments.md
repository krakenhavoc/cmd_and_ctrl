**The older world enchantments** (#1997, CR 704.5k, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) amendments of 2026-10-23) — the ten world enchantments ADR 0109 PR 7 left out, each needing one shape of its own. Revelation shipped with ADR 0199. Six shipped in the first part (#4239): Tombstone Stairwell's tokens created with it (`Card.CreatedWith`), Koskun Falls' pay-unless paid by tapping (`PayActionTap`), Field of Dreams' every library top revealed, Elkin Lair's "if the player hasn't played the card", Winter's Night's untap rider on a triggered mana ability, and Hall of Gemstone's recolouring of a land's mana. The last three shipped in the second part. **Arboria:** the engine now records who PUT each nontoken permanent onto the battlefield, which is not always the player it entered under (the ruling). That is a land play's player, a permanent spell's controller, the resolving spell or ability's controller (CR 608.2c), or the player an instruction names: a search, a manifest, or a put inside `Game.PutByEnteringPlayerForEffect` (CR 110.2a). The count is `PlayerTurnTally.NontokenPermanentsPut`, folded with the turn's spells into `Player.LastTurnCastOrPut` as each turn ends, and the attack clause `AttackTargetRestriction.PlayerMustHaveActedLastTurn` reads it for players only, not their planeswalkers. **The Abyss:** a trigger's target can be chosen by the player its event names (`TargetSpec.ChosenByTriggerActor`, `effects.OfThatPlayersChoice`), here the upkeep player, with legality judged for the controller. **Null Chamber:** a second chosen name (`Card.ChosenSecondName`), a card-name prompt that refuses a basic land card's name (`CardNamePrompt.NotBasicLandName`, CR 201.4a), an opponent chosen through a keyed prompt, and a cast and land-play ban by name (`game.SpellOrLandHasName`). **Cards:** Arboria, The Abyss, Magus of the Abyss and Null Chamber (Full).
