**Adventure casting** (#719, CR 715, [ADR 0034](decisions/0034-multi-face-cards.md) step 6) — the whole lifecycle on ADR 0066's one permission model. `CastableFaces` offers both halves of an `adventure` card, because CR 715.3 lets the caster choose; an Adventure spell that RESOLVES is exiled instead of going to a graveyard (CR 715.3d, `routeResolvedAdventureToExileLocked` in `game/adventure.go`, on the resolution path and NOT in `routeStackCardToGraveyardLocked`, which also carries a fizzle); and the permission that lands on it there is a card-scoped `CastPermission{Zone: exile, WhileInZone, CastOnly, Faces: [0]}` naming its OWNER (CR 715.4). It needs no sweep: the grant names the card's CR 400.7 object epoch, so casting the creature — or anything else moving the card — ends it. Countered, fizzled, discarded or milled, an adventure card is an ordinary card in an ordinary graveyard (CR 715.3e) and there is no grant. **The permission grew one field to say this**: `Face int` became `Faces []int`, because zero had to mean both "this grant does not speak about faces" and "the creature half", and an adventure's creature half IS face 0 — the wire's `exile_play.face` moved with it to `exile_play.faces`. The restriction belongs on the grant rather than on the card, and that is the point: a Ragavan that impulse-exiles an adventure card opens BOTH halves, and CR 715.4's grant opens one. `deck/validate.go` stopped declaring the `adventure` layout a simplification. **Cards:** Foulmire Knight // Profane Insight ships Full — the cheapest printed Adventure half that needs no announce-time choice — and Bonecrusher Giant // Stomp ships the TARGETED case (#992), which was a client gap rather than an engine one: the view published `target_mode` and `legal_targets` for the face that was up, so a human client asked to cast face 1 had no picker to open (the bot enumerator was already face-correct). The whole announce surface is published per castable face now and the face picker swaps the chosen half's in, so Petty Theft and Swift End need nothing further. An uncatalogued adventure card is unaffected — it has no announce data on either face and resolves by hand. Omens (shuffle instead of exile) are the same shape and are one branch away.
