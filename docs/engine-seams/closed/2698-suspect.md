**Suspect** (#2698, CR 701.60, [ADR 0071 amendment 2026-10-08](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)) — the first designation that GIVES a permanent abilities instead of switching its own on: `Card.Suspected` (+ `SuspectedAt`, the CR 613.7 timestamp), not copiable, cleared at both CR 400.7 sites, kept through a control change, carried by clone and the snapshot (additive to the v7 shape). It is not an `ActiveWhen` gate, because a suspected creature's menace and can't-block are on no Spec: `suspectContinuousEffectsLocked` turns each suspected permanent into one source-less layer-6 effect (the keyword-counter shape) that appends the `menace` and `can't block` (`KeywordCantBlock`) abilities, ordered at the moment it became suspected, so a "loses all abilities" older than the designation leaves them and a newer one takes both away. `foldSuspectedCantBlockLocked` sets the `CantBlock` bit from the finished ability list once the pass is over (like unleash), so the block gate, the enumerator, the view and the bot read it through `Restricted` unchanged, and a suspected creature that loses all abilities may block (#2737). `Game.SuspectForEffect` (creature on the battlefield, not already suspected — CR 701.60d), `UnsuspectForEffect`, `IsSuspected`; `PermanentInfo.Suspected` keeps the designation for last-known information (Agency Coroner). Card side (`cards/effects/suspect.go`): `Suspect`, `Unsuspect`, `UnsuspectAll{Match}`, `SuspectEachLegalTarget`, `UpToOneTargetCreature`, the `Suspected()` / `NotSuspected()` predicates, `SacrificeASuspectedCreature()` and the shared "suspect enchanted creature" enters trigger. Wire: `CardView.suspected` (public, kept on a face-down permanent) and a `SUSPECTED` badge in the existing designation slot, first in its priority chain; the bot's board text says `suspected`. **19 cards:** Person of Interest, Rune-Brand Juggler, J. Jonah Jameson, Rubblebelt Braggart, Repeat Offender, Clandestine Meddler, Absolving Lammasu, Agrus Kos, Eliminate the Impossible, Caught Red-Handed, Convenient Target, Case of the Stashed Skeleton, Reasonable Doubt, Agency Coroner and Deadly Complication ship `full`; Barbed Servitor, It Doesn't Add Up, Presumed Dead and Incriminating Impetus ship with a caveat. **Still open:** Frantic Scapegoat (choose among the creatures that entered together), Nelly Borca (a batched "one or more creatures an opponent controls deal combat damage to one or more of your opponents"), Hot Pursuit (a goad bound to one chosen creature plus a take-control trigger gated on two lost players) and Airtight Alibi ("can't become suspected").
