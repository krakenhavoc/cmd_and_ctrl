**Read ahead** (#2123, CR 702.155, CR 714.3b) — a Saga always started on chapter I, so no read-ahead Saga could be added. `read ahead` joins `canonicalKeywords` (redundant, CR 702.155c). It is a keyword entry replacement in riot's shape (`game/read_ahead.go`): the CR 614.12 look-ahead reports whether the entering permanent would have read ahead and its final chapter number, and the gather adds one replacement whose question is the new mandatory prompt `entry_read_ahead`, answered with `{option_index}` (option N is chapter N+1) and asked before the Saga enters (CR 614.12a). The choice belongs to the permanent, so a cast, a reanimation and a token copy all ask; an entry that cannot pause, or whose chooser has gone, takes chapter I, and a Saga with one chapter is not asked. The answer seeds the entry's lore counters, which land through the ordinary counter window, so Doubling Season doubles them, and is logged as `choose_option` ("chose chapter III for …"). Chapter abilities now fire from `applyCounterByLocked` for any lore counters put on a Saga on the battlefield (CR 714.2b), so a proliferate or a counter added by hand fires the chapter it crosses, which it never did before. `fireSagaChaptersLocked` applies CR 702.155a: the turn a Saga with read ahead entered, a chapter fires only if the Saga has exactly that chapter's number of lore counters, so skipped chapters never trigger and a Saga doubled past its final chapter fires nothing and is sacrificed. "Entered this turn" is the turn tally, or the landing itself while the entry's counters go on. The bot starts on chapter I. No snapshot change. **Cards (3, all Full):** The Cruelty of Gix, Love Song of Night and Day and The Elder Dragon War. **Still open:** the other seven read-ahead Sagas are card slices: Braids's Frightful Return, Founding the Third Path, The Phasing of Zhalfir, The Weatherseed Treaty, The World Spell, Urza Assembles the Titans and Yotia Declares War.
