**The MayChoice callers and a ward's life, sacrifice and discard confirms are keyed** (#4291, follow-up to #4264, amendment of 2026-10-24 to [ADR 0018](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — about ninety "you may" callers still passed `OnYes` / `OnNo` closures, and a ward's three non-mana legs closures over the warded permanent's spell, so a table waiting on any of them could be undone but not saved and restored. **The confirm carries what a branch reads:** `ConfirmPrompt{Asked, Word, Objects}` ride the prompt (fields it already had, so no snapshot-shape change) and a keyed answer is handed `ConfirmAnswer{Item, Word, Objects, LifeCost}` beside its `Carry`; `effects.MayAnswer` runs the body against a copy of the asking item. **Callers keyed:** every `MayChoice` in the catalog; eight keys are shared by the cards that print the same consequence (draw N, gain N life, create tokens, copy this spell, pay life and draw, search for a named card, fabricate, the Laccolith damage), the rest are one per card, and the "ask each player in turn" callers carry their two lists in one `Carry` (`joinCarry`). **Ward:** `confirm/ward-pay-life`, `confirm/ward-discard` and, per printed label, `confirm/ward-sacrifice-<slug>` with the `choose-cards/ward-sacrifice-<slug>` pick it chains. 105 keys appended to the ledger. A keyed `choose_cards` pick may opt into the live-zone re-check (`ChooseCardsPrompt.LiveZone`, `PendingChoice.ChooseLiveZone`, an additive snapshot field), so the ward's pick is withdrawn when its candidates leave, as a closure pick is (#1225). Closure counts unchanged.
