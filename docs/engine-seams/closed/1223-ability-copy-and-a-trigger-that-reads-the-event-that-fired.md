**Ability-copy, and a trigger that reads the event that fired it** (#1223, CR 707.10 / CR 603.2, [ADR 0043 amendment 2026-09-22](decisions/0043-copy-effects.md), [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-09-22](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) — two rows, closed together because the first cannot be proved without the second: CR 707.10 copies a triggered ability together with the event that triggered it, and until now that event lived in the `Effect` closure, where a copy reached it only by accident of sharing a func pointer. **The copy is spell_copy.go's prompt with one branch at the end.** `Game.CopyAbilityForEffect(itemID, controller, mayChooseNewTargets)` reuses the CR 707.10c "you may choose new targets for the copy" offer, its frame and its submit gate wholesale — `copySpellFrame` became `copyFrame`, and `createCopyLocked` is the ONE branch, on `item.Kind`: build a card on the stack, or build an item beside the source permanent. Four things are the ability copy's own. **There is no card** — an ability is a `StackMeta` entry alone, so nothing is pushed into a zone and `ceaseToExistLocked` needs no twin (CR 608.2n already ends every ability item). **It was not activated and it did not trigger** (CR 707.10a): no `EventActivateAbility`, no `EventTrigger`, said by what `createAbilityCopyLocked` does not emit rather than by a suppression flag — which is what keeps Rings of Brighthearth from copying its own copy forever and what keeps an "opponent activates an ability" watch from double-counting. **A mana ability cannot be copied** (CR 605.3b) and that falls out of the lookup finding nothing, not out of a check. **The triggering event travels**, which is the second row. What is copied is CR 707.10's "characteristics plus the choices made": targets, modes, X, the division, the reflexive payload, `Effect` and the two specs the CR 608.2b re-check reads — plus the announcement's `PaidCost` record MINUS its mana half, because "for each counter removed this way" is a fact about the announcement while nothing was spent to make a copy (the Dawnglow Infusion ruling). **Targeting an ability on the stack** is the clause ADR 0065 listed as open — generalised to the whole catalog by #1211, whose entry above is now the canonical statement: `TargetSpec.Abilities` + `AbilityOK` enumerate `StackMeta` by `Seq`, and a chosen ability rides an ordinary `TargetRef{Kind: TargetCard}` carrying the STACK ITEM's id rather than a fifth `TargetRefKind` — the wire, the picker, the bot enumerator and the re-check all key on "a uuid that has to still be there", which is as true of an item as of a card. The CR 702 keyword gate is deliberately not applied: an ability is neither a permanent nor a player, so nothing about it can have hexproof. **5 cards:** Strionic Resonator, Lithoform Engine, Rings of Brighthearth, Scrap Trawler `full`, Cloudstone Curio `caveats` (the Whitemane Lion posture — the permanent to return is picked as the trigger goes on the stack rather than at resolution), and Weaver of Harmony's copy activation came off its caveat with it. **Still open, and each a different shape**: Peter Parker's Camera and Gogo, Master of Mimicry (#395, #396), which copy on a different axis again; a multi-slot "change a target" (no printed card); and **power and toughness on the event's object snapshot** — "a creature with power equal to the power of the creature that died" is not answerable yet, because `lastKnownBattlefield` holds a `Characteristic` and the engine applies +1/+1 counters outside it (`Card.PowerForComparison`) while `MoveCard` clears them on the way out, so a `Power` field would silently read 2 for the 3/3 a counter made.
