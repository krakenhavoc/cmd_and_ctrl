**Preparation cards (CR 722): the prepared designation and casting the prepare spell as a copy** (#1328, CR 722.2-722.3c / 707.12 / 704.5e, [ADR 0090](decisions/0090-preparation-cards.md)) — the seam had no row; ADR 0034 recorded the mechanic as out of scope ("needs spell-copying, which does not exist") and `deck/validate.go` warned "preparing isn't implemented yet". **The designation** is `Card.Prepared`, beside ADR 0071's level and solved marker and under their rules (not copiable, cleared by CR 400.7, kept through phasing, carried by the snapshot), with one writer that refuses what CR 722.3a refuses — no prepare spell, or already prepared — as a no-op. It is not a `DesignationKind`: it switches a cast on, not a printed ability. **The copy** is a real object created in place in exile from the permanent's copiable values (so a Clone of a preparation card has its prepare spell, CR 722.2b), face 1 active so it resolves through the `"#1"` catalog entry, with the front face's card-level keywords dropped (a flash creature must not hand its sorcery flash). `Card.PrepareCopy` marks it not a card and keeps `MoveCard`'s CR 712.8a front-face reset off it; `Card.PreparedBy` names the permanent OBJECT {instance, epoch} and is cleared on every move. **The permission is derived** (`prepareCopyPermissionLocked`), consulted by `CastPermissionForLocked`, `CastPermissionOnCardForEffect` and `AnyCastPermissionsForEffect`, so the cast path, the bot enumerator and the wire's `exile_play` agree and CR 722.3c's "for as long as" holds by construction; a prepare copy answers ONLY through it. **The cast** is the ordinary exile cast plus two lines: `StackItem.IsCopy` (CR 707.10's exits end it) and the unprepare at CR 601.2i just before `EventCast`. **One sweep**, `prepareCopySweepSBALocked` beside the CR 704.5d token sweep, is CR 704.5e with CR 722.3c's exception; `phaseInLocked` makes a fresh copy for a permanent that "phases in prepared". "Enters prepared" is `effects.SelfEntersPrepared()` (CR 614.1d, `ReplacementEvent.EntersPrepared`, applied by all three battlefield landings before `EventETB`); "becomes prepared" is `effects.BecomePrepared`. Wire: `CardView.prepared` and a "PREPARED" designation badge; the copy rides the existing `exile_play`. **4 cards**, all `full`: Skycoach Conductor // All Aboard (#1306's card), Landscape Painter // Vibrant Idea, Encouraging Aviator // Jump, Skycoach Waypoint. **Still open:** a Clone entering as a copy of a preparation card does not pick up the copied card's "enters prepared" (the entry's replacements are gathered before the copy applies), CR 722.3d (a copy of a prepare SPELL is a prepare spell) and CR 722.5 (naming a prepare spell's name) have no reader, and #1340 — the spell copies CR 704.5e also covers, which `PrepareCopy` was deliberately not widened to. Trackers [#889](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/889) and [#885](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/885).
