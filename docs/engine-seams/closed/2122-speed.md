**Speed: start your engines! and max speed** (#2122, CR 702.178, CR 702.179, CR 704.5aa, [ADR 0138](decisions/0138-speed.md)) — players had no speed, so no Aetherdrift speed card could be added. `Player.Speed` (`game/speed.go`) is 0 for none, then 1 to `game.MaxSpeed` (4), and `setSpeedLocked` is its one write: it emits `EventSpeedChanged`, which bumps the layer version and is the narrated `speed` log line. `start your engines!` is a canonical keyword, stamped by the deck importer, and `startYourEnginesSBALocked` is the state-based action that gives a player with no speed who controls one speed 1. `speedTriggers` is the inherent, sourceless trigger (CR 702.179d), a listener like the monarch's: an opponent of the active player losing life (a negative life change, or damage that cost life) queues the keyed `speed/increase` item for the active player once each turn (`PlayerTurnTally.SpeedTriggered`), checked below 4 as it triggers and as it resolves (CR 603.4); it uses the stack. A player keeps their speed when the permanent that started it leaves. "Max speed — [ability]" (CR 702.178a) is not an ADR 0071 designation, because that gate reads the object and speed is the player's: `effects.MaxSpeedStatic`, `MaxSpeedSelfPump`, `MaxSpeedSelfKeywords`, `MaxSpeedTrigger`, `MaxSpeedReplacement` and `MaxSpeedCostModifier` gate `AppliesTo`, and `MaxSpeedActivated` and `MaxSpeedMana` add a `Condition`, so a max-speed activation is greyed until max speed, refused by the engine, skipped by the auto-tapper and not offered to a bot. `effects.YourSpeed` is "where X is your speed". The snapshot gains `seats[].speed` and `turnTally.players{}.speedTriggered` (additive), `PlayerView` a `speed`, every seat a speed chip, and the bot board text "speed N". **Cards (12, all Full):** Gastal Raider, Perilous Snare, Muraganda Raceway, Amonkhet Raceway, Vnwxt, Verbose Host, Avishkar Raceway, The Speed Demon, Racers' Scoreboard, Starting Column, Aether Syphon, Burnout Bashtronaut and Goblin Surveyor. **Still open:** the other 28 Commander-legal speed cards are card slices on the `speed` row's Waiting list (#2711); Lightwheel Enhancements also needs a cast-from-graveyard permission that exists only at max speed.
