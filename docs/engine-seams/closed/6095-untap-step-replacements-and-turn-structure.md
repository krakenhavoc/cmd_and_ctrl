**Untap-step replacements, a phase-in hold while the source stays tapped, and an upkeep after this step** (#6095, CR 500.9, 500.10a, 502.1, 502.3, 502.4, 603.7, 611.2b, 613.6, 614.1a, 614.5, 702.26a, 702.26d, 702.26g) — **"If this creature would untap during your untap step, remove a +1/+1 counter from it instead. If you do, untap it."** is `game.UntapCounterRemoval`, plain data on a definition (`CardDef.UntapCounterRemovals`, `game/untap_counter_removal.go`) read off the permanent's CatalogAbilityKey by the one untap primitive, `untapPermanentLocked`, after a stun counter's replacement and only during the untap step of the permanent's controller. A grant bundle carries it (`effects.AbilityGrant.UntapCounterRemovals`, merged by `copy_grants.go`), so the recipient has it while it has the grant and a later ability loss takes it (CR 613.6); two instances each replace the untap the other leaves (CR 614.5), so the creature needs a counter for each. **"It can't phase in for as long as ~ remains tapped"** is the #5664 bar with `Card.PhaseInBarredWhileTapped` set (`Game.CantPhaseInWhileTappedForEffect`): the source must be on the battlefield as the same object and tapped, whoever controls it; it never starts for an untapped source (CR 611.2b) and is cleared for good once it stops holding. `Game.PhaseInForEffect` phases named permanents in, with what phased out indirectly with them (CR 702.26g), after clearing any bar that has ended. The Pandorica's "When ~ becomes untapped or leaves the battlefield, that permanent phases in" is an event-keyed delayed trigger on `EventUntapCard` and `EventLTB` with an indefinite duration. **"Each land with an activated ability that isn't a mana ability doesn't untap"** needed no engine change: `game.ActivatedAbilitiesForCard` already lists the non-mana activated abilities through the layered view. **"Whenever ~ becomes untapped during your untap step, you get an additional upkeep step after this step"** needed none either: the trigger watches `EventUntapCard` in its controller's untap step, goes on the stack in the upkeep (CR 502.4), and `AddStepAfterCurrentForEffect` adds the upkeep directly after that one (CR 500.9), on its controller's turn only (CR 500.10a). Snapshot: `phaseInBarredWhileTapped` on a card, additive under v9. **Cards:** Bewitching Leechcraft, The Pandorica, Tsabo's Web, The Ninth Doctor (Full).
