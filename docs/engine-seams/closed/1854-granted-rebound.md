**Giving a spell rebound** (#1854, CR 613.1f / 702.88 / 616.1 / 400.7a, [ADR 0107 §3](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md#3-rebound-1854) decision 5, owner decision 3, Delivery PR 4) — a spell can gain an ability on the stack. The stack step of the layer pass is now layers 2 and 6, keywords only: after `stackControlPassLocked` settles each spell's controller, `stackKeywordPassLocked` (`game/spell_keywords.go`) applies, in timestamp order, every ScopedEffect pinned to the spell with an `addKeywords` mod and every battlefield static declared `StaticAbility.AffectsSpells`, and stamps the result on `Card.stackGranted`. `HasKeyword` and `Effective()` read it beside the printed keywords, so PR 3's `spellRebounds` picks a granted rebound up with no change, and the stack view shows it. `stackPinProblem` admits `addKeywords` beside `setController`, and now also runs at restore, so a newer binary's stack-pin mod is refused (`ErrUnknownEffectKey`) rather than restored as a record that does nothing; the PR 3 binary does not run that check, so a rollback to it restores a spell given rebound without it (weaker, never stronger). A static over spells is never gathered by the battlefield pass, a cast bumps the layer version while one is out, `resolveTopOfStackLocked` recomputes before it copies the resolving card, and `effects.Register` refuses one outside layer 6, on an emblem or from a declared zone. A keyword given to a permanent spell follows it onto the permanent (CR 400.7a: `inheritSpellControlLocked` re-pins the record even when nothing stole the spell). **CR 616.1:** rebound, buyback and an Adventure's exile all replace "put it into its owner's graveyard as it resolves", so when two apply the spell's controller chooses with an `option_pick` while the card waits on the stack (`game/resolution_exits.go`); the buyback-and-Adventure pair, settled until now by a stated judgement call for buyback, is the controller's choice too. Flashback still wins outright (CR 702.34a), and a flashed-back spell was never cast from a hand. Card helpers: `effects.SpellsYouControlHave`, `effects.ThatSpellGains`, `effects.YouCastFromYourHand`, and `game.ObjectAttackedThisTurn` for an intervening "if ~ attacked this turn" rechecked from last-known information. **Cards:** Cast Through Time and Taigam, Ojutai Master, both Full. **Moved off the row:** Narset Transcendent waits on its emblem's "can't cast" (#1899, `emblem-cast-restrictions`), and Ojer Pakpatiq, Deepest Epoch // Temple of Cyclical Time on returning a card to the battlefield transformed from a graveyard (#1900, `return-transformed`).
