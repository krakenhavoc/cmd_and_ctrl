**Granted blitz, priced at the spell's mana cost, and a reduction of blitz costs alone** (#5667) — a permanent can give blitz to the spells its controller casts. The key `granted-blitz` (`game.GrantedAltCostBlitz`) joins granted warp and granted evoke as a granted offer that carries a clause for the permanent the spell becomes: `queueAltCostEntryTriggerLocked` reads blitz's haste, "When this creature dies, draw a card." and end-step sacrifice (`ApplyBlitzRidersForEffect`) off the claim's key, because CR 702.152a hangs them on "this spell's blitz cost was paid" and the card prints no row for the offer. So the creature is blitzed even if the grantor left while the spell was on the stack, and a copy of the spell becomes a token that is blitzed too (`altCostHasEntryRider`, CR 707.10). `GrantedAlternativeCost.MinManaValue` is the floor twin of `MaxManaValue`, judged on the card's mana value as the offer is derived, before a value is announced for X (CR 601.2b), so an {X} counts as 0. `effects.BlitzForCreatureSpellsYouCast(n)` builds the offer with `PricedAtManaCost`, and `effects.Register` allows `AlternativeCost.Blitz` on a granted offer under that key only. `CostQuery.AltBlitz` tells a cost modifier that the alternative cost the cast claims is a blitz cost, printed or granted; the one pricer fills it from the claim (`altBlitzLocked`) and the legal-move enumerator from the offer it is announcing, so the two agree. `effects.ForItsBlitzCost()` is the predicate and `effects.BlitzCostsYouPayCostLessForEachCommanderCast()` the reduction, which takes generic mana only (CR 118.7a), after a commander's tax (CR 601.2f, 118.9d). The bot prices a `granted-blitz` cast as a blitz. Nothing new is stored: the claim rides on `StackItem.AltCost` as every claim does. Extends ADR 0118 §3. **Cards:** Henzie "Toolbox" Torre (Full).
