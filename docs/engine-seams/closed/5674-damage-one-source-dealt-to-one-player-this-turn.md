**Damage one source dealt to one player this turn** (#5674, CR 120.3, 400.7, 603.4, 608.2h) — "Whenever this creature deals damage to a player, if it has dealt 10 or more damage to that player this turn, they lose the game" asks how much one object has dealt to one player, combat and noncombat together. Nothing answered that: `TurnTally.DamageDealers` names which creatures hit which player but is deduplicated and carries no amount, `NoncombatDamage` has amounts for noncombat damage only, `PlayerTurnTally.CombatDamageToPlayers` is a per-controller total, and `DamagedObjects` (#1946) records damage to permanents. The record is `TurnTally.DamagedPlayers` (`game/damage_to_player_by.go`): one `PlayerDamageRecord{Source, Victim, Amount}` per damage event dealt to a player, written by the tally listener from `EventDealDamage`, so the amount is what was dealt after replacement and prevention and damage prevented to 0 is not there. It is the damage, not the life it cost: infect damage and damage to a locked life total count. The source is an `ObjectRef` (instance ID and `ObjectEpoch`), so a source that left and came back is a new object that has dealt nothing, and the record outlives a source that has died. It resets with the turn, and clone, undo and the snapshot carry it (additive, `damagedPlayers`, omitted when empty). The reader is `Game.DamageDealtToPlayerThisTurnByObjectForEffect(source, victim)`; the tally listener runs ahead of the trigger harvester, so a trigger on the damage that asks already counts that damage. **Card** (Full): Hidetsugu Consumes All // Vessel of the All-Consuming. The Vessel's intervening "if" reads the record as the damage is dealt and again as the trigger resolves, against the object that dealt it (`ctx.SourceRef()`), and its "whenever this creature deals damage" counter is one trigger per damage instance (`ThatMuchDamageDealtAtLeast(1)`), so trample damage split between a blocker and a player is one counter.
