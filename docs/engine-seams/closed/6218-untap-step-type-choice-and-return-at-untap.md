**Untap only a chosen card type, and "during your next untap step, as you untap"** (#6218, CR 502.3) — Storage Matrix's "each player chooses artifact, creature, or land during their untap step. That player can untap only permanents of the chosen type this step" is a `game.UntapTypeChoice` on `CardDef.UntapTypeChoices` (plain data: the lowercase types offered and `WhileUntapped`), read by `performUntapStepLocked`: once per live source the active player answers a keyed option pick (`option/untap-type-choice`) that carries the rest of the untap set as data, the set is narrowed to the chosen type, and it then goes on to ADR 0070's cap and opt-out determination (`determineUntapLocked`). Nothing untaps until the last answer, and the paused step is a restore point. Undiscovered Paradise's "During your next untap step, as you untap your permanents, return this land to its owner's hand" is `Game.ReturnAtNextUntapForEffect(card, player, label)`: one `ScopedEffect` (`ScopeGame`, `IndefiniteDuration`) with a `ModReturnAtNextUntap` mod (`returnAtNextUntap`) naming the player and the permanent as an `ObjectRef`, spent by that player's next untap step that happens, before the untap set is read; a permanent that has left and returned is a new object and is not returned. An older binary refuses a restore point that carries one. **Cards**: Storage Matrix (OLD b706) and Undiscovered Paradise (OLD b792) are built by their OLD batches.
