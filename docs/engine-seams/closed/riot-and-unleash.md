**Riot and unleash, and the entry look-ahead** (#1556, [ADR 0109](decisions/0109-rule-gates-land-types-mana-and-cost-components.md) §10, PR 9) — riot (CR 702.136a) and unleash (CR 702.98a) join `canonicalKeywords`, both cumulative (CR 702.136b; CR 113.2c for unleash). Both are entry replacements, and which ones apply is judged by the permanent "as it would exist on the battlefield" (CR 614.12): `entryLookAheadLocked` (`game/entry_lookahead.go`) runs a dry layer pass over a copy of the battlefield plus the entering card, under its would-be controller, as the copy it was told to enter as, and with the records pinned to it as a spell (CR 400.7a); nothing is written. The gather adds one replacement per instance it reports, so a printed riot under Rhythm of the Wild asks twice and a creature entering under an ability-removing effect is not asked. Riot is the new mandatory prompt `entry_riot` (`{apply}`: true the counter, false haste); haste becomes an indefinite `addKeywords` record pinned to the permanent (`RiotHasteLabel`), shown as the haste chip labelled "Riot" (`CardView.riot_haste`), and an entry that cannot pause takes the counter. Unleash is the existing optional entry counter (with `entry_keyword: "unleash"` on the prompt), and its "can't block as long as it has a +1/+1 counter on it" is folded into the restrictions after the layer pass (`foldUnleashLocked`). The bot takes riot's haste when the creature can still attack this turn and the counter otherwise, and always takes the unleash counter unless the creature would be its only untapped blocker on an opponent's turn. No snapshot change. **Cards:** Rhythm of the Wild, Gruul Beastmaster, Skarrgan Hellkite, Ravager Wurm, Clamor Shaman, Frenzied Arynx, Burning-Tree Vandal, Tesak, Judith's Hellhound, Grim Roustabout, Hellhole Flailer, Exava, Rakdos Blood Witch and Chaos Imps, all Full; Uncivil Unrest moves onto the keyword grant and drops its caveat. Rampaging Rendhorn, Zhur-Taa Goblin, Ghor-Clan Wrecker, Wrecking Beast and nine unleash creatures need no card file. **Still open:** Arcbound Slasher waits on modular (#2012), Gruul Spellbreaker on "you have hexproof" during your turn (#2013), Spider-Punk on abilities that can't be countered (#2014), and Domri, Chaos Bringer on a spend rider that grants a keyword to the spell (ADR 0109 PR 10).
