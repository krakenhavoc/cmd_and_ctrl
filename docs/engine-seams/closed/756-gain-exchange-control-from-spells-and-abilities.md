**Gain / exchange control from spells and abilities** (#756, [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md)) — `Game.GainControlForEffect(source, target, controller, duration, label)` registers a layer-2 `ScopedStatic` in the bucket Mind Control already used, so `Card.BaseController` is the reversion, CR 613.7 timestamp ordering against another control effect is free, and `materialiseControlLocked` carries the consequences: summoning sickness under the new controller (CR 302.6) and removal from combat (CR 506.4). The latter now clears the attack and block ANNOUNCEMENTS as well as the declarations, through one `removeFromCombatLocked` helper — without it a creature stolen mid-combat stayed marked as already-declared and could never fire a "whenever ~ attacks" trigger again that combat (#871). `ExchangeControlForEffect` is CR 701.12: both objects checked before either effect is registered (701.12b, all or nothing), two entries sharing one timestamp so they are one effect for CR 613.7, both `Indefinite` and each pinned to its own object. Card-side `GainControl{Target, Controller, Duration}` and `ExchangeControl{A, B}` in `cards/effects/control.go`. Shipped on Act of Treason (until end of turn, plus the untap and the haste grant the control change makes necessary), Agent of Treachery, Sower of Temptation and Switcheroo. **A layer-2 delta is now an event** (#930): `materialiseControlLocked` emits one `EventControlChanged{CardID, Actor = gained, Target = lost, Source}` per delta — after the pass, collected by value, so a harvested trigger cannot re-enter the recompute — which covers a theft, an exchange, an Aura destroyed and a duration expiring with one emission point. Card side: `WhenYouLoseControlOfThis` (the item is built for the player who LOST control), `WhenYouGainControlOfThis`, `WheneverAnOpponentGainsControlOfAPermanentYouOwn`. "An opponent of your choice gains control" is unblocked on both halves since #929 — the primitive already took an arbitrary controller, and the choose-a-player prompt has shipped — but no catalog card prints the clause yet, so nothing has been converted for it. Khârn the Betrayer is still uncatalogued for the OTHER half of his third ability: a damage-prevention replacement handing the permanent to "an opponent of your choice". Still open: Zidane, Tantalus Thief wants the sibling "a permanent **from you**" predicate; control of a SPELL (Commandeer) and of a player's turn (Mindslaver) are not layer 2 at all. Unblocked and still to be written: Zealous Conscripts (#384), Captivating Crew (#396), Mob Rule (#395), Coercive Recruiter (#397), Reins of Power (#400), Homeward Path, Humble Defector (#309), Gilded Drake, Perplexing Chimera.
