**Miracle: reveal the first card drawn each turn and cast it for its miracle cost** (#1665, CR 702.94, [ADR 0066 amendment 2026-09-28, #1665](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)). A card declares `AlternativeCosts: []game.AlternativeCost{Miracle("{W}")}`. That is a printed offer gated by `AlternativeCost.RequiresGrant`, and `buildDef` grows the keyword's trigger from it. The trigger watches `EventDrawCard` from the hand (`TriggeredAbility.Zones` now accepts `ZoneHand`) and applies only to the first card its owner drew this turn (`Game.DrawnThisTurn`), on any player's turn. Its `OptionalPrompt` asks "Reveal X for its miracle cost?". A "yes" reveals the card and stacks a keyed `miracle/offer` item. When that item resolves, it grants a per-object `CastPermission{Zone: hand, AltCostKey: miracle, Timing: flash}`, provided the revealed object is still in the hand (CR 702.94b). `CastPermission.ForClaim` scopes a hand permission to its own claim, so the printed cost keeps sorcery timing. The holder passing priority closes the window. **Cards:** Terminus, Thunderous Wrath, Devastation Tide, Reforge the Soul, Entreat the Angels, Banishing Stroke; Blessings of Nature's caveat cleared. **Still open:** Temporal Mastery (extra turns), Revenge of the Hunted (the "all creatures able to block it do so" requirement), Bonfire of the Damned ("target player or planeswalker" with a sweep that follows it), and the CR 117.3b response window before the cast on another player's turn (madness has the same gap).
