**Monstrosity** (#1700, CR 701.37, [ADR 0071 amendment 2026-09-28](decisions/0071-designations-that-switch-abilities-on.md)) — a sixth designation with Harnessed's lifecycle: `Card.Monstrous`, not copiable, cleared at both CR 400.7 sites, carried by clone and the snapshot (additive to the v7 shape). `DesignationMonstrous` / `game.Monstrous()` gates "as long as this creature is monstrous" lines through the four ability accessors with no other change. `Game.MonstrosityForEffect(cardID, n)` is the keyword action: it does nothing for a permanent that is already monstrous, checked on resolution so a second activation in response resolves to nothing; otherwise it puts N +1/+1 counters on through the CR 614 counter pipeline (Doubling Season and Hardened Scales apply, and a CR 616 ordering prompt pauses it), and as the placement's continuation sets the flag and emits `EventBecameMonstrous` with **Amount = the announced N**, not the counters that landed, so a doubled placement leaves a trigger's X alone. The event bumps the layer version (load-bearing at X = 0, where no counter event does) and is silent on the log; `CardView.monstrous` carries the badge. Card side (`cards/effects/monstrosity.go`): `Monstrosity(cost, n)`, `MonstrosityX(cost)`, `WhenBecomesMonstrous(label, effect)`, `MonstrosityXOf(item)`, `MonstrousKeywords(kws...)`. Polukranos's "X damage divided" needs no new divide shape: its `TargetsFrom` reads X off the trigger context and declares a fixed `Divide(X)` with at most X targets. **8 cards:** Polukranos, World Eater, Stormbreath Dragon, Hydra Broodmaster, Ember Swallower, Arbor Colossus, Fleecemane Lion and Domesticated Hydra ship `full`; Hundred-Handed One ships with a caveat (it blocks one creature, not a hundred — Brave the Sands' gap). **Still open:** Alpha Deathclaw and Protector of the Wastes ("enters or becomes monstrous", expressible, not attempted), cost-reduced monstrosity (Grim Giganotosaurus, Nemesis of Mortals), and an N that is not announced (Clay Golem's die roll, Maester Seymour).
