**Effects that last this combat, or while a Saga remains** (#2027, CR 611.2b, 511.3, 724.2d) — an effect could last this turn, until a player's next turn, or for as long as a permanent stays on the battlefield, but not only through the combat phase it was made in, and a scoped prevention shield could only be "this turn" or "until your next turn". `game.UntilEndOfCombat` is the seventh `DurationKind`, stamped with the combat phase instance (`Turn.Seq` and `Turn.PhaseID`, in `Duration.CombatTurn` and `CombatPhase`; `Game.UntilEndOfCombatDuration` returns false outside a combat phase, so an effect with no combat to last through never begins). `durationExpiredLocked` reads it from the cursor alone and `advanceCursorLocked` sweeps after every move out of a combat step, so it ends as the end of combat step ends (CR 511.3), or when an effect ends the phase (CR 724.2d), and an additional combat phase is a different combat. `game.DamageShield.Duration` (`effects.PreventDamageFromSource.Lasts`: `ShieldThisCombat`, `ShieldWhileSourceRemains`) lets a preventFromSource shield take either duration; `effects.DurationUntilEndOfCombat` builds one for `ScopedEffectFor` and `effects.BlockingCreature` is the "target blocking creature" predicate. A restore point carries the two new `Duration` fields (additive under v7); a binary from before refuses the new kind. **Cards:** Glyph of Destruction, Old Fat Spider Can't See Me, Sewers of Estark and Suppressor Skyguard (all `full`). **Still open:** Winter's Chill moved to `x-capped-by-a-count` (#2581), because "X can't be greater than the number of snow lands you control" has no shape.
