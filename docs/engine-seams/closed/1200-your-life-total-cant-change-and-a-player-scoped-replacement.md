**"Your life total can't change", and a player-scoped replacement with a CR 611.2 duration** (#1200, CR 119.7 / CR 119.8 / CR 120.3a, [ADR 0085](decisions/0085-life-total-cant-change.md)) — the seam had no row of its own either, only a mention on the two rows below, and it is Teferi's Protection's LAST caveat. #1200 filed it as a REPLACEMENT-registry problem: put a `Duration` on `ReplacementEffect`, sweep `Game.TurnScopedReplacements` through `durationExpiredLocked` instead of clearing wholesale, and register the clause there. **The ADR declines that and puts the clause on the player instead**, and the reason is the census: `TurnScopedReplacements` holds two CLOSURES per entry, is counted in `ContinuationCensus.TurnScopedReplacements` and is classified `dropped` by the snapshot drift test — so the one card famous for buying you a whole turn cycle would also have been the one card that takes undo and persistence away from the table for that turn cycle. [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md) Decision 8's deferral therefore still stands: the registry has no `Duration` field, because the card said to need one does not. **`PlayerStatic.LifeTotalLocked` is the home** — a third payload beside `Keyword` (#1197) and `Timing` (#1195) on one slice with one sweep, one `durationExpiredLocked` read, one clone and one snapshot field, told apart by its payload rather than a discriminator, and `carried` rather than censused because it is plain data. **Two homes, ADR 0072's two:** DERIVED from the battlefield (`effects.Spec.PlayerLifeTotalLocked` → `game.CatalogPlayerLifeTotalLocked`, read through `CatalogAbilityKey` on every query so two Platinum Emperions compose and a layer-6 ability strip unlocks) and GRANTED with a CR 611.2 duration (`GrantLifeTotalLockForEffect`, because Teferi's Protection is in exile a moment after it resolves). **FOUR consumers and TWO of them were already written and unreachable:** the built-in `lifeTotalCantChangeReplacement` on the CR 614 life window (`game/life_lock.go`, `Preemptive` for the reasons ADR 0072 §4 gives one event kind over — an amount replacement's rewrite is cancelled either way, a charged shield must not spend itself, and a life PAYMENT cannot pause to be asked); `changeLifeThroughReplacementsLocked`'s CR 614.10 cancel branch, whose comment had named *"your life total can't change"* since #482; `payLifeAsCostLocked`'s CR 119.8 refusal of a cancelled payment, whose comment ended *"a card that adds one should add that check to the cost validators it reaches"*; and the DAMAGE half, four lines in `applyResolvedDamageToPlayerLocked`, because damage does not fire the life window at all (CR 120.3) — the damage is still **dealt**, `EventDealDamage` still fires, the CR 903.10a commander tally still accrues and lifelink still credits, and only the life loss does not happen. **`CanPayLifeLocked` is that check**, one predicate replacing the open-coded `p.Life < n` at eight validators (`activated.go`, `mutations.go`, `alternative_cost.go` ×2, `phyrexian_mana.go`, `entry_choice.go` ×2, `legal/abilities.go` ×2), so the bot never proposes a cost the engine refuses and the client never shows a button that can only fail — #695's argument for CR 119.4, extended to CR 119.8 and CR 614.17b. `AlternativeCost.LifePayableBy` is deleted rather than kept beside it: two spellings of one affordability rule is the drift the predicate exists to stop. Poison and every other counter on a player are untouched, because a counter is its own CR 614 window (ADR 0056). **The wire** gets `PlayerView.life_total_locked`, a bool beside `keywords` rather than a token inside it — that field is bare ability TOKENS and a lock is not an ability the player has — and the client renders a `LIFE` badge in #979's language beside the hexproof and protection ones. **3 cards `full`:** Platinum Emperion (the derived half, off batch #453), Teferi's Reproach (the granted half aimed at an OPPONENT, with the duration counted on the target's seat-turn counter), and **Teferi's Protection, which goes from one caveat to none** — three printed clauses, three sprints, three ADRs (#1197 / [ADR 0072 amendment](decisions/0072-protection.md), #1199 / [ADR 0084](decisions/0084-phasing.md), #1200 / [ADR 0085](decisions/0085-life-total-cant-change.md)). **Still open and a seam of its own:** "you can't lose the game" (CR 104.3, Platinum Angel and Phyrexian Unlife) — a locked total cannot reach 0, but CR 104.3's other three routes are untouched and nothing here gates the SBA loop; see the "Win the game by effect" row above.
