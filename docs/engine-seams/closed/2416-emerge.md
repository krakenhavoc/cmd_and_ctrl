**Emerge** (#2416, [ADR 0135](decisions/0135-alternative-costs-that-tap-discard-awaken-and-emerge.md) §4, PR 5) — "Emerge [cost]" is an alternative cost (CR 118.9) that sacrifices one creature, or with "Emerge from [quality]" one permanent of that quality (CR 702.119b), and is reduced by its mana value (CR 702.119a). `effects.Emerge` and `effects.EmergeFrom` declare the offer, a one-permanent sacrifice flagged `ReducedBySacrificedManaValue`. The reduction is a cost reduction read off the named permanent while it is still on the battlefield (CR 702.119c, 601.2f; a token that isn't a copy is 0), taken off the generic part only (CR 118.7a) after any increase and never below {0}, so the payment, the auto-tap preview, the offer's per-candidate `sacrifice_prices` and the bot's enumerator read one price. A permanent named to any sacrifice cost on a cast may now tap for mana before it is sacrificed (CR 601.2g, owner decision 3), and the auto-tapper still never cracks it. The enumerator prices each emerge payment on its own and offers the best three the seat can afford, ranked by the creature's value less the mana it saves (owner decision 5). `PaidCost.AltCostObjects` records what an alternative cost paid with, and reaches the permanent and its enters trigger, so Adipose Offspring counts the sacrificed creature's toughness as it last existed. **Cards:** Abundant Maw, Adipose Offspring, Crabomination, Cresting Mosasaurus, Decimator of the Provinces, Drownyard Behemoth, Elder Deep-Fiend, It of the Horrid Swarm, Lashweed Lurker, Mockery of Nature, Twisted Riddlekeeper, Vexing Scuttler and Wretched Gryff (Full). Herigast, Erupting Nullkite (a granted emerge, ADR 0135 PR 6) and Distended Mindbender (a two-filter pick, #2115) wait on the `emerge` row.
