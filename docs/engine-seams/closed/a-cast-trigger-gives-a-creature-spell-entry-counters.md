**A cast trigger gives the creature spell it watched entry counters** (#4686, CR 614.1c, CR 400.7, CR 608.3f) — "whenever you cast a creature spell, that creature enters with X additional +1/+1 counters on it". The read side was #1852's `StackItem.PromisedCounters`, seeded onto the spell's entry by `applyCastEntryCountersLocked` so Hardened Scales and Doubling Season see the counters; only a spent next-spell promise wrote it. `game.MarkSpellEntersWithCountersForEffect(ref, PromisedCounter)` is the second writer (`game/spell_entry_counters_mark.go`): it marks a spell still on the stack as the same object the trigger saw (its epoch stamped at trigger time) and refuses an ability, a copy (which becomes a token without the mark) and a spell that has left the stack, so a countered trigger or a countered creature gives nothing to anything. The promise spend and the effect API share one append (`addPromisedCounterLocked`), which #4679's cast-permission rider can call too. The catalog side is `effects.WheneverYouCastThatEntersWithCounters`, X read off the spell's payment as it is cast and carried on the trigger, and `effects.CountersPerColorSpent`, converge on a permanent. Plain data already in the snapshot and the clone: no new field, no func route from `Game`. **Cards:** Wildgrowth Archaic — Full.
