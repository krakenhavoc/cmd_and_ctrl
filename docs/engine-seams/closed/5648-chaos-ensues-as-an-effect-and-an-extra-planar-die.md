**Chaos ensues as an effect, Will of the Planeswalkers, and a static extra planar die** (#5648, CR 311.7 / 701.31a / 701.38 / 614.1a / 706.6; follows [ADR 0216](decisions/0216-planechase-as-a-table-option.md) and [ADR 0146](decisions/0146-voting.md)) — "chaos ensues" existed only as the planar die's chaos face, so a resolving spell or ability that says it (CR 311.7's second case) triggered nothing. `game.ChaosEnsuesForEffect(player, source)` (`game/chaos_ensues.go`) emits the new event `chaos_ensues` (`EventChaosEnsues`), and `effects.WheneverChaosEnsues` now watches it beside the die's `roll_die` with the chaos face: one ability with two trigger events, controlled by the planar controller whoever controls the effect. No die is rolled, so nothing that watches or replaces a roll sees one.
- **Will of the Planeswalkers.** `effects.WillOfThePlaneswalkers{Card}` is the ability word's sentence as one step of a spell: ADR 0146's vote for planeswalk or chaos, then `PlaneswalkForEffect` for the caster when planeswalk gets more votes, or chaos ensues when chaos does or the vote is tied. Only the planar controller may planeswalk (CR 701.31a), so a Path whose caster is not the planar controller planeswalks nobody. The continuation is the keyed `vote/will-of-the-planeswalkers`, so an open ballot is a restore point.
- **No planar deck.** At a table that is not playing Planechase the vote still happens (extra votes and "whenever players finish voting" work), `ChaosEnsuesForEffect` emits nothing, nobody planeswalks, and Ichor Elixir replaces nothing: there is no planar die to roll.
- **A static replacement on planar dice.** A dice roll's window now says whether the dice are planar (`ReplacementEvent.RollPlanar`), and `ReplacementEvent.RollOneMoreAndIgnoreOne(chooser, source, label)` is "instead roll that many plus one and ignore one" for a permanent's replacement: one more die and a chooser, through the keyed question Squid Fire Knight's one-shot shield already asks, with nothing spent. It applies to the planar die special action (CR 901.9) and to a roll an effect asks for, once per roll for each Elixir. The ignore question names planar dice as such.
- **The wire.** One additive log kind, `chaos_ensues` (`label` is the card whose effect it was): "Chaos ensues (Path of the Animist)". The die's chaos face stays a `roll` entry with `planar_face`.
- **Snapshot.** No shape change. New effect keys: `vote/will-of-the-planeswalkers` and `choose-cards/path-of-the-schemer-reanimate`. No closure field changes.

**Cards:** Path of the Animist, Path of the Enigma, Path of the Ghosthunter, Path of the Pyromancer, Path of the Schemer and Ichor Elixir — Full. Missy's "chaos ensues" now makes chaos ensue at a Planechase table.
