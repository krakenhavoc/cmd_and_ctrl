---
title: "\"Cast as though it had flash if\" is a timing permission"
date: 2026-11-09
issues: [5207]
---
**"Cast as though it had flash if" is a timing permission** (#5207, [ADR 0025](decisions/0025-alternative-costs.md)'s 2026-11-09 amendment; CR 601.3, 601.3b, 601.3c, 118.9a) — the catalog built "You may cast this spell as though it had flash if …" as an alternative-cost offer at the printed cost (`AlternativeCost.AsThoughFlash`), so a flash cast could not also take another alternative cost, and #5211 marked eleven cards as caveats. A condition on the game or on a choice is now `Spec.FlashIf` (`game/flash_if.go`, catalog data): step 1c of the cast timing gate, its game condition read as the cast begins, and its "if X is N or less" (`XCeiling`) or "if it targets …" (`Targets`) binding only a cast that needs it (`Game.FlashIfReliedOnLocked`), read by the announce gate, the enumerator, the view's `x_max` and `legal_targets` and the auto-tap preview. A condition on paying a cost ("if you pay {2} more", "if you behold a Dragon") is an optional additional cost that opens the window (`AdditionalCost.AsThoughFlash`, `effects.FlashForMore`, `effects.OptionalBeholdOpeningFlash`), shipped on the wire as `optional_costs[i].opens_flash`. Coverage's `TestNoFlashIfClauseIsAnAlternativeCost` forbids the old shape. No snapshot change. **Cards now Full:** Scarring Memories, Molten Exhale, Asinine Antics, Oakshade Stalker, Silver Scrutiny, Timely Ward, Fated Clash, Bard's Company, Harbinger of the Tides and Mystical Tether; Serpent of the Pass, marked Full on the old shape, moves too. **Still open:** the view's per-offer `alternative_costs[i].x_max` and `legal_targets` do not narrow for a flash cast that claims an alternative cost (the server and the enumerator do), and the client does not yet pre-select an `opens_flash` cost.
