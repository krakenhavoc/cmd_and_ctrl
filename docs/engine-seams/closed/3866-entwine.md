**Entwine: one extra cost for choosing every mode** (#3866, CR 702.42, [ADR 0065](../../decisions/0065-modal-and-multi-target-clauses.md)'s 2026-10-16 amendment) — entwine is two things the engine already had, joined by the CR 601.2b announcement. The cost is an ADR 0073 optional additional cost keyed `game.EntwineKey`, paid at most once: `effects.Entwine("{2}")`, or `effects.EntwineSacrifice(n, label, preds…)` for "Entwine—Sacrifice N lands". The mode count is #1655's announcement-driven count raised to every bullet while that cost is announced: `effects.Entwining(ms)` is `ms.InsteadIf(len(ms.Options), effects.WasEntwined)`.
- **The count.** Unentwined, the printed count stands; entwined, every bullet and no fewer. A count between is refused, so Kaya's Guile ("Choose two … Entwine {3}") is two or all four, never three. The bullets run in printed order (CR 702.42b), as every modal spell's do.
- **What reads it.** The cast gate, the hand card's `modes.if_optional_paid`, the client's optional-cost offer ("Entwine {2}" where kicker is offered) and the enumerator's one expansion per announced optional-cost set all read `ModeBoundsForEffect`, so none offers a selection another refuses. No wire, snapshot or closure field was added. When the ticked entwine cost raises the count to every bullet, the mode picker opens with them all chosen.
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` (`checkEntwine`) refuses either half without the other, two entwine costs, a repeatable one, a Repeatable spec, entwine beside escalate, and an entwine count on a trigger's or an activated ability's modes.
- **Cards** (29, all Full): Spectral Shift, Abuna's Chant, Barbed Lightning, Betrayal of Flesh, Dream's Grip, Flourishing Strike, Goblin War Party, Grab the Reins, Gravedig, Incite War, Journey of Discovery, Kaya's Guile, Mirage Mockery, One Dozen Eyes, Plunge into Darkness, Promise of Power, Rain of Rust, Reap and Sow, Road of Return, Roar of the Kha, Rude Awakening, Savage Beating, Second Sight, Solar Tide, Stir the Pride, Temporal Cascade, Tooth and Nail, Unbounded Potential and Wail of the Nim. Left out for a bullet with no shape: Blinding Beam (#3995), Twisted Reflection (#3996) and Shriveling Rot (#3997).
