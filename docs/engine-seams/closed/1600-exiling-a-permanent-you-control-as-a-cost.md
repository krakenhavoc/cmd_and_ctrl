**Exiling a creature you control as a cost** (#1600, CR 118.3 / CR 602.2b / CR 605.3a, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md)'s 2026-10-03 amendment) — the ability half of the "Exiling a permanent you control as a cost" row. `game.ExilePermanentsCost` (`Count`, `CardType`, `ExcludeSource`, `Label`) is the battlefield sibling of `ExileCost` and `ReturnToHandCost`, on both owners: `AbilityCost.ExilePermanents` and `ManaAbilityShape.ExilePermanents`, built with `effects.ExileACreatureYouControl()` or `effects.ExileAPermanentYouControl(label, cardType)`. The clause is DATA (a permanent card type read off the permanent's current types), not a `TargetSpec`, so the ADR 0041 closure ratchet gains no route. One candidate walk, `ExilePermanentsOptionsForEffect`, feeds the view's `exile_permanent_label` / `exile_permanent_options` on both ability views, the legal enumerator (one move per creature) and `validateExilePermanentsCostLocked` (exactly the count, distinct, yours, of the type, never also sacrificed or returned by the same payment); the picks ride `exile_permanent_ids` on `activate_ability` and `activate_mana_ability`, and the client reuses `SacrificeCostModal` with the verb "Exile". The payment is the one exit primitive with `MustSettleNow`: leaves-the-battlefield triggers fire, dies and sacrifice triggers do not, a commander is asked CR 903.9 before anything is paid, and on a mana ability the triggers reach the stack after the mana is in the pool. The exiled permanents land on `PaidCost.Exiled`, and `PermanentInfo.ManaValue` (additive in snapshot schema v7) answers "the exiled creature's mana value" as it last existed. The auto-tapper never plans a mana ability with the component, and the heuristic prices an exiled permanent as it prices a sacrificed one. Proof cards, all `full`: The Soul Stone, Food Chain, City of Shadows and Altar of Bhaal // Bone Offering. Still open on the row: the casting-cost form (Lunar Hatchling's escape), a variable count (Fabrication Foundry), a clause a card type cannot say (Curie, Emergent Intelligence) and craft's two-zone clause.
