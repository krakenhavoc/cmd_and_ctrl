**Damage that can't be prevented** (#1853, [ADR 0107](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md) §5, PR 6) — CR 615.12 is a rule-modifying effect (CR 613.11) read at one gate, `damageUnpreventableLocked`, while the CR 614 window settles a damage event. It asks four sources in order: **the damage's own rider** (`DealDamage.CantBePrevented` on an ability or a target-dependent clause; `Spec.SpellDamageCantBePrevented`, a `game.SpellCondition` judged as the spell deals its damage, for a spell — Combust always, Banefire with X of 5 or more, Arrow Storm with raid); **the source's own static** ("Damage that would be dealt by this creature can't be prevented", read as the event opens and from last-known information once the source has left, CR 608.2h); **battlefield statics** (`Spec.DamageCantBePrevented`: all damage, combat damage, or combat damage dealt by creatures you control, read live and keyed by `CatalogAbilityKey`); and **turn grants** (`ModDamageCantBePrevented`, game-wide, or pinned to one creature, ended at cleanup, CR 514.2). Every prevention effect now declares `ReplacementEffect.Prevention` — protection's built-in (CR 702.16e), Fog's and Mending Hands' scoped shields and the catalog's three — and `TestDamageReplacementsDeclareWhetherTheyPrevent` reads every damage replacement's source and fails one that cancels or cuts the damage without saying what it is. While the gate says yes, the apply-loop settles each prevention effect once without running it (CR 615.12a): the damage is dealt, a charged shield keeps its charge, protection stops nothing, and no CR 616 prompt is asked about a shield that will do nothing. Lava Burst's "or dealt instead to another permanent or player" is the same treatment for `RedirectsDamage` replacements (`ModDamageCantBeRedirected` for a pinned grant). A spell's conditional "this spell can't be countered" (`Spec.CantBeCounteredIf`) shipped beside it for Banefire. The view shows a stack chip for a marked spell and a game-banner line for a live turn grant. **Cards:** Skullcrack, Call In a Professional, Banefire, Stomp, Combust, Pinpoint Avalanche, Arrow Storm, Lightning Surge, Urza's Rage, Lava Burst, Fear, Fire, Foes!, Flames of the Blood Hand, Flaring Pain, Impractical Joke, Wild Slash, Unstable Footing, Excruciator, Malignus, Sunspine Lynx, Frenzied Baloth, Pyrewood Gearhulk and Questing Beast (Full), and Leyline of Punishment (Caveats: no opening-hand start). **Moved to other rows:** Demonfire (#1886), Whippoorwill (#1887), Volcano Hellion (#1888), Everlasting Torment (#1889), Insult // Injury and Isengard Unleashed (#1890), Alchemist's Gambit (extra turns), Spider-Punk (riot).
