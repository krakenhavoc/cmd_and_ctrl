**Outside the game is the card database without a sideboard** (#3858, CR 400.11, 903.5e, [ADR 0198](decisions/0198-outside-the-game-is-the-card-database-without-a-sideboard.md)) — the owner's decision of 2026-10-15 gives a deck with no sideboard (plain Commander) the casual "your personal collection": "a card you own from outside the game" is any card in the card database that matches the effect's filter. `Player.HasSideboard` records whether the deck seated a sideboard; when it did, the sideboard stays the pool (ADR 0191), even once it is empty. Otherwise `game.QueueOutsidePickForEffect` asks the new `outside_collection` kind over `game.OutsideCollection` (`deck.NewCollection` on the loaded index: catalog cards first, then Commander-legal, then the most reprinted). The answer is `card_names`, checked against the filter and the set rule on submit, and each named card is minted the way a deck import builds one, owned by the chooser, into their outside-the-game container, from where the effect continues exactly as for a sideboard pick. The chooser searches the database through `GET /games/{id}/outside/cards`, filtered by the open pick; the enumerator offers the prompt's suggestions and the heuristic takes the best. The continuation rides the existing search frame, so the closure ratchet does not move. Snapshot: additive in v9. **Cards:** every wish built on `TakeFromOutside` (Burning, Living, Cunning, Golden, Death and Glittering Wish, Fae of Wishes, Mastermind's Acquisition) and Extrapolate the Impossible now work without a sideboard; Coax from the Blind Eternities and Karn, the Great Creator (both Full) are new. Still waiting: Spawnsire of Ulamog's {20} and Wish, which need a cast from outside the game.
