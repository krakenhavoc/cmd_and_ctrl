**Each player takes from their own look, against two predicates** (#1743, CR 101.4 / CR 101.4a / CR 701.20, [ADR 0013 amendment 2026-10-01](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md#5ai-amendment-2026-10-01-a-card-set-pick-is-a-run-too--every-player-chooses-from-their-own-look)) — Explore the Vastlands' "each player looks at the top five cards of their library and may reveal a land card and/or an instant or sorcery card from among them", which slice 296-m left uncatalogued because every look-at-top-N shape was ONE player choosing at most one card against ONE predicate. Two generalisations of the existing seam, no new prompt kind and no wire change. **The fan-out is a run:** `game.ChooseCardsRunThenForEffect(legs, then)` queues one ordinary `choose_cards` prompt per seat as one §5x run (the discard leg's link, which the catalog could not set) and hands `then` `PromptedPicks` once, after the last leg settles; the legs go up together in APNAP order and are answered privately (CR 101.4a), so nothing is revealed or moved until every seat has answered, a seat with no candidate is not asked, and a departing chooser's leg settles with nothing through the existing `dropDefault`. **Two predicates are slots:** `effects.TakeFromLibraryToHand.Slots` (`TakeSlot{Label, Match, Max}`) makes the pick one prompt over every card that fits any slot, with a set rule — a maximum bipartite matching, so a card matching both slots fills one — riding the prompt's existing `Validate`; the ceiling is what the slots can hold out of the actual candidates (three lands and no spell is "up to one"), and a mandatory slotted clause takes it as its floor, so no slotted prompt can be unanswerable. `internal/legal` needed nothing: every set it offers already goes through `ChooseCardsPickLegalLocked`. `effects.EachPlayerTakesFromLibrary` is the card side: each player LOOKS (only they know their five, and no seat sees another's prompt), every player's picks are revealed before any card moves, each player's rest goes to the bottom on their own `random_order` stream with every knower cleared, and the fan-out's `Then` runs last. **1 card:** Explore the Vastlands is catalogued under `<oracle>#1` and `full`; Wandering Archaic's caveat about it is cleared and the card is `full` on both faces. **Still open:** "each player REVEALS the top N" (the public version of the same fan-out) has no card in the gap asking for it.
