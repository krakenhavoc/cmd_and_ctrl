**Library to battlefield without a search, and a random-order bottom** (#745, PR #773) - `Game.PutFromLibraryOntoBattlefieldForEffect(cardID, LibraryEntryOptions{Controller, Tapped})` and its batch twin `PutCardsFromLibraryOntoBattlefieldForEffect`, sharing one body with the hand move (`battlefield_put.go`): controller stamped in the old zone, CR 614 pipeline before the move, move, then `EventZoneMove` / `EventETB` / `AsEnters`; no `EventSearchLibrary`, no shuffle, not a land drop (CR 305.4), a token refused like a nonpermanent (CR 111.8: `Card.IsToken`), resumable since #1322 (a card's entry question — a shockland's life, a Clone's copy — pauses the whole batch, which lands together once the last answer is in; `PutCardsFromLibraryOntoBattlefieldThenForEffect` is the door for a caller that reads the result). The batch runs every card's pipeline against the pre-entry board before anything moves and announces after everything has landed, so "any number" is a simultaneous entry. `Game.PutOnBottomInRandomOrderForEffect(actor, from, ids)` is the public random-order bottom, one draw on the actor's keyed `random_order` stream (ADR 0054 Decision 8, #744 sub-PR 1): it takes only the cards still in a zone of kind `from` (a saved ID whose card has moved on is skipped); from any zone but its owner's library it routes through the exit path (`ToBottom`, CR 614 / 903.9 apply), and a card already in its owner's library is reordered without a zone change; cascade now calls it. Also `LookAtTopOfLibraryForEffect` (knower = the looker only) and `PutIntoGraveyardForEffect` (not a mill). The card side is `effects.PutFromLibraryOntoBattlefield` over a `choose_cards` prompt with `Zone: ZoneLibrary` (the wire already withholds its options and bounds from every seat but the chooser), with `LookAtTopThenMayPutOntoBattlefield` and `RevealUntilThenPutOntoBattlefield` for the two whole sentences. Shipped on Chaos Warp, Coiling Oracle, Genesis Wave, Risen Reef, Explorer's Scope, Thrasios, Animist's Awakening, The Regalia, Lurking Predators, Atla Palani, Ureni of the Unwritten, Majestic Genesis, Gilgamesh (caveat: no reflexive attach, #636), Armored Skyhunter (caveat: Equipment only; an Aura needs a CR 303.4f entry choice) and Esika // The Prismatic Bridge (caveat on the front face: granted mana ability). Still waiting on something else: Tom Bombadil (a "final chapter ability resolves" event), Famished Worldsire (devour), Kamahl's Druidic Vow (the legendary-sorcery cast restriction), Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy (mana-trigger colour), Winota and Doors of Durin (unblocked by #1227: `ZoneEntryOptions.Attacking` is the non-token permanent put onto the battlefield attacking they were waiting on, and the library door takes the same options struct — what is left on those two is ordinary card work), Aang, at the Crossroads (transform), Lonis (a sacrifice-X cost). Cybership, once waiting on face-down (#656), is registered since #1270. Genesis Ultimatum was on that list for "a spell that exiles itself as it resolves" and no longer is: #489 taught the resolution frame not to re-route a spell its own effect has already moved (`spellMovedItselfLocked`), which also unblocks Ascend from Avernus and every other "exile this spell as it resolves" rider outside the flashback and warp paths.
