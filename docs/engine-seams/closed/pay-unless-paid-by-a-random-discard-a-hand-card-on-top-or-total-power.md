**Pay-unless paid by a random discard, a hand card on top of the library, or creatures with total power N** (#6187, CR 118.12a, 118.3, 701.9b) — three `game.PayAction` kinds beside the others. `PayActionDiscardRandom` (`"discard_random"`) names nothing: "yes" is a payment with `Count` cards in hand, and the engine draws them as the payment is made (`ChooseAtRandomForEffect`) and discards them as a cost's. `PayActionHandToLibraryTop` (`"hand_to_library_top"`) is a card pick from the payer's hand; each named card goes on top of their library, not a discard. `PayActionSacrificeTotalPower` (`"sacrifice_total_power"`, `PayAction.TotalPower`) is a card pick of no fixed size: one or more permanents of the clause's kind whose current powers add up to `TotalPower`. The view (`pay_random_discard`, `pay_cards.total_power`, `pay_cards.powers`), the enumerator (two total-power payments: payment order, and biggest first) and the client's picker come with the kinds. Card side (`cards/effects/pay_unless_payments.go`): `RandomDiscardPayment`, `HandToLibraryTopPayment`, `SacrificeTotalPowerPayment` and `EntersSacrificeUnlessPaying(name, payment)`. Snapshot: `payActionClause.TotalPower`, additive in v9. **Cards:** Balduvian Horde, Pillaging Horde, Tainted Specter and Phyrexian Dreadnought wait for their OLD batches.
