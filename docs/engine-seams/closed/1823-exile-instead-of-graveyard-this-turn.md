**Exile instead of your graveyard this turn** (#1823, [ADR 0108](decisions/0108-turn-scoped-effects-object-history-and-damage-shields.md) §4, PR 3) — "if a card would be put into your graveyard from anywhere this turn, exile that card instead" (CR 614.1a) is a `ScopedEffect` kind, `exileInsteadOfYourGraveyard` (`game.ModExileInsteadOfYourGraveyard`, a replacement-reader kind, scope game, reading `Player`), written by `Game.ExileInsteadOfYourGraveyardThisTurnForEffect` until end of turn. It applies to every zone move and every discard into that player's graveyard from any zone (a discard is its own replacement window, so the kind watches both), costs and effects alike, and the spell that made it, which goes to the graveyard after its effect starts. A token is not a card and is left alone. The graveyard a card goes to is its owner's, so a creature you stole and lost still dies into its owner's graveyard. The affected object's controller orders it against other replacements (CR 616.1). `effects.GraveyardPlayThisTurn` (`YawgmothsWillThisTurn()`) is the paired card-side helper: it writes the replacement and a stored `ScopeStanding` cast permission over the controller's graveyard together, so the "play lands and cast spells from your graveyard" half can never ship without the replacement that keeps it from being stronger than printed; `SpellsOnly` is Forgotten Cellar's spells-without-lands variant. Shipped on Yawgmoth's Will, Gaea's Will (suspend 4—{G}), Magus of the Will and Walk-In Closet // Forgotten Cellar (the left door is Glacierwood Siege's land permission over the graveyard, the right door's unlock trigger runs the helper).
