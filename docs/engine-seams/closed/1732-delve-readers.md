**Delve, the cards exiled with it and granted delve** (#1732, [ADR 0100](decisions/0100-delve-either-or-and-variable-sacrifice-costs.md) sub-PR 2) — a permanent whose spell delved is linked to the cards delve exiled to pay for it (CR 607.2q), and only while each is still in exile as that object (CR 400.7). `Game.DelvedCardsForEffect` applies that rule for every reader: `CastCounts.Delved` with `CountersPerDelved` is Murktide Regent's "enters with a +1/+1 counter on it for each instant and sorcery card exiled with it"; `CastProvenance.Delved` (`Card.Delved()`) carries the link onto the permanent and `PermanentInfo.Delved` onto its last-known information, which Soulflayer's layer-6 keywords (with `StaticAbility.DependsOnExile`, so a card leaving exile refreshes them) and Ethereal Forager's attack trigger (which still finds the cards after the Forager has left) read. `Spec.SpellsYouCastHaveDelve` is Teval, Arbiter of Virtue's "Spells you cast have delve", derived off the caster's permanents in the one `DelveForLocked` door, so the pricer, the view and the bot see it with no change. 5 cards shipped, Temporal Trespass among them now that extra turns exist. Still open: Tasigur, the Golden Fang, Afterlife from the Loam and Sorcerous Squall, held for their other text; see the Delve row.
