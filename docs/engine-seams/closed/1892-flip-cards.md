**Flip cards** (#1892, [ADR 0151](decisions/0151-flip-cards.md)): CR 710 and CR 110.5. Flipped is a status on the permanent, `game.Card.Flipped`, snapshot-carried and cleared by every move to a zone other than the battlefield (CR 710.4); a flip card (`game.LayoutFlip`, `game.IsFlipCard`) shows its flipped half, face 1 and catalog key `<oracle_id>#1`, exactly when it is flipped. `Game.FlipPermanentForEffect` (`game/flip.go`) is the verb, transform's shape without being transform: the same object keeps its counters, damage, attachments, tap state and timestamp, nothing re-triggers, it refuses a permanent that is already flipped, face down or not a flip card (`CanFlip`), and it emits `EventFlipPermanent`, which bumps the layer version and is narrated ("Akki Lavarunner flipped into Tok-Tok, Volcano Born"). `SetFace` keeps a flip card's top-half mana cost and colours on both halves (CR 710.1c), and the importer stamps per-half keywords. Copies follow CR 707.2 and 707.3: the copiable values are always the top half, and the copier's own status picks the half it shows (`settleFlipFace`). Homura's "return it to the battlefield flipped" is `Game.ReturnFromGraveyardFlippedForEffect`, which shares its body with the transform form (`returnFromGraveyardOnFaceLocked`). `effects.FlipThis` is the card primitive and `ReturnFromGraveyard{Flipped: true}` the return. Deck validation drops the flip banner (the layout map is now empty), `/cards/{id}/image?face=1` serves a flip card's one image, and the client turns a flipped permanent's image 180° (270° when tapped). **Cards:** Rune-Tail, Kitsune Ascendant, Akki Lavarunner and Homura, Human Ascendant (Full); the other 16 Commander-legal flip cards followed as a card batch (#3216): 14 are Full (the five ki cards share `kiFlipTriggers`, and Initiate of Blood's "when that creature dies this turn" is `flipWhenItDiesThisTurn`, the Whippoorwill delayed-trigger shape). Two audited against the engine wait on a seam of their own: Kitsune Mystic (an Aura moved to a creature it can't enchant stays put, #3160) and Sasaya, Orochi Ascendant ("Reveal your hand" as a cost, #3250).
