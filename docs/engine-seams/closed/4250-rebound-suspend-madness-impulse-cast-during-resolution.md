**Rebound, suspend, madness and the impulse-style offers cast during their resolution** (#4250, CR 608.2g, CR 702.88a, CR 702.62a, CR 702.35a; [ADR 0066](decisions/0066-granted-cast-and-play-permissions.md)'s 2026-10-23 #4250 amendment) — seven more doors that kept the pass-limited cast permission after #4243 now ask the `resolution_cast` prompt inside the resolution: rebound's upkeep trigger, suspend's last-counter trigger, madness's trigger, the exile-until shape (Tibalt's Trickery, Guff Rewrites History), the paid "if you don't" cast (Chandra, Torch of Defiance), and the free-or-hand offers (Ziatora's Envoy and Rashmi, Eternities Crafter from the top of the library, Beseech the Mirror from exile). Each stamps the permission its `may_cast` used to stamp on "yes" and queues the prompt over the one card; the cast is made during the resolution (a madness cost or a card's own cost paid in it), and the decline is the permission's lapse: the card stays in exile (rebound, suspend, the keeping exile-until form, paid-or-else, which runs its "if you don't"), goes into the graveyard (madness, a new `LapseToGraveyard`), to the bottom with the walked-past cards (exile-until), or into the hand (free-or-hand). `ResolutionCastCandidateLocked` accepts a library card a permission names, for Ziatora's Envoy's top card. A table paused on any of them is a restore point. **Cards that changed behaviour:** every rebound, suspend and madness card, Tibalt's Trickery, Guff Rewrites History, Chandra, Torch of Defiance, Ziatora's Envoy, Rashmi and Beseech the Mirror. The hand-cast offers (#2110) and the one-off pass-closed grants in `cards/effects/` keep the stand-in (#4250).
