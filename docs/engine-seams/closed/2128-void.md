**Void: a nonland permanent left the battlefield or a spell was warped this turn** (#2128, CR 207.2c, CR 702.185c, [ADR 0049](../../decisions/0049-card-engine-seam-review.md) amendment 2026-10-23) — two table-wide `TurnTally` cells: `NonlandPermanentsLeft` is bumped from every `EventLTB` whose permanent was not a land as it last existed on the battlefield (any route, any destination, any controller, tokens included), and `SpellsWarped` is bumped where a spell is cast for an alternative cost carrying the warp exile clause, whether or not it resolves. `Game.VoidThisTurn` reads both. Card side (`cards/effects/void.go`): `Void(g)`, `WhenThisEntersIfVoid`, `WheneverThisAttacksIfVoid` and `AtYourEndStepIfVoid` (an intervening if, checked as the trigger fires and again as it resolves, CR 603.4), `AtYourEndStepUnlessVoid` (Chorale of the Void) and `CostsLessIfVoid` (Temporal Intervention). Alpharael's "Ward—Discard a card at random" is `WardDiscardAtRandom`. Both cells are additive in snapshot v9. **Cards (all Full):** Voidforged Titan, Kavaron Skywarden, Insatiable Skittermaw, Interceptor Mechan, Elegy Acolyte, Hylderblade, Chorale of the Void, Alpharael, Stonechosen, Roving Actuator, Tragic Trajectory, Plasma Bolt, Decode Transmissions, Hymn of the Faller and Temporal Intervention. **Still open:** Axavar, Fate Thief needs heist (#4235), which the engine does not have.
