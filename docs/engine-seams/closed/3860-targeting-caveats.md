**Targeting caveats: which instance of target, a bar that isn't hexproof, a tax for targeting, a clause relative to the trigger, a printed {T} ability** (#3860, [ADR 0019 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md), [ADR 0038 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0038-protection-style-keywords.md), [ADR 0080 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0080-attack-taxes.md), [ADR 0118 amendment 2026-10-15](decisions/0118-strict-payment-by-default-and-alternative-costs-for-every-spell.md)) — six cards whose declared caveats were each a small gap in the shared targeting machinery. **Which instance of "target" (CR 115.7b, 601.2c):** a pinned retarget over a creature that fills several slots now asks which one changes (`finishPinnedRetargetLocked`, a keyed `option_pick`, restore-safe) instead of guessing the earlier one: Spellskite and Mizzium Meddler are `full`. **A bar that is not hexproof:** `ModCantBeTargetedByOpponents` protects against the CASTER's opponents, read live at `canBeTargetedBy` ahead of hexproof so a waiver can't lift it, and Vines of Vastwood targets any creature (`full`). **A tax for targeting (CR 601.2f):** `Spec.TargetTaxes` is an additional cost a permanent charges a spell that targets it, validated and paid by `CastSpell`, priced per target set by the enumerator (a seat that can't pay is not offered the target), named per candidate in the spell's legal-target clause (`life_taxes`) and read as a pure price by the bot (`MoveCost.TargetTaxLife`): Terror of the Peaks is `full`. **A clause relative to the trigger's event:** `TargetSpec.ControlledByThatPlayer` reads the damaged player off the event the trigger carries (`TargetSource.Trigger`), so Parapet Thrasher's artifact bullet offers only that opponent's artifacts and is off the menu when there are none (`full`). **A printed {T} ability:** the importer stamps `Card.PrintsTapAbility` from the Scryfall text (`game.PrintsTapAbilityText`), a multi-faced card is judged on the face that is up and a copy takes it, so Magewright's Stone can untap a creature the catalog doesn't run (`full`).
