**Three Marvel Commander gaps** (#4707, #4455, [ADR 0019](decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md), [ADR 0032](decisions/0032-planeswalkers.md), [ADR 0048](decisions/0048-cost-modification.md) and [ADR 0049](decisions/0049-card-engine-seam-review.md), each amended 2026-10-30; CR 704.5j, CR 601.2f, CR 602.2b, CR 118.7) — three small additive seams. **"That player controls", the event's Actor:** `TargetSpec.ControlledByEventActor`, built with `effects.ControlledByThatEventActor`, is `ControlledByThatPlayer`'s twin that reads the controller of the targeting spell or ability off an `EventBecomesTarget` trigger, judged in the one shared target walk. **A creature-only legend-rule exemption:** `LegendRuleExemptYourCreatures` (`effects.LegendRuleDoesntApplyToYourCreatures`) beside "yours" and "everyone"; a legendary artifact you control and an opponent's legends still answer to the rule. **A coloured reduction on an activation:** `CostModifier.Unit` on a `CostReduction` is what one point takes off, spent by `reduceByManaCost` (CR 118.7), built with `effects.ActivationCostsLessMana`; the condition reads the new `TurnTally.EnteredFliers`, a record of creatures that entered with flying (`Game.EnteredFlierUnderThisTurn`), additive under snapshot v9. **Cards** (3, all Full): Black Bolt, Inhuman King, Council of Reeds and Flying Drone.
