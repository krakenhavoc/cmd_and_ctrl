**Blocking requirements** (#1597, CR 509.1c, [ADR 0045 amendment 2026-09-28 (#1597)](decisions/0045-combat-restrictions.md) Decisions 55–58). "Blocks each combat if able", Lure's "all creatures able to block this creature do so", "must be blocked if able" and "must be blocked by exactly one creature if able" are enforced over each defending player's whole declaration, which must obey the most requirements obeyable without breaking a restriction — menace, flying and the other evasion, "can't block", block-count bounds and the whole-combat limits. `Characteristic.BlockRequirements` carries them on either side (the blocker for `blocks`, the attacker for the other three), written by layer statics and the new `addBlockRequirement` data record. `blockRequirementReachLocked` is the requirements a staged assignment obeys plus the best legal addition, searched as a min-cost flow per combination of attacker modes (a minimum is 0-or-at-least-m, not a capacity) and re-checked by the ordinary validator, so its witness is always a declaration the verb takes. The block validator refuses a declaration that lowers a pending defender's reach; the defender's pass, `finish_blocks` and `AdvanceStep` are the checkpoint. The option generator offers the witness as one `BlockOption.Required`, which the enumerator marks `AlwaysLegal` while it withholds the pass, so a declining bot blocks as required and the #328 signal never auto-completes a defender past a requirement. Refusals are `illegal_block` / `block_requirement`, and the wire adds `CardView.must_block`. **Grand Melee** loses its block-half caveat and ships `full`; **Lure**, **Prized Unicorn**, **Gaea's Protector**, **Razorgrass Screen**, **Irresistible Prey** and **Taunting Challenge** are new and `full`. **Still open:** provoke and "target creature blocks it this turn if able" (a requirement naming one attacker), predicated Lures (Marble Priest's Walls), blocking costs (CR 509.1d) and "blocks each attacking creature".
