**A creature that can't attack its owner** (#1794, CR 508.1c / CR 508.1d / CR 508.4c / CR 508.7b, [ADR 0106 §2](decisions/0106-five-small-seams-from-the-s50-rechecks.md)) — Xantcha, Sleeper Agent "can't attack its owner or planeswalkers its owner controls", a restriction keyed on the ATTACKER, which the old per-seat target check could not say. `Characteristic.AttackTargetRestrictions []game.AttackTargetRestriction{Source, SourceName, NotOwner, NotOwnersPlaneswalkers}` is `AttackRequirements`' twin: written by an ordinary layer-6 static (`effects.CantAttackItsOwner()`, `effects.CantAttackItsOwnerOrItsOwnersPlaneswalkers()`), only ever appended to, gone with the creature's own abilities (CR 613.1f), and additive in the snapshot through `lastKnownBattlefield` under schema v7. One predicate, `canAttackTargetWithLocked(attacker, target)`, is CR 506.2's controller check plus that list, reading the owner live (CR 108.3: a copy uses its own owner) and never refusing a battle. Both declaration verbs call it (the single verb refuses with "… can't attack its owner or planeswalkers its owner controls."; the bulk verb skips the entry), and so does the CR 508.1d search, so a creature that must attack and has only its owner to attack owes nothing. The enumerator walks a per-attacker `AttackTargetsForAttackerForEffect`, so the ADR 0105 digest's `attack_targets`, the client's per-attacker gate and the attack-all planner's per-seat count follow; `Turn.AttackTargets` stays per seat for prices and limits. CR 508.7b's reselection and CR 508.4c's entry attacking keep the controller-only check. The card view carries `attack_target_restrictions` with the owner resolved to a seat, and the board draws a "CAN'T ATTACK <name>" chip from it. **No card lands yet:** Xantcha also needs "any player may activate" (#1793), Alexios, Deimos of Kosmos "can't be sacrificed", and Elrond of the White Council secret council plus a mod kind that grants the restriction; all three are on the row's Waiting list.
