**Hand-exile costs tied to X or to a variable count** (#3175, CR 107.3a / 107.3b / 601.2b / 601.2f / 601.2h / 202.3e) — two spells kept a caveat because their cost reached into the hand in a way the cast path could not say: Shining Shoal's alternative cost ("exile a white card with mana value X from your hand rather than pay this spell's mana cost") and March of Swirling Mist's additional cost ("you may exile any number of blue cards from your hand. This spell costs {2} less to cast for each card exiled this way").
- **An alternative cost bound to X.** The pitch (`AlternativeCost.ExileFromHand`, Force of Will's component) may carry `ManaValueEqualsX` on its spec; `AlternativeCost.PaymentDefinesX` reports it. Such an offer is an alternative cost that includes X, so `CastCost.AltIncludesX` keeps CR 107.3b from locking X at 0 although its mana string is empty: the caster announces X (CR 601.2b) and `validateAlternativeCostPaymentLocked` holds the pitched card's mana value, read in hand where an {X} counts as 0 (CR 202.3e), to it. The offer itself is judged without X, so any matching card makes it available. On the stack X is the announced value.
- **A variable hand exile with a per-card discount.** `game.AdditionalCost.ExileFromHand` (`game.HandExileCost`, built by `effects.ExileAnyNumberFromHandCost(label, colour)`) names any number of matching cards in the caster's hand, none included, on `cast_spell`'s `exile_from_hand_ids` (`CastSpellParams.ExileFromHandIDs`). Each is distinct, the caster's own, matches the clause, is not the spell and is not also named to a discard or a pitch (CR 118.3). The count is `CostQuery.ExilingFromHand`, filled by the one pricer, so `effects.CostsLessPerExiledFromHand("{2}", …)` is part of the price CastSpell charges, the auto-tap preview shows and the enumerator offers; the announced {X} is generic mana a reduction may eat (#2701), the coloured symbols never. The cards are exiled with the spell on the stack through the exile-a-card cost's payer (not a discard), and a countered March keeps them in exile.
- **Views.** Shining Shoal's offer ships `x_locked_at_zero: false` and its `pay_options` carry `mana_value_equals_x` with each card's `mana_values` (the spell itself is left out of the list); the client sends the picked card's mana value as `x_value` and skips the X prompt. March's `additional_cost` ships `exile_from_hand_label` and `exile_from_hand_options` (min 0, the caster's frame only: a viewer shown the card does not learn which hidden cards are blue); the client opens the sacrifice picker with the verb "Exile" before the X prompt, so the X picker's price preview already carries the discount, and the auto-tap preview takes `exile_from_hand_ids`.
- **The enumerator.** One Shining Shoal pitch per distinct mana value among the seat's white cards (cheapest fuel first, X = 0 not offered), each with its X; one March cast per exile count from none up to the payment cap, each priced with its own discount. The bot prices a card exiled to a March like a discarded one, without a discard's payoffs.
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` allows the X bound only on a one-card hand pitch of a card that reads X (`checkAltCostXBound`), and refuses the hand exile on an optional cost or an either/or branch.
- **Cards:** Shining Shoal, March of Swirling Mist (Full).
