**The next damage from a source** (#1860, [ADR 0107](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md) §6, owner decisions 4 and 6, PR 7) — CR 615.8's one-use shield is `ModPreventNextFromSource`, a scoped prevention kind of plain data, so a table holding one is a restore point. It pins its source as an object (CR 400.7) — a chosen permanent spell covers the permanent it becomes (CR 609.7a), and a chosen permanent that has left covers its damage as it last existed — protects a player (optionally with that player's creatures), a pinned permanent or anything, rechecks the card's property against the source's last-known information as the damage would be dealt (CR 615.9; a failed recheck neither prevents nor uses the shield up, CR 609.7b), and names a follow-up body. It prevents the next INSTANCE of damage: one source dealing damage to several things at once is several engine events, so the shield stays live for the rest of the event batch it was spent in. **"The damage prevented this way"** (CR 615.5) runs on the damage event's tail once it settles, handed the amount, the source's controller and its colours; under damage that can't be prevented (CR 615.12) the shield is kept and the follow-up runs with zero. **`choose_source`** is a new card-set pick offering everything CR 609.7a allows — permanents, spells, face-up command-zone cards, and the objects stack items, waiting replacement and prevention records and waiting delayed triggers refer to, in public zones; the client captions each candidate with its controller and zone, the heuristic picks an opponent's spell on the stack and then the opponent's source with the most power, and the move label names each source's controller for the model tiers. The stale notes are fixed: the charged shield is CR 615.7's, and `effects/prevention.go` no longer says nobody prints the Circle form. **Cards** (41, all Full): the seven Circles of Protection, the seven Runes of Protection, Circle of Solace, Story Circle, Prismatic Circle, Greater Realm of Preservation, Haazda Shield Mate, Invulnerability, Kithkin Armor, Martyr's Cause, Mercenaries, Pentagram of the Ages, Pilgrim of Justice, Pilgrim of Virtue, Righteous Aura, Samite Blessing, Sanctum Guardian, Charm Peddler, Circle of Despair and Dazzling Reflection, and with the follow-up Awe Strike, Bone Mask, Cho-Arrim Alchemist, Deflecting Palm, Honorable Passage, Intervention Pact, New Way Forward, Reverse Damage and Shadowbane. **Moved to other rows:** Penance and Seasoned Tactician (#1902, their costs), Rhystic Circle (#1903), Desperate Gambit (#1890), Immortal Coil and Nine Lives (#1906, a static's follow-up). The not-one-use and redirecting shields against a chosen source are #1904 and #1905.
