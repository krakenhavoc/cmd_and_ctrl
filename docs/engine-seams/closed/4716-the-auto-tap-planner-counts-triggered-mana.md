**The auto-tap planner counts triggered mana** (#4716, CR 605.1b / 605.4a / 106.12, [ADR 0074](decisions/0074-triggered-mana-abilities.md) amendment 2026-10-30, replacing §7; follows [#763](engine-seams/closed/763-triggered-mana-abilities-and-the-colour-they-read.md)) — closes the "counting triggered mana in the auto-tap PLAN" item #763 left declared. In game c16a7f14 Crypt Ghast made each Swamp tap for {B}{B}, but the planner priced a Swamp at one {B}: it tapped five Swamps and cracked a Treasure for Veinripper's {3}{B}{B}{B}, which three Swamps pay, and a cast that only the trigger mana makes payable had no plan at all, so strict payment, the move list, the ready ring and the bots called it unpayable. The planner now asks the judge the executor fires with, `manaTriggerFiringsLocked` (slice 1, #4772), about the production each tap would make, behind the `manaTriggersExistLocked` gate, so there is one copy of each trigger's condition and a new card is planned with no declaration (slice 2, #4775).
- **Trigger slots.** What fires is appended to the candidate as `tapSource.TriggerSlots`, priced through the CR 614 window as not a tap, so Mana Reflection does not double Wild Growth's {G}. The solver, the generic recruit, the tiers and the hand demand read slots and needed no change. A Swamp under Crypt Ghast is a two-mana source. Only a `{T}` ability gets them, as only a `{T}` fires. `sortTapSources` scores restrictiveness on a source's own slots and breaks a tie toward the source with more trigger slots, so a Forest under Wild Growth pays {G}{G} alone.
- **A pick the trigger reads.** Mirari's Wake, Mana Flare, Zendikar Resurgent, Caged Sun, Forsaken Monument and Ultima depend on what the land made, so a source with a pick is judged per combination of its picks, capped at 36 (owner decision 1), and becomes one candidate per combination: `OneColor` for one pick, `tapSource.PinnedSlots` (per-slot colours in `plannedTap.Slots`) for more. A dual under Mirari's Wake pays {R}{R}, never {R}{G}. Above the cap, or with two or more picks after a production replacement changed the slots, the source gets no trigger mana and is planned as before.
- **A trigger's own choice is booked.** Fertile Ground's and Blighted Burgeoning's "any color", and Mana Flare after a land that made two types, are slots the solver books; the colour travels on `plannedTap.TriggerColors` and the executor's adder makes it instead of a greedy pick that could leave a hybrid pip unpaid. The costed plan (#2455) counts trigger mana too and names the trigger's permanent as the source of a Signet's funding mana.
- **The clone check guards it.** Every payer that carries a plan out asks `autoTapFundedLocked`: the plan, the #2461 clone check, and, when that fails on a board with a mana trigger, one more plan without trigger mana, which is the planner as it was. Every board that paid before still pays.
- **What does not change.** CR 106.7 "could produce" leaves trigger mana out, so the view's `produced` and Exotic Orchard are as they were. Stack triggers (Electro, Fire Nation Palace; CR 605.5a) are never counted. The preview gets no new field ([owner decision 3](decisions/0074-triggered-mana-abilities.md#owner-decisions-2026-10-30)). Mana a cost does not need still floats, because a land cannot be half-tapped. The bot's own turn plan still reads `produced` and so under-plans such boards until [#4770](https://github.com/krakenhavoc/cmd_and_ctrl/issues/4770).

**Cards:** no card is new. Every catalogued mana trigger is planned through the judge, among them Wild Growth, Overgrowth, Utopia Sprawl (once a colour is chosen), Fertile Ground, Blighted Burgeoning, Mana Flare, Winter's Night, Crypt Ghast, Caged Sun, Forsaken Monument, Ultima, Mirari's Wake, Zendikar Resurgent, Sasaya, Orochi Ascendant, Regal Behemoth, and the spell-made High Tide, Bubbling Muck and Molten Tide records. Their comments no longer declare the planner's blindness a simplification (slice 3).
