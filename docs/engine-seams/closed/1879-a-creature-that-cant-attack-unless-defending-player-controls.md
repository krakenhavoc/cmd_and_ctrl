**A creature that can't attack unless the defending player controls something** (#1879, CR 508.1c / CR 508.5 / CR 508.5a / CR 509.1b, [ADR 0107 §2](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md)) — Sea Serpent's "can't attack unless defending player controls an Island", and every other condition on the defending player the Commander-legal cards print, as plain-data clauses on ADR 0106 §2's per-target restriction. `AttackTargetRestriction` carries `DefenderMustControl []game.PermanentQuery` (an any-of list of closed permanent filters, `game/permanent_query.go`: card types, subtypes, supertypes, colours, a keyword, "enchanted"), `DefenderMustBePoisoned` (CR 122.1f), `DefenderMustBeMonarch` (CR 725.1), `DefenderGraveyardAtLeast` and `ControllerMustControlMore` ("you control more creatures than defending player", the "you" being the attacking creature's controller). Every clause set must hold. Each is written by an ordinary layer-6 self static (`effects.CantAttackUnlessDefendingPlayerControls`, `…IsPoisoned`, `…IsTheMonarch`, `…HasCardsInGraveyard`, `CantAttackUnlessYouControlMoreThanDefendingPlayer`) and goes with the creature's abilities (CR 613.1f). `canAttackTargetWithLocked` works out each target's defending player with `defendingPlayerForAttackLocked` (CR 508.5: the player, a planeswalker's controller, a battle's protector) and refuses the target when that player fails a clause, read live at declaration (an Island that leaves later changes nothing, CR 506.4a). So in Commander the creature may attack each opponent who meets the condition, their planeswalkers and the battles they protect, and no one else, worked out per creature (CR 508.5a). Both verbs refuse with the reason ("Sea Serpent can't attack Bob: Bob controls no Island.", "…: Bob isn't poisoned."), the CR 508.1d search counts only the targets left, and the enumerator's per-attacker list and the ADR 0105 digest's `attack_targets` follow. The card view's `attack_target_restrictions` gains one row per opponent who fails, with the reason in full (`reason`), and the chip's tooltip says it. A resolved effect grants the Island form with a new `ScopedEffect` mod kind, `cantAttackUnlessDefenderControls` (reads `Mod.Queries`): Veiled Serpent becomes "a 4/4 Serpent creature with 'This creature can't attack unless defending player controls an Island.'" A restriction a creature imposes on itself is its own ability, so a later layer-6 "loses all abilities" drops it and an earlier one does not (CR 613.1f, 613.7). The "can't block unless" halves are CR 509.1b block rules, `effects.CantBlockUnless` with `YouControlMoreThanTheAttackingPlayer` (the attacking player is the active player, CR 506.2) or `YouHaveCardsInHandAtLeast`. Every field is additive in the snapshot under schema v7; no closure routes. **Cards:** Armored Galleon, Chained Throatseeker, Crown-Hunter Hireling, Deep-Sea Serpent, Ethereal Whiskergill, Goblin Goon, Godhunter Octopus, Hammerhead Shark, Lurking Green Dragon, Mogg Toady, Monstrous Hound, Red Cliffs Armada, Sea Monster, Sealock Monster, Serpent of the Endless Sea, Slipstream Eel, Steam Frigate, Vantress Gargoyle, Veiled Serpent, Vodalian Serpent, Whimwader, Wu Warship and Zhou Yu, Chief Commander, all Full; the 13 serpents that also sacrifice themselves "when you control no Islands" landed with state triggers (#1858). Dreamwinder, Floodchaser and Kukemssa Serpent wait on a land becoming a basic land type until end of turn (#1881), and Goblin Rock Sled on what a creature did during your last turn (#1882).
