**A creature that can't attack unless the defending player controls something** (#1879, CR 508.1c / CR 508.5 / CR 508.5a, [ADR 0107 §2](decisions/0107-state-triggers-rebound-disturb-and-damage-prevention.md)) — Sea Serpent's "can't attack unless defending player controls an Island", as one more data field on ADR 0106 §2's per-target restriction. `AttackTargetRestriction.DefenderMustControl []game.PermanentQuery` is an any-of list of closed, plain-data permanent filters (`game/permanent_query.go`: card types, subtypes, supertypes, colours, a keyword, "enchanted"), written by `effects.CantAttackUnlessDefendingPlayerControls(...)` as an ordinary layer-6 self static and gone with the creature's abilities (CR 613.1f). It is a slice, so the restriction compares with `Equal` rather than `==` in the CR 613.8 dependency probe. `canAttackTargetWithLocked` gains one branch: it works out the target's defending player with `defendingPlayerForAttackLocked` (CR 508.5: the player, a planeswalker's controller, a battle's protector) and refuses the target when that player controls no match on the battlefield, read live at declaration (an Island that leaves later changes nothing, CR 506.4a). So in Commander the creature may attack each opponent with an Island, their planeswalkers and the battles they protect, and no one else, worked out per creature (CR 508.5a). Both verbs refuse with "Sea Serpent can't attack Bob: Bob controls no Island.", the CR 508.1d search counts only the targets left, and the enumerator's per-attacker list and the ADR 0105 digest's `attack_targets` follow, so the client's rings needed no change. The card view's `attack_target_restrictions` gains one row per opponent with no match (`unless`: "Island"), stamped by `stampDefenderRefusals`, and the chip's tooltip says "Bob controls no Island". The field is additive in the snapshot under schema v7. **Cards:** Armored Galleon, Deep-Sea Serpent, Ethereal Whiskergill, Godhunter Octopus, Hammerhead Shark, Lurking Green Dragon, Red Cliffs Armada, Sea Monster, Sealock Monster, Serpent of the Endless Sea, Slipstream Eel, Steam Frigate, Vodalian Serpent, Whimwader, Wu Warship and Zhou Yu, Chief Commander, all Full. **Still open:** 13 of the cards also sacrifice themselves "when you control no Islands" and wait on state triggers (#1858). Dreamwinder, Floodchaser and Kukemssa Serpent wait on a land becoming a basic land type until end of turn (#1881), Goblin Rock Sled on what a creature did during your last turn (#1882), and Veiled Serpent on a resolved effect that grants the restriction. Six cards print a condition other than controlling a permanent (poisoned, the monarch, more creatures or lands, a graveyard count) and wait on the row.
