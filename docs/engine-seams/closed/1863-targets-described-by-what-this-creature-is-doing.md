**Targets described by what this creature is doing** (#1863, CR 601.2c, CR 602.2b, CR 509.1h, CR 608.2b, CR 608.2h, [ADR 0019 amendment 2026-10-07](decisions/0019-structured-targeting.md)) — "target creature it's blocking", "target creature with flying blocked by this creature", "target creature that's blocking equipped creature". `game.TargetSpec.CombatWithSource` is a `SourceCombat{Of, Role}` (data, like #2146's `RelativeToSource`), judged in the one shared walk beside `CardOK`, so the legal set, the announce check (CR 601.2c), the resolution re-check (CR 608.2b), the client's `legal_targets` and the bot's enumerated moves are one answer. `Of` is the ability's source or the permanent it is attached to; `Role` is `blocked_by` (an attacker that creature is blocking) or `blocking` (a creature blocking it). A creature removed from combat in response is no longer a legal target (CR 506.4). A source that has left is read from its last-known record, which gained `PermanentInfo.Blocking` (CR 608.2h), reached through `TargetSource.Departed`; a walk with no source, or a host that has gone, admits nothing rather than everything. Card side: `effects.BlockedBySource`, `BlockingSource`, `BlockingEquipped` and `BlockedByEquipped`. No wire change. **Cards:** Wall of Vipers, Whip Vine, Goblin Snowman, Old Man of the Sea (the existing `PowerNoGreater` plus ADR 0109 §3's duration) and Plasma Caster, all `Full`.
