**Durations of continuous effects** (#755, [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md)) — `ScopedStatic.Duration` replaces `ExpiresAfterTurn`, and one expiry function (`durationExpiredLocked` in `game/duration.go`) decides when every continuous effect in the game is over. Four kinds: `UntilEndOfTurn` (CR 514.2, unchanged), `UntilYourNextTurn` (ends as that player's next turn begins, counted on the new `Player.TurnsBegun` from [ADR 0059](decisions/0059-turn-machinery.md) Decision 1 — which also ends it at the moment a departed player's turn would have begun, CR 800.4m), `ForAsLongAs` (CR 611.2b, re-evaluated at the top of every layer pass, with `WhileSourceOnBattlefield` and `WhileYouControlSource` conditions keyed on instance ID plus battlefield-entry stamp so a flicker ends it, CR 400.7) and `Indefinite` (CR 611.2a). The sweep runs at the cleanup step, at the turn-began hook and at the top of the layer recompute, and nowhere else. `Duration` is plain data — no closures — so it clones and is ready to persist when #515 makes the ability re-derivable; an optional pin drops an entry when the one object it exists to affect is gone, which is what keeps an indefinite effect from sitting in the snapshot census for the rest of the game. The registry is renamed `Game.ScopedStatics` / `RegisterScopedStaticForEffect` (the census keeps its `turnScopedStatics` wire key). Card-side: `StaticForDuration`, `SnapshotAffected` and the four `Duration…` builders in `cards/effects/durations.go`; `StaticUntilEOT` forwards to them. Shipped on Mass Diminish (until your next turn, with flashback), Sower of Temptation (for as long as), Agent of Treachery (no stated duration). Not folded in: `TurnScopedReplacements`, ADR 0057's `TurnScopedGameEndGates` and ADR 0045's `TurnScopedBlockRules` still clear wholesale at cleanup, because no card needs a longer duration from them yet. Decision 8 was tested by #1200 and held: Teferi's Protection's *"your life total can't change"* looked like the first replacement that wanted a duration and turned out to be a **player-scoped** statement, so it went on `Player.Statics` with this same `Duration` and this same `durationExpiredLocked` ([ADR 0085](decisions/0085-life-total-cant-change.md) Decision 1) — which also names the reason a closure registry is the wrong place to put a turn-cycle-long effect: it is censused `dropped` and costs the table its restore point for as long as the effect lasts. Teferi, Time Raveler's +1 and Alela's goad still carry their caveats — both are "until your next turn" in prose and neither is a layer effect (a cast-timing permission, #760; a goad marker the engine does not enforce).
