**This turn and next turn, and a table-wide targeting bar** (#5718, CR 113.3b, 113.3c, 303.4a, 500.7, 514.2, 601.2c, 608.2b, 611.2c, [ADR 0063](decisions/0063-durations-and-control.md)) — Peace Talks' "this turn and next turn, creatures can't attack, and players and permanents can't be the targets of spells or activated abilities". The duration is the fourteenth kind, `game.ThisTurnAndNextTurn` (persisted as 13), stamped by `Game.ThisTurnAndNextTurnDuration()` with the `Turn.Seq` it was made in and over at the cleanup step of any later turn, so it lasts exactly two turns whoever takes the second (an extra turn right after this one is the next turn); a turn more than one past the stamp ends it as it begins, the backstop for a next turn that ended without a cleanup step. The attack ban is can't-attack on `ScopeCreatures`, read live. The targeting bar is one `ScopeGame` record with the mod `cantBeTargetedBySpellsOrActivated` (`Game.PlayersAndPermanentsCantBeTargetedForEffect`), asked by both halves of the targeting choke point (`canBeTargetedBy` for a permanent, `canPlayerBeTargetedByLocked` for a player), so the picker, the legal-move enumerator, the announce gate and the resolution re-check agree. It refuses a spell or an activated ability and lets a triggered ability through: `TargetSource.IsTriggered` is set by `WithTrigger` on every trigger walk, by `stackItemSourceLocked` for a triggered item and by `copyTargetSourceLocked` for a copy of one. Card side: `effects.DurationThisTurnAndNextTurn(ctx)`. Snapshot: no new field; an older binary refuses a restore point holding either through `DurationKind.Known` and the unknown mod kind. **Cards:** Peace Talks (Full).
