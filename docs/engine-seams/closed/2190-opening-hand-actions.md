**Beginning the game with a card on the battlefield** (#2190, [ADR 0133](decisions/0133-opening-hand-actions.md), CR 103.6a) — nothing between "every seat has kept" and "the first step begins" was a moment a card could speak at, so "If this card is in your opening hand, you may begin the game with it on the battlefield" shipped as a caveat on every Leyline, and Gemstone Caverns could not begin the game with its luck counter at all. `Spec.OpeningHand` (`effects.BeginTheGameOnTheBattlefield`, riders `NotTheStartingPlayer`, `WithCounter`, `ThenExileFromHand`) is plain data on `game.OpeningHandAction`. `settleMulliganLocked` asks once, as the window closes: a `confirm` to each seat in turn order from the starting seat, per qualifying card. Yes puts the card onto the battlefield through the ordinary hand door, then places the counters and queues a one-card `choose_cards` exile pick. The prompts park the table like any open choice; the first step's entry hooks are not held, so a departing seat can never wedge the table. The prompt names a card in a hidden hand, so it is `PendingChoice.private` and the wire's `private_text` strips its words from every viewer but the chooser. The enumerator and the heuristic already answer both kinds (`aiseat/opening_hand_test.go` plays the opening to the end). **Cards:** Gemstone Caverns, Leyline of Anticipation, Leyline of Lifeforce, Leyline of Mutation, Leyline of Punishment, Leyline of Sanctity, Leyline of the Void and Leyline Axe (all now `full`). **Still open:** the Chancellors' "reveal this card from your opening hand" (a delayed trigger, no slot yet); the uncatalogued Leylines wait on their own static abilities.
