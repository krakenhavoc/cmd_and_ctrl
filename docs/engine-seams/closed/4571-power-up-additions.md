**Three power-up additions** (#4571, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) second amendment of 2026-10-29, Decisions 72 to 74, CR 702.193, CR 106.6, CR 101.2) — three small rules on the power-up bit, each read where the rule already lives. "Can be activated an additional time" is `CardDef.PowerUpExtras` (`game.PowerUpExtra`, built with `effects.YourPowerUpAbilitiesActivateAnAdditionalTime`): `Game.PowerUpLimitFor` is the printed once plus every battlefield static that reaches the ability, and `Game.PowerUpUsed` compares the activation record with it, so the engine, the legal enumerator and the view's `power_up_used` agree and nothing is stored. "During that turn, power-up abilities can't be activated" is a `ModCantActivatePowerUp` rules effect carried by the queued extra turn (`TakeExtraTurn.DuringThatTurn`), refused by `ActivationGateLocked` through the new `ActivationAbility.PowerUp` bit; `AnyActivationRestrictionsForEffect` reports it, so the enumerator drops the move and the view stamps `cant_activate`. "Spend this mana only to activate power-up abilities" is the `game.ManaRestrictPowerUp` purpose tag, matched against `ManaSpendContext.PowerUp`, which the activation, the enumerator and the cost preview set from the row. The snapshot gains one new `mods[].kind` value and the spend context's `PowerUp` key (`pendingChoices[].assist.spend`), both additive under v9. **Cards:** Wonder Man, Hollywood Hero, Kang the Conqueror and Quinjet Technician (Full).
