**Paying energy while a spell or ability resolves** (#1995, [ADR 0129](decisions/0129-energy-getting-and-paying-it.md) §3 and owner decision 3, PR 3) — a fixed amount ("you may pay {E}{E}. If you do", "sacrifice it unless you pay {E}", "an amount of {E} equal to …") is the `pay_unless` prompt with a `PayActionEnergy` payment (`game.QueueEnergyPaymentForEffect`; `effects.MayPayEnergy` and `PayEnergyUnless`), paid through `payEnergyLocked`, the one path that pays energy. A "yes" from a payer short of the energy is the decline (CR 118.3), and every energy prompt holds the step it was asked in. "Pay {E}{E}. If you can't" is mandatory and asks nothing (`effects.PayEnergyOrElse`). A chosen amount ("pay any amount of {E}", "one or more {E}") is the new `pay_amount` prompt (`effects.PayEnergyAmount`): 0 or `min`..`max`, `max` the payer's energy, with a `goal` the card computes and a `unit`. It blocks the table; its continuation rides the `chooseValueResume` frame, so it adds no closure route; a departed payer's drop runs the rest of the card with nothing paid (CR 800.4f). The client answers it with a stepper in the action dock and greys an energy Pay the seat cannot afford; the enumerator offers 0, `min`, `goal` and `max`; the heuristic pays the goal or nothing and prices a fixed payment at `Weights.Energy`; the board text names both prompts. **Cards** (21, all Full): the Thriving cycle (Grubs, Ibex, Rats, Rhino, Skyclaw, Turtle), Scrapper Champion, the Aether Chaser cycle (Chaser, Herder, Inspector, Poisoner, Swooper), Riparian Tiger, Aetherstream Leopard, Voltaic Brawler, Hexgold Slith, Lathnu Hellion, Harnessed Lightning, Galvanic Discharge, Die Young and Greenbelt Rampager. The rest of `energy-resolution-payments` lands in a follow-up card PR.
