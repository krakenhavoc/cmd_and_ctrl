**Returning a permanent you control as an additional cost** (#5247, CR 601.2b / 601.2g / 601.2h / 118.3 / 400.3 / 903.9b) — activated abilities could return a permanent you control to its owner's hand as a cost (`AbilityCost.ReturnToHand`) but a spell could not, so Fear of Isolation ("As an additional cost to cast this spell, return a permanent you control to its owner's hand") had no way to be cast. `game.AdditionalCost.ReturnToHand` is that same `ReturnToHandCost` on a cast's mandatory slot, built with `effects.ReturnPermanentToHandCost(label, preds...)`, so the candidate walk (`ReturnToHandOptionsForEffect`), the payability predicate, the clause check and the payer are the activation's own.
- **The wire.** The caster names the permanent on `cast_spell`'s `return_ids` (`CastSpellParams.ReturnIDs`, the name an activation already uses). It is validated at announce with the rest of the cost: exactly the clause's count, each once, the caster's own, matching the clause without the targeting gate (a hexproof or tapped permanent pays), and not also named to a sacrifice, an exile or an alternative cost (one permanent pays one component). A `return_ids` on a spell with no such cost is refused rather than ignored.
- **The payment.** Paid with the spell already on the stack, beside the sacrifices and the exiles, each permanent to its OWNER's hand, so one you control but don't own goes to its owner. A commander among them is asked CR 903.9b before anything is paid, and the cast parks on that question (the parked announcement carries `ReturnIDs`, so a restore taken while it is open keeps the pick). The auto-tapper treats a permanent named here as sacrifice-named: an Island may tap for the spell's {U} and then be returned (CR 601.2g before 601.2h), a Treasure may not be cracked for it.
- **Uncastable without one.** With nothing to return the cost cannot be paid (CR 118.3): the view's cast gate (`AnyAdditionalCostBranchPayableLocked`) says no, the legal-move enumerator offers no cast, and `CastSpell` refuses one.
- **Views.** `additional_cost.return_label` / `return_options` (the activation's picker view, off the same walk); the client's cast chain opens the sacrifice picker with the verb "Return" after the exile step (skipped when the board offers exactly one answer), and the auto-tap preview takes `return_ids`. The legal-move enumerator offers one payment per cast (the policy's cheapest fuel first), names it in the move label ("returning Island") and in `return_ids`; the bot prices the returned permanent as a self-return cost (half the permanent, all of a token).
- **Guards.** `effects.Register` refuses the component on an optional cost and on an either/or branch, and holds the mandatory one to the activated form's clause check (a count of at least one over permanents).
- **Cards:** Fear of Isolation (Full).
