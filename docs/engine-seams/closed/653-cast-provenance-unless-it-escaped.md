**Cast provenance, "unless it escaped"** (#653, CR 400.7d, CR 702.138b) — `Card.Provenance` is a `CastProvenance{AltCost, FromZone}` record of the SPELL that became the permanent, stamped at the one place a spell becomes one and cleared by `MoveCard` on the way off the battlefield (CR 400.7). `Card.NamedTribe`'s lifecycle exactly: per-entry, carried by clone and snapshot, classified in `snapshot_drift_test.go`. Readers are `ctx.Escaped()` and `ctx.CastProvenance()`, and which OBJECT they ask about is the point — the SOURCE PERMANENT, not the item being resolved, because by the time "sacrifice it unless it escaped" resolves the item on the stack is that trigger and the spell finished two steps ago (`ctx.PaidAltCost`, what an overloaded Cyclonic Rift reads, is the wrong question here). **One place, and it had to be made one**: stack resolution kept its own inline copy of the battlefield push while the RESUME of the same event went through #919's `executeEntryToBattlefieldLocked`; the inline copy is gone and the stamp is one line in the finisher, before `EventETB` so the entry trigger already sees it. **Cards:** Phlage, Titan of Fire's Fury, Uro, Titan of Nature's Wrath (its other blocker, "put a land card from your hand onto the battlefield", shipped with #654) and Pharika's Spawn, all Full; the shared clause is `effects.SacrificeThisUnlessItEscaped`. Still waiting, each on something that is not provenance: **Kroxa** on "each opponent who didn't discard a nonland card this way" (a discarded-card report, #650 — only an event-log scan exists); **Tizerus Charger** on "escapes with your choice of a +1/+1 counter or a flying counter", which is a CHOICE the fixed `EntersWithCounterName` pair cannot express AND a keyword counter the engine does not read; **Skyway Robber** on "escapes with [an ability]" (CR 702.138d) plus "cast from among cards exiled with this creature". The record is the growth point for all three and for #664's "if it was kicked": a sibling field on `CastProvenance`, not a second record.
