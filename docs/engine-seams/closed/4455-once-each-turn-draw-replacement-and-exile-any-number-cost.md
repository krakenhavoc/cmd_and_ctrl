**A once-each-turn draw replacement, and an exile-any-number cost with a mana-symbol floor** (#4455, CR 614.1a, 614.5, 614.11, 702.142a, 707.12) — two small seams, and the two cards that needed them. **The first time you would draw a card each turn:** `DrawBecomes.FirstTimeEachTurn` makes a draw replacement one-shot per turn per object. Its `Replace` marks the turn (`Game.MarkReplacementAppliedThisTurnForEffect`, stored in `TurnTally.ReplacementsApplied` under `ObjectTallyKey(source, epoch, label)`), its `AppliesTo` reads the mark, and the tally is flushed with the turn, cloned for undo and carried by the snapshot additively. The "except the first card you draw during each of your draw steps" exemption (`ExceptInOwnDrawStep`) is checked first and writes no mark, so the draw step's turn-based draw does not use up the turn's one replacement: a later draw is four, an extra draw in the draw step is four, and on another player's turn the first draw is four. **Cards:** Reed Richards, Smartest Man — Full. **Exile any number of cards with a total:** `ExileCost.AnyNumber` with `ExileCost.SymbolTotal` (`game.ManaSymbolTotal{Color, AtLeast}`, plain data so the clause adds no closure route to `Game`) is a CR 602 ability's cost the activator pays with as many cards as they like from their own graveyard, provided the printed mana costs of the chosen cards hold at least the floor's symbols of the colour (a hybrid, Phyrexian or `{2/B}` symbol that offers the colour counts once, CR 700.5). `validateExileCardsCostLocked` refuses a pick below the floor before anything is paid, the view ships `exile_cost_any_number`, `exile_cost_symbol_color` and `exile_cost_symbol_min` and greys the row while the graveyard cannot reach the floor, the client picker (`DiscardCostModal`'s variable mode) shows the running total, and the bot pays the cheapest fuel first until the floor is reached. `effects.Register` refuses the clause on a cast or a mana ability. **Up to N of the copies:** `CopyAndCast.AtMostCasts` (`CardCopyOffer.MaxCasts`) makes the copies one pass-closed `AnyNumber` permission with `CastsLeft = N`; each cast strikes its own copy and spends one, and the Nth closes the permission, so the copies not cast cease to exist (CR 707.12a). **Cards:** Baron Helmut Zemo — Full.
