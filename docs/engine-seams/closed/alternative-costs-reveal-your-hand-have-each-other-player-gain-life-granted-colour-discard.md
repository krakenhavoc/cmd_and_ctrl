**Alternative costs: reveal your hand, have each other player gain life, and a granted colour-matched discard** (#6214, CR 118.9, 601.2f) — three additions to `game.AlternativeCost`. `RevealHand` is Land Grant's whole payment (the hand shown to every seat as the cost is paid, with the spell already on the stack; the "if you have no land cards in hand" gate is the card's own `Condition`). `EachOtherPlayerGainsLife` is Reverent Silence's 6 and Skyshroud Cutter's 5, paid as a cost through `gainLifeAsCostLocked` (the path a splice cost's gain uses) for every other seat still in the game. `DiscardSharesColorWithSpell` narrows a `DiscardFromHand` component to a card sharing a colour with the SPELL being cast, read wherever the spell is as the cost is offered or paid, the way conspire reads its source; it is the one card component a granted offer may now carry besides emerge's, web-slinging's and collect evidence's, which is Dream Halls' "rather than pay the mana cost for a spell, its controller may discard a card that shares a color with that spell" (a `GrantedAlternativeCost` with `EveryPlayer`). No snapshot change: the offers are derived, and a claim rides the existing `StackItem.AltCost` key. **Cards:** Land Grant (OLD b689), Reverent Silence (b679), Skyshroud Cutter (b680) and Dream Halls (b750) wait for their OLD batches.
