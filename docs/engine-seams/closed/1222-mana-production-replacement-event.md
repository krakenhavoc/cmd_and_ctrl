**Mana-production replacement event** (#1222, CR 106.12b, [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-replacement-effects.md) §5ab, [ADR 0061 note 2026-09-22](decisions/0061-token-creation-and-discard-are-replaceable-events.md)) — *"If you tap a permanent for mana, it produces twice as much of that mana instead."* The mana PIPELINE was never the gap: since #763 every mana in the game arrives through one of four sites, each already knowing the colour, the restrictions and the player. What there was no event for is the AMOUNT, and ADR 0013 §13 is where it was last looked at — S17 sub-PR 6 named `RepEventManaProduced` a "seventh integration point outside the core scope" and deferred it. **`RepEventProduceMana`** is opened once per production, before any of it is in the pool, on the shape ADR 0061 decision 1 built for a token creation and #1003 reused for `RepEventMill`. It carries **COLOURS rather than a count** (`ManaColors []string`, one entry per mana) because the cards say "twice as much of THAT mana" — a doubled Forest is {G}{G}, a doubled Sol Ring {C}{C}{C}{C} — with `MultiplyMana(n)` as the whole arithmetic, a method rather than a card-side loop so a card cannot quietly rewrite the COLOURS, which CR 106.12b does not license. It also carries **`ManaFromTap`**, the printed condition (CR 106.12a) and the same bit `PendingChoice.ManaTapped` carries for ADR 0074 §3's triggers: a spell's "Add {B}{B}{B}", an untapped mana ability and a triggered mana ability's own output (Wild Growth's extra {G}, which comes from the Aura) are productions and none of them is a tap. **One body, four sites**: `produceManaLocked` (`game/produce_mana.go`) is now the only place in the engine that mints a `ManaToken` and emits `EventManaAdded` — `ActivateManaAbility`'s printed slot, `ResolveManaChoice`'s answered PICK, `materializePlanLocked`'s own taps, and `addManaSlotsLocked` for a spell and for #763's triggers. The pick is the interesting one: a pipe slot has no settled colour at the activation, so the window opens when the pick is ANSWERED — the only moment a Birds of Paradise's colour exists, which is ADR 0074 §3's reason for firing the triggers there — and the consequence is what the card wants, a doubled Birds being ONE pick minting two of the chosen colour and therefore unable to pay {W}{U}. **It can never pause**, and that is a rule rather than a cost argument: CR 605.3b makes activating a mana ability one indivisible step with no priority window inside it, and the auto-tapper's contract is "no further player decisions", so every event sets `mustSettleNow` and the CR 616.1 ordering is applied in gather order — unobservable for both printed cards, since ×2 then ×3 is ×6 either way. That buys **no tail**: the three switches naming what a kind owes on a pause, a cancellation or a dropped prompt all say "nothing, and here is why". **The auto-tap PLANNER prices through the same predicate** (`producedManaPreviewLocked` walks the same gathered `AppliesTo`/`Replace` pairs against a scratch event, read-only with its own applied set, because `gatherTapSources` runs under a read lock): a Mana-Reflected land pays {G}{G} on its own, and `priceProducedSlotsLocked` says so in the grammar the planner already has — a doubled single-option slot becomes two slots, a doubled multi-option slot becomes #779's "N mana of any one color" pick. Both halves are gated on `producesManaReplacementsExistLocked`, one battlefield walk, because on ~every board there is no such card. **Cards: Mana Reflection and Nyxbloom Ancient**, both `full`, one clause each (`cards/effects/mana_replacements.go`). **Still open**: a replacement that rewrites the COLOURS rather than the amount (Mycosynth Lattice's "no land's mana ability adds non-colorless"), which decision 1 deliberately does not offer the catalog; and Zhur-Taa Ancient, which reads like this family and is not — "whenever a player taps a land for mana, that player adds one mana of any type that land produced" is a TRIGGERED mana ability (ADR 0074), not a replacement.
