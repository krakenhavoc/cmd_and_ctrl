#4728 · combat arrows · option B

Combat tags

No lines at all. Every combat fact is a word on the card it is about: above an attacker, where it is going (the defender's seat dot and name); below it, what happened (a shield and the blocker count, or an open path and the damage that gets through). Each blocker says what it is blocking. Each defender gets a tally next to their avatar: how many are coming and how much gets through. Pointing at any tagged card outlines the whole pairing and dims the rest.

Scene: your turn, blockers declared. Grizzly Bears attacks Luke and is blocked by Wall of Omens and Llanowar Elves. Serra Angel attacks Mara, unblocked. Goblin Guide attacks Rin's planeswalker Teferi, unblocked. Card faces are placeholders for Scryfall art.

Who is attacked

Read straight off the attacker (→ ● LUKE) and off the defender (⚔ 1 · −4). A planeswalker is named (→ ● TEFERI, dot in Rin's colour) and carries its own tally.

Blocks, several blockers

Each blocker reads Blocking Grizzly Bears; the attacker reads ×2; Luke's tally reads held. Nothing to trace, but a pairing across the table is only found by reading, or by pointing.

Colour-blind safety

The strongest: every signal is a word or an icon, and the seat colour always sits beside a name. The tags are real text, so a screen reader can have them too, which an SVG arrow never gives.

Phone and 820px

The weakest. Tags are wider than a card, so they need a compact form (icon and initial) on a small table, and at 820px the attacker row's tags nearly touch. Six attackers in one row would collide.

Fixed table layout

Every mark is measured from the DOM anchors the table already has (data-instance-id, data-seat-id, via lib/boardAnchor.ts), so it follows reflow, a rotated or expanded board (ADR 0120) and an avatar stand-in (ADR 0077) the way today's arrows do. Nothing sits under an avatar or over the action dock (ADR 0111, ADR 0204).

Tags sit above and below tiles, so the row gaps in Board.svelte must leave room; an expanded board and the reflow's avatar cards need their own placement.

Cost to build

Tier 3. A new tag layer in board/Card.svelte (or a new board/CombatTags.svelte overlay), a tally in board/PlayerIdentity.svelte and on planeswalkers, new contract labels in lib/labels.ts (with e2e and *.hint.ts updates), a shield glyph in lib/icons.ts, and row spacing in Board.svelte. Removing the arrows also removes what the ADR 0053 damage beats pulse along and what #4327's staged intent draws, so both need a new form and an ADR 0053 amendment.
