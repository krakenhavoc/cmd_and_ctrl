## Context

### The rules

From the pinned Comprehensive Rules (`MagicCompRules 20260925.txt`):

- **CR 701.42a:** "To meld the two cards in a meld pair, put them onto the battlefield with their back faces up and combined. The resulting permanent is a single object represented by two cards." **701.42b:** only the two cards of one meld pair can be melded; tokens and other cards can't. **701.42c:** objects that can't be melded "stay in their current zone".
- **CR 712.4a–c:** one card of each pair has the ability that exiles both and melds them. The back faces are used only to determine the melded permanent's characteristics (712.4b). Meld cards can't transform (712.4c). **712.5** lists the seven pairs.
- **CR 712.8g:** a melded permanent has only the combined back face's characteristics, and its mana value is the sum of its front faces'. A copy of one has mana value 0 (also 202.3c).
- **CR 712.14c:** melded cards enter as a single permanent with their back faces up. **712.16:** melded permanents can't be turned face down.
- **CR 712.21:** when a melded permanent leaves the battlefield, one permanent leaves and two cards go to the new zone ("whenever a creature dies" triggers once, "whenever a card is put into a graveyard from anywhere" twice). **712.21a:** in a graveyard or library its owner arranges the two cards in any order. **712.21c:** an effect that can find the new object finds both cards and does the same to each. **712.21d / 903.9c:** a melded commander sent to the command zone takes only its commander card there; the other card goes to "the appropriate zone".
- **CR 903.3b:** if a commander is melded, the melded permanent is that player's commander.

### What the engine had

`game.Card` is one card, and nearly every reader keys on `Card.InstanceID`. ADR 0079 left meld out for exactly that reason ("Two cards becoming one object is a different representation problem"). The deck importer copied Scryfall's `layout: "meld"` and nothing else: Scryfall ships each meld card as an ordinary single-faced record, and the combined back face as a separate record with its own oracle ID ("Urza, Planeswalker"), joined to the halves only through each record's `all_parts` list (`meld_part`, `meld_result`). The index dropped `all_parts`. `deck.Validate` warned "imports as its front face only; melding isn't implemented yet".

## Decisions

### 1. A melded permanent is one `Card`, and its two cards ride inside it

The melded permanent is a single `game.Card` whose printed fields — name, type line, colours, power and toughness, loyalty, keywords — are the combined back face's, and whose **`OracleID` and `ScryfallID` are the back face's own Scryfall record**. Its two cards are `Card.MeldedFrom []Card`, as they sat in exile when they were melded.

This is the whole of the design, and the reason is that everything else then needs nothing:

- **The catalog** keys on `CatalogKey`, which is the oracle ID, so the back face's own `Spec` (Urza, Planeswalker's loyalty abilities) is the melded permanent's, with no `#face` key and no alias.
- **The image** is `/cards/{scryfall_id}/image` of the back face's printing.
- **The coverage flag** (`NeedsEffect`, the `unimplemented` badge) is the back face's, stamped at import (decision 2).
- **The layer engine** reads the printed fields like any card's.
- **A copy effect** reads `PrintedValues`, which has no slot for `MeldedFrom` or `Meld`, so a copy of a melded permanent is the back face and nothing else. Its printed mana cost is the back face's none, so its mana value is 0, which is CR 712.8g's second sentence with no code.

The alternatives were worse. Two cards on the battlefield with a link between them would make every battlefield walk, every count of permanents and every "this permanent" reader learn that two cards may be one object. Putting the back face in `Faces` (as transform does) would key the catalog as `<front oracle>#1`, which would need the back face registered under both halves' keys, and would leave the image and the coverage flag pointing at the front.

`Card.Layout` is `meld` on the melded permanent and on each meld card; `Faces` stays nil on both.

### 2. The deck importer stamps the combined back face on both halves

`cards.Card` gains `AllParts`, and `Index.Load` keeps only its meld entries (dropping every token part, which most cards have) and then attaches the back face's record to each meld front (`Card.MeldResult`, not serialised). `deck.toGameCard` turns that into `game.Card.Meld *MeldPrint`: the back face's oracle and printing IDs, its printed characteristics as a `game.Face`, its `NeedsEffect` and its produced mana. Both halves carry it, so either can be the one whose ability fires.

`Meld` is the CARD's, never the object's: nothing writes it after import, and `PrintedValues` has no slot for it, so a Clone copying Urza, Lord Protector is not a meld card (CR 701.42b). `CanMeld(a, b)` is two meld cards, neither a token nor melded, with different oracle IDs and the same back face.

A meld card whose back face the index does not hold imports as an ordinary card, and its meld ability finds no pair and does nothing. That is the weaker direction, which is the allowed one.

### 3. The verb: `Game.MeldForEffect`, and the printed condition on the card

Every printed meld ability is one sentence with the same tail: "If you both own and control <this> and a <type> named <partner>, exile them, then meld them into <back face>." The head is printed text and varies (Urza's is an activated ability, Graf Rats' a beginning-of-combat trigger, Mishra, Claimed by Gix's wants both attacking), so the card writes it:

- `effects.MeldWith{Partner, PartnerType}` checks the condition at resolution — the source on the battlefield owned and controlled by the ability's controller, and a permanent of that type with that name owned and controlled by them too — and calls the verb. With either missing nothing happens, so a meld ability whose partner died in response does not exile its own source. The partner is found by NAME, which is what the card says; a real meld card is preferred when several qualify.
- `effects.YouOwnAndControlThisAndANamed` is the same condition for a trigger's intervening "if" (CR 603.4).

`Game.MeldForEffect(source, partner, controller)`:

1. exiles both as one simultaneous exit (`ExileCardsThenForEffect`), so a commander is exiled like any card and the rest waits on any prompt;
2. if both reached exile and `CanMeld`, swaps them in exile for the melded object (`meldedPermanentOf`) and returns it through the ordinary exile entry (`ReturnFromExileToBattlefieldThenForEffect`), which mints the new object (CR 400.7), runs the CR 614 pipeline (a replacement that taps or redirects an entering permanent applies) and fires its enters triggers; a planeswalker back face gets its loyalty (CR 306.5b);
3. otherwise leaves them in exile (CR 701.42c): a Clone named Urza is exiled with the Mightstone and both stay there.

The melded object sits in exile for the width of one locked mutation, the same trade ADR 0079 decision 5 made for "return it transformed". An entry that does not happen (cancelled or redirected) turns it back into two cards in exile (`unmeldInExileLocked`), so a melded object is never left anywhere but the battlefield. `EventMeld` is emitted after the entry with the two cards' names, and the log narrates it (`meld`): "Urza, Lord Protector and The Mightstone and Weakstone melded into Urza, Planeswalker".

### 4. Leaving the battlefield: `MoveCard` splits it

Every zone change comes down to `MoveCard`, so that is where CR 712.21 lives: a melded object that lands anywhere but the battlefield or the stack is pushed as its two cards (`splitMeldedForMove`). The **carrier** — the first card of `MeldedFrom` — takes the melded object's `InstanceID`. That one choice makes every existing caller right:

- a caller that moved "the card with this ID" and then looks it up in the destination finds a real card there;
- the move emits one zone move and one leaves-the-battlefield event naming one object, which is CR 712.21's "one permanent leaves the battlefield", so "whenever a creature dies" fires once and reads the melded permanent's last-known information;
- the other card keeps its own ID and gets its own zone-move event from the two exits that can carry a melded permanent (`executeZoneRouteLocked` and `executeBattlefieldLeaveLocked`, through `landMeldPassengersLocked`), so "whenever a card is put into a graveyard from anywhere" sees two cards. Its knowledge set and its place in a library (top, bottom or a depth) follow the carrier's.

**The order (CR 712.21a).** When both land in their owner's graveyard or library, the owner is asked which is above the other: an `option_pick` with a keyed continuation (`meld/order`, `RegisterOptionPickThen`), so a table waiting on the answer is still a restore point. A library that is about to be shuffled is not asked.

**Finding both (CR 712.21c).** The carrier remembers the other card (`Card.MeldSplitFrom`, an `ObjectRef`) until its next move. An exile return of the carrier (`returnFromExileToBattlefieldLocked`, which every blink and "return the exiled card" uses) returns that card too, as its own permanent. Otherworldly Journey's ruling example is exactly this.

**The commander (CR 903.3b, 903.9c).** The carrier is the commander card when either card is one, and the melded object is the commander. Dying or being exiled, both cards land and CR 903.9a's question is asked of the commander card alone (ADR 0115) with no special case. Bounced or tucked, the CR 903.9b offer redirects the move to the command zone; `MoveCard` puts both there and `sendMeldPassengersOnLocked` sends the card that is not a commander on to the hand or library the move was going to.

### 5. The other rules that read a melded permanent

- **Mana value (CR 712.8g, 202.3c):** `manaCostForValue` reads the cards' front-face costs concatenated: Urza, Planeswalker is 8, Hanweir, the Writhing Township is 3.
- **Face down (CR 712.16):** `CanTurnFaceDown` refuses a melded permanent.
- **Transform (CR 712.4c):** already refused by ADR 0079's layout allowlist; no change.
- **Snapshots:** `Meld`, `MeldedFrom` (recursive `cardSnapshot`) and `MeldSplitFrom` are additive carried fields of schema v7. `cloneCard` deep-copies the cards. The closure ratchet classifies `Card.MeldedFrom` as `rebuilt`: a melded permanent's cards are never tokens, so they carry no card-instance abilities, and `meldedPermanentOf` clears those slices to keep it so.

### 6. The wire and the client

`CardView.melded_from` is a melded permanent's two cards (name, type, cost, P/T and each card's own image) and `CardView.melds_into` a meld card's combined back face. Both are cleared on the non-knower redaction with `faces`. The board needs nothing: the melded permanent's `scryfall_id` is the back face's printing, so its tile shows the combined art. The hover panel shows the two cards (or the back face) as insets in the corner the other face uses, and its footer says "melded from Urza, Lord Protector + The Mightstone and Weakstone".

### 7. Deck import and validation

`meld` leaves `layoutSimplifications`: a meld card imports as itself with its back face stamped and melds when its ability resolves. A combined back face listed as a card — in the 99 or as a commander — is a fatal `meld_back_face` violation naming the two cards to add instead (CR 712.4b: it is half of two oversized card backs, never a card). Scryfall marks it commander-legal, so without this a Brisela, Voice of Nightmares commander would have validated.

### 8. What Urza, Planeswalker needed

Two small engine pieces, each the first card to print its shape:

- **"You may activate the loyalty abilities of Urza twice each turn rather than only once."** `Spec.LoyaltyTwiceEachTurn`, read through `CatalogAbilityKey`, and `Game.LoyaltyActivatedTwiceThisTurn` beside CR 606.3's existing map, flushed and forgotten with it. `Game.LoyaltySpentLocked` is the one predicate the activation gate, the sandbox verb, the legal-move enumerator and the wire's `loyalty_activated` all ask.
- **"Artifact, instant, and sorcery spells you cast this turn cost {2} less to cast."** `NextSpellPromise.EveryMatchingSpell` (a promise that is never spent and ends with its duration) and `PermissionFilter.ArtifactInstantOrSorceryOnly`.

## Out of scope

1. **The other five pairs.** Graf Rats and Midnight Scavengers, Bruna and Gisela, Argoth and Titania, and Fang and Vanille need no more engine than this ADR; their cards are not in the catalog yet and are card work. Mishra, Claimed by Gix's "It enters tapped and attacking" needs `MeldWith` to carry an entry that attacks, which no card has asked for.
2. **CR 712.21b**, choosing the two cards' relative timestamps when a melded permanent is exiled: the other card is always the later. Nothing in the catalog reads the order of cards in exile (Duplicant's "last creature card exiled" is the ruling's example and is not a catalog card).
3. **CR 712.21c beyond exile returns.** A "return that card to its owner's hand" from a graveyard, or any other follow-up that finds the moved object outside the exile return, finds the carrier only. `MeldSplitFrom` is the hook such a follow-up would read.
4. **A melded permanent exiled face down.** The carrier lands face down and the other card face up; no catalog card exiles a permanent face down.

## Consequences

**Cards.** Urza, Lord Protector, Urza, Planeswalker, Hanweir Battlements and Hanweir, the Writhing Township ship Full; The Mightstone and Weakstone and Hanweir Garrison lose their meld caveats.

**Cost.** Three `Card` fields, one `Game` map, one event and log kind, a keyed option-pick continuation, two wire fields, and the importer's `all_parts`. No schema bump.

**The thing most likely to be got wrong later.** Reaching into `Card.MeldedFrom` to find "the cards on the battlefield". They are not on the battlefield; the melded permanent is, and it is one object. A card that counts permanents counts it once, a card that counts cards that moved counts two (CR 712.21e), and only `MoveCard` turns one into the other.
