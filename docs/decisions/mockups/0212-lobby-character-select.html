  • The ready-check moment, as a still: the last seat's check lands (here seat 3's tile has a brief ring), the counter reaches 4/4 and the primary turns from Waiting for 1 player to Start game with one soft ring. With reduced motion (any of the three signals in DESIGN.md §4) the checks and the counter simply appear, and the live-region line says the same thing.
  • "Ready" is a check shape plus the word, on a mint disc (--mint, success). It is never --ready, which means "a legal action now" on the board. Seat 4's green equals mint, which is why the word is always printed.
  • A seat who doesn't host sees the same seats with Waiting for Luke to start as a disabled primary (DESIGN.md: disabled, not hidden).
  • Two or three seated players can start too, as today (2 to 4 seats): the primary then reads Start game · 3 players and the open seat is drawn as "closes at start". Whether that needs a confirm is an open question in the ADR.
