# Engine seams

A seam is an engine gap a card in the catalog (`server/internal/cards/effects`)
is waiting on: an event kind, a cost component, a prompt shape, or a
primitive that `server/internal/game` does not yet provide. A seam is not a
bug: bugs found by the roadmap sweep (issues #446, #478, #482, #489, #492)
are tracked and fixed on their own, separately from this list.

This file is the seam registry for the card-coverage roadmap (Discussion #559 item 6, issue #580). It replaces
the seam lists that used to live only in agent prompts and in scattered
batch-issue comments. The rule going forward: every batch PR appends its
own skipped cards to the table below, merging into an existing row when the
seam is the same one already listed, and a seam moves from Open to Closed
on the day the engine PR that closes it lands.

The table began as every seam reported against **two or more** waiting
cards in the first pass over the first 3600 cards (batches 01-36, issues
#294-#313 and #383-#399); the rows added from the 2026-09-16 audit (below)
reach batch #467, and a few of them start with fewer recorded skips. A seam that blocks exactly one card today is
noted on that card's own batch issue rather than duplicated here; it will
earn a row here the moment a second card waits on it.

**Count** is the number of cards that batch issues and batch PRs have
recorded as skipped for the seam: the cards named in the row plus its
"+N more" (on a row added from the audit, only the cards before "audit
adds:"). It changes only when a batch PR appends a skip or a skip
moves to the row it really belongs in, so it undercounts a seam that
other batches hit without writing the skip here.

**Audit** comes from the card-coverage audit of 2026-09-16, which
classified every gap card on the 60 batch issues (#294-#313, #383-#403,
#448-#467; 5,084 cards). It gives the number of cards where the seam is
the *only* core blocker, then the number where it is *any* core blocker.
It is a classifier's attribution, not a skip list, and it is never added
to Count. For each seam the verifiers sampled the only-blocker cards and
found between about 5% and 85% of them also blocked by something else or
misattributed, so read it as an upper bound. For a row tracked by one of #742-#765, that
issue has the verified estimate and the cards that were checked. A figure marked †
was not verified against the code. Rows outside the audit's top 40 seams
are left blank, except stack-item retarget, whose undercount the audit
called out.

The rows from "Transform a permanent / enter transformed" to the end of
the table were added from that audit (2026-09-17). Their Count is **—**
unless skips already recorded in this file moved into them (Station and
library-top cast), and their Cards waiting column lists cards the audit's
verifiers checked, each with its batch issue. They follow the same rule
as every other row: a batch PR appends its skips, and the row moves to
Closed when the engine PR lands.

## Closed seams

- **Activation condition on a non-mana activated ability** (#743) - `ActivatedAbilityShape.Condition` / `effects.ActivatedAbility.Condition`, the same `func(g, controller, source) bool` mana abilities carry ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) activation-condition addendum). Checked once in `ActivateCatalogAbility` right after the timing check, before X, targets or any cost (`ErrConditionNotMet`, nothing paid), never at resolution (CR 602.1b), and mirrored in the legal enumerator. `ActivatedAbilityView` and `ManaAbilityView` both carry `condition_unmet`, and the client greys the row. Builders in `cards/effects/activation_conditions.go`: `DuringYourTurn`, `OpponentControlsAtLeast`, `OpponentControlsMore`, `GraveyardAtLeast`, `NoCardsInHand`, `SourceHasCountersAtLeast`, `LifeAtLeastAboveStarting`, beside the existing `ControlsAtLeast`. Sanctum of Eternity lost its sorcery-speed caveat. Shipped on Tectonic Edge, Weathered Wayfarer, Bonders' Enclave, Cryptic Caves, Endless Atlas, Kelpie Guide, Barbarian Ring, Cephalid Coliseum, Rivendell, Sea Gate Wreckage, Minas Tirith, Idol of Oblivion, Speaker of the Heavens, Luminarch Ascension, Sliver Hive, Inventors' Fair, Leechridden Swamp, Mistveil Plains, Lagomos, Hand of Hatred, Hall of Oracles, The Seedcore, Goro-Goro, Disciple of Ryusei, Lilypad Village and Arch of Orazca (the city's blessing read live, with Tendershoot Dryad's caveat). Still waiting on other rows: Kitsa, Otterball Elite (prowess, #706), Glint-Horn Buccaneer (discard-a-card cost, #660), Barad-dûr (amass), Varragoth, Bloodsky Sire and Broadside Bombardiers (per-source activation count), Hydra Broodmaster and Alpha Deathclaw (monstrosity), Metallurgic Summonings (exile-this cost), Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim (reading the sacrificed creature), and Desert (the engine clears combat as the end of combat step begins, so no creature is attacking in the only step Desert can be activated; CR 511.3 keeps them in combat until the step ends, #785). - **Sacrifice cost of more than one permanent** (#747) - a sacrifice clause's count is its `TargetSpec`'s `Min == Max`, built with `SacrificeN(n, label, preds…)` for an activated or mana ability (`.SacrificeOther` for the mana one) and `SacrificeNCost(n, label, preds…)` for an additional cost to cast ([ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) and [ADR 0021](decisions/0021-additional-costs.md) addenda). One validator serves all three sites: exactly N distinct permanents the payer controls, each matching the clause, never the source when the cost also sacrifices it. One payment leaves as one simultaneous exit, so a dies-watcher paid in with the rest sees every death. The enumerator offers one payment per move for N ≥ 2, tokens first, then lower mana value, then the source last; the view ships `sacrifice_options.min`/`max` as N with the options in that order, and the client's picker is a multi-select that confirms at exactly N with a "Choose for me" button. `effects.Register` refuses variable counts. Shipped on Priest of Forgotten Gods, Kuldotha Forgemaster, Tamiyo's Journal, Teysa, Orzhov Scion, Hedron Detonator, Olivia, Opulent Outlaw, Breya, Etherium Shaper and Whisper, Blood Liturgist, and it cleared the caveats on Savvy Hunter, Samwise Gamgee, Sai, Master Thopterist, Magda, the Hoardmaster and Magda, Brazen Outlaw. Still open: Transmutation Font's "with different names" (the set-level row above) and variable counts (the row above). Not this seam: Liliana, Dreadhorde General (a resolution-time edict), Phyrexian Soulgorger (cumulative upkeep, #567), Jolene, the Plunder Queen (a token-creation replacement), Nemata, Primeval Warden (a reflexive "when you do" trigger off a replacement) and Ulamog, the Defiler (ward's sacrifice cost, `WardCost.Sacrifice`, is a separate shape). Unblocked by the cost and still to be written, each with another gap the audit named: Pippin, Warden of Isengard (#448, partner with) and Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus (#303).
- **A spell's own cost modifier** (#746) - `Spec.SelfCostModifiers`, read through `CardDef` by `game.SelfCostModifiersFor(card)` for the card being priced and never from the battlefield ([ADR 0048](decisions/0048-cost-modification.md) addendum). Self modifiers join the board's increase, reduction and floor passes (CR 601.2f), bound to the card as cast with the caster as its controller, so a reduction still stops at generic mana; they apply from any zone the spell is cast from (CR 113.6d), to an alternative cost (CR 118.9d), and for the face being cast. `CostQuery.Targets` carries the announced targets, shown only to a modifier that sets `ReadsTargets`; the legal-move enumerator prices each target set on its own when `CastPriceReadsTargetsForEffect` says something reads targets, and prices once up front otherwise. `CostModifier.Unit` lets an increase add coloured mana (strive). Constructors: `AffinityFor` (CR 702.41a, one instance per entry, CR 702.41b), `CostsMorePerTargetBeyondFirst`, `CostsLessIfItTargets`, plus counting amounts and conditions in `effects/self_cost_modifier.go`. The X picker prices a target-priced spell at one target and quotes the clause from `CardView.target_cost_notes`. Shipped on Thought Monitor, Myr Enforcer, Thoughtcast, Voyage Home, Ghalta, Primal Hunger, Vanquish the Horde, Fireball, Call the Coppercoats, Winged Words, Wizard's Retort, Into the Story, Hour of Revelation, Mortality Spear, Curtains' Call, Price of Fame, Shadow of Mortality, Witherbloom, the Balancer, Mycosynth Golem, Hamza, Guardian of Arashin, Ancient Stone Idol and Hagra Mauling; Blasphemous Act dropped its caveat. Unblocked and still to be written: Not of This World (#385), Sapling Nursery (#450), Thrumming Hivepool (#455), Diamond Weapon (#457), and the audit's clause-only cards (The Great Henge, Embercleave, Excalibur, Sword of Eden, Rumbleweed, The Magic Mirror, among others), each of which still needs its own check for other gaps.
- **Reflexive triggers, "when you do" (CR 603.12)** (#636) - a trigger CREATED by a resolving effect, written `ReflexiveTrigger{Label, Targets, Optional, Cards, Effect}.Apply(ctx)` (or `WhenYouDo(label, effect)` for the plain case) from inside the parent ability's `Effect`, on the branch where the condition actually held. The payload rides `StackItem.Payload` (`ctx.PayloadCards()`), which is what keeps the `Effect` capture-free across `Clone` / undo.
