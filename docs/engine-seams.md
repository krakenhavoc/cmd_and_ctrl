# Engine seams

A seam is an engine gap a card in the catalog (`server/internal/cards/effects`)
is waiting on: an event kind, a cost component, a prompt shape, or a
primitive that `server/internal/game` does not yet provide. A seam is not a
bug: bugs found by the roadmap sweep (issues #446, #478, #482, #489, #492)
are tracked and fixed on their own, separately from this list.

This file is the seam registry for the card-coverage roadmap (Discussion #559 item 6, issue #580). It replaces
the seam lists that used to live only in agent prompts and in scattered
batch-issue comments. The rule going forward: every batch PR appends its
own skipped cards to the table below, merging into an existing row when the
seam is the same one already listed, and a seam moves from Open to Closed
on the day the engine PR that closes it lands.

The table began as every seam reported against **two or more** waiting
cards in the first pass over the first 3600 cards (batches 01-36, issues
#294-#313 and #383-#399); the rows added from the 2026-09-16 audit (below)
reach batch #467, and a few of them start with fewer recorded skips. A seam that blocks exactly one card today is
noted on that card's own batch issue rather than duplicated here; it will
earn a row here the moment a second card waits on it.

**Count** is the number of cards that batch issues and batch PRs have
recorded as skipped for the seam: the cards named in the row plus its
"+N more" (on a row added from the audit, only the cards before "audit
adds:"). It changes only when a batch PR appends a skip or a skip
moves to the row it really belongs in, so it undercounts a seam that
other batches hit without writing the skip here.

**Audit** comes from the card-coverage audit of 2026-09-16, which
classified every gap card on the 60 batch issues (#294-#313, #383-#403,
#448-#467; 5,084 cards). It gives the number of cards where the seam is
the *only* core blocker, then the number where it is *any* core blocker.
It is a classifier's attribution, not a skip list, and it is never added
to Count. For each seam the verifiers sampled the only-blocker cards and
found between about 5% and 85% of them also blocked by something else or
misattributed, so read it as an upper bound. For a row tracked by one of #742-#765, that
issue has the verified estimate and the cards that were checked. A figure marked †
was not verified against the code. Rows outside the audit's top 40 seams
are left blank, except stack-item retarget, whose undercount the audit
called out.

The rows from "Transform a permanent / enter transformed" to the end of
the table were added from that audit (2026-09-17). Their Count is **—**
unless skips already recorded in this file moved into them (Station and
library-top cast), and their Cards waiting column lists cards the audit's
verifiers checked, each with its batch issue. They follow the same rule
as every other row: a batch PR appends its skips, and the row moves to
Closed when the engine PR lands.

## Closed seams

- **Layer dependency ordering (CR 613.8) and effects that outlive their source (CR 613.6)** (#668 / #669 / #670, [ADR 0067](decisions/0067-layer-dependency-ordering.md)): layer 4 is ordered by CR 613.8 dependency, detected by TRIAL APPLICATION against a fixed base rather than by a declared read/write set on every static, with the CR 613.8c re-check after each application and a timestamp fallback on a loop (`game/layer_dependency.go`). All five catalogued pairs come out the same whichever card entered first (Urborg + Song of the Dryads, Urborg + a slumbering Arixmethes, Maskwood Nexus + a crewed Vehicle, + The Warring Triad, + Arixmethes), and Urborg + a Song on a permanent that was ALREADY a land stays independent. An ability removal is applied in the layer it happens in and reaches forwards only, so a silenced source still applied in the earlier layers (Magus of the Moon's 2021-03-19 ruling) and a multi-layer effect carries on through `ContinuesAfterRemoval` (CR 613.6, Humility + Opalescence); the S24 ability-removal fixed point is gone and the recompute is one pass. CR 305.7's loss moved into layer 4 (`effects.SetsBasicLandType`), so a grant that lands in layer 6 survives it. "Is every creature type" is a layer-4 fact on the Characteristic, not the changeling keyword (#670). CR 704.5p joined `attachmentLegalLocked` as the enabling rule (#675's SBA half). **Cards:** Magus of the Moon ships; Urborg, Song of the Dryads and Arixmethes are `CompletenessFull`; Maskwood Nexus and The Warring Triad lost their dependency caveats. Arcane Adaptation, Leyline of Transformation, Encroaching Mycosynth, Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth and Prismatic Omen are unblocked and still need their own files. Adding a dependency-ordered bucket other than layer 4 needs a catalogued pair and a benchmark — every layer costs 14x on a saturated board.

- **The rest of counter costs** (#789, [ADR 0020](decisions/0020-activated-abilities.md) addendum 2026-09-18) - one `game.CounterRemovalCost` with TWO owners: `AbilityCost.RemoveCounters` and, new, `ManaAbilityShape.RemoveCounters`. It covers five printed shapes — from the source, from one other permanent you control, of any kind, a count the activator announces (`RemoveCountersXFromThis`, reaching the effect through `StackItem.Paid.CountersRemoved` and `ManaAbilityShape.ProducedForPaid`), and a removal split across permanents (`RemoveCountersAmong`, validated as a set like a crew payment). `AbilityCost.AddCounter` is the other direction, Devoted Druid's "put a -1/-1 counter on this creature": paid at announce, not replaceable (CR 121.1), and refused by one `canPlaceCounterLocked` predicate (CR 118.3). The auto-tapper plans a counter-cost mana source only when it can both decide and afford the cost, so a Vivid land out of charge counters is not a five-colour source. Shipped on Vivid Creek, Vivid Grove, Ramos Dragon Engine, Mage-Ring Network, Devoted Druid, Hopeful Initiate and Iron Spider (whose caveat is cleared). The counter-cost row above is still open for ONE shape: an any-kind removal split across permanents (Tekuthal).

- **Mana-spent-to-cast record on the stack item** (#761, [ADR 0068](decisions/0068-the-mana-spent-on-a-spell.md)) - `StackItem.Paid` is a `game.PaidCost{Mana, OnPaper, CountersRemoved, CountersAdded, LifePaid}`: `attemptSpend` and `SpendManaFor` return the tokens they spent, and every payment path records them. A permissive or `ForceCast` cast records `OnPaper` — "the engine waived this charge" — rather than an empty record, so "if no mana was spent" is never ambiguous, and every reader takes unknown as the weaker-than-printed answer. A copy of a spell records a real zero (CR 707.10). The solver gained one strategy switch, `SpendDistinctColors`, thrown by `Spec.WantsDistinctColors` and applied to the GENERIC half only, so it can never change whether a cost is payable. `EventManaSpent` and `StackItemView` carry the amount and the distinct colours. Readers: `ctx.ColorsSpent`, `ColorsSpentCount`, `ManaSpentOfColor`, `NoManaSpent`, `ManaSpentKnown`, `Paid`, and `Game.StackItemPaidForEffect` for a cast trigger. Shipped on Painful Truths (converge), Etched Oracle (sunburst), Slaying Fire (adamant) and Vexing Bauble ("if no mana was spent"). The row above is still open for SPEND RIDERS.
- **Emblems (CR 114)** (#623, [ADR 0064](decisions/0064-emblems.md)): an emblem is a `game.Card` with a synthetic `"emblem:<source key>"` catalog key, living in `Player.Emblems` — a second command-zone slice beside the commander pile. Its statics join the layer pass's existing source gather (`activeStaticAbilitiesLocked` → `emblemContinuousEffectsLocked`) and its triggers join the harvester's existing zone walk (`triggerHarvester.OnEvent` → `harvestFromEmblemsLocked`), so a card author writes an emblem's abilities in exactly the vocabulary a permanent's are written in — one new `Spec.Emblem` slot, `CreateEmblem{}` as the ability's whole effect. The split slice is the enforcement of CR 114: `ZoneRef` is `{Kind, Owner}`, so every cast, move, route and lookup resolves `{command, owner}` to the commander pile and can never name an emblem. Snapshot carries it (schema 1 → 2, so a pre-emblem binary refuses a post-emblem restore point rather than silently dropping them), clone carries it, and CR 800.4a is the only exit. `GameView.seats[i].emblems[]` is public and unredacted; the board shows chips beside the player identity with the printed text on hover. **2 of the row's 4 cards converted**: Elspeth, Sun's Champion (−7, static) and Teferi, Hero of Dominaria (−8, targeted trigger). Wrenn and Six's −7 stays omitted — its emblem's only text is a retrace grant, which needs retrace and #652; its caveat now names #652 rather than emblems. Invasion of New Phyrexia (#626) is no longer blocked on emblems and still waits on its reflexive tap prompt.

- **A discard is a replaceable event with a cause** (#650, [ADR 0061](decisions/0061-token-creation-and-discard-are-replaceable-events.md)): the shared exit primitive opens `RepEventDiscard` rather than an anonymous hand → graveyard move, carrying `DiscardPlayer`, `DiscardCause` (`"effect"` / `"cost"` / `"cleanup"` — the distinction the rules draw, per ADR 0013 §10a, not "voluntary") and the causing `Source`, alongside the move payload a `Replace` rewrites. `EventDiscardCard` still fires wherever the card lands (CR 701.8a defines a discard by the move OUT of the hand), now carrying the cause, and the CR 903.9 built-in watches both kinds so a discarded commander is still offered the command zone. A COST discard still settles without asking (CR 601.2h), so an `Optional` replacement on one is skipped un-applied. Cards declare it through `DiscardBecomes{Dst, Causes, Optional, …}.Build()`. Shipped on **Library of Leng** (1 card, caveat: no ordering choice when one effect saves several cards), which batch 28 (#390) had skipped. Madness (#657) now needs only itself — the event it was waiting on is here. Still open and NOT closed by this: search-to-graveyard (`effect_api.go`) and surveil (`ResolveSurveil`) still bypass the CR 614 window, so **Rest in Peace** stays on #383's skip list; a public "discard these card IDs" primitive (Kroxa, the Obstinate Baloth redirect) is still unwritten.

- **Token creation is a replaceable event, and a token takes the entry pipeline** (#762, [ADR 0061](decisions/0061-token-creation-and-discard-are-replaceable-events.md)): `RepEventCreateTokens` is opened once per creation INSTRUCTION (CR 701.7b) and carries token GROUPS — template, count and entry clause per kind — so a doubler multiplies the counts (`ev.MultiplyTokens`) and Academy Manufactor rewrites the kind set (`ev.ReplaceTokenKindsWhere`). Two copies of one doubler are ×4 with no prompt (#792's identical window); two DIFFERENT effects are a CR 616 ordering prompt the creation's controller answers, and either half can pause — the rest of the batch rides the entry's continuation, and `CreateTokensThenForEffect` is how a caller keeps its "then". Every token the window settles on then enters through `enterBattlefieldThroughPipelineLocked`, #478's one effect-side entry primitive, finished by the same `executeEntryToBattlefieldLocked` a fetched, reanimated or blinked permanent takes — so enters-tapped, enters-with-counters and `fireETBHookLocked` all reach a token, a paused token entry resumes through that frame, and the rest of the batch rides its `entryTail`. Shipped on 4 new cards — **Parallel Lives**, **Anointed Procession**, **Academy Manufactor** and **Mondrak, Glory Dominus** (caveat: its Phyrexian-mana ability) — and cleared **9 catalog caveats**: Doubling Season and Primal Vigor's token halves, the enters-tapped gap on Urabrask the Hidden, Manglehorn and Kinjalli's Sunwing, the enters-with-counters gap on Renata, Called to the Hunt, Dragonstorm Globe and Arwen, Weaver of Hope, and All Will Be One's token clause. Still open and named in #762: a choice INSIDE a creation replacement (Jinnie Fay, Esix, Mirrormind Crown, Moonlit Meditation), "the first time you would create tokens each turn", and CR 111.13's copy-becomes-a-token (#666).

- **A free yes/no for a resolving effect's own controller** (#796, [ADR 0018 amendment 2026-09-18](decisions/0018-triggers-on-the-stack.md)) - `effects.MayChoice{Player, Question, YesLabel, NoLabel, LifeCost, OnYes, OnNo}` (`effects/may_choice.go`): "you may [do X]. If you do, [Y]" asked while an effect RESOLVES, where X is neither a search nor a cost the engine already prompts for. No new prompt kind — it is built on `PendingChoiceConfirm` (#552), which is already a two-way question with card-supplied continuations, addressed by `Chooser`, classified in the gate, enumerated for bots and rendered by the client modal. `Player` defaults to the controller ("you may") and names any seat otherwise, which is the whole of what #568's opponent-facing cards needed. Branches are ctx-taking effects bound to a FRESH `Context` on the game the resolver hands back, so they rewind with an undo like every other continuation. **3 cards converted:** Eden, Seat of the Sanctum (now `CompletenessFull` — one activation, the question after the mill, and the #636 reflexive trigger picking its target after the sacrifice, so a card the mill just binned is a legal pick); Mask of Memory (the optional draw is a real question, and the linked discard happens only on the branch that drew); Crossway Troublemakers (the pay-2-life decision moved from the trigger's CR 603.5 prompt to the ability's resolution, where the card prints it). `OtherThan(id)` joined `effects/targets.go` for Eden's "another".

- **An opponent's non-mana choice at resolution** (#568, same ADR amendment) - `PendingChoiceOptionPick` (`option_pick`, `game/option_pick.go`): "choose one of the following", addressed to ANY seat, answered with `{option_index: N}` (routed by kind — zero is the commonest answer). An option is a label, a declared life cost, and optionally the cards it is about; what it MEANS lives in the frame the queuing effect supplies. **A pile split needs no kind at all** — it is two chained prompts to two different seats (#552): `choose_cards` to the splitter with a floor of zero, then an `option_pick` back to the controller whose two options each carry a pile (`Game.QueuePileSplitForEffect` / `effects.PileSplit`). Legality is enforced at QUEUE time (CR 608.2 "as much as possible"): an option the chooser cannot take is never offered, and the first option must be one that always works, because that is the answer `legal.choiceMoves` marks always-legal. **Redaction is one function**, `protocol.redactChoiceCards`, applied to `Options` and to every option's cards: a non-knower loses the card entirely (dropped, not a back — an instance ID out of a hidden zone is the correlation handle PR #513 closed), and the CHOOSER keeps unknown cards as answerable backs only over their own material or another player's HAND, whose size is public (CR 400.2). So a cross-seat prompt over a library must reveal first (#549), which every printed card of the family does. **5 cards converted:** Fact or Fiction (with a caveat: at more than two seats the splitter is the next player in turn order, not an opponent you choose — that needs the still-open "choose a player" prompt); Torment of Hailfire (X repetitions x each opponent, asked strictly one at a time so a later repetition sees an earlier one's answer); Combustible Gearhulk; Charismatic Conqueror; Painful Quandary. **Still waiting: Chain of Vapor** — its copy leg is "they may copy THIS spell", and `CopySpellForEffect` reads the source off `Game.StackMeta`, which `resolveTopOfStackLocked` deletes before it calls `OnResolve`; a spell cannot copy itself from inside its own resolution until that is addressed. Braids, Arisen Nightmare, Tempt with Bunnies and Druid of Purification are unblocked by this seam and still need their own files and their own gap check.

The sweep runs at the cleanup step, at the turn-began hook and at the top of the layer recompute, and nowhere else. `Duration` is plain data — no closures — so it clones and is ready to persist when #515 makes the ability re-derivable; an optional pin drops an entry when the one object it exists to affect is gone, which is what keeps an indefinite effect from sitting in the snapshot census for the rest of the game. The registry is renamed `Game.ScopedStatics` / `RegisterScopedStaticForEffect` (the census keeps its `turnScopedStatics` wire key). Card-side: `StaticForDuration`, `SnapshotAffected` and the four `Duration…` builders in `cards/effects/durations.go`; `StaticUntilEOT` forwards to them. Shipped on Mass Diminish (until your next turn, with flashback), Sower of Temptation (for as long as), Agent of Treachery (no stated duration). Not folded in: `TurnScopedReplacements`, ADR 0057's `TurnScopedGameEndGates` and ADR 0045's `TurnScopedBlockRules` still clear wholesale at cleanup, because no card needs a longer duration from them yet. Teferi, Time Raveler's +1 and Alela's goad still carry their caveats — both are "until your next turn" in prose and neither is a layer effect (a cast-timing permission, #760; a goad marker the engine does not enforce).
