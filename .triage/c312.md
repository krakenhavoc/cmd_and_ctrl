### Worth knowing before working this batch

- The roadmap's own warning applies with full force: *re-check the triage
  before working a batch, because the blockers move faster than the issues do.*
- **Cost modification (#93) is the one the roadmap's stale-detector note misses.**
  It is the #1 blocker in all three ranking tables and it closed 2026-09-11,
  the same day batches 21–40 were ranked.
- A card in the freed list can still turn out to need a seam that does not
  exist. The detectors err toward optimism by design; the card-file author
  is the final arbiter, and a skip is a legitimate outcome — declare it here.
- The #259 rule still governs: a simplification that makes a card **weaker**
  than printed is acceptable and must be declared; **stronger** is not, and
  the card gets skipped instead.
